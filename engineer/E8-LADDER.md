# E8-LADDER — the structural-capability ladder (pre-registration, DRAFT — pending user rulings)

**Status: DRAFT PRE-REGISTRATION. Committed for the user's ruling. Nothing is
scored under it.** The only execution this doc authorizes now is the **sizing-only
null-filter smoke** (§8): one 150-eval sizing-only run per drafted goal, to verify
each goal actually resists sizing before it is admitted to the ladder. The scored
ladder — the three (four) matched-budget arms of §4 at N seeds — **waits for the
user rulings in §7**. No arm beyond the null-filter is built or run here.

Written in the E6-BUDGET / E7-MOVES pre-registration shape: hypothesis, the object
the user rules on (the goal set), the arms, the metrics, the acceptance criterion
and the binding falsifier — all fixed **before** any scored eval — then, after a
user GO, run and append the outcome clearly marked post-hoc.

This rung is **engineer-line G-throughput work** (a measurement of the G2 structural
capability as a *rate*), and inherits every binding rule from its parents:

- **The primary metric is SPICE-minutes to first feasible** (ROADMAP §6,
  G0-FAIRNESS §4 addendum). That is the axis the ladder is scored on; evals and
  wall-clock are reported alongside.
- **The nudge policy (E7-MOVES §0) is binding here too.** No arm is fed a named
  circuit solution. The "expected structural answer class" column of the goal
  table (§3) is for the analyst's post-hoc reading ONLY and is **never** passed to
  any arm. Goals are authored from *measured ceilings*, not from known topology
  answers — see OQ-3.
- **Goldens GREEN before the first commit and after the last** (`python
  lna/ref/check_ref.py`); no write under `lna/`; append-only; two-line branch law.

## 0. What this rung measures, and how it differs from E-7

E-7 (G2) asked a **reachability** question on ONE wall (the D5 output-class wall):
*can* guided primitive-move search compose a non-class-A output stage, and does it
beat random at matched budget. Its smoke established the mechanics on the flagship;
the full tier is the binding G-vs-R read.

E-8 (this rung) asks a **throughput** question across MANY walls: given a *set* of
small structural gaps — each an existing reached spec plus one small extension
chosen so sizing alone plausibly fails — **how long does the program take to solve
each one**, in evals AND in SPICE-minutes, and **how many** does each arm solve at
a matched budget. It is the E-7 capability turned into a rate, measured on tasks
chosen so the simulations are fast (the in-house harness: op + sp + noise, ~2
ngspice calls/eval, seconds — E-6-BUDGET §3, PROTOCOL §2.1).

The two rungs share the primitive move repertoire ruled in E-7 (P1–P5, P7, plus
atomic `add_and_connect_device`; **P6 REJECTED**, E-7 Rulings 2026-08-20) and the
same nudge policy. E-8 does not re-open those rulings; it consumes them.

## 1. Purpose (stated before any number is seen)

> **Quantify the G2 capability — diagnosis-guided structural editing — as a
> THROUGHPUT number.** Per goal, and per arm: the number of evals and the number of
> SPICE-minutes to the first design that satisfies the goal's extended spec, on
> fast-simulating tasks. The ladder headline is the number of goals each arm solves
> at a matched budget, and the distribution of times-to-solve.**

The falsifiable content is in §6: whether **blame-guided** structural editing —
diagnosis auto-generated by `lna/blame.py` + `lna/binding_probe.py`, with *no
human diagnosis string* — solves more goals, faster, than **random-edit** at a
matched budget. If it does not, automatic diagnosis adds nothing over
structure-blind editing on this ladder, and the Q3 diagnosis→intervention
integration is refuted for it.

The purpose is deliberately **not** to beat tuned-main on its home turf (ROADMAP
§1 — that expectation carries no information). It is to measure how fast the
autonomous loop crosses a graded set of small structural steps beyond the specs it
has already reached.

## 5. Metrics

Recorded per **(goal, arm, seed)**, aggregated per (goal, arm):

- **PRIMARY: `spice_min_to_first_feasible`** — SPICE-minutes (Σ per-eval `sim_s/60`,
  from the `cost.wall_s` stamps `Env.evaluate` already records, G0-FAIRNESS §4.2)
  to the first design satisfying the goal's extended spec. Censored at the budget
  cap (recorded `null`) if never reached.
- **`evals_to_first_feasible`** — env-counted evals to the same point (the E-3-scale
  cost read).
- **`wall_min_to_first_feasible`** — wall-clock minutes (secondary; hardware-
  dependent, G0-FAIRNESS §4.3).
- **edit-count-to-solve** — number of composed primitive edits in the solving
  topology (0 for a sizing-only solve → flags a sizing-reachable goal).

**Ladder headline (two readings, both reported):**
1. **goals-solved count** per arm at the matched budget cap (the throughput number).
2. the **time distribution** — per arm, the set of `spice_min_to_first_feasible`
   over the solved goals (median + spread), so "solves more" and "solves faster"
   are separable.

A goal solved by the **sizing-only** arm is marked **sizing-reachable** and
excluded from the structural ladder score (kept in the doc as documented, §8).

## 6. Falsifier (pre-stated, before any scored eval)

> **If blame-guided editing (arm c) solves no more goals than random-edit (arm b)
> at the matched budget — and is no faster in SPICE-minutes on the goals both
> solve — then automatic diagnosis adds nothing over structure-blind editing on
> this ladder, and the Q3 diagnosis→intervention integration
> (`blame.py`+`binding_probe.py` → move prior) is REFUTED for this ladder.**

Secondary negatives, pre-stated so they are not read post-hoc:

- **If BOTH (b) and (c) solve ~zero structural goals** at the full budget, the
  ruled primitive repertoire (E-7 P1–P5/P7/`add_and_connect_device`) is itself
  insufficient for this graded set — the finding points at the repertoire (E-7 §3
  minimal-additional-primitive), not at the diagnosis.
- **If the sizing-only null solves many goals** (§8 shows them falling to sizing),
  the goal set was mis-authored — the deltas were too small to be structural — and
  the ladder is re-drawn with larger deltas before any scored run (this is exactly
  what the §8 smoke exists to catch **before** spending the scored budget).
- The **oracle arm (d)**, if run, calibrates: if (d) ≫ (c) ≈ (b), the ceiling is
  *diagnosis quality* (the auto-diagnosis is weak, but diagnosis in principle
  helps); if (d) ≈ (c) ≈ (b), the ceiling is the *repertoire or the search*, not
  the diagnosis.

## 8. The null-filter smoke — the ONLY execution authorized now

**What runs:** for each of the 11 goals, **one 150-eval sizing-only run** (arm a)
from the goal's reached design point, driven through `engineer/env.py`. Purpose:
verify each goal **resists** sizing — i.e. 150 evals of CMA-ES sizing the *pinned
reached topology* against the *goal's extended spec* does NOT reach a feasible
point. A goal that resists is a genuine structural goal; a goal that **falls to
sizing** is flagged **documented-sizing-reachable** and dropped from the ladder
score (kept here for the record).

**Containment.** The extended spec is applied WITHOUT writing any spec file: the env
is built on the *base* spec (real ngspice metrics per eval), and the goal's
**delta'd feasibility** is evaluated by the smoke runner's own arithmetic on the
env-produced `metrics`/`margins` (a modified in-memory constraint set). No write
under `lna/`; no new `lna/specs/*.yaml`. The runner lives only in
`.claude/jobs/a8f610e5/tmp`. CMA-ES optimizes the env's base ZOAF objective (which
already drives S21 up / Idd down / NF down / S11 deeper — the directions the deltas
need); the smoke asks whether *any* of its 150 evals clears the delta'd bar.

**Budget/parallelism.** 150 evals × 11 goals; ≤ 8 concurrent ngspice. Seeds: the
smoke runs seed 1 per goal (a resist/fall check, not a rate — §4.3 R-4 smoke).

**Scope limit (binding, inherited from E-6 §7 / E-7 §4.3):** *the smoke can REFUTE
a goal (show it falls to sizing) or validate that a goal resists; it CANNOT confirm
the §1 throughput hypothesis or the §6 falsifier — those are the scored tier.* A
goal that resists the 150-eval null is admitted as a *candidate* structural goal,
not proven to need structure at unbounded sizing budget.
