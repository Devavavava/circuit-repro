==== ANSWER t2-wb1020-gain-0161 ====
1. DIAGNOSIS
mu_min_wide is 0.895, and in band mu_min is only 1.06 and k_min 1.11.
In the push-pull core the NMOS and PMOS Cgd's add up, so reverse isolation is poor (S12 -24 dB). Both terminations are reactive:
- the gate is fed through the series inductor L1, so its source impedance is inductive;
- the drain sees L2+C2, which is inductive above its series resonance.
This tuned-input/tuned-output coupling through Cgd gives a negative real part out of band. The core has no resistive damping (s22_max is -3.7 dB, nearly reflective). Gain, NF and Idd are all at zero margin, so sizing cannot de-Q it.

2. EDIT: RC shunt feedback from drain to NMOS gate
Add RF plus a DC-block CF from n2 to n1. This gives broadband resistive damping at both ports, and the bias is unchanged.

```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R RF n2 n8
C CF n8 n1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```

3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide improves from 0.895 to about 1.05-1.10 (about +0.15).
Secondary effects:
- s22_max improves by about 3 to 5 dB.
- s21 drops by about 0.3 to 0.5 dB, which the sizer recovers through RF.

==== ANSWER t2-nb090-noise-0012 ====
1. DIAGNOSIS
mu_min_wide is 0.63, the worst margin by far.
The shunt-peaking inductor L1 makes the drain load inductive at high frequency. Through Cgd, an inductive drain load reflects a negative resistance onto the gate (the Miller effect with a phase-inverted inductive load), so the input becomes active out of band.
Gain is exactly at its 9.8 dB floor at the 6.1 mA cap. A single NMOS transconductor with a resistive feedback match has no gm headroom to trade for damping.

2. EDIT: current-reuse CMOS inverter with resistive feedback
Replace the shunt-peaked load L1+R2 with a PMOS whose gate is shared with the NMOS. R1 self-biases the inverter at mid-supply and sets the match.
- The drain load is now purely resistive (the ro's, R1 and 50 ohm), so the inductive Miller negative resistance is gone.
- gm doubles at the same current.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
PMOS PM1 n2 n1 VDD VDD
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```

3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide improves from 0.63 to about 1.1 (+0.45).
Secondary effects:
- s21 gains about 3 dB of headroom.
- NF stays around 1.5 dB.
