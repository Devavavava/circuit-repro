1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding failure is the input match (s11 = -4.9 dB). C1 connects directly to NM1's gate, so Zin at 900 MHz is Cgs in parallel with the large bias-feed resistor R2: strongly capacitive, with essentially no real part near 50 ohm. No element synthesizes a resistive input, and lowering R2 enough to terminate the source would blow the 2.02 dB NF budget. The mismatch also wastes transducer gain, so s21 (11.8 dB) and NF both sit at their gates.

2. EDIT: make stage 1 an inductively degenerated CS: a series gate inductor L4 after the DC block, plus a source inductor L5 from NM1's source to ground. This gives a noiseless Re(Zin) = wT*Ls (about 50 ohm), with Lg + Ls resonating Cgs at 900 MHz. To respect the 4-inductor limit, the interstage series L2 is removed: C2 couples the L1-tuned drain directly into NM2's gate, and L1 absorbs Cgs2. The mirror bias is unchanged.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L L4 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 n8 VSS
L L5 n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n6 n5 VSS VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```
3. PREDICTION: s11_max_db improves from -4.9 dB to about -12 dB (about 7 dB better; it passes -7.85). Side effects: s21 rises by about 3-5 dB and NF drops by about 0.2-0.4 dB (noise-optimal match, plus higher gate Q). mu_wide improves because the series L2/Cgs2 resonance is gone.
