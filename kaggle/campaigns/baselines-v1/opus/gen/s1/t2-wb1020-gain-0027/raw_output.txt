1. DIAGNOSIS
The worst failure is mu_min_wide at 0.880 out of band; in-band mu is 1.09. The current-reuse stage is all reactive outside the band. The input is the high-Q series L1-Cgs resonance, and the output is the high-impedance shared drain with the series L2-C2 trap (s22_max -4.7 dB). The only reverse path, Cgd of NM1 and PM1, sees nearly lossless terminations at both ends, so the device is potentially unstable somewhere in 0.01-50 GHz. Every in-band gate is at the edge, so sizing alone has no room left.

2. EDIT: add DC-blocked resistive shunt feedback RF-CF from the shared drain n2 to the NMOS gate n1. It adds broadband dissipation at both ports, lowers both |S11| and |S22| out of band, and broadens the match. The bias networks are unchanged.

```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
R RF n2 n7
C CF n7 n1
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```

3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide improves by about +0.15 to +0.2, from 0.88 to about 1.03-1.08. In-band mu_min rises to about 1.2. S21 drops about 0.5 dB at fixed gm. s11_max at the band edges improves by about 1-2 dB. NF moves by about +0.1 dB.
