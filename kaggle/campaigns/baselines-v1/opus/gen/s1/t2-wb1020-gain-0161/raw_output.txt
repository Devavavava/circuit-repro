1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding failure is wideband stability (mu_wide = 0.90). The output is high-Q and poorly matched (s22_max = -3.7 dB, delta_max = 0.44). The shared drain of the current-reuse pair is loaded by the series L2-C2 branch to ground. Above its series resonance that branch is inductive, so between the series and parallel resonances (multi-GHz) the drain sees a nearly lossless inductive load. Through the combined Cgd of NM1 + PM1 (both gates AC-tied to the input), the Miller term jwCgd(1 + gm*Z_L) gives the input a negative real part, and the series gate inductor L1 resonating with Cgs adds voltage gain at the gate. Out of band, |S12*S21| therefore exceeds the loss margin and mu < 1. Nothing resistive damps the load where L2 dominates.

2. EDIT: put a damping resistor R6 across L2 (n2 to n4). It is DC-isolated by C2, so the bias is unchanged. At high frequency the branch becomes about R6 to ground: a resistive HF termination that bounds the drain impedance, removes the inductive-load negative conductance, and lowers the output Q. That also flattens the 1-2 GHz response and improves s22.
```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
R R6 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```
3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz improves from 0.90 to about 1.10 (+0.2; it passes). s22_max improves by about 2-3 dB. Peak s21 drops by about 0.3 dB, but s21_min across the band holds because the response is flatter.
