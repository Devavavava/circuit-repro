1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding failure is wideband stability, narrowly (mu_wide = 0.988). The output is high-Q (s22_max = -3.7 dB, delta_max = 0.32), and ripple is pinned at the gate. The current-reuse drain is loaded by a series L2-C2 branch to ground. Above that branch's series resonance it is inductive and almost lossless. The NM1 + PM1 Cgd (gates AC-tied via C1) then gives a Miller admittance jwCgd(1 + gm*Z_L) with a negative real part, and the series gate inductor L1 provides resonant gate-voltage gain. In that out-of-band window mu dips just below 1.

2. EDIT: add a damping resistor R6 across L2 (n2 to n4). The bias is unchanged (C2 blocks DC). At high frequency the branch becomes a resistive termination to ground, so the drain impedance can't turn strongly inductive, and the output Q drops. That improves both s22 and the in-band peaking that is setting the ripple.
```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
R R6 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```
3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz improves from 0.99 to about 1.15 (+0.15; it passes). s22_max improves by about 2-3 dB, and ripple drops by about 0.2 dB. s21 costs about 0.2 dB, which the sizer recovers with the Idd margin (0.08 mA spare).
