1. DIAGNOSIS
mu_min_wide is 0.92, and in band mu and k are only about 1.03.
In the push-pull core the summed NMOS and PMOS Cgd give S12 of only -25.6 dB. Both terminations are reactive:
- the gate is fed through the series inductor L1, so its source impedance is inductive;
- the drain sees the L2+C2 branch, which is inductive above its series resonance.
This TITO coupling makes a negative-resistance region away from 900 MHz. The output is nearly reflective (s22 -4.5 dB), so there is no broadband loss to damp it. NF and gain are at zero margin.

2. EDIT: RC shunt feedback from drain to NMOS gate
Add RF plus a DC-block CF from n2 to n1. This provides broadband resistive damping of both ports and a better s22, and leaves the bias unchanged. s11 has slack (0.18) to absorb the change in Rin.

```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R RF n2 n8
C CF n8 n1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```

3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide improves from 0.92 to about 1.08 (+0.15).
Secondary effects:
- In-band mu rises to about 1.2.
- s22 improves by about 3 dB.
- s21 drops by about 0.3 dB.
- NF rises by about 0.05 dB.
The sizer can recover the last two with a large RF.
