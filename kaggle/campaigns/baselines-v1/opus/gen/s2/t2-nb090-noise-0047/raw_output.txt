1. DIAGNOSIS
mu_min_wide is 0.68. The shunt-peaking inductor L1 makes the drain load inductive at high frequency. Through Cgd that reflects a negative input resistance onto the gate, so the circuit is potentially unstable out of band.
Gain is also 0.65 dB short (9.34 vs 9.99 dB) at the 5.9 mA cap. One NMOS cannot provide enough gm per mA while R1 holds the 50-ohm match.

2. EDIT: CMOS inverter (current-reuse) resistive-feedback LNA
Replace L1+R2 with a PMOS whose gate is shared with the input. R1 self-biases the inverter and sets the match. The load becomes purely resistive, and gm doubles at the same current.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
PMOS PM1 n2 n1 VDD VDD
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```

3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide improves from 0.68 to about 1.1 (+0.4).
Secondary effects:
- s21 rises by about 3 dB, to about 12 dB.
- NF stays around 1.6 to 1.8 dB.
