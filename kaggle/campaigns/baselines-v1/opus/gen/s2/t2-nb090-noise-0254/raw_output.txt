1. DIAGNOSIS
s11_max_db is -3.0 dB because NM1's gate is connected to the port through only a DC block. The input is a capacitor (Cgs plus the Miller Cgd) with no controllable real part. The only real part is the noisy bias feed R2, and NF (1.349 vs a 1.349 cap) will not allow lowering it.
The poor match also wastes the gate voltage gain, so s21 is 5.5 dB short.
mu_min_wide is 0.80 because stage 2 is a TITO configuration: the series inductor L2 on its gate and the L3 tank on its drain, coupled through Cgd.

2. EDIT: inductive source degeneration plus gate inductor on NM1, cascodes on both stages, interstage series L removed
This keeps 4 inductors. The edit provides a noiseless 50-ohm real input at resonance and kills the Cgd feedback.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L LG n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n8 n1 n9 VSS
L LS n9 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n10 n5 VSS VSS
NMOS NM5 n6 VDD n10 VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```

3. PREDICTION
s11_max_db improves from -3.0 dB to about -10 dB (about 7 dB better).
Secondary effects:
- s21 rises by about 4 to 5 dB.
- mu_min_wide rises from 0.80 to above 1.
- NF stays roughly flat.
