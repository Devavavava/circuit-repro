1. DIAGNOSIS
s11_max_db is -3.9 dB. NM1's gate goes to the port through only a DC block, so the input is capacitive (Cgs plus the Miller Cgd). Its only real part is the bias feed R2, which the NF cap (1.718 dB, at its limit) forbids lowering. Without an inductor and a noiseless real part, Cgs cannot be resonated to 50 ohms.
mu_min_wide is 0.83 because stage 2 is a TITO configuration (series inductor L2 on its gate, L3 tank on its drain, coupled through Cgd).

2. EDIT: gate inductor plus source-degeneration inductor on NM1, cascodes on both stages, interstage series L removed
This keeps 4 inductors.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L LG n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n8 n1 n9 VSS
L LS n9 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n10 n5 VSS VSS
NMOS NM5 n6 VDD n10 VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```

3. PREDICTION
s11_max_db improves from -3.9 dB to about -10.5 dB (about 6.5 dB better).
Secondary effects:
- s21 gains about 3 dB of headroom.
- mu_min_wide rises above 1.
- NF stays roughly flat.
