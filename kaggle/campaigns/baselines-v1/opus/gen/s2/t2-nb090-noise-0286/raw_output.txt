1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is s11 (-3.7 dB against -7.85 dB), and s21 is also 5.7 dB short (13.8 against 19.4 dB). Stage 1's input is just C1 onto a bare gate: Cgs in parallel with the bias R2, purely capacitive with no 50-ohm real part. So the input reflects most of the incident power, and that same mismatch wastes gain because there is no resonant voltage boost at the gate. Stage 1's Cgd also gives Miller loading at the input, and its loaded output impedance at L1 limits stage-1 gain. With NF pinned at 1.50 dB and Idd at its cap, the sizer cannot fix either problem by resizing.

2. EDIT (combined input-stage rework):
(a) Inductively degenerated input. LS on NM1's source gives the real part wT*LS of about 50 ohms, and LG in series with the gate resonates Cgs at f0. This sets the match and gives a gate voltage gain of Q.
(b) Cascode NM4 (gate at VDD) on stage 1. It removes Cgd Miller loading from the match, raises the impedance at the L1 tank (more stage-1 gain), and keeps reverse isolation high.
(c) The interstage L2 is removed to respect the 4-inductor limit: C2 couples the L1 tank straight to NM2's gate.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L LG n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n9 n1 n8 VSS
L LS n8 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n9 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n6 n5 VSS VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```
3. PREDICTION: s11_max_db improves from -3.7 dB to about -12 to -15 dB (around 9-11 dB better). s21 rises by about 5-6 dB, to roughly 19-20 dB, from the input Q boost plus the cascode's higher tank impedance. NF stays near 1.4-1.5 dB.
