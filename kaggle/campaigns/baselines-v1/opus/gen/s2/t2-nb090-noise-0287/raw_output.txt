1. DIAGNOSIS
s11_max_db is -4.9 dB. NM1's gate goes straight to the port through a DC block, so the input is mainly capacitive (Cgs plus the Miller Cgd). Its only real part is the bias feed R2, which NF (at its 2.02 dB cap) will not let the sizer reduce. A capacitive input cannot be resonated to 50 ohms without an inductor and a noiseless real part.
mu_min_wide is 0.85 because stage 2 is a TITO configuration (series inductor L2 on its gate, L3 tank on its drain, coupled through Cgd).

2. EDIT: inductively degenerated input stage plus cascodes
Add a gate inductor LG and a source-degeneration inductor LS on NM1, cascode both stages (gates to VDD), and remove the interstage series L2 to keep 4 inductors.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L LG n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n8 n1 n9 VSS
L LS n9 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n10 n5 VSS VSS
NMOS NM5 n6 VDD n10 VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```

3. PREDICTION
s11_max_db improves from -4.9 dB to about -11 dB (about 6 dB better).
Secondary effects:
- s21 gains about 3 dB of headroom.
- mu_min_wide rises above 1.
- NF stays roughly flat or improves slightly.
