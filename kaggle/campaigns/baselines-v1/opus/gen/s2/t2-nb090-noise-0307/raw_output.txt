1. DIAGNOSIS
s11_max_db is -4.9 dB. The first-stage gate is driven through a DC block with no matching network, so the input is capacitive (Cgs plus the Miller Cgd). Its only real part is the noisy bias feed R2, and the NF cap (2.018 dB, at its limit) blocks using that resistor to match.
mu_min_wide is 0.77 because stage 2 is a TITO configuration (series inductor L2 on its gate, L3 tank on its drain, coupled through Cgd).

2. EDIT: inductively degenerated, cascoded first stage, stage 2 cascoded, interstage series L removed
This keeps 4 inductors.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L LG n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n8 n1 n9 VSS
L LS n9 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n10 n5 VSS VSS
NMOS NM5 n6 VDD n10 VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```

3. PREDICTION
s11_max_db improves from -4.9 dB to about -11 dB (about 6 dB better).
Secondary effects:
- s21 gains about 3 dB of headroom.
- mu_min_wide rises above 1.
- NF stays roughly flat.
