1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is s11 (-3.1 dB against -7.88 dB), and s21 is also 4 dB short (14.8 against 18.8 dB). Stage 1's input is C1 straight onto NM1's gate, whose impedance is Cgs in parallel with the bias R2. That is reactive and high-impedance, with no element that produces 50 ohms of real part. The mismatch reflects most of the incident power and gives up the resonant gate-voltage gain, which costs both s11 and s21. NF at 1.34 dB and Idd are pinned, so R2 cannot be lowered and current cannot be raised.

2. EDIT (input-stage rework):
(a) Source-degeneration inductor LS plus series gate inductor LG on NM1. This gives a real input impedance of wT*LS, about 50 ohms, with no added noise, resonance at 900 MHz, and a Q gain boost at the gate.
(b) Cascode NM4 (gate at VDD) on stage 1. It eliminates Cgd Miller loading, so the match is clean, and raises the impedance at the L1 tank for more stage-1 gain at the same current.
(c) The interstage L2 is removed to stay within 4 inductors: C2 couples the L1 tank directly to NM2's gate.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L LG n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n9 n1 n8 VSS
L LS n8 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n9 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n6 n5 VSS VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```
3. PREDICTION: s11_max_db improves from -3.1 dB to about -12 to -15 dB (around 9-11 dB better). s21 rises by about 4-5 dB, to roughly 19 dB. NF stays near 1.3 dB.
