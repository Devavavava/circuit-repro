1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is s11 (-2.8 dB against -7.84 dB), and s21 is also 4 dB short (14.9 against 18.9 dB). Stage 1's input is C1 onto a bare gate: Cgs in parallel with the bias R2, almost purely capacitive, with nothing that produces a 50-ohm real part. The input therefore reflects most of the power. That same mismatch removes the resonant voltage gain an LC-matched input would give at the gate. Stage 1's Cgd also adds Miller capacitance and limits the impedance seen at the L1 tank. With NF (1.27 dB) and Idd both pinned, resizing cannot fix either problem.

2. EDIT (input-stage rework):
(a) Inductive degeneration LS plus series gate inductor LG on NM1. This gives a noiseless real part wT*LS of about 50 ohms, resonance at f0, and a Q voltage boost at the gate.
(b) Cascode NM4 (gate at VDD) on stage 1. It removes Cgd Miller loading from the input match and raises the impedance at the L1 tank for more stage-1 gain, at no extra current.
(c) The interstage L2 is removed to respect the 4-inductor limit: C2 couples the L1 tank directly to NM2's gate.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L LG n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n9 n1 n8 VSS
L LS n8 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n9 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n6 n5 VSS VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```
3. PREDICTION: s11_max_db improves from -2.8 dB to about -12 to -15 dB (around 10 dB better). s21 rises by about 4-5 dB, to roughly 19-20 dB. NF stays around 1.2-1.3 dB.
