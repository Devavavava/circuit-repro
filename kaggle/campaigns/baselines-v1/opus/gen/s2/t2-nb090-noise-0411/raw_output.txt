1. DIAGNOSIS
s11_max_db is -1.85 dB, far from the -7.9 dB gate. The first stage is a bare common-source gate driven through a DC block, so the input impedance at 900 MHz is essentially Cgs plus the Miller-multiplied Cgd, with almost no real part.
The only resistance available is the bias feed R2 and parasitic gate resistance. Making R2 small enough to match would destroy NF, which is already at its 1.13 dB cap, so the sizer is stuck.
The lack of match also costs the voltage gain at the gate, which is why s21 is 7 dB short (14.8 vs 21.8).
mu_min_wide is 0.78 because stage 2 is a TITO configuration: the series inductor L2 on its gate and the L3 tank on its drain, coupled through Cgd.

2. EDIT: inductively degenerated input plus cascoded stages
- Add a series gate inductor LG and a source-degeneration inductor LS on NM1. This gives a noiseless real part (gm*LS/Cgs) that resonates with Cgs at f0.
- Add common-gate cascodes (gates to VDD) on both stages.
- Remove the interstage series L2 (direct C2 coupling), keeping 4 inductors.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L LG n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n8 n1 n9 VSS
L LS n9 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n10 n5 VSS VSS
NMOS NM5 n6 VDD n10 VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```

3. PREDICTION
s11_max_db improves from -1.85 dB to about -10 dB (roughly 8 dB better).
Secondary effects:
- s21 rises by about 4 to 6 dB from the gate Q-boost and the higher cascode load resistance.
- mu_min_wide rises above 1.
- NF stays roughly flat to slightly better.
