1. DIAGNOSIS
mu_min_wide is 0.82. In the push-pull core the summed NMOS and PMOS Cgd give poor isolation (S12 -25 dB). The gate termination is inductive through the series L1, and the drain load is inductive through L2+C2 above its series resonance. This tuned-input/tuned-output coupling makes a negative-resistance region out of band.
The core has no broadband resistive loss. NF, s11, gain and Idd are all at zero margin, so the sizer cannot de-Q it.

2. EDIT: RC shunt feedback from drain to NMOS gate
Add RF plus a DC-block CF from n2 to n1. This adds broadband resistive damping at both ports and leaves the bias unchanged.

```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R RF n2 n8
C CF n8 n1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```

3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide improves from 0.82 to about 1.05 (+0.2).
Secondary effects:
- s22 improves by about 3 dB.
- s21 drops by about 0.5 dB.
- NF rises by about 0.1 dB.
The last two are recoverable through RF sizing.
