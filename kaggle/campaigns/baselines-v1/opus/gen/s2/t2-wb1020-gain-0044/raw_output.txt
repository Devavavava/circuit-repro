1. DIAGNOSIS
mu_min_wide is 0.71 and s21 is 1.1 dB short.
The shunt-peaking inductor L1 makes the drain load inductive at high frequency. Through Cgd that reflects a negative resistance onto the gate, so the input reflection exceeds 1 out of band.
The single NMOS at the 8.8 mA cap is gm-per-mA limited for 10.6 dB with a feedback match.

2. EDIT: CMOS inverter (current-reuse) resistive-feedback LNA
Replace L1+R2 with a PMOS whose gate is shared with the input, self-biased through R1. The drain load becomes resistive, and gm doubles at the same current.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
PMOS PM1 n2 n1 VDD VDD
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```

3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide improves from 0.71 to about 1.1 (+0.4).
Secondary effects:
- s21 rises by about 3 dB.
- NF stays around 2 dB.
- Ripple stays below 0.5 dB because the load is flat and resistive.
