1. DIAGNOSIS
mu_min_wide is 0.982. The push-pull core has double Cgd feedback (S12 -26 dB) and reactive terminations at both ports:
- the series input inductor L1 makes the gate termination inductive;
- the L2+C2 drain branch is inductive above its series resonance.
This TITO-type coupling gives a negative-resistance region out of band. There is no broadband resistive damping (s22_max -5.2 dB). Gain, NF, s11 and Idd are all at zero margin, so the sizer cannot reduce the reactive Q.

2. EDIT: RC shunt feedback from drain to NMOS gate
Add RF plus a DC-block CF from n2 to n1. This gives broadband resistive damping and a better s22, and leaves the bias unchanged.

```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R RF n2 n8
C CF n8 n1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```

3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide improves from 0.982 to about 1.10 (+0.1).
Secondary effects:
- s22_max improves by about 3 dB.
- s21 drops by less than about 0.4 dB, which the sizer can recover.
