1. DIAGNOSIS
mu_min_wide is 0.951. The push-pull core has summed Cgd feedback (S12 -26 dB) and reactive terminations: an inductive gate termination through the series L1, and an inductive drain branch (L2+C2 above its series resonance). This TITO coupling makes a negative-resistance region out of band.
The core has no resistive damping (s22_max -4.2 dB). Gain, s11 and Idd are at zero margin.

2. EDIT: RC shunt feedback from drain to NMOS gate
Add RF plus a DC-block CF from n2 to n1. This gives broadband resistive damping, and the bias is unchanged.

```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R RF n2 n8
C CF n8 n1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```

3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide improves from 0.951 to about 1.10 (+0.15).
Secondary effects:
- s22_max improves by about 3 to 4 dB.
- s21 drops by less than about 0.4 dB, which the sizer recovers.
