1. DIAGNOSIS
mu_min_wide is 0.954. In band the amplifier is only marginally stable (mu_min 1.08, k_min 1.2), and out of band it becomes potentially unstable.
The cause is the reactive terminations of a poorly isolated push-pull core. The NMOS and PMOS Cgd's add up (S12 is only -26 dB). The gate is fed through the series inductor L1, which gives an inductive source impedance. The drain sees the L2+C2 branch, which is inductive above its series resonance. Together these form a TITO negative-resistance mechanism.
The output is nearly reflective (s22_max -3.3 dB) and the core has no resistive damping. NF, gain and Idd are all at zero margin, so the sizer cannot lower Q any further.

2. EDIT: RC shunt feedback from drain to NMOS gate
Add RF in series with a DC-block CF from n2 to n1. This provides broadband resistive damping of both ports and does not change the bias.

```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R RF n2 n8
C CF n8 n1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```

3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide improves from 0.954 to about 1.10 (+0.15).
Secondary effects:
- s22_max improves by about 3 to 5 dB.
- s21 drops by less than about 0.5 dB, which the sizer can recover with a large RF.
- NF rises by less than 0.1 dB.
