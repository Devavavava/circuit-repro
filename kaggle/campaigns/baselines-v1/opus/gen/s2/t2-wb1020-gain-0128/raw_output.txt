1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is broadband stability (mu over 0.01-50 GHz = 0.917). In-band mu is only 1.03 and k_min is 1.08, so the circuit sits at the stability edge even in band. The series gate inductor L1 resonates with the large combined inverter gate capacitance (NM1, plus PM1 through C1) with almost no resistive damping. The output node is weakly loaded and reactive (s22_max -3.7 dB): L2 to AC ground through C2, in parallel with drain capacitance. Above the band it turns inductive, and the combined Cgd reflects that as negative conductance at the gate, so the input-loop reflection exceeds 1 at some multi-GHz frequency. NF, s21, s11 and Idd margins are all essentially zero, so the fix must be nearly free in band.

2. EDIT: put a damping resistor RD in parallel with the gate inductor L1. In the 1-2 GHz band, w*L1 is much smaller than RD, so the signal goes through L1 and in-band NF and gain are almost unchanged. Out of band, w*L1 is much larger than RD, so RD sets a resistive gate drive and kills the Q of the input resonance that the Cgd-coupled negative resistance would otherwise excite.
```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
R RD n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```
3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz improves from 0.917 to about 1.03-1.1 (+0.1 to +0.18), and in-band mu also rises to about 1.1. In-band s21 and NF should change by less than 0.15 dB.
