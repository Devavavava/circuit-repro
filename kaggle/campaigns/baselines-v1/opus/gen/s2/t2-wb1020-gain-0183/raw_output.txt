1. DIAGNOSIS
mu_min_wide is 0.71 and s21 is 2.4 dB short.
The shunt-peaked load (L1 in series with R2) becomes inductive at high frequency. Through Cgd it reflects a negative resistance into the gate, so the input reflection exceeds 1 out of band.
A single NMOS at the 9.3 mA cap cannot reach 12 dB while R1 holds the 50-ohm match. The limit is gm per mA.

2. EDIT: CMOS inverter (current-reuse) resistive-feedback LNA
Remove the inductive load and add a PMOS sharing the input gate, self-biased through R1. This doubles gm at the same Idd and makes the load purely resistive.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
PMOS PM1 n2 n1 VDD VDD
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```

3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide improves from 0.71 to about 1.1 (+0.4).
Secondary effects:
- s21 rises by about 3 dB, to 12.5 dB or more.
- NF stays around 2 dB.
