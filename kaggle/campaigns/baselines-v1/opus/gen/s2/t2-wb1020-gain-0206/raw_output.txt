1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is broadband stability (mu over 0.01-50 GHz = 0.66; in-band mu is 1.14). The shunt-peaked load (L1 + R2) on the single common-source device NM1 turns inductive above the band. Through Cgd, that inductive drain impedance reflects as negative conductance at the gate. At those frequencies Cgd bypasses the resistive feedback R1, so nothing damps it, and |s11| or |s22| exceeds the passivity bound somewhere in the multi-GHz to tens-of-GHz range. Gain is pinned at its limit, so the sizer cannot shrink L1 enough to remove the inductive region.

2. EDIT: add a common-gate cascode NM2 (gate at VDD) between NM1's drain and the load, with feedback R1 taken from the cascode drain (the output). The cascode source presents a low resistive 1/gm2 to NM1's Cgd, which breaks the Miller-inductive negative-resistance loop and improves internal reverse isolation.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n8 n1 VSS VSS
NMOS NM2 n2 VDD n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n3
R R2 n3 n2
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```
3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz improves from 0.66 to about 1.0-1.1 (+0.35 to +0.45). s21 rises by about 1 dB at the same current.
