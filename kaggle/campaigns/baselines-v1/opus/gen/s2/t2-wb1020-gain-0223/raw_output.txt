1. DIAGNOSIS
mu_min_wide is 0.65. The shunt-peaked load (L1 in series with R2) is inductive at high frequency. Through Cgd it produces a negative input resistance at the gate, so there is potential instability out of band.
s21 is 0.6 dB short at the 6.7 mA cap, a gm-per-mA limit of the single-NMOS feedback stage.

2. EDIT: CMOS inverter (current-reuse) resistive-feedback LNA
Replace the inductive load with a PMOS sharing the input gate, self-biased through R1. The load is resistive and gm doubles at the same current.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
PMOS PM1 n2 n1 VDD VDD
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```

3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide improves from 0.65 to about 1.1 (+0.45).
Secondary effects:
- s21 rises by about 3 dB.
- NF stays around 2 dB, under the 3.3 dB cap.
