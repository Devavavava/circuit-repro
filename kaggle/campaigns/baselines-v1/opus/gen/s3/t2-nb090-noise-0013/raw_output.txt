1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is out-of-band stability: mu over 0.01-50 GHz is 0.916, while in-band mu is 1.03. NF is at its gate and S21 is just short. The shared output of the current-reuse pair drives only ro_n||ro_p and an inductive shunt tank (L2 to ground through DC-block C2). That tank is high Q and essentially lossless, which S22 of about -4.6 dB confirms. Where it is inductive, the Cgd's of NM1 and PM1 reflect a negative conductance onto the gate (about -w^2*(gm_n+gm_p)*Cgd*L2). That negative conductance meets the high-Q series resonance of L1 with the gate capacitance, and mu falls below 1 off band.

2. EDIT: a de-Q resistor R6 across the output tank inductor L2, kept off the DC path by C2. Out of band, the output branch becomes a bounded resistance, which damps the inductive Miller drive and the tank resonance. In band, R6 can be large, and its noise is negligible because it is referred through the stage gain, which matters because NF is pinned.
```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
R R6 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```
3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz improves from 0.92 to about 1.05-1.1 (+0.15). S22 improves by about 2-3 dB. S21 falls by about 0.2 dB, and NF rises by about 0.02 dB or less.
