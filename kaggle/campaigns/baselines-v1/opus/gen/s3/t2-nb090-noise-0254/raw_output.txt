1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is the input match: S11 is -3.0 dB. The stage-1 gate is driven only through the DC-block C1, with a large bias-feed resistor R2 to the mirror. Zin is therefore about 1/(jw*Cgs) in parallel with R2: mostly reactive, with no 50-ohm real part, so the sizer cannot match it. The resulting mismatch loss and the lack of input-network voltage gain also leave S21 5.5 dB short (14.6 vs 20.1 dB). The structure lacks the classical noiseless matching pair: Lg to resonate Cgs, and Ls to synthesize Re{Zin} = wT*Ls = 50 ohm.

2. EDIT: an inductively degenerated stage 1, with Lg in series with the gate and Ls from the NM1 source to ground. To respect the 4-inductor limit, the interstage series inductor L2 is removed and C2 couples the L1-tuned drain straight to the NM2 gate. Bias is unchanged.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L Lg n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 ns VSS
L Ls ns VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n6 n5 VSS VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```
3. PREDICTION: S11_max improves from -3.0 dB to about -11 dB (+8 dB). S21 rises by about 4-6 dB, toward the 20 dB gate. NF stays at about 1.2-1.4 dB. Wideband mu gains about +0.1-0.2 from the Ls degeneration.
