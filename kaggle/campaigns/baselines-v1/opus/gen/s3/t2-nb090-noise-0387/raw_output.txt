1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding s11_max_db (-5.00 dB vs -7.85 dB) is structural. C1 drives the bare NM1 gate, and the only shunt element is the bias resistor R2. Zin is roughly R2 || 1/(jwCgs): capacitive at 900 MHz, with no noiseless 50-ohm real part and no resonating inductor.
Matching with R2 would add noise, and NF is pinned at its gate (2.179 vs 2.1814 dB). So the sizer accepts |Gamma| of about 0.56. s21 is also marginally short (13.95 vs 13.99 dB) from the mismatch loss. Stability is fine in band (mu 2.38).

2. EDIT: an inductively degenerated stage-1 input.
- Series gate inductor L4 resonates Cgs.
- Source inductor L5 sets a noiseless 50-ohm real part.
- Interstage L2 is removed (4-inductor screen). C2 couples directly to NM2, and L1 tunes the interstage node.
```netlist
* lna-a4-twostage edit: inductively degenerated stage-1 input (Lg=L4, Ls=L5), C-coupled interstage
C C1 VIN1 n0
L L4 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 n8 VSS
L L5 n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n6 n5 VSS VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```
3. PREDICTION
- Binding metric: s11_max_db improves from -5.0 dB to about -12 dB (~7 dB better), clearing the gate.
- s21: rises ~+2 to +4 dB.
- NF: moves by roughly +/-0.2 dB.
- mu: stays >1.
