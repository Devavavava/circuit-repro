1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is out-of-band stability: mu over 0.01-50 GHz is 0.82, while in-band mu is 1.21. NF, S11, S21 and Idd are all pinned at their gates. The shared output of the current-reuse inverter is an inductive shunt tank (L2 to ground through DC-block C2) with only ro_n||ro_p as loss. Where that load is inductive, the Cgd's of NM1 and PM1 turn it into a negative conductance at the gate (about -w^2*(gm_n+gm_p)*Cgd*L2). That conductance faces the high-Q series resonance of L1 with the gate capacitance. Because nothing resistively damps the output tank or bridges output to input, mu falls below 1 off band.

2. EDIT: a de-Q resistor R6 across the output tank inductor L2, kept off the DC path by C2. Out of band, the output impedance becomes bounded and resistive, which suppresses the inductive Miller drive and damps the L2 resonance. In band, R6 can be large. Its noise is referred through the stage gain, which matters because NF is pinned.
```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
R R6 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```
3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz improves from 0.82 to about 1.05 (+0.2 to +0.25). S22 improves by about 2 dB. S21 falls by about 0.2-0.3 dB at fixed sizing, and NF rises by about 0.03 dB or less.
