1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding metric is mu_min_wide = 0.974, even though in-band mu is 1.10. The current-reuse inverter has no resistive damping:
- The gate is driven through the series inductor L1, an inductive source.
- The output n2 sees ro_n||ro_p plus the L2-C2 branch, which turns inductive above its series resonance.
- So the output is poorly loaded (s22_max -2.96 dB).
Out of band (multi-GHz), inductive gate and inductive drain terminations coupled through Cgd_n + Cgd_p (s12 -25.9 dB) form a tuned-gate/tuned-drain negative-resistance mechanism, and mu drops below 1. Every other gate (s21, NF, s11, Idd) is pinned at its limit, so the sizer cannot trade gain or Q for damping.

2. EDIT: add resistive shunt feedback R6 from the output n2 to the NMOS gate n1, and self-bias NM1 through it.
- This removes the NMOS mirror reference R1/NM3/R2; its current returns to the core.
- R6 adds broadband loss linking both ports, which lowers |s22| and removes the out-of-band negative conductance.
- L1 keeps the in-band resonant match.
```netlist
* lna-a2-current-reuse edit: resistive shunt feedback R6 (out->NMOS gate), NM1 self-biased via R6
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R6 n2 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```
3. PREDICTION
- Binding metric: mu_min_wide improves from 0.974 to ~1.15-1.3 (about +0.2).
- s22_max: improves by ~4 dB.
- s21: changes by roughly -0.5 to +0.5 dB.
- NF: rises ~+0.15 dB (inside the small margin, helped by removing the R2 gate-feed noise).
