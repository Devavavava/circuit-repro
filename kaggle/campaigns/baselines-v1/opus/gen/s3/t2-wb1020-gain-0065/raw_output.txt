1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding metric is mu_min_wide = 0.886. In band the amplifier is comfortably stable (mu_min 1.47, s22_max -7.95 dB), so the instability is purely out of band.
Above the band, the series gate inductor L1 presents an inductive source to the inverter gates, and the L2-C2 output branch turns inductive above its series resonance. Through Cgd_n + Cgd_p (s12 -26.8 dB), this tuned-gate/tuned-drain arrangement produces negative port resistance at multi-GHz. Nothing resistive in the circuit damps those frequencies.

2. EDIT: add resistive shunt feedback R6 from the output n2 to the NMOS gate n1, and self-bias NM1 through it.
- This removes the NMOS mirror reference R1/NM3/R2 and its wasted current.
- R6 gives frequency-independent damping between the two ports, removing the out-of-band negative conductance.
```netlist
* lna-a2-current-reuse edit: resistive shunt feedback R6 (out->NMOS gate), NM1 self-biased via R6
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R6 n2 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```
3. PREDICTION
- Binding metric: mu_min_wide improves from 0.886 to ~1.15-1.3 (about +0.3).
- In-band mu: stays above 1.4.
- s21: changes by roughly -0.5 to +0.5 dB.
- NF: rises ~+0.1 dB.
