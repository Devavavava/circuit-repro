1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is out-of-band stability: mu over 0.01-50 GHz is 0.70, while in-band mu is 1.13. S21 is also 3.2 dB short (9.74 vs 12.99 dB) at the Idd cap, and NF has a large margin (1.41 vs 4.55 dB). The single CS device sees an inductive shunt-peaked drain load (L1 in series with R2), and its Cgd turns this into a negative gate conductance above the band (about -w^2*gm*Cgd*L1). This grows with gm*Z_L, so the optimizer cannot raise gain without losing wideband mu. The low gm*ro of the single device driving the port directly further limits S21.

2. EDIT: cascode the input device with a common-gate NM2 whose gate is on VDD (AC ground). This isolates NM1's Cgd from the inductive load (it now sees about 1/gm2), puts NM2's Cgd to AC ground, and raises output resistance and reverse isolation. The resistive shunt feedback and its self-bias are kept.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 nc n1 VSS VSS
NMOS NM2 n2 VDD nc VSS
L L1 VDD n3
R R2 n3 n2
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```
3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz improves from 0.70 to about 1.1-1.2 (+0.4 to +0.5). S21 improves by about 1-1.5 dB.
