1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding metric is mu_min_wide = 0.895, and in-band mu and k are exactly 1.0000 (the sizer is riding the stability edge). The current-reuse inverter has no resistive damping:
- The gate is driven through series L1, an inductive source.
- The output n2 is loaded only by ro_n||ro_p and the L2-C2 branch, which is inductive above its series resonance (s22_max -3.16 dB).
Inductive gate and inductive drain coupled through Cgd_n + Cgd_p (s12 -25.2 dB) give tuned-gate/tuned-drain negative resistance out of band. Because s21 is wanted higher (13.42 vs >=13.63 dB), the sizer cannot reduce gain to buy stability; both fail together.

2. EDIT: add resistive shunt feedback R6 from the output n2 to the NMOS gate n1, and self-bias NM1 through it.
- This removes the NMOS mirror reference R1/NM3/R2 and returns its current to the core.
- R6 provides broadband loss linking the two ports, removing the negative conductance and lowering |s22|.
- L1 keeps the in-band match.
```netlist
* lna-a2-current-reuse edit: resistive shunt feedback R6 (out->NMOS gate), NM1 self-biased via R6
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R6 n2 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```
3. PREDICTION
- Binding metric: mu_min_wide improves from 0.895 to ~1.1-1.25 (about +0.25). In-band mu rises to about 1.3.
- s21: changes by roughly -0.3 to +0.5 dB; reclaimed current and freed stability margin should let it reach 13.6 dB.
- NF: rises ~+0.1 dB.
