1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is out-of-band stability: mu over 0.01-50 GHz is 0.90, while in-band mu is 1.06. NF, S11, S21 and Idd are pinned at their gates. The current-reuse output node drives an essentially lossless inductive tank (L2 via C2 to ground), with only ro_n||ro_p in parallel, so S22_max is -3.7 dB. Through the Cgd's of NM1 and PM1, this inductive load produces a negative conductance at the gate (about -w^2*(gm_n+gm_p)*Cgd*L2). That conductance faces the high-Q series-resonant input (L1 with the gate capacitance). With no resistive damping element, mu drops below 1 off band.

2. EDIT: a de-Q resistor R6 across the output tank inductor L2, AC-coupled through C2 and therefore off the DC path. Out of band, the output impedance is bounded and resistive, which damps the inductive Miller drive and the L2 resonance. In band, R6 can stay large, so the S21 and NF costs are small, which matters because NF is pinned.
```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
R R6 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```
3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz improves from 0.90 to about 1.05-1.1 (+0.15 to +0.2). S22_max improves by about 2-3 dB. S21 falls by about 0.2-0.3 dB at fixed sizing, and NF rises by about 0.03 dB or less.
