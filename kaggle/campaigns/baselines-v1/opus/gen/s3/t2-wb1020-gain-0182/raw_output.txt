1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding metric is mu_min_wide (0.01-50 GHz) = 0.709, with in-band mu 1.14. The single common-source NM1 has its Cgd bridging the gate to a drain whose shunt-peaked load (R2+L1) becomes inductive at multi-GHz. Miller feedback of an inductive load through Cgd creates negative input/output conductance.
The R1 feedback path also gives large reverse transmission (s12 -20 dB), pushing |s12*s21| up. The sizer can only keep in-band stability by capping gain, so s21 also fails (9.49 vs >=9.88 dB) while Idd and s11 are pinned.

2. EDIT: cascode NM2 (gate at VDD) between NM1's drain and the output/peaking node, keeping R1 feedback from the output to NM1's gate.
- This shields NM1's Cgd from the inductive load and removes the negative-conductance mechanism.
- It also raises the output resistance, and with it the loop gain.
```netlist
* lna-a3-shunt-feedback edit: cascode NM2 (gate at VDD) inside the R1 feedback loop
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n4 n1 VSS VSS
NMOS NM2 n2 VDD n4 VSS
L L1 VDD n3
R R2 n3 n2
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```
3. PREDICTION
- Binding metric: mu_min_wide improves from 0.71 to ~1.05-1.15 (about +0.35-0.4).
- s21: rises ~+1 to +2 dB.
- NF: rises by at most 0.2 dB.
