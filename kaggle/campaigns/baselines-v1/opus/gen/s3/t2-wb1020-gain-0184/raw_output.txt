1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding metric is s21_db = 10.38 vs the 12.61 dB gate, and mu_min_wide (0.868) is also failing.
The output already drives 50 ohm almost directly (s22 -9.5 dB). So gain is set by the total transconductance, gm_n + gm_p, times about 50 ohm || ro, and the 0.377 dB ripple gate forbids a high-Q input or load to add voltage gain.
That gm is starved in two ways:
- Part of the 6.8 mA budget is burnt in the two bias-reference branches (R1/NM3 and R4/PM2).
- The sizer had to detune and shave gm to fight the out-of-band tuned-gate/tuned-drain instability (inductive L1 gate source plus an L2-C2 load that is inductive above resonance, coupled through Cgd).
Gain and wideband stability are therefore traded against each other on a fixed current budget.

2. EDIT: convert to the classic self-biased resistive-feedback inverter.
- Both gates are tied at n1 (C1 removed), so the full NMOS+PMOS gm acts on one node.
- R6 from output to gate sets the DC at the inverter trip point and provides broadband resistive damping.
- Both mirror reference branches are deleted, so 100% of Idd flows in the gm devices.
```netlist
* lna-a2-current-reuse edit: self-biased resistive-feedback inverter (R6 out->common gate), mirror refs removed
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
PMOS PM1 n2 n1 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R6 n2 n1
```
3. PREDICTION
- Binding metric: s21_db improves by about +1.5 to +2 dB (10.4 to ~12.0-12.5 dB), driven by the reclaimed reference current and by no longer having to trade gain for stability.
- mu_min_wide: rises from 0.87 to above 1.1.
- s11: stays near -8 to -10 dB.
- NF: rises ~+0.1 to +0.2 dB.
