1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding metric is mu_min_wide (0.01-50 GHz) = 0.663, and in-band mu is 1.14. In this single common-source shunt-feedback stage, NM1's Cgd connects the gate to a drain whose load (R2 + L1 shunt peaking) becomes inductive at multi-GHz frequencies. Miller feedback of an inductive drain load through Cgd gives a negative real part at the input, and correspondingly at the output.
On top of that, R1 sets a strong reverse path (s12 = -18.4 dB). The sizer can only keep in-band mu > 1 by holding gain exactly at its floor (s21 = 9.4876 = gate), with Idd and s11 also pinned. The out-of-band region stays potentially unstable for every sizing.

2. EDIT: cascode NM2 (gate at VDD) between NM1's drain and the output/peaking node, keeping R1 from the output to NM1's gate.
- The inductive load is moved off NM1's Cgd, which now sees the low impedance at the cascode source. This removes the negative-conductance mechanism.
- The extra output resistance raises loop gain, so match and gain are easier to reach together.
```netlist
* lna-a3-shunt-feedback edit: cascode NM2 (gate at VDD) inside the R1 feedback loop
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n4 n1 VSS VSS
NMOS NM2 n2 VDD n4 VSS
L L1 VDD n3
R R2 n3 n2
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```
3. PREDICTION
- Binding metric: mu_min_wide improves from 0.66 to ~1.05-1.15 (about +0.4).
- s21: rises ~+1 to +2 dB at equal Idd.
- NF: rises by at most 0.2 dB (it has more than 1.9 dB of margin).
