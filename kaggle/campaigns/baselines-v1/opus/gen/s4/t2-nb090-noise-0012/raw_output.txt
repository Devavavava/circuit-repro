1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is broadband stability (mu_min over 0.01-50 GHz = 0.63; in-band mu = 1.29). S21 sits exactly at its gate (9.80 dB), and NF has 0.28 dB of slack. The shunt-feedback CS stage's load is L1 in series with R2, which becomes inductive above R2/L1. Cgd and R1 couple that inductive drain impedance back to the input and give negative port conductance out of band.

The sizer cannot shrink L1's effect without losing the peaking it is using to reach 9.8 dB of S21. A single NMOS at 6.1 mA gives S21 of about 1 - gm*Z0, roughly 9.8 dB at most.

2. EDIT: replace the L1+R2 load with a PMOS driven from the same gate, giving a resistive-feedback CMOS inverter (current reuse). The load becomes ro || port (RC-only), so no inductive load can produce negative resistance, and gm_n + gm_p from the same current gives more gain and lower NF. R1 still provides the self-bias and the match.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
PMOS PM1 n2 n1 VDD VDD
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```
3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide (binding) improves from 0.63 to ~1.2+ (+0.55). S21 rises by ~2-3 dB to ~12 dB, and NF stays ~1.4-1.6 dB.
