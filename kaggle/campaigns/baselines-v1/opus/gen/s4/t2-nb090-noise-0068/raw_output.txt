1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is mu_min_wide (0.874). In-band mu (1.009, k about 1.03) is also marginal, and s21 is 0.6 dB short.
- The current-reuse inverter couples the shared drain n2 back to the AC-tied NMOS/PMOS gates through both Cgd's (S12 about -25.9 dB, large for a 14.5 dB-gain stage).
- The output is poorly loaded (s22 about -4.2 dB), and the input is a series L1 into the gate-capacitance resonance. In band, and even more out of band, both ports are close to lossless, so the S12*S21 loop puts mu below 1.
- The low ro_n || ro_p output resistance in 45 nm also caps the gain at 6 mA.

2. EDIT: NMOS cascode in the current-reuse stack
Add NM2 (gate to VDD) between n2 and the NM1 drain. This shields the NMOS Cgd from the output, so S12 falls by about 6-10 dB. It also raises the output resistance, so gain increases at the same 6 mA.
```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n7 n1 VSS VSS
NMOS NM2 n2 VDD n7 VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```
3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide should improve by about 0.2, from 0.87 to about 1.05-1.1. In-band mu should rise to about 1.3. s21 should improve by about 1 dB, to about 15.5 dB.
