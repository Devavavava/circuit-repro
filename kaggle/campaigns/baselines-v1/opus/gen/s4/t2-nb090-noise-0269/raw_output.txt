1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is input match (S11 = -3.9 dB against -7.86 dB). The input is C1 straight onto the gate of NM1. The input impedance is therefore C1 in series with Cgs, shunted only by the bias feed resistor R2: almost purely capacitive, with no real part at 50 ohm. No sizing can make a reactive gate look like 50 ohm. The only lever, a small R2, adds thermal noise directly at the input, and NF is already at its limit (1.716 against 1.718).

The mismatch also costs roughly 2-3 dB of transducer gain. Stage 2 also has a tuned gate (series L2 plus the L1 tank via C2), a tuned drain (L3) and Cgd coupling: a tuned-gate/tuned-drain arrangement that drives mu over 0.01-50 GHz to 0.83 even though in-band mu is 2.3.

2. EDIT: make stage 1 an inductively-degenerated cascode.
- Add a series gate inductor Lg (between C1 and the gate) and a source inductor Ls. Together they give a resonant, noiseless real input resistance gm*Ls/Cgs = 50 ohm.
- Delete the interstage series L2 (C2 alone couples the stages) to stay at 4 inductors and remove stage 2's tuned gate.
- Cascode both stages with their cascode gates tied to VDD, for isolation and stability.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L Lg n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n8 n1 n3 VSS
L Ls n3 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n9 n5 VSS VSS
NMOS NM5 n6 VDD n9 VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```
3. PREDICTION: s11_max_db (binding) improves from -3.9 dB to ~-12 dB (about 8 dB better). S21 rises by ~4-6 dB because of the match and the cascode gain, NF stays ~1.6-1.7 dB, and mu_min_wide rises above 1 (~1.2).
