1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is input match (S11 = -4.9 dB against -7.85 dB). Stage 1 has no input-matching network: C1 feeds the gate of NM1 directly, and the only shunt element is the bias feed R2. The port sees C1 in series with Cgs, a nearly lossless capacitive impedance, so most power is reflected.

The only way to create a real part with this topology is to lower R2, which injects thermal noise at the input, and NF is at its limit (2.021 against 2.021). Separately, stage 2 forms a tuned-gate/tuned-drain loop: series L2 plus the L1 tank via C2 on its gate, L3 on its drain, coupled through Cgd. That loop gives mu over 0.01-50 GHz of 0.85 even though in-band mu is 2.3.

2. EDIT: make stage 1 an inductively-degenerated cascode.
- Add a series gate inductor Lg (C1 -> Lg -> gate) and a source inductor Ls, giving a noiseless resonant real input of gm*Ls/Cgs = 50 ohm.
- Drop the interstage series L2 (C2 couples directly) to stay at 4 inductors and remove stage 2's tuned gate.
- Cascode both stages, with their cascode gates tied to VDD.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L Lg n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n8 n1 n3 VSS
L Ls n3 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n9 n5 VSS VSS
NMOS NM5 n6 VDD n9 VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```
3. PREDICTION: s11_max_db (binding) improves from -4.9 dB to ~-12 dB (about 7 dB better). S21 rises by ~4-6 dB, NF stays ~1.8-2.0 dB, and mu_min_wide rises above 1.
