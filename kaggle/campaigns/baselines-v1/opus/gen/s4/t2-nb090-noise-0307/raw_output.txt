1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is input match (S11 = -4.9 dB against -7.85 dB). Stage 1 has no matching network: C1 feeds the gate of NM1 directly, shunted only by the bias feed R2. The input impedance is C1 in series with Cgs, almost purely reactive. The only resistive lever, R2, injects noise at the input, and NF is at its limit (2.017 against 2.018).

In addition, stage 2's series gate inductor L2 plus the L1 tank, together with the L3 drain tank and Cgd, form a tuned-gate/tuned-drain loop, giving mu over 0.01-50 GHz of 0.77 even though in-band mu is 2.4.

2. EDIT: make stage 1 an inductively-degenerated cascode.
- Add Lg in series with the gate and Ls at the NM1 source, for a noiseless resonant 50-ohm real part, gm*Ls/Cgs.
- Delete the interstage L2 (keeping the 4-inductor budget and removing stage 2's tuned gate).
- Cascode both stages, with their cascode gates tied to VDD.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L Lg n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n8 n1 n3 VSS
L Ls n3 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n9 n5 VSS VSS
NMOS NM5 n6 VDD n9 VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```
3. PREDICTION: s11_max_db (binding) improves from -4.9 dB to ~-12 dB (about 7 dB better). S21 rises by ~4-6 dB, NF stays ~1.8-2.0 dB, and mu_min_wide rises above 1.
