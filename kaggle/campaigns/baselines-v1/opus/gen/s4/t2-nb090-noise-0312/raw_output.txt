1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is input match (S11 = -3.1 dB against -7.85 dB), and S21 is also 4 dB short (14.7 against 18.65 dB). Stage 1 has C1 driving the gate of NM1 directly, so the input impedance is C1 in series with Cgs, shunted only by the bias feed R2. It has essentially no real part, so it reflects most of the incident power.

Making R2 small would create a real part but put a thermal-noise resistor at the input, and NF is at its limit (1.352 against 1.357). The ~3 dB of mismatch loss is also why S21 is short. Stage 2's tuned gate (series L2 plus the L1 tank) and tuned drain (L3), coupled through Cgd, form a TGTD-like loop, giving mu over 0.01-50 GHz of 0.82.

2. EDIT: make stage 1 an inductively-degenerated cascode.
- Add a series gate inductor Lg and a source inductor Ls, giving a noiseless resonant 50-ohm input (gm*Ls/Cgs).
- Delete the interstage series L2 to keep 4 inductors and remove stage 2's tuned gate.
- Cascode both stages, with their cascode gates tied to VDD.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L Lg n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n8 n1 n3 VSS
L Ls n3 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n9 n5 VSS VSS
NMOS NM5 n6 VDD n9 VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```
3. PREDICTION: s11_max_db (binding) improves from -3.1 dB to ~-12 dB (about 9 dB better). S21 rises by ~5-7 dB to about 19-21 dB, NF stays ~1.3 dB, and mu_min_wide rises above 1.
