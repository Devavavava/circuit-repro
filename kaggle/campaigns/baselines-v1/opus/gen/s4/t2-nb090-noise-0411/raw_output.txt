1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is input match (S11 = -1.8 dB against -7.92 dB), with S21 7 dB short (14.8 against 21.8 dB). Stage 1 has no matching network: C1 drives the gate of NM1 directly, and R2 is the only shunt element. The port sees C1 in series with Cgs, an almost lossless capacitive impedance, so nearly all power is reflected.

Lowering R2 to create a real part puts thermal noise at the input, and NF is at its very tight limit (1.131 against 1.131). The 4-5 dB of mismatch loss accounts for most of the gain deficit. Stage 2's series gate L2 plus the L1 tank, its drain tank L3 and Cgd form a tuned-gate/tuned-drain loop, so mu over 0.01-50 GHz is 0.78.

2. EDIT: build an inductively-degenerated cascode input stage.
- Add a series gate inductor Lg and a source inductor Ls, giving a noiseless resonant 50-ohm real part, gm*Ls/Cgs.
- Delete the interstage L2 to stay at 4 inductors and remove stage 2's tuned gate.
- Cascode both stages, with their cascode gates tied to VDD, for gain and stability.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L Lg n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n8 n1 n3 VSS
L Ls n3 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n8 VSS
L L1 VDD n2
C C2 n2 n5
NMOS NM2 n9 n5 VSS VSS
NMOS NM5 n6 VDD n9 VSS
L L3 VDD n6
C C3 n6 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n7
NMOS NM3 n7 n7 VSS VSS
R R2 n7 n1
R R3 n7 n5
```
3. PREDICTION: s11_max_db (binding) improves from -1.8 dB to ~-11 dB (about 9 dB better). S21 rises by ~6-8 dB to ~21-22 dB, NF stays ~1.1-1.2 dB, and mu_min_wide rises above 1.
