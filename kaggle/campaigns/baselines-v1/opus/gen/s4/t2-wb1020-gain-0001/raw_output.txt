1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is broadband stability (mu_min over 0.01-50 GHz = 0.64; in-band mu = 1.11), and S21 is 0.34 dB short (10.76 against 11.11 dB). The shunt-peaked load of the CS stage (L1 in series with R2) becomes inductive above R2/L1. Via Cgd and the feedback resistor R1, this inductive drain impedance appears as negative conductance at both ports out of band, so mu drops to 0.64.

Gain is limited to about 1 - gm*Z0 with a single NMOS even at 9.8 mA. NF has 2.2 dB of slack.

2. EDIT: replace the L1+R2 load with a PMOS sharing the input gate, making a resistive-feedback CMOS inverter LNA (current reuse). With no inductor the circuit is RC-only, so out-of-band negative resistance disappears; gm_n + gm_p gives more gain at the same current; R1 keeps the match and the self-bias.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
PMOS PM1 n2 n1 VDD VDD
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```
3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide (binding) improves from 0.64 to ~1.2+ (+0.55). S21 rises by ~2-3 dB to ~13 dB, and NF_max stays ~1.4-1.8 dB.
