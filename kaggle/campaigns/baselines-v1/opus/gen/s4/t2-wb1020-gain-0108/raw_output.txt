1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is mu_min_wide (0.997, which misses by 0.3%).
- The limit is the reverse path through the combined Cgd of NM1 and PM1, from the shared drain n2 to the AC-tied gates (S12 about -26.6 dB).
- Out of band, the input (series L1 into the gate capacitance) and the output (L2-C2 tank behind C3, s22_max about -4.1 dB) become nearly reactive, and the residual S12*S21 pulls mu just below 1.
- Gain is at its floor, so the sizer could not trade gain for stability.

2. EDIT: NMOS cascode in the current-reuse stack
Add NM2 (gate to VDD) between n2 and the NM1 drain. This removes the NMOS Cgd from the feedback path, so S12 falls, and raises the output resistance, which gives gain margin.
```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n7 n1 VSS VSS
NMOS NM2 n2 VDD n7 VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```
3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide should improve by about 0.1-0.15, from 0.997 to about 1.1, and pass. s21 should improve by about 1 dB.
