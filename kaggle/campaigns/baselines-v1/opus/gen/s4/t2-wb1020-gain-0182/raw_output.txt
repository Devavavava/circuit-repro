1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is mu_min_wide (0.71). s21 is also 0.4 dB short.
- In the single-device shunt-feedback CS, R1 in parallel with Cgd(NM1) ties the output directly to the input, so S12 is large (about -20 dB).
- The shunt-peaking L1 raises and phase-shifts the drain impedance above the band. Out of band, the ports become reactive (DC blocks C1/C2, Cgs, drain capacitance), so |S11| and |S22| approach 1 while S12*S21 persists, pulling mu well below 1.
- Gain is capped by the ro-limited gm*Rout of one device at 8.4 mA.

2. EDIT: cascode the shunt-feedback CS
Add NM2 (gate to VDD) stacked on NM1, with feedback R1 from the cascode drain (the output) to the gate, so self-bias is kept. This eliminates the Cgd/Miller reverse path and the drain-gate phase interaction with L1, and raises the output resistance (more gain).
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n4 n1 VSS VSS
NMOS NM2 n2 VDD n4 VSS
L L1 VDD n3
R R2 n3 n2
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```
3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide should improve by about 0.3-0.35, from 0.71 to about 1.0-1.05. s21 should improve by about 1.5-2 dB, to about 11 dB.
