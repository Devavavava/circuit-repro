1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is broadband stability (mu_min over 0.01-50 GHz = 0.71; in-band mu = 1.13), with S21 2.4 dB short (9.64 against 12.04 dB). The shunt-peaked load (L1 in series with R2) turns the drain impedance inductive above R2/L1. Through Cgd and the feedback resistor R1, that inductive load shows up as negative conductance at the input and output out of band, so mu drops to 0.71.

Gain is capped at about 1 - gm*Z0 by a single NMOS at the 9.3 mA cap. NF has 2.2 dB of slack.

2. EDIT: replace the L1+R2 load with a PMOS driven by the same input gate, making a resistive-feedback CMOS inverter LNA (current reuse). This edit:
- removes the inductor, so the network is RC-only and stable;
- gives gm_n + gm_p at the same current, for about +60-80% gm and higher S21;
- keeps R1 setting the broadband 50-ohm match and the self-bias.
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
PMOS PM1 n2 n1 VDD VDD
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```
3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide (binding) improves from 0.71 to ~1.2+ (+0.5). S21 rises by ~2.5-3 dB to ~12-12.5 dB, and NF_max stays ~1.5-1.8 dB.
