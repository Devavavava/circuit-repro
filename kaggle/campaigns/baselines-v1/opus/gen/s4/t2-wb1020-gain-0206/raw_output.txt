1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is mu_min_wide (0.66).
- The single-stage shunt-feedback CS couples the output straight back to the input through R1 in parallel with Cgd(NM1) (S12 about -18.4 dB, which is large).
- The shunt-peaking inductor L1 makes the drain impedance peak and turn inductive above the band. With reactive terminations out of band (C1/C2 DC blocks, Cgs, drain capacitance), |S11| and |S22| approach 1 while S12*S21 is still significant, so mu is far below 1.
- Gain is pinned at its floor by ro-limited gm*Rout at 6.8 mA.

2. EDIT: cascode on the shunt-feedback CS
Add NM2 (gate to VDD) between NM1 and the shunt-peaked load. The feedback R1 is taken from the cascode drain back to the gate, so self-bias is unchanged. The cascode kills the Cgd/Miller reverse path and the drain-gate phase interaction with L1, and raises the output resistance (more gain at the same current).
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n4 n1 VSS VSS
NMOS NM2 n2 VDD n4 VSS
L L1 VDD n3
R R2 n3 n2
C C2 n2 VOUT1
```
3. PREDICTION
mu_min_wide should improve by about 0.35-0.4, from 0.66 to about 1.0-1.05. s21 should improve by about 2 dB.
