1. DIAGNOSIS
The binding constraint is broadband stability (mu_min over 0.01-50 GHz = 0.988; in-band mu = 1.13; S12 = -25 dB), with S21 also just short (11.14 against 11.15 dB). In the un-cascoded current-reuse stage, output n2 feeds back to the input through the Cgd of NM1 and PM1 (PM1's gate is AC-coupled to the input by C1).

The shunt load L2 (AC-grounded by C2) is inductive below resonance. There, Cgd Miller feedback presents a negative conductance at the gate, and the lossless series L1 into Cgs input, which has no degeneration, does nothing to damp it. Out of band, |S11| and |S22| approach 1 and mu dips below 1. All in-band margins are at zero, so there is no slack for the sizer to trade gain against stability.

2. EDIT: cascode NM1 (cascode gate to VDD), and add a source-degeneration inductor L3 on NM1 to provide a noiseless real input resistance and some local series feedback.
```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n7 n1 n8 VSS
L L3 n8 VSS
NMOS NM4 n2 VDD n7 VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VSS
R R5 n6 n3
```
3. PREDICTION: mu_min_wide (binding) improves from 0.99 to ~1.15 (+0.15 to +0.2). S12 improves by ~10 dB, and S21 gains ~1 dB, clearing 11.15 dB.
