### Diagnosis
The binding constraint `s21_db` fails due to **insufficient voltage gain** in the common-gate (CG) topology. The CG configuration inherently has limited gain (typically ~gm·R_L), and the inductive load (L2) may not provide optimal impedance matching at 1.6 GHz. The lack of a resistive load or feedback mechanism exacerbates the gain deficit, while the narrowband design further limits the ability to compensate via reactive components.

---

### Structural Edit 1: Replace inductive load with resistive load (common-gate to resistive load)
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 VSS
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 n0 VSS
R Rload VDD n2
C C2 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n3
NMOS NM3 n3 n3 VSS VSS
R R2 n3 n1
C Cbyp n1 VSS
```
**Predicted change**: `s21_db` improves by ~6–8 dB due to a resistive load (Rload) providing a more predictable and higher-impedance match than an inductor at 1.6 GHz.
