The failure is due to the common-gate LNA's inherent low gain and poor load impedance matching at 1.6 GHz. The inductive load (L2) and capacitive output (C2) fail to provide sufficient transconductance amplification and impedance transformation, resulting in a 8.4 dB gain (s21_db) far below the 16 dB target. The topology lacks a resistive load or cascode structure to enhance gain.
