1. **Diagnosis**: The binding constraint (S21 < 14 dB) is due to insufficient voltage gain from the common-gate stage. The inductive load (L2) provides frequency-dependent reactance but fails to deliver the required gm × RL product for 14 dB gain at 1.6 GHz. The topology lacks a second gain stage or a high-Z load to compensate for narrowband limitations.

---

2. **Structural Edit 1: Replace inductive load with resistive load**  
```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 VSS
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 n0 VSS
R R2 VDD n2
C C2 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n3
NMOS NM3 n3 n3 VSS VSS
R R2 n3 n1
C Cbyp n1 VSS
```  
**Predicted effect**: S21_db improves by ~4–5 dB (resistive load directly increases gain, bypassing inductor’s frequency dependence).
