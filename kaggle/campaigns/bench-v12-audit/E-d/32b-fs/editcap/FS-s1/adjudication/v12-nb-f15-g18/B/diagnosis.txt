1. **DIAGNOSIS**: The binding constraint `s21_db` is failing because the inductive load (L2) provides insufficient real impedance at 1.6 GHz. Inductive loads depend on frequency and are limited by parasitic capacitance and core losses, resulting in a low effective load impedance for the common-gate stage. This limits voltage gain despite high transconductance (gm) from the common-gate configuration.
