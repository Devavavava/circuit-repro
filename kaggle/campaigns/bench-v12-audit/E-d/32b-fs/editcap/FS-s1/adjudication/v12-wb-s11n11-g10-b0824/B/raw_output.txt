1. **DIAGNOSIS**  
The S11 failure stems from the LC input matching network's narrowband nature. The current-reuse push-pull topology uses a resonant L1-C4 LC tank at the input, which cannot maintain low return loss across the 3:1 bandwidth (0.8–2.4 GHz). The LC tank's high Q and reactive impedance cause S11 to degrade at band edges, violating the -11 dB spec.

---

2. **STRUCTURAL EDITS**  

```netlist
* Edit 1: Add series resistor at input to broaden LC tank's bandwidth
C C4 VIN1 n0
R Rin VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 n2 n4
C C2 n4 VSS
C C3 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
PMOS PM2 n6 n6 VDD VDD
R R4 n6 VDD
R R5 n6 n3
```
**PREDICTION**: S11 improves by ~3–4 dB due to reduced LC tank Q via series damping.
