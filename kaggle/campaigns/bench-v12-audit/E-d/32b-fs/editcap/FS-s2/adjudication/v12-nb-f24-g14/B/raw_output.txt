1. **DIAGNOSIS**: The binding constraint (s21_db) fails due to insufficient load impedance in the common-gate stage. The inductive load (L2) at the drain of NM1 provides reactive impedance but lacks sufficient real (resistive) component to maximize gain. The narrowband design requires a higher Q load to increase transconductance-to-load ratio.
