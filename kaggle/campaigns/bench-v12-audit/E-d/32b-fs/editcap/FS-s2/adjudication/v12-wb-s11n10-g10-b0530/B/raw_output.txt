1. **DIAGNOSIS**: The binding constraint `s11_max_db` (-6.07 dB) fails the -10 dB spec due to insufficient broadband input impedance matching. The LC network (L1/C1/C4) is inherently narrowband and cannot maintain low return loss across the 0.5–3 GHz band. Resistive feedback or shunt elements are needed to broaden the real part of Zin.
