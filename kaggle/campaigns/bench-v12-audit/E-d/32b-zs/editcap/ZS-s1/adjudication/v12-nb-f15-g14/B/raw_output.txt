1. **DIAGNOSIS**: The common-gate topology inherently suffers from limited voltage gain due to the transconductance-to-load impedance ratio (S21 ≈ R_L / (1/gm)). The narrowband inductive load (L2) cannot compensate for the low gm of the single NMOS stage, and the common-gate configuration’s output impedance is insufficient to meet the 14 dB gain requirement despite tuning.

---

2. **STRUCTURAL EDITS**

```netlist
* Edit 1: Replace common-gate with common-source topology for higher gain.
* Input at gate, source grounded via L1, drain loaded with L2.
C C1 VIN1 n1
L L1 n0 VSS
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 n0 VSS
L L2 VDD n2
C C2 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n3
NMOS NM3 n3 n3 VSS VSS
R R2 n3 n1
```
**Predicted effect**: s21_db improves by ~5 dB (common-source provides higher intrinsic gain than common-gate).
