1. **DIAGNOSIS**: The binding constraint (S21_db) is failing due to insufficient gain in the common-gate topology. The common-gate stage's transconductance (gm) is limited by the NMOS's intrinsic characteristics and the inductive load (L2) not providing sufficient impedance at 2.4 GHz. The design prioritizes noise figure and input match over gain, leading to a 9.4 dB gain shortfall.
