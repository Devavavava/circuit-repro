### DIAGNOSIS  
The binding constraint `s11_max_db` fails because the input impedance is poorly matched across the wideband (0.8–2.4 GHz). The existing LC input matching network (C4 + L1 + C1) has insufficient bandwidth or resonance tuning, leading to high reflection (poor S11). The current-reuse push-pull topology inherently has a narrow Q, but the LC network’s high Q exacerbates the issue, causing S11 to peak above -11 dB.
