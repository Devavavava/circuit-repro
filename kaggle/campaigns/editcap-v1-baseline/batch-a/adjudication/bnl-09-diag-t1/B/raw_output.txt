### Diagnosis  
The binding constraint (`s21_db`) is critically undershoot by ~10.3 dB, indicating insufficient voltage gain. The common-gate topology inherently limits gain due to its low transconductance (1/gm) matching strategy and output loading. The inductive load (L2) likely fails to resonate properly at 915 MHz, and the single-stage configuration lacks sufficient amplification. The noise figure is marginally over budget, but gain is the primary bottleneck.

---

### Edit 1: Cascode Stacked Common-Source  
Replace the common-gate with a cascode common-source stage to increase gain and output impedance.  
```netlist
* lna-a5-cascode: cascode CS for higher gain, inductive load
C C1 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 VSS
NMOS NM1 n2 n0 VSS VSS
NMOS NM2 n3 n4 n2 VSS
L L2 n3 VDD
C C2 n3 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n4
C Cbyp n4 VSS
```
**Prediction**: `s21_db` improves by ~8 dB (cascode increases gain via higher output impedance and gm multiplication).
