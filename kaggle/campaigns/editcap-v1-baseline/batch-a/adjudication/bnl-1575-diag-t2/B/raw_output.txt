1. The binding constraint is **s21_db (gain)** failing by ~9 dB. The common-gate topology's voltage gain is limited by the low transconductance (gm) of a single NMOS and the inductive load (L2) not achieving sufficient impedance at 1.58 GHz. Structural changes must enhance transconductance and/or load impedance.

```netlist
* Edit 1: Add a PMOS cascode in the drain path of NM1 to increase output impedance
* (common-gate + cascode) and improve gain. Gate of PMOS tied to existing bias node n3.
C C1 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 VSS
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 n0 VSS
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
L L2 VDD n2
C C2 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n3
NMOS NM3 n3 n3 VSS VSS
R R2 n3 n1
C Cbyp n1 VSS
```
**Prediction**: s21_db improves by ~5 dB (from 7.14 dB to ~12 dB). The cascode increases output impedance, amplifying the gain (R_L * gm).
