1. **DIAGNOSIS**: The noise figure (NF) is over the limit due to excessive gate thermal noise from R2 and suboptimal inductive degeneration. The current mirror biasing (R2) introduces thermal noise directly at the input transistor's gate. The inductor L1's Q-factor and tuning may not sufficiently suppress noise from NM1's gate-source. The cascode structure adds minimal noise but is compromised by the bias resistor.

2. **EDIT 1 (Add shunt capacitor to short R2's high-frequency noise)**  
```netlist

L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 n3 VSS
```
