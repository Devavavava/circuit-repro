1. **DIAGNOSIS:**  
   The failing `s11_max_db` constraint (-0.73 dB vs. -12 dB target) is caused by inadequate input impedance matching over the 0.5–3 GHz band. The current series-inductor (L1) and shunt-capacitor (C4) input network fails to achieve low VSWR, likely due to insufficient degrees of freedom in the matching topology. The LC network cannot flatten the input impedance across the wideband, leading to high return loss.
