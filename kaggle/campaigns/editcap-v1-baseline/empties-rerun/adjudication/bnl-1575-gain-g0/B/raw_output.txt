1. **DIAGNOSIS**: The common-gate stage's voltage gain is insufficient due to low transconductance (gm) and limited load impedance. The inductor L2's impedance at 1.58 GHz is insufficient to maximize gain, and the single transistor's gm is constrained by the current mirror biasing. The cascode structure can increase output impedance and gain by stacking transistors.

```netlist
* Add PMOS cascode to increase output impedance and gain
C C1 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 VSS
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 n0 VSS
PMOS PM1 n3 n3 n2 VDD
L L2 VDD n3
C C2 n3 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n4
NMOS NM3 n4 n4 VSS VSS
R R2 n4 n1
C Cbyp n1 VSS
```

**PREDICTION**: s21_db improves by ~10 dB (cascode increases output impedance).

---

2. **DIAGNOSIS**: The single inductor L2 provides limited load impedance. A parallel LC tank at resonance maximizes impedance, enhancing gain.

```netlist
* Replace L2 with LC tank for higher Q and impedance at f0
C C1 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 VSS
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 n0 VSS
L L2 n2 VDD
C C3 n2 VDD
C C2 n2 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n3
NMOS NM3 n3 n3 VSS VSS
R R2 n3 n1
C Cbyp n1 VSS
```

**PREDICTION**: s21_db improves by ~8 dB (resonant tank maximizes load impedance).

---

3. **DIAGNOSIS**: The common-gate stage alone cannot meet gain. Adding a common-source PMOS stage after the common-gate provides additional gain through cascading.

```netlist
* Add common-source PMOS after common-gate for cascaded gain
C C1 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 VSS
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 n0 VSS
PMOS PM1 n3 n4 n2 VDD
L L2 VDD n3
C C2 n3 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n4
C Cbyp n1 VSS
```

**PREDICTION**: s21_db improves by ~12 dB (cascaded gain stages multiply overall gain).
