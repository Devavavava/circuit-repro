1. **DIAGNOSIS**: The binding constraint (s21_db) is limited by insufficient transconductance gain (gm) and load impedance mismatch. The inductive degeneration (L1) and cascode (NM2) topology underperform due to parasitic inductance and lack of optimal impedance scaling at 5.8 GHz.

---

```netlist
* Edit 1: Add shunt resistor to increase real load impedance at cascode node
* (R3 in parallel with L2 to enhance gain)
C C1 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 n3 VSS
L L2 n3 VSS
R R3 n3 VSS
NMOS NM2 n4 VDD n2 VSS
L L3 VDD n4
C C2 n4 VOUT1
R R1 VDD n5
NMOS NM3 n5 n5 VSS VSS
R R2 n5 n1
```
**PREDICTION**: s21_db improves by ~1.2 dB (from 18.9 to ~20.1 dB) via increased load impedance.
