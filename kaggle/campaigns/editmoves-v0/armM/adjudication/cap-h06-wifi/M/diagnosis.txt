DIAGNOSIS: The primary issue is excessive noise figure (NF) due to insufficient output impedance and poor isolation between input and output, exacerbated by the diode-connected NM2 and PM2 devices which degrade stability and noise performance. The cascode structure is absent, leading to low output resistance and high feedback, increasing NF and reducing gain. Additionally, the current mirror-like biasing (via R1–R2 and R3–R4) is poorly optimized, resulting in high Idd and marginal S11/S21.
