DIAGNOSIS: The primary failure is poor input match (S11_db = -1.84 dB, margin -0.85 dB), which is worse than the required -12 dB. The input network (C1-L1) is a simple series LC with no matching optimization; the inductor L1 is likely too small or mismatched to resonate properly at 1.575 GHz with C1, leading to high reflection. The NM1 gate is biased via R1-R2, but the input impedance is dominated by the non-optimized LC network, and the diode-connected NM2 and PM2 provide no input matching benefit. The circuit lacks any intentional matching structure at the input.
