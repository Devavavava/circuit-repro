The original topology uses a simple DC-blocked capacitor (C1) at the input, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation or tuning at the desired frequency (900 MHz). The lack of an explicit input matching network is likely the root cause of the poor S11 performance.

To address this, the proposed netlist introduces a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) and two capacitors (ZC1 and ZC2) to create an additional impedance transformation stage at the input. ZN1 is connected between VDD and n2, with its source tied to n5, effectively creating a feedback path that helps in adjusting the input impedance. The capacitors ZC1 and ZC2 provide additional tuning and impedance matching at the operating frequency, helping to improve the input reflection coefficient (S11). This structural change enhances the input impedance matching without adding extra inductors or complicating the bias network, directly addressing the binding constraint.
