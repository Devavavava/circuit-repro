The failure is primarily due to the poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being -2.62409 dB, which is well above the required -7.9087 dB. This indicates that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source, leading to significant signal reflection at the input port. The original topology lacks an explicit input matching network, relying instead on the LC tank and the source inductance to provide impedance transformation. This is insufficient for achieving the required match at 900 MHz, especially when the sizing process fails to compensate for the mismatch due to the complex interplay between the inductors and the transistor's intrinsic impedance.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the gate of NM1. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path at high frequencies, helping to stabilize the input impedance and improve the match by reducing the reactance seen at the input node. This structural change directly enhances the input matching without introducing additional inductors or complicating the topology. The capacitor helps to resonate with the parasitic inductance of the input node, allowing for a better impedance match at the target frequency.
