The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second stage's performance. The second stage's transconductance is being compromised due to the lack of a direct DC path from the gate to the source, which is essential for proper biasing and signal amplification. The current configuration relies on a current mirror for biasing, but it lacks a direct connection that could stabilize the second stage's operation and improve its gain.

To address this, I added a direct DC path from the second stage's gate (n5) to its source (VSS) by introducing a resistor (Rrep1) and a capacitor (Crep1) from the second stage's source to VDD. This modification ensures a stable bias point and improves the second stage's transconductance, thereby boosting the overall gain of the LNA.
