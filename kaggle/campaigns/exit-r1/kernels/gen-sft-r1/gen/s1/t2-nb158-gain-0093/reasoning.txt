This configuration improves gain by leveraging the high transconductance of the common-source stage and the impedance transformation of the common-gate stage. The inductive source (L1) ensures a low impedance at the input frequency, while the resonant load (L2+C2) provides a high impedance at f0, enhancing the voltage gain.
