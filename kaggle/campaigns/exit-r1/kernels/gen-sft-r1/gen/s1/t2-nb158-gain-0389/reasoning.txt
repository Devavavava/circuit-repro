The original topology introduces multiple resonant structures and feedback paths that can lead to instability outside the narrowband center frequency, especially when the second stage's gate is driven through an inductive path (L2) from the first stage's drain. This configuration can create a feedback loop that reduces the stability margin over a wide frequency range.

To address this, I modified the topology to eliminate the interstage LC network (L2 and C2) and reconfigure the second stage to be a common-gate (CG) configuration instead of a common-source (CS) configuration. This change reduces the feedback path from the second stage to the first stage and improves the stability by reducing the phase shift and loop gain at higher frequencies. Additionally, the second stage is now directly driven from the first stage's drain without an inductive path, which simplifies the feedback dynamics and improves the stability margin across the wideband.
