The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the resonant LC interstage network (L2 and C2) and the lack of a direct feedback path to stabilize the second stage. This leads to a narrow stability band and a low mu_min, which fails the constraint. The interstage LC network introduces a resonant peak that interacts poorly with the second stage's output impedance, causing instability at frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (f0).

To address this, I restructured the topology to include a direct feedback path from the output of the second stage (n6) to the bias network (R1 and NM3), while removing the interstage LC network and replacing it with a simpler inductor (L2) connected directly from the output of the first stage (n2) to the gate of the second stage (n6). This eliminates the resonant LC interstage network, reducing the risk of instability and improving the stability margin. Additionally, I connected the source of the first stage (VSS) to the input of the second stage (n5) to reduce the output impedance of the first stage and improve the overall stability of the system.
