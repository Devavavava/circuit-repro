The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is negative, indicating instability over a wide frequency range. This is caused by the interstage LC network (L2 and C2) creating a resonant feedback path between the two stages, leading to potential oscillation at frequencies outside the narrowband design point. The current topology lacks sufficient damping or isolation between stages, especially at higher frequencies, which reduces the stability margin.

To address this, I added a series resistor (ZR1) between VDD and the drain of NM1, and a shunt capacitor (ZC1) between the drain of NM2 and VDD. The resistor ZR1 introduces a small amount of damping at the input stage, reducing the risk of oscillation by limiting the gain at high frequencies. The capacitor ZC1 provides a low-impedance path to ground at high frequencies, further stabilizing the second stage and reducing feedback through the interstage LC network. These changes improve the stability margin without significantly affecting the gain or noise figure.
