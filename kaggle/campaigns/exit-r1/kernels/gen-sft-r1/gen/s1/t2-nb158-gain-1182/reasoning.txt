The failure is due to insufficient gain from the two-stage LNA, as evidenced by the s21_db metric being 17.2141 dB, which is 0.232893 dB below the required 22.4403 dB. The structure of the anchor netlist, with two common-source stages and LC-tuned loads, is inherently limited in gain due to the voltage division caused by the inductive loads and the lack of a direct current path for signal amplification. The second stage’s gate is driven by an LC network, which introduces phase shifts and reduces the effective signal transfer to the second stage.

To address this, I propose a structural change that replaces the second stage’s LC-tuned load with a direct connection to VDD through a capacitor, and introduces a new NMOS transistor to provide a stronger signal amplification path. This change enhances the signal transfer from the first stage to the second stage by eliminating the LC network at the second stage’s drain, which reduces phase loss and improves gain. The new transistor (NM2) is configured as a common-gate stage, which boosts the signal level more effectively than the original common-source configuration.
