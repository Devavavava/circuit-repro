The two-stage topology with LC interstage coupling introduces additional poles and zeros that can destabilize the system, especially when the second stage's gate is driven through an inductive path, leading to phase shifts that reduce mu_min.

To address this, I restructured the topology to eliminate the LC interstage coupling and instead use a direct connection between the two stages with a source-degeneration inductor (L2) to improve stability. The first stage now uses a common-gate configuration (NM1) with an input capacitor (C1) and a source inductor (L1) to provide impedance transformation and improve matching. The second stage is a common-source configuration (NM2) with a load inductor (L3) and an output capacitor (C2). This topology reduces the number of resonant elements that could cause phase shifts and instability, while maintaining high gain and low noise performance.
