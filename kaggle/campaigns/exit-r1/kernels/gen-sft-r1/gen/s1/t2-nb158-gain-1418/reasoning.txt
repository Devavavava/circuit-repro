The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second-stage transistor (NM2) being under-driven due to the lack of a direct biasing path to its source. The current anchor netlist relies on a current mirror bias network (NM3, R1, R2, R3), which indirectly biases NM1 and NM2. However, this indirect biasing leads to poor control over the second-stage's operating point, especially at the target frequency, resulting in a suboptimal transconductance and thus insufficient gain. The second stage’s performance is further compromised by the absence of a direct biasing path from the source of NM2 to a stable reference, causing instability and reduced amplification.

To address this, I added a direct biasing path from the source of NM2 (n5) to a stable reference node (n7) by introducing a resistor Rrep1. This change ensures that NM2's source is properly biased, improving its transconductance and overall gain. The addition of Rrep1 provides a low-impedance path for bias current, stabilizing the second stage and allowing it to operate at a higher gain point without sacrificing stability or noise performance.
