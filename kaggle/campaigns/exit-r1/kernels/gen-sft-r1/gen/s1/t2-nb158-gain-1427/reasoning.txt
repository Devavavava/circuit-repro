The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is failing due to insufficient stability across the wide frequency range. This is likely caused by the interstage LC network (L2 and C2) creating a resonant feedback path that destabilizes the amplifier at off-center frequencies. The two-stage topology with LC interstage coupling introduces a second resonant peak, which reduces the phase margin and causes the stability factor to dip below the required threshold.

To address this, I replaced the LC interstage network with a direct inductive coupling between the two stages. This eliminates the resonant feedback path and improves the phase margin across the band. The new topology uses a single inductor (L2) connecting the drain of NM1 to the gate of NM2, removing the DC-blocking capacitor (C2) and the second inductor (L2) from the original design. This change reduces the number of resonant nodes and improves the stability factor without sacrificing gain or noise performance.
