The binding constraint is the gain (s21_db), which is too low by about 1.8 dB. The original topology uses a push-pull current-reuse configuration with a tuned load (L2+C2), but the gain is insufficient due to poor signal coupling and possibly high output impedance from the current-reuse structure. The PMOS and NMOS stages are arranged in a way that limits the voltage swing and signal transfer efficiency at the target frequency.

To improve the gain, I replaced the push-pull current-reuse configuration with a common-gate (CG) stage for the NMOS transistor (NM2), which provides better signal coupling and lower output impedance. The CG stage allows the signal to bypass the gate and flow directly through the source, improving the voltage gain and reducing the impact of output impedance on the gain. Additionally, I restructured the biasing and current-setting network to ensure stable operation and better signal integrity. By using a CG stage and reorganizing the current paths, the signal transfer is enhanced, directly addressing the insufficient gain issue.
