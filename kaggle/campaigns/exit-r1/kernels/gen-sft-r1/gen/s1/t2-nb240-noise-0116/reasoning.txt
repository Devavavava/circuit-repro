To address this, the topology is restructured into a common-gate (CG) configuration for the input stage, which provides better impedance matching and higher gain at the desired frequency. The output stage is simplified to a single NMOS transistor with an inductive load, which improves gain and reduces noise contributions. The biasing network is reorganized to ensure stable operation without interfering with the signal path. These changes eliminate the push-pull structure, reduce parasitic effects, and improve the overall gain and stability of the LNA.
