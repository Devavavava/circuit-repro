The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_db) being too high, which is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure. The anchor netlist includes a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for biasing, which introduces additional noise into the signal path. Additionally, the current-reuse topology may not be effectively isolating the noise from the biasing network, leading to a higher-than-acceptable noise figure.

To address this, I replaced the diode-connected NM3 with a NMOS transistor (NM2) connected to a different node (n4), which is part of the tuned load (L2 and C2). This change helps to reduce the noise contribution from the biasing network by decoupling it from the signal path more effectively. The PMOS gate biasing structure was also modified to use a different node (n6) and connected to the new NM2, which helps in better noise isolation and reduces the noise figure.
