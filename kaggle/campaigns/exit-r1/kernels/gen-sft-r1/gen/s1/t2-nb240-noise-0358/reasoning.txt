The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is failing due to insufficient phase margin in the feedback path of the two-stage amplifier. The current topology lacks explicit feedback compensation, and the interstage LC network may be causing resonant peaks that reduce stability across the band. The second stage's gate is driven through a series resistor (R3) from the bias network, which introduces a delay and reduces the phase margin at higher frequencies, leading to a lower `mu_min`.

To address this, I added a feedback capacitor (ZC1) from the output (VOUT1) to the bias node (n7), providing a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals to ground. This introduces a phase lead at higher frequencies, improving the phase margin and increasing the stability factor. The capacitor is placed in the bias network to avoid altering the DC biasing of the transistors while enhancing stability.
