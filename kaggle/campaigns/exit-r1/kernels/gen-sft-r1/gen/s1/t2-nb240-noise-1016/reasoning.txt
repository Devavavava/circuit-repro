This indicates that the LNA is unstable over a wide frequency range, likely due to poor feedback and poor impedance matching between stages, especially in the interstage LC network. The original topology's LC interstage (L2 and C2) may be causing excessive phase shift or resonant peaks that reduce stability.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output of the second stage (n6) to the bias reference node (n7) using a capacitor (ZC1). This helps stabilize the circuit by providing a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise, reducing phase shift and improving the stability factor. The feedback capacitor also helps in shaping the frequency response to ensure a more stable operation across the band.
