The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the interstage LC network and the lack of a feedback path to stabilize the second stage. The original topology lacks a direct feedback mechanism that could help maintain stability across the wide frequency range, leading to a marginal mu_min that fails the specification. The LC interstage network, while useful for tuning, does not inherently provide the necessary phase compensation to ensure stability at all frequencies.

To address this, I added a direct feedback path from the output of the second stage (n6) to the bias node (n7) using a capacitor (ZC1), which introduces a controlled phase shift and helps stabilize the second stage. This change provides a more robust feedback mechanism that enhances the stability margin without significantly affecting other performance metrics like noise figure or gain.
