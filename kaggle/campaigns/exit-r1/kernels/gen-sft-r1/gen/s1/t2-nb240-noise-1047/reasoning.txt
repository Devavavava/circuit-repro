The original topology lacks a component that can provide this feedback, leading to a stability margin that is insufficient at frequencies beyond the operating band.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the output (VOUT1) and the second stage's drain node (n6), and a resistor (ZR2) between the output (VOUT1) and ground (VSS). These additions provide a controlled feedback path from the output to the input, improving the stability margin without significantly degrading the noise figure or gain. The capacitor ZC1 helps in shunting high-frequency signals to ground, reducing unwanted feedback at higher frequencies, while the resistor ZR2 provides a DC path to ground, ensuring the output remains stable under varying load conditions.
