The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by about 0.2 dB. This is likely caused by the second-stage transistor (NM2) contributing more noise than expected, possibly due to its high impedance at the drain, which is loaded by a simple inductor (L3) to VDD. This configuration may not provide sufficient noise suppression or impedance matching at the second stage, leading to a higher overall noise contribution. Additionally, the biasing network, while present, may not be optimally configured to minimize noise in the second stage.

To address this, I modified the second-stage transistor's drain load from a simple inductor (L3) to a series inductor (L3) and capacitor (C2), forming an LC tank. This change improves the impedance matching at the second stage and helps in filtering out noise, thereby reducing the noise contribution from NM2. Additionally, I restructured the biasing network to ensure that the gate of NM2 is properly biased without introducing unnecessary noise. This structural change enhances the noise performance of the second stage, which is critical for meeting the noise figure specification.
