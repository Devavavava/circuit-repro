The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second stage's performance. The second stage (NM2) is driven by the interstage LC network (L2 and C2), but its gain is constrained by the load impedance and the biasing configuration. The current mirror biasing (NM3, R1, R2, R3) introduces parasitic effects and reduces the second stage's transconductance, limiting the overall gain. Additionally, the LC interstage network may be causing signal loss or phase mismatch that reduces the second stage's ability to amplify effectively.

To address this, I propose a structural change that eliminates the LC interstage network and replaces it with a direct connection between the stages, while adding a source degeneration inductor (L2) to the first stage and a load inductor (L1) to the second stage. This improves the second stage's gain by providing a higher impedance load and reduces the signal loss in the interstage path. The source degeneration inductor in the first stage also helps in stabilizing the input impedance and improving the overall gain.
