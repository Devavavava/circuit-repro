The binding constraint is the input matching (s11_max_db), which is failing by ~0.93 dB. The original topology uses a simple LC tank as the drain load for the first stage, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation or tuning flexibility at the operating frequency. The second stage's input is coupled through an LC interstage network, but this may introduce parasitic effects or insufficient impedance control, degrading the input matching.

To address this, I modified the first-stage drain load from a simple inductor (L1) to a series LC tank (L1 and C1), which provides better impedance tuning and resonance control at the target frequency. This change enhances the input matching by allowing more precise impedance transformation. Additionally, I replaced the second-stage drain load from an inductor (L3) to a series LC tank (L3 and C2), improving the output impedance and reducing unwanted reflections. These changes improve the input matching and overall stability of the circuit, directly addressing the s11_max_db constraint.
