The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second stage's inability to deliver sufficient amplification at the target frequency. The original topology employs a two-stage common-source LNA with tuned inductive loads, but the second stage's gain is constrained by the interstage LC network and the load inductance (L3), which may not be optimally resonating at the desired frequency. The lack of a direct feedback or impedance transformation mechanism between stages may also be contributing to the reduced gain.

To address this, I introduced a resistor (Rrep1) in the bias network, connecting the drain of NM3 (the current mirror reference) to VDD. This addition provides a controlled impedance path from the current mirror to the power supply, improving the stability and gain of the second stage by reducing the impact of parasitic effects and ensuring a more predictable current flow. This change enhances the second stage's ability to amplify the signal effectively at the target frequency, thereby improving the s21_db metric.
