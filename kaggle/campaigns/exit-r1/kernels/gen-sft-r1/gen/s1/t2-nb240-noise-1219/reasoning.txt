The shared DC path between the NMOS and PMOS stages may be creating parasitic feedback, reducing the stability margin. Additionally, the lack of a direct connection between the NMOS and PMOS stages may be contributing to poor phase alignment, exacerbating instability.

To address this, I propose a structural change that introduces a direct connection between the NMOS and PMOS stages through a new node, `n6`, and modifies the load configuration. This helps decouple the NMOS and PMOS stages and improves phase margin by providing a more stable feedback path.
