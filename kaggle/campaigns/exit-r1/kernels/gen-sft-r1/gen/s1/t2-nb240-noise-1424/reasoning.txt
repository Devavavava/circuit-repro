The failure is due to the poor stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) caused by the LC interstage network (L2 and C2) and the feedback paths from the second stage (NM2) to the first stage (NM1). This creates a resonant feedback path that destabilizes the circuit at frequencies near the operating point, leading to a negative stability margin.

To address this, the LC interstage network is replaced with a direct inductive coupling between the two stages, removing the resonant feedback path. Additionally, the second stage is modified to use a common-gate configuration (NM2 with source connected to VDD) instead of a common-source configuration, which inherently improves stability by reducing feedback from the output to the input. This change also allows for a more direct signal path, improving gain and reducing noise contributions from the second stage.
