To address this, I added a capacitor `ZC1` between the bias reference node `n7` and the interstage LC network node `n4`. This introduces a low-impedance path at higher frequencies, effectively damping any parasitic feedback loops that could cause instability. The capacitor provides a frequency-dependent feedback mechanism that enhances stability without significantly affecting the narrowband performance, as it acts as a short at the frequencies where instability is observed.
