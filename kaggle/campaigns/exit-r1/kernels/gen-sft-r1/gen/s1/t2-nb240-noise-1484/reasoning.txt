The binding constraint is the stability factor (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is negative, indicating instability across the 0.01–50 GHz range. This is likely caused by the second-stage NMOS (NM2) having a weak feedback path or insufficient isolation from the output, leading to potential oscillations. The original topology uses a direct connection between the interstage LC network (L2 and C2) and the second-stage gate, which may introduce a resonant feedback path at higher frequencies, reducing stability.

To address this, I added a small capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the interstage LC network (n2 and n4), which helps suppress high-frequency feedback and improve stability. Additionally, I introduced a resistor (ZR2) from the output (VOUT1) to ground (VSS), which provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, further stabilizing the circuit. These changes enhance the phase margin and reduce the likelihood of oscillation, directly addressing the mu_min_wide constraint.
