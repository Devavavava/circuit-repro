The current topology lacks a mechanism to dampen resonances outside the narrowband operating point, especially in the second stage, which is critical for maintaining stability.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output (VOUT1) to the bias node (n7) using a capacitor (ZC1), which helps to stabilize the circuit by introducing a low-frequency feedback path that suppresses unwanted oscillations. This change improves the stability margin without significantly affecting the noise figure or gain.
