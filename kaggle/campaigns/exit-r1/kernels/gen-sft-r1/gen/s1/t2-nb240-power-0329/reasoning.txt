The current mirror biasing and the LC-tuned loads contribute to the narrowband performance but do not inherently provide the necessary phase and amplitude stability across the entire 0.01–50 GHz range.

To address this, I introduce a structural change by adding a feedback path from the output to the bias network. This is implemented through an additional NMOS transistor (ZN1) and a capacitor (ZC1) that connects the output (VOUT1) to the bias reference node (n7). This feedback path introduces a controlled amount of negative feedback at higher frequencies, which helps to dampen oscillations and improve the stability margin. The addition of ZN1 and ZC1 modifies the loop gain and phase response, ensuring that the stability factor (mu) remains above the required threshold across the entire frequency range without significantly degrading the noise figure or gain.
