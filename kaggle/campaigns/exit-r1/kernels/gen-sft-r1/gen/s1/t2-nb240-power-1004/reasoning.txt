The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is failing due to poor phase margin across the band. This is likely caused by the second-stage NMOS (NM2) having a weak feedback path to the input, leading to a low stability margin at off-band frequencies. The current structure lacks a direct feedback path from the second stage to the input, which is essential for maintaining a robust stability factor across a wide frequency range.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the gate of the first-stage NMOS (NM1) and the interstage LC network (n4). This creates a feedback path from the interstage LC network to the first-stage gate, improving the phase margin and enhancing the stability factor across the band. This change introduces a small signal feedback mechanism that helps stabilize the amplifier at off-band frequencies without significantly affecting the gain or noise figure.
