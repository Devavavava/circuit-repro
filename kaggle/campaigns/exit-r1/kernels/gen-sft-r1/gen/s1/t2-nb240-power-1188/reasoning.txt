Additionally, the NMOS transistor NM3 is connected as a diode, which may not provide sufficient noise isolation or stability at the input.

To address this, I added a series capacitor (ZC1) between the drain of NM1 (n2) and the input node (n0), which helps in filtering out noise and improving the noise figure by providing a more controlled impedance at the input. This change enhances the noise suppression at the input node, directly addressing the noise figure constraint.
