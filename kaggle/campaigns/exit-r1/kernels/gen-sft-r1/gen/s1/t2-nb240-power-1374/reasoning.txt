The failure is primarily due to noise figure (NF) being too high, exceeding the specification by approximately 0.2 dB. The current mirror (NM3, R1, R2, R3) introduces additional noise, and the second stage (NM2) lacks sufficient noise suppression due to its configuration and the absence of a noise-canceling element.

To address this, I modified the topology by introducing a noise-canceling capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and n4, which helps reduce the noise contribution from the second stage by providing a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise. Additionally, I changed the configuration of the second stage by connecting the source of NM2 to n5 and the drain to n6, and the drain of NM1 to n2, which helps in better noise suppression and signal amplification. These changes help reduce the noise figure while maintaining the required gain and stability.
