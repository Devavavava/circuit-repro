The failure is due to the poor stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) caused by the feedback path through the interstage LC network and the second stage's output. This creates a resonant feedback loop that destabilizes the circuit at off-band frequencies, especially in the wideband range. The second stage's gate is driven by the LC interstage network, which introduces a phase shift that reduces the phase margin, leading to a negative `mu_min` value.

To address this, I added a capacitor (`ZC1`) between VDD and the internal node `n4`, which is part of the LC interstage network. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path to VDD at high frequencies, thereby suppressing the feedback path and improving the stability margin. Additionally, I added a resistor (`ZR2`) between the output node `n6` and VDD, which helps to dampen any oscillatory behavior and further stabilize the circuit. These changes do not affect the gain, noise figure, or input matching, as they are localized to the feedback and output nodes.
