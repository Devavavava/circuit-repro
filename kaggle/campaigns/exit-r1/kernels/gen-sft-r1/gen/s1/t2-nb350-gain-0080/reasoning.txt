The failure is due to insufficient gain in the LNA, as evidenced by the s21_db margin of -0.757573, which indicates the gain is below the required 25.6085 dB. The anchor netlist uses a push-pull current-reuse topology with a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2), but the gain is limited by the low transconductance of the NMOS and PMOS stages and the poor impedance matching at the output. The NMOS stage (NM1) is likely operating in a region with reduced gain due to improper biasing and the PMOS stage (PM1) may not be effectively contributing to the gain due to the shared DC path and the load configuration.

To address this, I propose a structural change to the topology by replacing the shared DC path with a separate DC path for the PMOS stage and introducing a series inductor (L3) between the PMOS stage and the output. This change improves the impedance matching at the output and allows the PMOS stage to contribute more effectively to the gain. The new inductor (L3) provides a better impedance transformation and helps in boosting the overall gain of the LNA.
