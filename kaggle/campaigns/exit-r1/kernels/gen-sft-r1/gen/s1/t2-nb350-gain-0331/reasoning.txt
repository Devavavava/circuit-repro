The binding constraint is the stability metric `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is failing due to insufficient phase margin across the operating band. This is likely caused by the current-reuse topology and the shared DC path, which introduces parasitic feedback and poor impedance matching at higher frequencies. The PMOS and NMOS stages are tightly coupled through the shared nodes, leading to instability in the wider frequency range.

To address this, I replaced the shared DC path with a decoupled configuration, isolating the NMOS and PMOS stages. This is achieved by using a separate inductor (L3) to decouple the PMOS stage from the NMOS stage, and by reconfiguring the bias network to avoid direct DC coupling. This change improves the impedance matching and reduces parasitic feedback, enhancing the phase margin across the band. The new inductor L3 and the reorganized bias network (NM3, R1, R2) ensure better isolation and stability.
