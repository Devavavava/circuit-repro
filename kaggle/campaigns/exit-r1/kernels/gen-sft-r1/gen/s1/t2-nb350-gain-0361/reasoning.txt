The original topology relies on LC tuning for both stages and interstage coupling, which, while effective for narrowband gain and matching, introduces resonant peaks and phase shifts that can lead to instability at off-band frequencies.

This change improves the stability margin without significantly affecting the narrowband performance, as the feedback is only active outside the design band.
