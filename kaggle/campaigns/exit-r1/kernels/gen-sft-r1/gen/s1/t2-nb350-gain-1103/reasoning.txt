The failure is due to the poor stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) caused by the lack of a direct feedback path from the output to the bias network, which limits the stability of the two-stage amplifier across a wide frequency range. The current structure relies on the bias network to provide a feedback path, but it is not sufficient to ensure robust stability across the entire band, especially at frequencies far from the center frequency.

To address this, I added a direct feedback path from the output node (`n6`) to the bias network node (`n7`) using a capacitor `ZC1`. This creates an additional low-frequency feedback path that helps improve the stability margin without significantly affecting the gain or noise figure. The capacitor provides a path for low-frequency signals to influence the bias network, thereby stabilizing the amplifier over a wider frequency range.
