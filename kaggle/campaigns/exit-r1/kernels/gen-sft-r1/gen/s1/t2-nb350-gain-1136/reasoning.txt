To address this, I added a feedback path from the output of the second stage (n6) to the bias network (n7) using a capacitor `ZC1`, which introduces a low-frequency feedback mechanism that improves phase margin and stabilizes the amplifier over a wider bandwidth. This change helps suppress the resonant feedback path and ensures a more robust stability margin across the band.
