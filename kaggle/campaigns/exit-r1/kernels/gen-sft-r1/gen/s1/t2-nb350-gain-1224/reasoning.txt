The failure is due to insufficient gain from the LNA, as the s21_db margin is negative by ~0.094 dB, falling short of the required 9.9353 dB. The current-reuse topology in the anchor netlist, while effective in noise and power, may not be optimally configured for maximum gain at 3.5 GHz. The push-pull structure with a shared DC path and the tuned load (L2+C2) may be introducing parasitic effects or insufficient impedance transformation at the operating frequency, limiting the achievable gain.

To address this, the topology is restructured to use a cascode configuration with a separate inductor for the output stage, improving impedance matching and gain. The NMOS and PMOS transistors are repositioned to ensure a more direct signal path, and the current mirror is simplified to reduce noise and improve linearity. The output is now driven through an inductor (L3) to the load, improving the output impedance and gain at the target frequency.
