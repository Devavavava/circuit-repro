The failure is due to the poor stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) caused by the resonant LC networks in the interstage and drain loads, which introduce phase shifts that reduce the stability of the two-stage amplifier. The original topology's LC interstage (L2 and C2) and tuned drain loads (L1 and L3) create resonant peaks that can lead to instability, especially over a wide frequency range.

To address this, I replaced the LC interstage with a direct connection between the two stages, removing the series inductor L2 and the interstage capacitor C2. This reduces the phase shift introduced by the LC network and improves the stability margin. Additionally, I restructured the drain load of the first stage (L1) to be connected directly to the second stage's gate, simplifying the signal path and reducing the number of resonant elements.
