This resistor introduces a controlled feedback path from the input to the gate of the diode-connected NMOS transistor (NM3), which helps dampen unwanted oscillations and improve the phase margin across the wideband. The resistor ZR1 acts as a low-frequency stabilizing element, ensuring that the amplifier remains stable even at the lower end of the operating frequency range.
