The failure is primarily due to noise figure (NF) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.07 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure, particularly from the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and the current-setting PMOS (PM2). The noise from these components is amplified due to the current-reuse topology and the lack of proper noise suppression in the DC biasing path.

To address this, I replaced the NM3 diode with a PMOS transistor (PM2) connected to the same gate node as the main amplifier, and restructured the biasing network to reduce noise coupling. This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network by isolating it from the signal path and using a more noise-efficient current mirror configuration.
