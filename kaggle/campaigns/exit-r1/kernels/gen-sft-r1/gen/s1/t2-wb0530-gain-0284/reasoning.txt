The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the NMOS transistor NM3 in the bias network. NM3 is connected as a diode (its source and drain are tied together), and it is biased through R2 and R1. This configuration introduces significant noise into the signal path, especially at high frequencies, which is exacerbated in the current-reuse topology. The noise from NM3 is amplified by the gain of the LNA and contributes to the overall noise figure, pushing it above the specification limit.

To address this, the NM3 diode is replaced with a PMOS transistor (ZP1) connected as a current mirror. This change eliminates the noise contribution from the NM3 diode and provides a more stable and lower-noise biasing mechanism. The PMOS current mirror (ZP1 and PM2) ensures a more accurate and stable current reference, reducing the noise floor. Additionally, the load network is modified by replacing the inductor L2 with a capacitor ZC1, which helps in reducing the noise contribution from the load at high frequencies. These structural changes reduce the noise figure and bring it within the required specification.
