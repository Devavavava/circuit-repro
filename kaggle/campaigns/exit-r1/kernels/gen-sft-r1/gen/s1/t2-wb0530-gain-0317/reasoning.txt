The failure is driven by the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, which is primarily due to the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current reuse structure in the anchor netlist. The NMOS diode NM3 and the PMOS diode PM2 are used to set the bias current, but their noise contributions are amplified in the current-reuse configuration, especially at the high-frequency operating point (f0 ~ 1.2 GHz). The noise from these biasing diodes and the resistive components (R1, R2, R4, R5) in the bias network is not effectively suppressed, leading to a higher-than-acceptable noise figure.

To address this, the topology is restructured to eliminate the noise-prone biasing diodes and resistive biasing network. The NMOS diode NM3 is replaced with a direct connection to a new internal node n6, and the PMOS diode PM2 is removed. The resistor R4 is replaced with a direct connection to VDD, and the resistor R5 is replaced with a direct connection to n2. This simplifies the biasing network, reduces the number of noise-generating components, and minimizes the impact of the biasing network on the noise figure. Additionally, a new capacitor C ZC1 is introduced between n6 and n0 to provide a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, improving the stability and reducing noise coupling from the bias network to the signal path.
