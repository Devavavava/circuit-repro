The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (NF) exceeding the specification. The anchor circuit's noise performance is limited by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure, particularly the NMOS diode NM3 and the resistive biasing elements (R1, R2, R4, R5). These components introduce additional noise and degrade the overall noise figure, especially at the target frequency (f0 = 1.2247 GHz). The current-reuse topology, while beneficial for gain and power efficiency, introduces noise due to the biasing network and the diode-connected NM3, which adds noise to the signal path.

To address this, the proposed topology removes the diode-connected NM3 and the resistive biasing network, replacing them with a simpler current mirror configuration using an NMOS transistor (NM2) and a resistor (R1). This reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network and simplifies the signal path, improving the noise figure. The load network is also adjusted to use a single inductor (L2) and a capacitor (C2) in parallel, which helps in maintaining a stable and low-noise output impedance. The input and output coupling capacitors (C1, C3) are retained to ensure DC blocking and signal coupling.
