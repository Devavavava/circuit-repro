The anchor netlist lacks a direct connection between the output and input nodes, which limits the phase margin across the wideband frequency range, leading to instability.

This capacitor provides a low-impedance path at high frequencies, improving the phase margin and stabilizing the amplifier over the wideband range. This change ensures that the feedback loop is active across the entire frequency range of interest, preventing oscillations and improving the stability metric.
