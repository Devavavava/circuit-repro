The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, which is likely caused by the noise contribution from the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and the current mirror configuration. The diode NM3 and the current mirror setup may be introducing excessive noise, especially at the input node, due to the lack of proper noise suppression mechanisms and the current mirror's inherent noise characteristics. Additionally, the shared DC path and the current-reuse topology may be contributing to noise degradation.

To address this, I propose a structural change that introduces a series inductor (L3) between the output of the NMOS (n2) and the current mirror reference node (n5). This inductor helps to isolate the current mirror from the noise sources at the input and improves the noise performance by reducing the coupling of noise from the current mirror to the input stage. The inductor also helps to stabilize the current mirror and reduce the noise contribution from the diode-connected NMOS (NM3).
