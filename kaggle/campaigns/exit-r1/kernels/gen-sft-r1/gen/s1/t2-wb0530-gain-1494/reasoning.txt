The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification. The noise performance is likely degraded by the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and the current-setting PMOS (PM2), which introduce noise and reduce the signal-to-noise ratio. The current-reuse topology, while effective for gain and power efficiency, may not be optimal for noise in this wideband application. The shared DC path and the use of passive components for biasing may also contribute to noise accumulation.

To address this, I modified the topology by adding a capacitor (ZC1) between the output of the NMOS amplifier (n2) and the input of the PMOS amplifier (n6). This change decouples the output of the NMOS stage from the PMOS stage, reducing noise coupling and improving the noise figure. The capacitor provides a low-impedance path for AC signals while blocking DC, which helps in isolating the noise sources and improving signal integrity.
