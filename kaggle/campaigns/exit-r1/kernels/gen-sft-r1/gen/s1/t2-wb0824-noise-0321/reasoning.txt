The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the lack of a proper feedback mechanism to suppress oscillations across the wideband frequency range. The original topology lacks a direct connection between the output and the feedback path, which is essential for maintaining phase and gain stability over the entire band. The absence of a feedback capacitor and a second inductor in the output path limits the phase margin, leading to instability at certain frequencies.

To address this, I added a feedback capacitor (C ZC1) between the output (n4) and the input node (n2), and a second inductor (L ZL1) between the output node (n2) and the power supply (VDD). These additions provide a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals to return to the input, improving phase margin and ensuring stability across the wideband range. The capacitor ZC1 helps in reducing the feedback phase shift at high frequencies, while the inductor ZL1 acts as a frequency-dependent impedance that enhances the phase margin at the lower end of the band.
