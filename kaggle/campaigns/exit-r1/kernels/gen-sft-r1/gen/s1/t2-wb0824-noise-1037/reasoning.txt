The failure is primarily due to poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint failing by ~0.5 dB. The anchor netlist uses a simple NMOS/PMOS push-pull configuration with a current-reuse topology, but lacks sufficient impedance transformation and tuning at the input to achieve the required -7.8761 dB minimum reflection. The input is DC-blocked with C4, but the series inductor L1 and the NMOS transistor NM1 do not provide sufficient impedance tuning at the target frequency (f0=1385640646.0551019 Hz). The lack of a dedicated input tuning element limits the ability to achieve a low enough S11.

To address this, I added a series inductor L3 between the output of the NMOS transistor (n2) and the input node (n0), and a capacitor Cx between n5 and VDD. The inductor L3 provides additional impedance transformation at the input, allowing for better matching at the target frequency. The capacitor Cx introduces a parallel resonant path at the gate of the NM3 diode, improving the stability and tuning of the input impedance. These changes enhance the input matching, directly improving the s11_max_db metric.
