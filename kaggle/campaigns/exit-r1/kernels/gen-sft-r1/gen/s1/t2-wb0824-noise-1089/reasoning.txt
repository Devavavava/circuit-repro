The binding constraint is the input reflection coefficient (s11_max_db), which is too high (-4.65 dB) and fails by ~0.42 dB. This indicates poor impedance matching at the input. The anchor netlist uses a simple inductor (L1) and capacitor (C4) for input DC blocking and impedance transformation, but the lack of a dedicated input matching network is likely the root cause. The NMOS transistor NM1 is directly connected to the inductor L1, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation over the wideband frequency range specified (f0 = 1.385 GHz). The absence of a second inductor or capacitor in the input path limits the ability to tune the input impedance, especially at the edges of the band.

To address this, I added a second inductor (L3) in parallel with the capacitor C1, connecting node n3 (the drain of NM1) to node n6 (the gate of PM2). This forms a shunt inductor that helps to tune the input impedance by providing an additional reactive component at high frequencies, thereby improving the impedance match and reducing s11_max_db.
