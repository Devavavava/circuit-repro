The failure is driven by the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.15 dB. This is likely due to the noise contribution from the NMOS transistor NM3 in the bias network, which is directly connected to the signal path through the shared node n5. This connection introduces a noise source into the signal path, degrading the noise figure. The current-reuse topology, while beneficial for gain and power efficiency, may not be optimally suppressing noise in this configuration.

To address this, I modified the bias network to isolate the noise source from the signal path. Specifically, I replaced the NMOS transistor NM3 with a PMOS transistor ZP1, connected to a new internal node x1, and added a capacitor ZC1 to decouple the bias network from the signal path. This change ensures that the bias current is set without introducing a noise-generating component into the signal path, thereby reducing the noise figure.
