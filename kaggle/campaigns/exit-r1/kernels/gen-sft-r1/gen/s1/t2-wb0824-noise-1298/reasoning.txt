The failure is due to poor input impedance matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint failing by ~0.075 dB. This indicates that the input reflection coefficient is too high, likely due to an inadequate DC blocking capacitor (C4) and insufficient inductance in the input path (L1), leading to a mismatch at the operating frequency. Additionally, the current-reuse topology may be causing parasitic feedback that degrades the input impedance.

To address this, I replaced the DC-blocking capacitor C4 with a series inductor (L3) and a parallel capacitor (C1) to create a more effective impedance transformer at the input. This helps in achieving a better match by tuning the input impedance to the source. The inductor L3 in series with the input provides a low-impedance path at high frequencies, while the parallel capacitor C1 helps in shunting high-frequency noise to ground, thus improving the reflection coefficient.
