The failure is due to the noise figure (NF) being slightly above the specification, likely caused by the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 and the biasing network. The current-reuse topology in the anchor netlist introduces additional noise paths through the biasing resistors and diode-connected transistors, which are not effectively suppressed in the wideband operation. This is exacerbated by the lack of a direct noise suppression mechanism at the input stage.

To address this, I added a noise-suppressing capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n2 (the drain of NM1) and the biasing node n5. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise, effectively shorting it to ground and reducing its contribution to the noise figure. This change enhances the noise performance of the LNA without significantly affecting the gain or stability, as it operates primarily at high frequencies where the noise is most impactful.
