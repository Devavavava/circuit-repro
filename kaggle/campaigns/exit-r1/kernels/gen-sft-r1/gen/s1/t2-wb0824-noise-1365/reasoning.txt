The failure is due to poor input impedance matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint failing by ~0.3 dB. The original topology uses a capacitive input (C4) and an inductor (L1) to DC-block and couple the signal, but the lack of a proper impedance transformation mechanism at the input leads to a mismatch. The NMOS and PMOS stages are configured in a push-pull configuration, but without a dedicated input impedance tuning element, the reflection coefficient at the input is too high.

To address this, I added a series inductor (L3) and a resistor (Rrep1) to the input path, forming a series LC network (L3 and C4) that improves impedance matching at the operating frequency. This change allows for better control over the input impedance, reducing reflections and improving the S11 performance. The addition of Rrep1 provides a controlled DC path and helps stabilize the input node, ensuring the amplifier sees a more predictable impedance.
