The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, exceeding the maximum allowed value of 2.0748 dB by approximately 0.2 dB. This indicates that the noise contribution from the transistor NM1 is too high, likely due to its high noise generation at the input node. The original topology uses a current-reuse configuration with a shared DC path and a tuned load, but the noise performance is compromised by the lack of a noise-shaping mechanism at the input.

To address this, I modified the topology by adding a capacitor (ZC1) between the input node (n0) and the internal node n6, which is connected to the current-setting network. This addition helps to decouple the input from the noise-generating current-setting network, reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network to the signal path. The capacitor ZC1 acts as a noise-shaping element, filtering out noise from the current mirror and improving the noise figure of the LNA.
