The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.23 dB. This is likely caused by the current-reuse topology in the anchor netlist, where the noise contributions from the NMOS and PMOS transistors are not sufficiently suppressed. The noise performance is also affected by the shared DC path and the load configuration, which may not be optimally tuned for low-noise operation at the specified wideband frequency.

To address this, I propose a structural change that eliminates the shared DC path and reconfigures the load network to reduce noise contributions. The new topology uses a separate DC path for the NMOS and PMOS stages, and replaces the tuned load with a different configuration that better suppresses noise. This is achieved by introducing additional capacitors and inductors to isolate the noise-sensitive nodes and improve impedance matching.
