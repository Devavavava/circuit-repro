The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is below the required minimum. This is likely caused by the feedback path from the output to the input through the inductive load and the current-reuse structure, leading to instability at certain frequencies within the wideband range. The current topology allows for excessive phase shift or gain at specific frequencies, reducing the stability margin.

To address this, I added a resistor (ZR1) from the internal node n5 (which is connected to the gate of NM3 and R2) to the output node n2. This resistor provides a controlled feedback path that dampens oscillations and improves the stability margin by reducing the phase shift and gain at problematic frequencies. The resistor is placed in a position where it can influence the feedback loop without significantly degrading the noise figure or gain.
