The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the single-stage shunt-feedback topology. The lack of a second-stage or additional feedback mechanism leads to poor phase margin across the wideband frequency range, resulting in instability at certain frequencies. This is exacerbated by the resistive shunt feedback and the inductor-based peaking load, which may not provide sufficient phase compensation over the entire band.

To address this, I added a second-stage common-source amplifier with an inductor-coupled load and additional feedback paths. This includes a second NMOS transistor (NM2) and a PMOS transistor (PM1) to form a second stage, along with additional inductors (L2, L3) and capacitors (C1, C2, C3, C4) to provide better phase compensation and stability. The resistive divider (R1, R2, R4) and additional capacitors (C5) help in biasing and stabilizing the second stage. The addition of PMOS transistor PM1 and NMOS transistor NM3 provides additional feedback and biasing paths to improve stability and phase margin across the wideband range.
