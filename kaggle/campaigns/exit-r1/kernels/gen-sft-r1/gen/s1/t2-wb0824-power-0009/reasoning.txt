To address this, I introduce a PMOS transistor (PM1) in a cascode configuration with the NMOS transistor (NM1), and add a second inductor (L2) and capacitor (C2) in a feedback path from the output to the cascode node. This adds a second stage of amplification and introduces additional phase compensation through the inductor and capacitor, improving the stability margin across the wideband range.
