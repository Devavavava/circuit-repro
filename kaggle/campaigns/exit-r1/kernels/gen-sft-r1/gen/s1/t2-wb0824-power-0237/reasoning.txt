The failure is due to the noise figure (NF) exceeding the specification, primarily caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure. The NMOS transistor NM3 in the anchor netlist is connected as a diode (n5-n5) and biased through R1 and R2, which introduces additional noise into the signal path. This noise is amplified by the current-reuse topology, leading to an NF that is too high. Additionally, the PMOS gate biasing through PM2 and R4/R5 adds further noise and complexity to the signal path, degrading the overall noise performance.

To address this, the NM3 diode is reconnected to a different node (n4 instead of VSS), which is part of the tuned load (L2+C2). This change decouples the biasing network from the signal path, reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network. By connecting NM3 to n4, the biasing current is now sourced from the tuned load, which helps to maintain a cleaner signal path and reduce noise injection. This structural change directly impacts the noise figure by isolating the biasing network from the main signal path, thereby improving the NF and meeting the specification.
