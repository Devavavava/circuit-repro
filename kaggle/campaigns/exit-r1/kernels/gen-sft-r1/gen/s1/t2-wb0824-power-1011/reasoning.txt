The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) across the wideband frequency range. The original topology uses a resistive shunt feedback and a shunt-peaked inductive load, which may not provide sufficient phase margin at higher frequencies, leading to instability. The feedback resistor R1 and the inductive peaking element L1+R2 may not adequately suppress oscillations over the entire band, especially when the circuit is sized for maximum gain and minimum noise.

To address this, I propose a structural change that introduces a second-stage cascode configuration with an additional inductor and capacitor to enhance the phase margin and stabilize the amplifier over the wideband range. This topology adds a second NMOS transistor (NM2) in a cascode configuration with an inductor (L2) and a capacitor (C2) to provide additional phase shift and improve stability. Additionally, a second feedback resistor (R3) and a self-biasing network (R4, NM3) are introduced to ensure stable DC operation and further enhance the phase margin.
