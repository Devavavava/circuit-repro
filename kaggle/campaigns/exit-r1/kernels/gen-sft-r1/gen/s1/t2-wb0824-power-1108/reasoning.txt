The failure is primarily due to the poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint failing by ~0.11 dB. The anchor netlist uses a series inductor (L1) and capacitor (C4) for DC blocking, but lacks a direct connection from the input to a node that can influence the input impedance. The absence of a direct feedback or impedance control mechanism from the input to the internal nodes results in a mismatch that cannot be corrected by sizing alone.

To address this, I added a direct connection from the input (VIN1) to the internal node n2, which is the drain of the NMOS transistor NM1. This change introduces a direct coupling path from the input to the active transistor, allowing for better impedance control and improved matching. The addition of the capacitor C ZC1 between n5 and n4 also helps in stabilizing the node and reducing unwanted feedback that could degrade the input matching.
