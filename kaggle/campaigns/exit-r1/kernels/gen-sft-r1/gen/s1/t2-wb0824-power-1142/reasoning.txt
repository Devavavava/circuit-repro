To address this, I added a PMOS transistor (PM1) in a cascode configuration with the NMOS transistor (NM1), forming a PMOS-NMOS cascode pair. A capacitor (C2) was also added between VDD and VOUT1 to further stabilize the output node and reduce high-frequency oscillations.
