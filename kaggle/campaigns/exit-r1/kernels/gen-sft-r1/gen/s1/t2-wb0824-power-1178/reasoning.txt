The original topology uses a current-reuse push-pull CS LNA with a shared DC path, which may lead to poor noise performance at the target frequency due to the inherent noise of the current mirror and the shared load. Additionally, the noise from NM3 and PM2 may be contributing to the overall noise figure.

To address this, I propose a structural change to the topology by introducing a direct coupling between the output node (n4) and the gate of NM3 (n5), and adding a PMOS transistor (ZP1) to create a separate DC path from VDD to n5. This change isolates the noise contributions from the current mirror and the shared load, reducing the overall noise figure. The addition of ZP1 provides a more stable and lower-noise biasing path for NM3, while the direct coupling between n4 and n5 ensures that the output signal is more effectively fed back to the noise-sensitive node, improving the noise performance.
