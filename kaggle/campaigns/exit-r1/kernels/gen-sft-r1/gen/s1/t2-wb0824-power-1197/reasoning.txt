The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, despite the other metrics being within bounds. The noise performance is likely being degraded by the current-reuse topology and the shared DC path, which introduces additional noise contributions from the biasing network and the current mirror. The NMOS current mirror (NM3) and the PMOS current setting (PM2) may be introducing noise that is amplified in the signal path, especially at the wideband frequency (1.38 GHz). The shared DC path and the lack of isolation between the biasing network and the signal path may also be contributing to noise degradation.

To address this, I propose a structural change that isolates the biasing network from the signal path and introduces a separate DC path for the current mirror. This is achieved by adding a new PMOS device (PM2) connected to a new internal node (n6), which is then connected to a new resistor (R4) to ground. This change decouples the current mirror from the signal path, reducing noise contributions from the biasing network. Additionally, the new PMOS (PM2) and resistor (R4) provide a more stable current reference, improving noise performance.
