The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by poor feedback and phase compensation in the current-reuse topology. The original design lacks a direct feedback path from the output to the input, leading to a narrow stability band and insufficient phase margin across the wideband frequency range. This results in instability at the edges of the operating band, causing the mu_min constraint to fail.

To address this, I added a direct feedback path from the output (VOUT1) to the internal node n6 (the gate of PM2) via a new NMOS transistor ZN1. This creates a local feedback loop that enhances phase compensation and broadens the stability band. The feedback path helps to reduce phase lag at high frequencies and ensures a more consistent stability margin across the wideband range.
