The failure is due to the noise figure (NF) being too high, exceeding the maximum allowed value of 2.2546 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the NMOS transistor NM3 in the bias network, which is directly connected to the ground (VSS) and may introduce additional noise into the signal path. The current-reuse topology, while effective in improving gain, may not be sufficiently isolating the bias network from the signal path, leading to increased noise.

To address this, I introduce a new node (x1) and connect it to the drain of NM3 and the source of PM2. This change modifies the biasing path and isolates the noise-sensitive NM3 transistor from the signal path, reducing its contribution to the overall noise figure. The addition of the resistor Rrep1 between x1 and n6 further helps in stabilizing the bias current and reducing noise coupling.
