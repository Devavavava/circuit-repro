The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, which is likely caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure. The NMOS bias diode (NM3) and the PMOS current-setting diode (PM2) introduce additional noise due to their low impedance and high current density, especially in the absence of proper noise isolation. The current-reuse topology may also be causing a feedback path that increases the noise floor at the input.

To address this, I modified the topology by replacing the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor (Rrep1) connected to the same internal node (n5), which acts as a noise-isolating impedance. This change decouples the biasing network from the signal path, reducing noise coupling and lowering the noise figure. Additionally, I restructured the current-setting path for the PMOS gate by connecting the diode (PM2) to a new internal node (n6) and adding a resistor (Rrep1) from n6 to n5, which helps in better current regulation and noise suppression.
