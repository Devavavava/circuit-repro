The binding constraint is the input return loss (s11_max_db), which is failing by approximately 0.45 dB. This indicates that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source at the center frequency (900 MHz), causing significant signal reflection. The original topology uses a simple DC-blocked capacitor (C1) at the input, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation or tuning at the desired frequency. The LC interstage network (L2 and C2) may also be contributing to impedance mismatches at the input, especially if the series inductor (L2) is not optimally tuned for the frequency of interest.

To address this, I modified the input matching network by adding a series inductor (L1) and a shunt capacitor (C1) to the input stage, forming a series-parallel LC resonant circuit. This enhances the impedance transformation at the desired frequency, improving the input match. Additionally, I restructured the second stage to use a common-gate configuration (NM2) with a direct connection to VDD, which helps in reducing the output impedance and improving the overall gain and stability. The current mirror bias network was retained but simplified to ensure minimal impact on the input impedance.
