The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_db) exceeding the specification, despite the other metrics being close to or slightly below their limits. The anchor netlist uses a current-reuse topology with a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2), but it appears that the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current mirror are not adequately suppressed. The NMOS and PMOS transistors in the biasing network (NM3, PM2) and the resistive components (R1, R2, R4, R5) likely introduce additional noise that is not effectively managed by the current-reuse structure, especially at the target frequency of 900 MHz. The noise from the biasing network is not sufficiently isolated from the signal path, leading to a higher-than-allowed noise figure.

To address this, I propose a structural change that isolates the biasing network from the signal path and reduces the noise contribution from the current mirror. This is achieved by removing the direct connection between the biasing network and the signal path and instead using a separate DC path for the biasing network. This change minimizes the noise coupling from the biasing network to the signal path and improves the noise figure.
