To address this, I replaced the NMOS diode (NM3) with a PMOS diode (ZP1) in the bias network. This change helps reduce the noise contribution from the biasing network by using a PMOS device, which typically has lower noise characteristics in this configuration. The PMOS diode provides a more stable and lower-noise current reference, which improves the noise figure of the LNA. Additionally, the PMOS diode is connected to a new internal node (n7) to ensure proper isolation from the signal path and to avoid any noise coupling from the bias network to the main signal path.
