The failure is due to poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint failing by ~0.55 dB. The original topology uses a direct coupling capacitor C1 from VIN1 to the gate of NM1, without any impedance transformation or matching network. This leads to a mismatch between the input signal source and the LNA's input impedance, especially at the center frequency f0 = 900 MHz.

To address this, I added a series inductor L2 between the drain of NM1 and the LC interstage network, which helps in tuning the input impedance and improving matching. Additionally, I introduced a new node x1 and connected it to the drain of NM3 and the gate of NM2, creating a feedback path that enhances the stability and improves the input impedance. The capacitor ZC1 between VDD and the drain of NM1 helps in further tuning the input impedance and reducing unwanted resonances.
