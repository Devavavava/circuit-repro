The binding constraint is the input return loss (s11_max_db), which is failing by ~0.34 dB. This indicates that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source, likely due to the LC tank at the input (L1 and C1) not being optimally tuned or the lack of a dedicated input matching network. The current topology relies on the LC tank for both tuning and matching, which may not provide sufficient control over the input impedance at the desired frequency. Additionally, the second stage's gate is driven through an LC interstage network (L2 and C2), which may introduce unwanted phase shifts or impedance mismatches that affect the overall input matching.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (C ZC1) between VDD and the drain of NM1, which helps in fine-tuning the input impedance by providing a parallel resonant path at the operating frequency. This allows for better control over the input impedance without significantly affecting the gain or noise figure. The capacitor also helps in reducing the reactive component of the input impedance, leading to a better match.
