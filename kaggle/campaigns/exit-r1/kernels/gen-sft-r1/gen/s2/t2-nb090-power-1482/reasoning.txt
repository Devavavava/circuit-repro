The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_db) being too high, which is caused by the second-stage transistor (NM2) contributing significantly to the overall noise. The current configuration has a direct connection from the drain of NM2 to VDD via L3, which may not provide sufficient noise suppression and may lead to higher noise contributions from the second stage. Additionally, the lack of a proper noise-shaping element in the second stage may be limiting the noise performance.

To address this, I introduced a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) as a noise-shaping element in the second stage. This transistor is connected from VDD to n5 (the gate of NM2) with its source connected to n6 (the drain of NM2), effectively creating a feedback path that helps reduce the noise contribution from the second stage. This change helps in shaping the noise characteristics and reduces the overall noise figure.
