The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` is failing due to insufficient stability margin across the wide frequency range. The original topology, while effective at the center frequency (f0), lacks sufficient feedback and impedance control to maintain stability at off-band frequencies. The interstage LC network and the second-stage load inductors create resonances that can lead to phase shifts and potential instability outside the narrowband design focus.

To address this, I added a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) and a capacitor (ZC1) to introduce additional feedback and impedance control. The NMOS ZN1 is connected between VDD and VSS with its gate tied to the output node (n6), providing a low-impedance path for feedback and helping to dampen unwanted oscillations. The capacitor ZC1 is placed between VDD and the interstage node (n4), which helps stabilize the interstage LC network by reducing high-frequency resonances and improving the phase margin across the wideband range. These changes enhance the stability of the LNA without significantly affecting the gain or noise figure.
