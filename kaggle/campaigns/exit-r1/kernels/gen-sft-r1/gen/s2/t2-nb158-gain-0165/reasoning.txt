The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the low transconductance and poor impedance matching in the current-reuse push-pull topology. The push-pull configuration introduces additional losses and phase shifts, especially at the narrowband frequency, which degrades the gain and stability. The current-reuse structure also causes a mismatch in the signal paths, leading to a reduction in the overall gain.

To address this, I propose replacing the push-pull current-reuse topology with a single-stage common-source (CS) LNA with a resonant inductive load. This configuration offers higher transconductance and better impedance matching at the target frequency, thereby improving the gain and stability. The removal of the push-pull structure eliminates the additional losses and phase shifts, while the resonant load enhances the gain at the narrowband frequency.
