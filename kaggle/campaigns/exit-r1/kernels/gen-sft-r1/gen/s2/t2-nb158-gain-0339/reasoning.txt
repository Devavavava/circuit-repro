The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the interstage LC network (C2 and L2), which introduces a resonant peak in the feedback path and destabilizes the amplifier over a wide frequency range. This resonant feedback causes the phase margin to drop below the required threshold, leading to instability.

To address this, I replaced the LC interstage network with a capacitive coupling (C2) directly connecting the first stage's drain to the second stage's gate, eliminating the inductive component that caused the resonant feedback. Additionally, I restructured the second stage's configuration to a common-gate topology (NM2 with source connected to VDD), which inherently improves stability by reducing the feedback from the output to the input. This change also allows for better impedance matching and reduces the phase shift introduced by the second stage, thereby improving the stability margin.
