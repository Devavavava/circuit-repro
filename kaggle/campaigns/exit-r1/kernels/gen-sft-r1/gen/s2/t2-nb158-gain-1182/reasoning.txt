The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second-stage transistor NM2 and the interstage LC network. The second-stage's transconductance and output impedance are not sufficient to achieve the required gain, especially when the LC interstage network introduces additional loss or mismatch at the operating frequency. The current configuration relies on the LC interstage for impedance transformation and signal coupling, but it may not be effectively boosting the signal level due to impedance mismatches or insufficient amplification from NM2.

To address this, I propose adding a shunt capacitor (ZC1) across the drain of NM1 and VDD, which helps in improving the output impedance of the first stage and reducing signal loss at the operating frequency. Additionally, I introduce a resistor (ZR1) from the output of the second stage to VDD, which helps in stabilizing the second-stage output and improving the overall gain by reducing the Miller effect and enhancing the transconductance of NM2. These changes improve the signal transfer between stages and enhance the gain of the second stage, directly addressing the insufficient s21_db.
