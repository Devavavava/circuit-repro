The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second-stage transistor (NM2) and the interstage LC network. The second stage is not sufficiently amplifying the signal due to the LC interstage network acting as a bandpass filter that attenuates the signal at the center frequency, reducing the overall gain. The second-stage transistor may also be operating in a suboptimal region due to the biasing network and lack of proper impedance matching at the input of the second stage.

To address this, I propose a structural change that improves the impedance matching and signal transfer between the two stages. The original netlist uses an LC interstage network (L2 and C2) that may be causing signal loss and phase shift at the operating frequency. By replacing the LC interstage network with a direct connection between the first and second stages, and adding a series inductor at the input of the second stage, the signal path becomes more efficient and the second stage can operate in a better region. This change also eliminates the potential for signal attenuation from the LC interstage network, directly improving the gain.
