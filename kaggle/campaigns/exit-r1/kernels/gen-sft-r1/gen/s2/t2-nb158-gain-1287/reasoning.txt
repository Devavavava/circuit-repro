The failure is primarily due to poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being -5.12 dB, which is 0.35 dB below the required -7.91 dB. This indicates that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source, causing significant signal reflection at the input port. The root cause lies in the lack of a dedicated input matching network, relying only on the intrinsic impedance of the first transistor and the LC tank. This setup is insufficient for achieving the required impedance match at the narrowband frequency of 1.575 GHz.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the interstage LC network (n4) and the bias node (n7). This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, effectively improving the input impedance match by allowing better control over the phase and magnitude of the input impedance at the operating frequency. This change enhances the reflection coefficient (S11) without significantly affecting the gain or noise figure, as the capacitor does not introduce additional losses or nonlinearities.
