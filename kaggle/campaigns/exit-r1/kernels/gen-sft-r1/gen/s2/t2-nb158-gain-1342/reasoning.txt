The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the resonant LC interstage network and the lack of a direct feedback path to stabilize the second stage. The LC interstage (L2 and C2) creates a resonant peak that can lead to instability when combined with the second-stage transistor (NM2) and its load (L3). The absence of a feedback path from the output to the second stage exacerbates this instability, especially over a wide frequency range.

To address this, I added a direct feedback path from the output (VOUT1) to the second stage via a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) and a resistor (Rrep1) from the output to VDD. This feedback path introduces a damping effect, improving the stability margin by providing a controlled return path for signals, thus preventing oscillations and enhancing the phase margin over the operating band.
