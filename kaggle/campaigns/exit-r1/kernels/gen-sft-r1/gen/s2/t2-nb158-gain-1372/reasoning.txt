This additional capacitor provides an alternative path for high-frequency signals, improving the input impedance matching by allowing better tuning of the input network. The capacitor helps to stabilize and adjust the impedance seen at the input, thereby improving the reflection coefficient (S11) without significantly affecting the gain or noise figure.
