The failure is due to insufficient gain in the second stage, leading to a low S21_db value. The original topology uses a common-source configuration for both stages, but the second stage is loaded with an inductor (L3) directly to VDD, which may not provide enough impedance transformation or voltage gain. The interstage LC network (C2 and L2) may not be effectively coupling the stages, leading to a reduced overall gain. The biasing network, while functional, does not directly contribute to the gain limitation.

To address this, I modified the second stage by replacing the inductor load (L3) with a capacitor (C2) connected to a new internal node (n3), and then added a new inductor (L2) from this node to ground. This change transforms the second stage into a common-gate configuration, which provides better impedance transformation and higher voltage gain. The common-gate stage is more effective at boosting the signal from the first stage, especially when loaded with a low-impedance path to ground via the inductor. This structural change directly improves the S21_db by increasing the voltage gain of the second stage.
