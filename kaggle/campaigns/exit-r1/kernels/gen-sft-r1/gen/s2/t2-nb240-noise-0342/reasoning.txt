The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the interstage LC network and the biasing structure. The LC interstage (L2 and C2) introduces a resonant feedback path that destabilizes the amplifier at frequencies beyond the design band, especially when the biasing network (R1, R2, R3, NM3) introduces additional phase shifts. This results in a lower stability factor (mu) at off-band frequencies, leading to the margin being negative.

To address this, I replaced the LC interstage with a direct connection between the stages, removing L2 and C2, and restructured the biasing network to reduce phase shifts. This change eliminates the resonant feedback path that destabilizes the amplifier at off-band frequencies, improving the stability margin.
