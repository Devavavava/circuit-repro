The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the lack of a feedback path between the two stages, leading to potential oscillations across the wide frequency band. This is exacerbated by the absence of a direct connection between the output of the second stage and the bias network or a feedback path to the first stage, which limits the phase margin and introduces instability.

To address this, I added a direct connection from the output of the second stage (n6) to the bias network (n7) via a resistor (ZR1), and from the bias network (n7) to the input of the first stage (n1) via a capacitor (ZC2). This introduces a feedback path and helps stabilize the amplifier over the wide frequency range, improving the stability margin.
