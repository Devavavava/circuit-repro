The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the interstage LC network and the feedback paths in the two-stage topology. The LC interstage (L2 and C2) introduces a resonant feedback path that destabilizes the circuit across a wide frequency range, especially at frequencies near the resonant frequency of the interstage LC network. This feedback reduces the stability margin (mu_min), causing the circuit to fail the specification.

To address this, I restructured the topology by removing the LC interstage network and replacing it with a direct connection between the two stages. This eliminates the resonant feedback path and improves stability. Additionally, I reconfigured the second stage to use a common-gate configuration (NM2) with a source inductor (L2) to improve impedance matching and reduce feedback effects. The output capacitor (C2) is now directly connected to the output, simplifying the topology and reducing parasitic feedback paths.
