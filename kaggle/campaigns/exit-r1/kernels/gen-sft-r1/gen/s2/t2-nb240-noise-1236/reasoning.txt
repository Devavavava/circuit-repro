The binding constraint is the stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is failing due to insufficient phase margin in the feedback loop of the two-stage LNA. This is likely caused by the lack of a direct feedback path or a phase-shifting element between the stages, leading to potential instability across the wide frequency band. The original topology uses an LC interstage network, which may not provide sufficient phase control or damping at off-band frequencies, especially when the second stage's output is directly coupled to the output through a capacitor, without any impedance or phase-shifting element.

To address this, I added a resistor (ZR2) from the output (VOUT1) to ground (VSS), which introduces a controlled impedance and provides a low-frequency feedback path, improving the phase margin and stabilizing the LNA across the wide band. This change directly addresses the stability issue without altering the core topology or adding unnecessary components.
