The absence of a dedicated feedback path or a stabilizing component in the signal path leads to a low mu_min value, causing instability.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output to the second-stage gate using a resistor (Rrep1) and a capacitor (ZC2) to ground, which introduces a controlled feedback mechanism. This helps to stabilize the amplifier by reducing the gain at higher frequencies and improving the phase margin. Additionally, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the first-stage drain and the interstage LC network to provide a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, reducing unwanted resonances and improving the stability margin.
