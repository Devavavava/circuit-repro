While this configuration achieves good noise figure (NF), gain (S21), and power consumption (IDD), it lacks sufficient feedback and damping mechanisms to maintain stability over a broad band. The current mirror biasing and LC resonances create a narrowband response, and the absence of a direct feedback path from the output to the input or intermediate stages leads to instability at off-band frequencies, resulting in a low stability factor (mu) at some points in the 0.01–50 GHz range.

To address this, the topology is modified by adding a feedback path from the output to the intermediate stage. This is achieved by introducing a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) that connects the output node (n6) to the drain of the first stage (n2), with its source connected to the second stage's source (n5). This creates a local feedback loop that helps dampen oscillations and improve stability across a wider frequency range.
