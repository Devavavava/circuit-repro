The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second-stage transistor NM2's inability to deliver sufficient voltage gain due to the lack of a proper load impedance at its drain. The original topology uses a simple inductor L3 connected to VDD as the load for NM2, which at 2.4 GHz may not provide a sufficiently high impedance to enable the required gain. This results in a lower-than-targeted s21_db, causing the failure.

To address this, I introduce a series inductor (L2) and a parallel capacitor (C2) between the output of the first stage and the input of the second stage, forming a resonant network that enhances the impedance seen by NM2 at the operating frequency. This improves the voltage gain of the second stage by providing a higher impedance load. Additionally, I reconfigure the second stage's drain load to use a capacitor (C3) in parallel with an inductor (L3), which ensures a high-impedance load at 2.4 GHz. The bias network is restructured to use a current mirror with a resistor (R2) and a diode-connected transistor (NM3) to ensure stable biasing without affecting the signal path.
