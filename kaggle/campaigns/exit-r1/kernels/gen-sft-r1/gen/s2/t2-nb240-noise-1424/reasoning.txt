The failure is due to the poor stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) caused by the resonant LC tank in the interstage network (L2 and C2), which introduces a feedback path that destabilizes the amplifier over a wide frequency range. This is exacerbated by the current mirror biasing network (R1, NM3, R2, R3), which adds additional parasitic feedback paths and reduces the phase margin at the operating frequency.

To address this, the interstage LC network is replaced with a series inductor (L2) directly connecting the drain of NM1 to the gate of NM2, eliminating the resonant feedback path. The original LC interstage (L2 and C2) is replaced with a simple inductor (L2) to provide impedance transformation while avoiding resonant feedback. Additionally, the drain load of the second stage is restructured to use a series inductor (L3) to VDD, which improves stability by reducing the feedback path from the output to the input. The output capacitor (C3) is repositioned to connect directly to the output node (VOUT1), ensuring clean signal coupling without introducing additional resonant nodes.
