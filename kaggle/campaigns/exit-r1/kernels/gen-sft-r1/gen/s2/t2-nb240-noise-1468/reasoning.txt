The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is negative, indicating instability over a wide frequency range. This is likely caused by the lack of a direct feedback path or stabilization element in the circuit, particularly in the second stage. The current topology relies on the LC tank for tuning and impedance transformation, but it does not inherently provide sufficient feedback to suppress oscillations across the band.

To address this, I added a small capacitor (`ZC1`) between the drain of the first stage (n2) and the interstage LC network (n4). This capacitor introduces a low-impedance path at higher frequencies, effectively creating a feedback mechanism that stabilizes the second stage and suppresses unwanted oscillations. This change does not interfere with the narrowband tuning at 2.4 GHz but improves stability across the entire 0.01–50 GHz range.
