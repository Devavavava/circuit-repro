The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the interstage LC network and the feedback path through the bias network. This leads to a narrow stability band and a low stability factor at off-band frequencies. The original topology's interstage LC network and the current mirror biasing create a feedback path that reduces the stability margin, especially at higher frequencies.

To address this, I replaced the interstage LC network with a direct connection between the two stages, eliminating the feedback path that was causing instability. Additionally, I reconfigured the bias network to use a separate biasing structure that does not interfere with the signal path, improving the stability margin. This change reduces the number of inductors and simplifies the signal path, leading to better stability and performance across the frequency band.
