The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the LC interstage network and the lack of a direct feedback path between the stages. The current topology lacks a mechanism to suppress oscillations across a wide frequency range, especially at frequencies near the operating point. This is exacerbated by the absence of a direct connection between the output of the first stage and the second stage, which limits the phase margin and leads to a low stability factor.

To address this, I added a feedback capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the internal node n4, which is part of the LC interstage network. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, effectively damping oscillations and improving the stability margin. The addition of ZC1 introduces a controlled phase shift at higher frequencies, which helps to increase the stability factor (mu) without significantly affecting the gain or noise figure.
