The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second stage's transconductance and the load impedance. The original topology uses a tuned drain load (L3) for the second stage, which may not provide sufficient impedance at the operating frequency, leading to a lower voltage gain than required. Additionally, the second stage's input is coupled through an LC network (L2 and C2), which may introduce parasitic effects or insufficient signal transfer at the desired frequency.

To address this, I replaced the second stage's configuration from a common-source (CS) NMOS with a common-gate (CG) NMOS (NM2), which offers a higher transconductance and a more favorable input-output impedance match at the operating frequency. This change improves the voltage gain of the second stage and enhances the overall gain of the LNA. The LC interstage network was also modified to connect directly to the new common-gate stage, ensuring a more efficient signal transfer and reducing parasitic effects.
