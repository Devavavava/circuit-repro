The failure is due to insufficient gain in the second stage, which is likely caused by the lack of a direct feedback or impedance control mechanism between the stages. The current topology relies on LC tuning for both stages and an interstage inductor, but it lacks a mechanism to ensure sufficient signal transfer and amplification from the first to the second stage. This results in a suboptimal gain margin, as seen in the s21_db constraint failing by ~0.22 dB.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the internal node n4, which is the node connected to the LC interstage network. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, ensuring that the second stage receives a stronger signal from the first stage. This change enhances the signal transfer between stages, thereby improving the gain without significantly affecting other performance metrics like noise figure or power consumption.
