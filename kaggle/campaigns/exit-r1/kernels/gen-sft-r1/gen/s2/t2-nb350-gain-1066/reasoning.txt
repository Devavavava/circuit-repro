The interstage LC network (L2 and C2) is not effectively decoupling the stages, allowing for unwanted feedback paths that destabilize the circuit at off-center frequencies.

To address this, I restructured the circuit to include an additional inductor (L2) in the source of the second-stage transistor (NM2), which increases its output conductance and improves the stability margin. Additionally, I replaced the original LC load on the first stage with a direct connection to VDD, and reconfigured the bias network to use a separate current mirror (NM3) with a direct connection to the first stage’s gate. This change ensures better DC biasing and reduces the impact of the second stage on the first, thereby improving the stability margin over the entire band.
