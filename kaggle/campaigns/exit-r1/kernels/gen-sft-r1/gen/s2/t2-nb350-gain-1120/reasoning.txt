The failure is due to insufficient gain in the LNA, as the achieved s21_db of 10.3429 dB falls short of the required 14.2693 dB by approximately 3.9 dB. This indicates that the current topology is not effectively amplifying the signal at the target frequency. The structure uses a current-reuse push-pull configuration with a shared DC path and a tuned load, but it lacks sufficient gain boosting mechanisms and may suffer from poor impedance matching or inadequate signal swing.

To address this, I propose a structural change that replaces the push-pull configuration with a common-gate (CG) stage for the NMOS transistor, which improves gain and bandwidth. Additionally, the PMOS transistor is reconfigured into a common-source (CS) stage with a separate biasing path. A new inductor (L3) is added to the output path to enhance impedance transformation and improve gain. The diode-connected NMOS (NM3) is replaced with a resistor (Rrep1) to provide a more stable bias reference. These changes improve the signal path and increase the overall gain while maintaining low noise and stability.
