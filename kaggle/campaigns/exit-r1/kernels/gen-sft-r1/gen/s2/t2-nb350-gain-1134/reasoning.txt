The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the lack of a proper feedback path and the absence of a direct connection between the output and the bias network. This results in insufficient phase margin and poor stability across the wide frequency range. Additionally, the absence of a direct connection from the output to the bias network limits the ability to control the output impedance and stabilize the circuit.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output to the bias network by introducing a capacitor (ZC1) between VOUT1 and the bias node (n7), and a resistor (ZR2) from the bias node (n7) to VDD. This provides a low-impedance path for feedback, improving the stability margin by allowing the bias network to respond to output variations. Additionally, I added a resistor (ZR3) from the output (VOUT1) to VDD to provide a DC path and stabilize the output impedance.
