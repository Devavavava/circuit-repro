This is exacerbated by the lack of a direct feedback suppression mechanism between the stages, allowing unwanted oscillations to occur in the broader frequency range.

To address this, the topology was modified to include a direct feedback suppression path using an additional NMOS transistor (ZN1) and a capacitor (ZC1) between the first stage's drain (n2) and the second stage's source (n5). This creates a low-impedance path for feedback at higher frequencies, effectively dampening the resonant feedback from the interstage LC network and improving the stability margin across the wideband range. The addition of ZN1 and ZC1 adds a controlled feedback mechanism that suppresses unwanted oscillations without significantly affecting the gain or noise figure of the LNA.
