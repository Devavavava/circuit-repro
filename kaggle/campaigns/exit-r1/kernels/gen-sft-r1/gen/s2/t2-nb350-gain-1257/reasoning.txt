The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the lack of a feedback path and the absence of a stabilizing element in the second stage. This results in a narrow stability band and insufficient phase margin at the operating frequency, leading to instability. The current structure lacks a direct feedback path from the output to the input or a stabilizing inductor/capacitor in the second stage, which is necessary to ensure stability over a wide frequency range.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output to the input using a capacitor (C ZC1) and a resistor (R Rrep1) in the bias network, which helps in stabilizing the second stage. Additionally, I introduced a capacitor (C ZC2) in the interstage LC network to provide a low-impedance path at high frequencies, improving phase margin and stability.
