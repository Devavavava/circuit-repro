This capacitor provides additional damping at higher frequencies, reducing the resonant feedback that destabilizes the second stage. The addition of ZC1 improves the phase margin and suppresses unwanted oscillations across the wideband, directly increasing the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`.
