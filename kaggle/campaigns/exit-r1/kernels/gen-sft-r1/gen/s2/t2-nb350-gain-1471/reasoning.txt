The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_db) exceeding the specification by approximately 0.266 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure in the original topology. The NMOS diode NM3 and the PMOS diode PM2, along with their associated resistors, introduce noise that is amplified in the current-reuse configuration, degrading the noise performance. Additionally, the shared DC path and the lack of isolation between the biasing and signal paths may contribute to increased noise and reduced stability.

To address this, I modified the topology to isolate the biasing network from the signal path and reduce the noise contribution from the biasing structure. I replaced the NMOS diode NM3 with a NMOS transistor NM3 configured as a current source, and added a resistor Rrep1 to provide a separate biasing path. This change improves the noise performance by reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network and enhances the stability of the circuit.
