The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the lack of a feedback mechanism to suppress oscillations across the wide frequency range. The original topology lacks a direct feedback path from the output to the input, which is essential for ensuring stability, especially in a two-stage amplifier with resonant loads. This absence allows for a lower stability factor (mu) at off-band frequencies, leading to instability.

To address this, I added a direct feedback path from the output to the input by introducing a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) and a capacitor (ZC1) to provide a low-impedance path for feedback at higher frequencies. This change improves the stability margin by introducing a controlled amount of negative feedback, which reduces the likelihood of oscillation and increases the phase margin across the frequency band.
