This capacitor provides a low-impedance path at high frequencies, allowing the bias network to respond more effectively to output variations and thus improving the stability margin. Additionally, I added a resistor (`ZR1`) between the bias reference node (`n7`) and the drain of the first stage (`n2`) to provide a local feedback path that helps stabilize the first stage and reduce the impact of phase shifts from the LC network.
