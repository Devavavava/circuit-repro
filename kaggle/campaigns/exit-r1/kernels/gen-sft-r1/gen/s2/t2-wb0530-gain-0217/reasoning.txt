The failure is due to the noise figure (NF) being too high, exceeding the specified maximum of 2.583 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current mirror, which introduces additional noise into the signal path. The NMOS current mirror (NM3) and the PMOS current mirror (PM2) are not optimally configured to minimize noise, and the DC biasing network (R1, R2, R4, R5) adds noise and affects the signal integrity.

To address this, I modified the topology by changing the NMOS current mirror (NM3) to be connected to the same internal node (n6) as the PMOS current mirror (PM2), and I replaced the DC biasing network with a direct connection from the NMOS current mirror to the VSS. This reduces the noise contribution from the bias network and improves the signal path by simplifying the current mirror configuration. Additionally, I removed the unnecessary capacitor C3 and replaced it with a direct connection from the output node to the load, which helps in reducing noise and improving the signal-to-noise ratio.
