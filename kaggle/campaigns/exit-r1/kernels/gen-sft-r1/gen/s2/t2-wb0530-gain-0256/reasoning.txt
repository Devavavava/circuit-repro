The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the resonant load (L2+C2) and the feedback path from the output to the input through the shared DC path. This creates a phase shift that reduces the stability margin, especially at lower frequencies. The current-reuse topology, while effective for gain and noise, introduces feedback paths that can destabilize the circuit when not properly managed.

To address this, I replaced the resonant load (L2+C2) with a series inductor (L3) and a capacitor (C3) to the ground, which helps in shaping the impedance and reducing unwanted feedback paths. Additionally, I restructured the bias network by connecting the diode NM3 to a new internal node (n4) and added a resistor (R3) from the output node (n2) to the new internal node (n4), which helps in decoupling the feedback and improving the stability margin.
