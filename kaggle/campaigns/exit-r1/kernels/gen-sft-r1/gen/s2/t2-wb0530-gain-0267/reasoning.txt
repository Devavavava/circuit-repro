The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the feedback and resonance interactions in the current-reuse topology. The shared DC path and the tuned load (L2+C2) create a resonant feedback path that destabilizes the amplifier at the operating frequency, especially when the current-reuse configuration introduces additional phase shifts. This leads to a reduced stability margin, causing the circuit to fail the mu_min constraint.

To address this, I restructured the circuit to isolate the feedback paths and eliminate the resonant feedback loop. I replaced the shared DC path with a separate biasing network and reconfigured the load to avoid resonant feedback. The PMOS and NMOS transistors are now used in a different topology, and the load is restructured to ensure a more stable operation over the wideband range.
