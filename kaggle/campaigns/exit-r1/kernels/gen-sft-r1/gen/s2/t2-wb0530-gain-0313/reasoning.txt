The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure. In the anchor netlist, the noise from the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and the current-setting PMOS (PM2) is not adequately suppressed, leading to an elevated noise floor. Additionally, the current-reuse structure introduces noise from the biasing network, which is not properly isolated from the signal path.

To address this, I replaced the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor (Rrep1) connected to the gate of PM1 and an internal node (n4), which helps to reduce the noise contribution from the biasing network by providing a more stable and predictable gate voltage for PM1. This change also decouples the biasing network from the signal path, reducing noise coupling. The resistor Rrep1 acts as a noise-suppressing element, improving the noise figure by isolating the signal path from the noisy biasing network.
