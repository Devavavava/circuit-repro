The binding constraint is the input matching (s11_max_db), which is failing by ~0.33 dB. This is likely due to the input impedance not being sufficiently matched to the source, which is exacerbated by the current-reuse topology and the DC-blocking capacitor C4 at the input. The capacitor introduces a phase shift and impedance mismatch at the operating frequency, degrading the reflection coefficient. Additionally, the shared DC path and the tuned load (L2+C2) may be causing unwanted resonances or impedance mismatches at the input node.

To address this, I reconfigured the input network by connecting the DC-blocking capacitor C4 to an internal node (n2) instead of the input node (VIN1), and introduced a new inductor L3 to provide a low-impedance path to ground at the input node. This change helps in better impedance matching by modifying the input network's frequency response and reducing the phase shift caused by the capacitor. The new inductor L3 also helps in stabilizing the input impedance over the wideband frequency range, improving the reflection coefficient.
