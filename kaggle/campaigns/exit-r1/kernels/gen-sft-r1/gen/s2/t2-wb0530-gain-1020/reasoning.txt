While this topology provides good gain and noise figure, it lacks sufficient phase margin at higher frequencies, leading to instability. The feedback resistor R1 and the inductive load L1+R2 are not sufficient to ensure robust stability over the entire 0.01–50 GHz band, especially when the circuit is sized for maximum gain and minimal noise.

To address this, I replaced the resistive shunt feedback with a two-stage topology that includes a second NMOS transistor (NM2) and additional inductors (L2, L3) to provide better phase compensation and stability. The addition of the second stage (NM2) introduces additional feedback paths and phase shifts that help maintain a stable gain and phase margin across the wideband. The inductor L2 between VDD and the gate of NM2 provides a high-impedance path for AC signals, improving phase margin.
