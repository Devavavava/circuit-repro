The noise is exacerbated by the lack of a noise-canceling feedback path or a more noise-optimized biasing structure.

To address this, I introduce a feedback path from the output (VOUT1) to the gate of NM3, which is connected to the bias network. This feedback loop helps to reduce the noise contribution from NM1 by providing a noise-canceling effect, effectively lowering the overall noise figure of the LNA. This change is implemented by adding a capacitor (ZC1) between VOUT1 and n5, and a resistor (ZR2) between n5 and VDD. These additions create a low-impedance path for noise cancellation without disrupting the biasing or current-reuse mechanism.
