The failure is primarily due to noise figure (NF) being too high, exceeding the maximum allowed value of 2.3662 dB. The NMOS current mirror (NM3) and its associated resistors (R1, R2) likely introduce additional noise that is not effectively suppressed in the current configuration, especially at the operating frequency of 1.22 GHz. Additionally, the PMOS gate biasing (PM2, R4, R5) may be contributing to noise due to the lack of a direct DC path to ground for the gate node (n6), causing potential noise coupling.

To address the noise issue, I modified the topology by replacing the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor (Rrep1) connecting n5 to VSS, effectively turning the current mirror into a simple resistor load. This change eliminates the noise contribution from the current mirror and reduces the overall noise floor. Additionally, I added a resistor (Rrep2) from n6 to VSS to provide a direct DC path for the PMOS gate biasing, reducing noise coupling and improving stability. These structural changes reduce noise contributions from the biasing network and improve the signal-to-noise ratio at the operating frequency, thereby lowering the noise figure and meeting the specification.
