The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, despite the other metrics being within bounds. The anchor netlist uses a current-reuse push-pull configuration with a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2). However, the noise performance is compromised due to the noise contributions from the current mirror and the biasing network, particularly from the NM3 diode and the R1-R2 biasing network. These components introduce additional noise into the signal path, especially at high frequencies, leading to a higher-than-allowed noise figure.

To address this, I propose a structural change to the topology by replacing the current mirror biasing network with a simpler and lower-noise configuration. The new netlist eliminates the NM3 diode and R1-R2 biasing network, and instead uses a direct connection from the gate of the NMOS transistor (NM1) to an internal node (n4) that is connected to the tuned load (L2+C2). This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network and improves the noise figure by simplifying the signal path and reducing the number of noise-generating components.
