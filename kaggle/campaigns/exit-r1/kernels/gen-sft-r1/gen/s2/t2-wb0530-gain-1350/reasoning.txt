The binding constraint is the s11_max_db, which measures the maximum input reflection coefficient. The failure indicates that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source, leading to a poor match and a reflection coefficient that exceeds the specification. This is likely due to the input network's inability to effectively transform the impedance across the wideband frequency range, especially at the operating frequency of 1.22 GHz.

The anchor netlist uses a simple inductor (L1) and capacitor (C4) for DC blocking and input coupling, but this may not provide sufficient impedance transformation across the wideband. The input impedance is dominated by the series combination of L1 and the gate-source impedance of NM1, which may not be well-matched at the target frequency. Additionally, the lack of a dedicated impedance-matching network at the input may contribute to the poor s11 performance.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n5 and VDD. This capacitor provides an additional impedance path at high frequencies, helping to improve the input impedance match by shunting high-frequency components to ground. This change introduces a frequency-dependent impedance at the input side, which helps flatten the input impedance across the wideband and reduces the reflection coefficient. The capacitor ZC1 is strategically placed to modify the input impedance without disrupting the existing biasing and current-reuse structure.
