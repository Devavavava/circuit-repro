The failure is primarily due to poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db margin being -0.339178 (i.e., -5.28 dB, which is below the -7.99 dB target). This indicates that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source, leading to a higher than acceptable reflection coefficient at the input port. The original topology lacks direct coupling from the input to the gate of the input transistor, which limits the ability to tune the input impedance effectively.

To address this, I added a direct coupling from the input node (n0) to the gate of the NMOS transistor (NM2) through a capacitor (C ZC1), and added a new NMOS transistor (NM2) connected from n3 to n4 with its source tied to n0. This creates a second stage of impedance transformation at the input, allowing for better control over the input impedance and improving the match. The addition of NM2 and C ZC1 introduces a new feedback path and additional degrees of freedom for impedance tuning at the input, directly addressing the poor s11 performance.
