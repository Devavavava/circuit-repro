The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the current-reuse topology and the shared DC path in the anchor netlist. The shared DC path between the NMOS and PMOS stages introduces a feedback loop that destabilizes the amplifier across the wideband frequency range, especially at lower frequencies where the stability margin is most critical. This is exacerbated by the current mirror and resistor biasing network, which may not adequately suppress oscillatory modes over the entire band.

To address this, I replaced the shared DC path with a separate DC path for the PMOS stage, isolating the two stages and reducing the feedback that causes instability. This is achieved by connecting the PMOS gate to a dedicated DC reference (n6) instead of the shared node (n5), and by removing the resistor R5 that connected the PMOS gate to the NMOS stage. This change breaks the feedback path and improves the stability margin across the wideband range.
