The failure is due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.2 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 being too high, possibly due to its gate being biased through a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and a resistor (R2), which introduces additional noise and degrades the noise figure. Additionally, the current-reuse structure may be causing unwanted noise coupling or increasing the noise contribution from the biasing network.

To address this, the topology was modified by removing the resistor R2 that connected the diode-connected NM3 to the input node n1, and instead connecting it directly to VSS. This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network and improves the noise figure by eliminating the additional noise path through R2. The rest of the topology remains the same, preserving the current-reuse structure and the tuned load for wideband performance.
