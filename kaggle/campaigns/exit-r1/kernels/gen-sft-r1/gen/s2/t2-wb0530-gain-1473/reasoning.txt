The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, which is likely caused by the noise contribution from the NMOS transistor NM3 in the bias network. The current-reuse topology, while effective for gain and power efficiency, introduces noise from the biasing NM3, which is amplified in the signal path due to the lack of a proper noise shielding mechanism. This is exacerbated by the shared DC path and the absence of a noise-isolating component between the bias network and the signal path.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the internal bias node n5 and the signal node n2. This capacitor acts as a noise-shielding element, decoupling the high-noise bias network from the signal path at high frequencies, thereby reducing the noise contribution from NM3 into the signal chain. This structural change directly targets the noise figure constraint without affecting the gain, stability, or power consumption.
