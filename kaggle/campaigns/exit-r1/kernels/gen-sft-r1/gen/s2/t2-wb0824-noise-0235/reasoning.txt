The failure is due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by approximately 0.22 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure, particularly from the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and the resistive biasing elements (R1, R2, R4, R5). These components introduce additional noise into the signal path, degrading the noise figure.

To address this, I modified the biasing structure by replacing the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) with a PMOS transistor (ZP1) connected to an internal node (n7), which is tied to VDD through a resistor (Rrep1). This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network by using a different transistor type and reconfiguring the current path to minimize noise coupling into the signal chain. The addition of the resistor Rrep1 and the internal node n7 helps stabilize the bias current and reduce noise from the biasing network.
