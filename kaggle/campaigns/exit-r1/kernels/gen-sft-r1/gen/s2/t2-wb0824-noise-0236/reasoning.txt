The failure is primarily due to poor input impedance matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db margin being -0.294261 (i.e., -5.53652 dB achieved, which is below the required -7.845 dB). This indicates that the input reflection coefficient is too high, likely due to the lack of a proper input impedance tuning mechanism. The original circuit lacks a dedicated impedance matching network at the input, and the existing components (C4, L1, and the NMOS/PMOS stage) do not sufficiently shape the input impedance across the wideband frequency range. Additionally, the shared DC path and the absence of a dedicated input tuning inductor may contribute to poor impedance matching at certain frequencies.

To address this, I added a series inductor (L3) between the internal node n6 (which connects to the PMOS gate biasing network) and the input node n1. This inductor provides an additional degree of freedom to tune the input impedance, especially at high frequencies, by introducing a frequency-dependent reactance that helps flatten the input impedance across the wideband range. This change allows for better control over the input reflection coefficient, thereby improving s11_max_db and meeting the specification.
