The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, failing by ~0.12 dB. The anchor netlist uses a standard current-reuse LNA topology with a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2). However, the noise performance is likely degraded by the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and the current-setting PMOS (PM2), which introduce noise and nonlinearity. The noise contribution from the biasing network and the current mirror may be too high for the target NF, especially at the center frequency (f0 = 1.385 GHz). The shared DC path may also lead to poor impedance matching and increased noise coupling.

To address this, I replaced the standard current-reuse topology with a cascode configuration using a NMOS transistor (NM2) in the signal path. This change improves the noise performance by isolating the input from the noise sources in the biasing network. The NMOS cascode (NM2) provides better noise matching and reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network. The removal of the PMOS current mirror (PM2) and its associated resistors (R4, R5) simplifies the biasing network and reduces noise sources. The tuned load (L2+C2) is retained but now operates in a more stable environment due to the improved impedance matching from the cascode configuration. This structural change should reduce the noise figure and meet the NF_max_db constraint.
