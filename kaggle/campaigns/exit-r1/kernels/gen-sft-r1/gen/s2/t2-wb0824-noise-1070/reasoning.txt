The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is limited by the noise contributions from the transistors and the biasing network. The original circuit uses a current-reuse topology with a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and a PMOS (PM2) for biasing, which introduces additional noise due to the diode-connected devices and the resistive biasing network (R1, R2, R4, R5). This increases the overall noise figure beyond the specification. Additionally, the current-reuse structure may not be optimal for wideband noise performance due to the complex feedback and impedance mismatches.

To address this, the topology is restructured to simplify the biasing network and reduce noise contributions. The NMOS and PMOS transistors are reconnected to minimize the number of noise-generating components. The diode-connected NM3 and PM2 are replaced with a simpler configuration, and the resistive biasing network is replaced with a more direct current path. The inductor L2 is reconnected to the output node to improve impedance matching and reduce noise coupling. These changes reduce the noise contributions from the biasing network and improve the overall noise figure while maintaining the required gain and stability.
