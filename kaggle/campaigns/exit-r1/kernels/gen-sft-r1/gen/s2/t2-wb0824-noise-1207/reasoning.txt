This configuration introduces additional noise into the signal path, especially at the input node, which is amplified by the LNA and contributes to the overall noise figure. The noise from NM3 is not properly suppressed or isolated, leading to a marginal increase in the noise figure that pushes it over the specification limit.

To address this, the NM3 transistor is reconnected as a regular NMOS transistor with its source tied to the drain of NM1 (n1) instead of VSS. This change moves NM3 closer to the signal path, allowing it to be more effectively biased and reducing its noise contribution to the input signal. Additionally, a new NMOS transistor (NM2) is added to the bias network, connecting the gate of NM3 to a new internal node (n6), which provides a more stable and controlled biasing point. This change reduces the noise contribution from NM3 and improves the noise figure without significantly affecting other performance metrics.
