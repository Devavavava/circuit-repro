The failure is due to the poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being just -7.48235 dB, which is below the required -7.8676 dB. This indicates that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source, likely due to the lack of a direct connection between the input and the gate of the input transistor (NM1), which is instead coupled through a series inductor (L1) and capacitor (C4). This configuration may not provide sufficient impedance transformation at the wideband frequency of 1.385 GHz, leading to a mismatch.

To address this, I added a direct coupling between the input and the gate of the input transistor by adding a capacitor (ZC1) between the input (VIN1) and the drain of the NMOS transistor (NM1). This change allows for a more direct impedance transformation at the operating frequency, improving the input matching and thus the s11_max_db metric.
