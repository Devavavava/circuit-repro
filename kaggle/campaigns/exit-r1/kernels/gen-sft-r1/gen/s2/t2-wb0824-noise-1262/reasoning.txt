The failure is primarily due to poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint failing by ~0.18 dB. The input impedance is likely too high or not well-matched to the source, leading to a poor return loss.

To address this, I propose adding a shunt capacitor (C ZC1) across the input inductor (L1) and a series capacitor (C ZC2) in the signal path between the NMOS transistor (NM1) and the inductor (L2). These additions help fine-tune the input impedance and improve the matching over the wideband range. The shunt capacitor (C ZC1) provides additional tuning at higher frequencies, while the series capacitor (C ZC2) helps lower the impedance at lower frequencies, both contributing to a more stable and matched input impedance.
