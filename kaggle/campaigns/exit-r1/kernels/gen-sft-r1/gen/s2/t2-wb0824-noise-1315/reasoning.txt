The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the single-stage resistive shunt-feedback topology. The feedback resistor R1 and shunt inductor L1 create a narrowband stability window, which fails to maintain sufficient phase margin across the wideband frequency range (0.01–50 GHz). This results in instability at certain frequencies, as evidenced by the negative margin for the mu_min_wide constraint. The single-stage configuration also limits the ability to control the phase response effectively across the entire band.

To address this, I propose a dual-stage topology with additional feedback and inductive peaking elements to enhance stability and broaden the phase margin. The new netlist introduces a second NMOS transistor (NM2) and a PMOS transistor (PM1) to form a second stage, along with additional inductors (L2, L3) and capacitors (C3, C1, C2) to provide multiple peaking points and additional feedback paths. This structure allows for better control over the phase response and ensures a more robust stability margin across the wideband range.
