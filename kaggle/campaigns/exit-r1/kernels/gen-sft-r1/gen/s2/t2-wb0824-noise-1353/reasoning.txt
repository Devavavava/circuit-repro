The failure is primarily due to poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being violated by ~0.54 dB. The original topology uses a simple inductor-capacitor (L1-C4) input network, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation and tuning across the wideband frequency range. Additionally, the current-reuse structure with the diode-connected NM3 and PM2 may introduce parasitic effects or insufficient feedback to stabilize the input impedance.

To address this, I modified the input network by adding a second inductor (L2) and a series capacitor (C2) to create a more effective impedance transformer and improve broadband matching. I also changed the configuration of the NM3 and PM2 transistors to use a different topology that reduces parasitic feedback and improves stability. The R2 resistor was moved to connect to a new internal node (n3), which helps in better control of the biasing and reduces unwanted coupling.
