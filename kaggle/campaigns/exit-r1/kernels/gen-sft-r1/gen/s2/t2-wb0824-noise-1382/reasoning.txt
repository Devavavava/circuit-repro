The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is dominated by the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 and the noise coupling through the biasing network. The original topology uses a diode-connected NM3 and a current mirror configuration with R1 and R2, which introduces noise and increases the noise figure due to the resistive elements and the current mirror's noise gain. The PMOS gate biasing via PM2 and R4/R5 also adds noise and degrades the noise performance, particularly at high frequencies.

To address this, the topology is restructured to eliminate the current mirror and diode-connected NM3, replacing them with a simpler biasing network using a resistor Rrep1 directly from n5 to VDD. This reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network and minimizes the noise coupling into the signal path. The PMOS gate biasing is simplified by connecting PM2's source directly to n0, which is the node after the input capacitor C1, thereby reducing the noise path and improving the signal-to-noise ratio. These changes reduce the noise figure by minimizing the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current mirror, leading to a lower overall noise figure and meeting the NF_max_db constraint.
