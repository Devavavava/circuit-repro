The binding constraint is the input return loss (s11_max_db), which is too high (failed by ~0.2 dB). The anchor circuit uses a standard current-reuse LNA topology with a shared DC path and a tuned load. The poor s11 is likely due to the input impedance mismatch caused by the DC-blocking capacitor C4 and the series inductor L1, which may not be optimally tuned for the wideband frequency (f0=1.385 GHz). The input network may not be sufficiently matched across the band, especially at the extremes of the frequency range.

To improve s11, I added a new capacitor ZC1 between VDD and the internal node n5, which is the gate of the NMOS biasing transistor NM3. This provides an additional AC path to ground at high frequencies, helping to stabilize and improve the input impedance matching. The capacitor ZC1 acts as a high-frequency bypass, reducing the impact of the biasing network on the input impedance and thereby improving the return loss.
