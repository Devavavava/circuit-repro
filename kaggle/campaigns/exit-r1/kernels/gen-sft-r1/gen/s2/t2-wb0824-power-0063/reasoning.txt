The binding constraint is the minimum stability factor (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is failing due to insufficient phase margin and poor feedback stability across the wideband frequency range. The original topology relies on a resistive shunt feedback and a shunt-peaked inductor load, which may not provide sufficient phase compensation at the higher frequencies in the band, leading to a reduced stability factor.

To address this, the topology is modified to include a second NMOS transistor (NM2) with a current mirror configuration, providing additional phase compensation and improving the feedback loop's stability across the wideband. The addition of a second inductor (L2) and a capacitor (C2) in a peaking configuration helps to further stabilize the output and improve the phase margin. The inclusion of a PMOS transistor (PM1) and a resistor (R4) in a biasing network ensures proper DC operating points and enhances the stability of the feedback loop. These changes collectively improve the stability factor across the entire frequency range, meeting the required mu_min constraint.
