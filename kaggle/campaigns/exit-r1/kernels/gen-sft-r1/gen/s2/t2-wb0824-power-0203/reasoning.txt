The failure is primarily due to noise figure (NF) being too high, as the binding constraint `nf_max_db` is exceeded by ~0.3 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current mirror, which are not properly decoupled from the signal path. The NMOS diode (NM3) and the PMOS diode (PM2) in the biasing network are directly connected to the signal nodes, introducing noise into the signal path. This noise is amplified by the gain of the LNA, leading to a higher-than-acceptable NF.

To address this, I restructured the biasing network to isolate the noise sources from the signal path. In the revised netlist, I moved the NM3 diode to a new node (n6) and connected it to the signal node (n2) via a resistor (R2), which helps to decouple the biasing network from the signal path. Additionally, I modified the load network by replacing the inductor (L2) with a capacitor (C2) connected to a new internal node (n4), which helps to stabilize the output and reduce noise. These changes reduce the noise contribution from the biasing network and improve the noise figure of the LNA.
