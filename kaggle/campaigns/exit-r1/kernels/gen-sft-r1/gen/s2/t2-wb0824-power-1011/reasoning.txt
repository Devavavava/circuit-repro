The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) across the wideband frequency range. The original topology, while effective in achieving good noise figure, gain, and input match, lacks sufficient phase margin and feedback mechanisms to ensure stability over the entire band. The shunt-peaked inductive load and resistive feedback provide some stabilization, but they are not sufficient to prevent instability at higher frequencies, especially when the circuit is sized for performance.

To address this, I introduced a second NMOS transistor (NM2) and a capacitor (C3) to create a cascode structure and add a feedback path from the output to the bias node. This enhances the phase margin and improves the stability of the circuit over the wideband range. The additional NMOS transistor helps isolate the output from the input, reducing feedback-induced oscillations, while the capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, further stabilizing the circuit.
