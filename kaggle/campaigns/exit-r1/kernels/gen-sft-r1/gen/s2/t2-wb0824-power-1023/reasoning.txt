The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_max_db), which is too high by ~0.18 dB. This is likely due to the noise contribution from the current mirror (NM3 and R2) and the biasing network (R1, R4, R5), which introduce additional noise into the signal path. The current-reuse topology, while effective for gain and power efficiency, can degrade noise performance if not carefully balanced. The noise from the biasing network and the current mirror is likely coupling into the signal path, increasing the overall noise figure.

To address this, I replaced the current mirror (NM3 and R2) with a direct connection from the gate of NM1 to the output of the current source (n6). This reduces the noise contribution from the current mirror by eliminating the NM3 transistor and its associated biasing network. Additionally, I modified the load network by connecting L2 to VDD instead of n2, which helps in better impedance matching and reduces noise coupling from the power supply. These changes simplify the signal path, reduce noise sources, and improve the noise figure while maintaining the required gain and stability.
