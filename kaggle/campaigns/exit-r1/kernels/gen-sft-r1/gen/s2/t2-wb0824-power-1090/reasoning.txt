The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.17 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure, particularly from the diode-connected NM3 and PM2 transistors, which add noise due to their operation in the subthreshold or linear region. The current-reuse topology, while beneficial for gain and power efficiency, may be introducing additional noise if not properly balanced with the signal path.

To address this, I modified the biasing structure by replacing the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor (ZR1) connected between n5 and VDD, and added a new capacitor (ZC1) between n5 and n6. This change decouples the biasing network from the signal path, reducing noise coupling from the biasing network to the signal chain. Additionally, the new capacitor ZC1 helps to stabilize the biasing node and filter out high-frequency noise, which contributes to a lower noise figure. These changes improve the noise performance by reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network and improving the signal-to-noise ratio at the input.
