The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) across the wideband frequency range. The original topology relies on a resistive shunt feedback and a shunt-peaked inductive load, which may not provide sufficient phase margin at higher frequencies, especially when the load impedance varies with frequency. This leads to a reduced stability margin, causing the circuit to fail the mu_min constraint.

To address this, I propose a structural change that introduces a second NMOS transistor (NM2) and an additional inductor (L3) in a common-gate configuration. This configuration provides additional phase compensation and helps maintain a higher stability margin across the wideband frequency range. The addition of NM2 and L3 introduces a feedback path that enhances phase margin, while the additional capacitor (C3) helps in stabilizing the output node and reducing unwanted oscillations. These changes improve the stability margin without significantly affecting the gain and noise figure of the circuit.
