The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) across the wideband frequency range, which is critical for the LNA's robustness. The original topology has a shared DC path and a current-reuse structure that may lead to poor phase margin at certain frequencies, especially in the wideband regime. The lack of a dedicated stabilization element or a more controlled feedback path may be causing instability.

To address this, I added a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) connected between the output node (n4) and ground (VSS), with its gate tied to the input node (n0). This addition introduces a feedback path from the output to the input through the NMOS transistor, which helps in stabilizing the amplifier over the wideband frequency range. The new transistor acts as a low-frequency feedback element, improving the phase margin and thus increasing the stability margin (`mu_min`). The change is structural and does not involve any biasing or sizing, as required.
