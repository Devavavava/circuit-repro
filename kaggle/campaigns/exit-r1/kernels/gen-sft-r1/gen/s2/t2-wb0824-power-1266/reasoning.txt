The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, despite other metrics like S11, S21, and Idd being within limits. The noise performance is likely being degraded by the current-reuse topology and the shared DC path, which introduces additional noise contributions from the biasing network and the diode-connected transistors (NM3 and PM2). The noise from these components is likely being amplified or not sufficiently suppressed in the signal path, especially at the wideband frequency of ~1.38 GHz.

To address this, I modified the topology by introducing a separate DC path for the input signal using an additional inductor (L3) and a capacitor (C4) to decouple the input node (n0) from the bias network. This change isolates the signal path from the noise sources in the biasing network, reducing the noise contribution from the current-reuse structure. Additionally, I replaced the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor (Rrep1) connected to an internal node (n6), which helps in reducing the noise generated by the diode-connected transistor and provides a more stable bias point. These changes reduce the noise figure while maintaining the desired performance in other metrics.
