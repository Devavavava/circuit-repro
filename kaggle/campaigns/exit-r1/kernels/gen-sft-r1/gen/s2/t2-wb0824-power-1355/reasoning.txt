The failure is primarily due to noise figure (NF) being too high, as indicated by the binding constraint `nf_max_db` with a margin of -0.238336. This suggests the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current reuse path is not adequately suppressed. The original topology uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for biasing, which introduces noise and limits the noise performance of the LNA. Additionally, the current reuse path may not be optimally isolated from the noise-sensitive signal path.

To address this, the biasing network is restructured to reduce noise contributions. The NM3 diode is replaced with a PMOS (ZP1) connected to an internal node (n7) and a new resistor (Rrep1) is added to provide a more stable and lower-noise current reference. This change isolates the biasing network from the signal path, reducing noise coupling and improving the noise figure. The new resistor Rrep1 helps in stabilizing the bias current and reducing noise from the biasing network.
