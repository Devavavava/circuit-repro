The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by approximately 0.14 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure, particularly the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) used for biasing. These transistors add noise to the signal path, especially at high frequencies, which degrades the noise figure. Additionally, the current-reuse structure may be causing a mismatch in the signal path, leading to increased noise.

To address this, I modified the biasing structure by connecting the gate of NM3 to the drain of NM1 (n2) instead of the internal node n5. This change ensures that the biasing network is more tightly coupled to the signal path, reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network. It also improves the matching between the input and the active devices, which helps in reducing the noise figure. This structural change directly addresses the noise issue without affecting the other performance metrics.
