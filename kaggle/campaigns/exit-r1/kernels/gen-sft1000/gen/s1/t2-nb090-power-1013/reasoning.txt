The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_db) being too high, which is caused by the second stage's noise contribution being amplified due to the lack of a proper noise suppression mechanism. The interstage LC network, while effective for impedance matching and gain boosting, does not inherently suppress noise from the second stage. Additionally, the absence of a noise-canceling component at the output leads to an elevated noise floor. The current structure lacks a mechanism to isolate the second stage from noise sources, especially at the output.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the interstage node (n4), which helps in decoupling the second stage from the power supply and reduces noise coupling. I also introduced a resistor (ZR2) from the output (VOUT1) to ground, which provides a low-impedance path for noise to be dissipated, thereby reducing the noise contribution at the output. These changes improve the noise figure by isolating the second stage from noise sources and providing a path for noise dissipation.
