The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is below the required 28.3101 dB. The two-stage LNA with LC interstage has a poor interstage impedance match, leading to signal loss between stages. The LC interstage (C2 and L2) introduces a resonant network that may not be effectively coupling the output of the first stage to the input of the second stage, especially at the operating frequency of 900 MHz. This results in a low overall gain, as the second stage is not being sufficiently driven by the first.

To address this, I restructured the topology by replacing the LC interstage with a direct connection between the first and second stages, using a source-degeneration inductor (L2) in the first stage and a capacitive load (C2) in the second stage. This improves impedance matching and signal transfer between stages, thereby boosting the gain. The bias network was also reorganized to ensure stable operation without introducing additional inductors or complicating the signal path.
