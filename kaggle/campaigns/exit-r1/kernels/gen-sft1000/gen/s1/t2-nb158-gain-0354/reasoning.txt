The second stage's gate is driven by the LC interstage, which introduces a phase shift and can lead to positive feedback at certain frequencies. To address this, the LC interstage is replaced with a direct inductive coupling from the first stage’s drain to the second stage’s gate, eliminating the resonant feedback path. This reduces the phase shift and feedback gain at problematic frequencies, improving stability. The second stage’s drain load is also restructured to use a series inductor (L3) and a capacitor (C2) to VDD, which helps isolate the second stage and improve impedance matching. The biasing network is simplified to reduce parasitic effects and improve signal integrity.
