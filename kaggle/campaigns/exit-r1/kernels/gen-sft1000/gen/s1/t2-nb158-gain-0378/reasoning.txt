The binding constraint is the minimum stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is failing due to poor phase and amplitude stability across the band. The two-stage structure with LC interstage and tuned loads causes resonant feedback and phase shifts that reduce stability, especially at frequencies off the center frequency. The current topology lacks sufficient phase compensation and has a narrow stability band, leading to a negative margin at the target frequency.

To address this, I restructured the circuit into a single-stage common-gate (CG) topology with a source inductor and a second stage with a common-source (CS) configuration, adding a third inductor in the second stage's source to enhance phase compensation. This reduces feedback and improves stability across the band. The removal of the interstage LC network and the use of a CG stage instead of a CS stage in the first stage reduce resonant feedback and broaden the stability band. The additional inductor in the second stage's source helps to isolate the stages and improve phase margin.
