The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is failing due to insufficient phase margin in the feedback path of the two-stage LNA. This is likely caused by the resonant LC interstage network (L2 and C2) creating a feedback path that reduces the phase margin at frequencies beyond the design band, especially in the wideband analysis. The current topology lacks a mechanism to dampen this feedback, leading to instability.

To address this, I added a damping capacitor `ZC2` between the drain of the first stage (node n2) and the interstage node (n4), which helps suppress unwanted resonances in the feedback path. Additionally, I added a resistor `ZR2` between the interstage node (n4) and the second stage's gate node (n5), which introduces a controlled impedance to reduce the feedback gain at higher frequencies. These changes improve the phase margin and stabilize the amplifier across the wideband range.
