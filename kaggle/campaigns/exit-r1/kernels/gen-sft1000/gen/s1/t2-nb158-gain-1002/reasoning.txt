The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is too low by ~0.44 dB. This is likely due to the second stage's drain load (L3) being a simple inductor, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation at the operating frequency, leading to reduced voltage gain. The LC interstage network (L2 and C2) may also be causing signal loss or phase shifts that degrade the overall gain.

To address this, I replaced the simple inductor (L3) in the second stage's drain load with a series LC tank (L3 and C3), which can provide a higher impedance at the resonant frequency, thus boosting the gain. Additionally, I restructured the DC-blocking and output coupling network to simplify the signal path and reduce parasitic effects. The biasing network was also reorganized to ensure stable and consistent gate biasing for both stages.
