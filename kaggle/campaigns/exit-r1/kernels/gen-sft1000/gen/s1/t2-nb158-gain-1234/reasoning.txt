The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` failed due to poor stability across the wide frequency range. The original topology, while effective at the center frequency, lacks sufficient feedback and isolation mechanisms to suppress oscillations at off-band frequencies. This is exacerbated by the LC-tuned loads and the lack of a direct feedback path from the output to the input stages, which limits the phase margin and stability bandwidth.

To address this, I introduced a direct feedback path from the output to the input stage by adding an NMOS transistor (ZN1) and a capacitor (ZC1) in parallel with the LC interstage network. The NMOS transistor ZN1 acts as a low-impedance path from the output to the interstage node (n4), while the capacitor ZC1 provides a high-frequency bypass to the output node (VOUT1). This enhances the phase margin and suppresses oscillations at off-band frequencies, thereby improving the stability factor `mu` across the entire 0.01–50 GHz range.
