The binding constraint, `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, measures the minimum stability factor across a wide frequency range, and it failed with a margin of -0.14857, indicating instability outside the design band. The anchor netlist features a two-stage CS LNA with LC interstage and tuned drain loads, but the lack of a proper feedback mechanism and the absence of a stabilizing element in the second stage likely caused the instability. Specifically, the second stage's gate is driven by an LC network, which may introduce phase shifts and resonate at unintended frequencies, reducing the stability margin.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output of the second stage (node n6) to the source of the second transistor (NM2), using a capacitor (ZC1) and a resistor (ZR2). This introduces a negative feedback mechanism that stabilizes the second stage and suppresses oscillations across the wide frequency range. Additionally, I introduced a resistor (Rrep1) from the output of the second stage (n6) to VDD, which helps in reducing the output impedance and further enhances stability. These changes increase the phase margin and improve the stability factor across the band, directly addressing the binding constraint.
