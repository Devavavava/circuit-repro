This is likely caused by the LC interstage network (L2 and C2) creating a resonant peak that introduces phase shifts and reduces the stability margin at off-band frequencies. The two-stage structure with LC loads and interstage coupling is prone to feedback at higher frequencies, especially when the interstage LC network is not properly damped or decoupled.

To address this, I replaced the LC interstage network with a direct connection between the two stages, using a capacitor (C2) to block DC while allowing AC coupling. This change eliminates the resonant LC network that was causing instability. Additionally, I simplified the drain load of the first stage to a single inductor (L1) and restructured the second stage to use a common-source configuration with a direct connection to the output capacitor (C3), reducing the number of inductors and simplifying the feedback path. This reduces the likelihood of resonant feedback and improves stability across the band.
