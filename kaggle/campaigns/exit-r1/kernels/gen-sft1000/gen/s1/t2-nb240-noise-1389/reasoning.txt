To address this, I modified the topology by removing the LC interstage network (L2 and C2) and reconfiguring the second stage to use a direct connection from the first stage's drain to the second stage's gate, while adding a source inductor (L2) to the second stage. This change eliminates the resonant LC network that caused phase shifts, and the source inductor helps stabilize the second stage by reducing feedback effects at off-band frequencies. Additionally, the output capacitor (C3) was reconnected directly to the output node, simplifying the signal path and reducing parasitic effects that could destabilize the amplifier.
