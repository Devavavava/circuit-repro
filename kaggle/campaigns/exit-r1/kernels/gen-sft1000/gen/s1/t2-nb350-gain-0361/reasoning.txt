The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is negative, indicating instability over a wide frequency range. This is likely due to the second stage's feedback path through the LC interstage network (L2 and C2), which introduces a resonant peak and phase shift that destabilizes the amplifier at frequencies beyond the design band. The two-stage structure with LC interstage coupling creates a feedback path that reduces the phase margin, especially at higher frequencies.

To address this, I modified the topology to eliminate the LC interstage feedback path. The second stage is now directly connected to the first stage without the LC coupling, and the drain load of the second stage is connected to VDD via an inductor (L2). This change reduces the feedback path and stabilizes the amplifier over a wider frequency range. Additionally, the biasing network is simplified to use a single current mirror (NM3) with a resistor (R2) connecting its source to the gate of the first stage, and a resistor (R1) connecting the current mirror's drain to VDD. This simplification reduces the number of inductors and improves stability.
