The failure is due to insufficient stability and poor phase margin in the wideband stability factor (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is negative, indicating instability outside the narrowband center frequency. This is likely caused by the LC interstage network (L2 and C2) creating resonances that interact poorly with the second stage's output impedance, leading to phase shifts that reduce stability across the band. The current structure lacks a mechanism to dampen these resonances or provide additional phase margin in the wideband.

To address this, I added a compensation capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and n4, which is the node before the interstage LC network. This helps to stabilize the interstage network by providing a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, reducing unwanted resonances and improving phase margin. Additionally, I introduced a resistor (ZR1) from VOUT1 to VSS and another (ZR2) from VDD to n6, which adds controlled impedance to the output and second-stage drain, further stabilizing the circuit and improving the wideband stability factor.
