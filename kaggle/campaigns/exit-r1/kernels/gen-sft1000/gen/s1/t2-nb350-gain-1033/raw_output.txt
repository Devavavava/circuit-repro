These resonant elements, while effective at the center frequency (f0 = 3.5 GHz), create phase and magnitude variations across the band, which can lead to instability or oscillation at frequencies near the edges of the band. The current topology's resonant LC loads and interstage LC network may be contributing to a narrow stability band, which is insufficient for the required wideband stability margin.

To address this, I propose a structural change that eliminates the LC resonant loads and replaces them with inductive peaking and capacitive feedback, which provide better stability across a wide frequency range. This configuration avoids resonant peaking at off-band frequencies and ensures a more stable and predictable gain and phase response over the entire 0.01–50 GHz range.
