The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` is failing due to insufficient stability margin across the wide frequency band, particularly at frequencies outside the center frequency (f0 = 3.5 GHz). The original topology, while effective at f0, lacks sufficient phase and gain margin across the band, leading to a negative margin in the wideband stability metric. This is likely due to the LC interstage tuning and the current-mirror biasing network, which may introduce resonances or feedback paths that reduce stability at off-center frequencies.

To address this, I added a compensation capacitor `ZC2` between VDD and the interstage node `n4`, and another capacitor `ZC1` between an internal node `n0` (a new node at the drain of NM1) and the gate of NM2. These capacitors help dampen resonances and improve phase margin across the band. Additionally, I introduced a new inductor `ZL1` between the drain of NM1 and the bias node `n7`, which helps isolate the bias network from the signal path and enhances stability by reducing feedback at high frequencies.
