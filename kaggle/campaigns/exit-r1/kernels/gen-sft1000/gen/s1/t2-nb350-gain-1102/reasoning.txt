This is exacerbated by the lack of damping in the LC circuits, leading to a negative stability margin at the operating frequency.

To address this, I added a damping capacitor (ZC1) between the interstage node (n4) and the bias node (n7). This capacitor provides a low-impedance path to ground at high frequencies, reducing the phase shift and improving the stability margin. Additionally, I added two resistors (ZR1 and ZR2) to the bias network to provide additional damping and isolate the bias node from the LC circuits, further stabilizing the system.
