This is particularly problematic for the stability factor `mu`, which is critical for ensuring oscillation-free operation. The current topology lacks sufficient isolation between stages to suppress this feedback at off-center frequencies, leading to a negative margin in the stability metric.

To address this, I modified the topology by removing the interstage LC network (L2 and C2) and instead connected the second stage directly to the first stage's drain via a capacitor (C2). This change reduces feedback between stages and improves stability. Additionally, I restructured the second stage's drain load to be a direct connection to VDD with a capacitor (C3) to the output, simplifying the load and reducing parasitic interactions. The biasing network was preserved, ensuring consistent gate biasing without introducing additional feedback paths.
