The failure is due to the poor stability and phase margin in the wideband stability metric `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is negatively impacted by the LC interstage network and the current mirror biasing scheme. These components introduce resonances and feedback paths that degrade stability across the wideband range, leading to a margin of -0.248, which violates the requirement.

To address this, I replaced the LC interstage network with a direct coupling between the two stages using an inductor (L2) and a capacitor (C2), and restructured the biasing network to remove the current mirror and replace it with a direct gate biasing scheme using resistors. This reduces the number of feedback paths and resonant nodes, improving stability across the wideband range.
