The binding constraint is the stability metric `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is failing due to insufficient phase margin across the band. This is likely caused by the feedback through the interstage LC network (L2 and C2), which introduces a resonant peak that destabilizes the system at off-band frequencies. The two-stage structure with LC interstage coupling creates a feedback path that reduces the stability margin, especially at higher frequencies beyond the narrowband center frequency.

To address this, I restructured the topology to eliminate the interstage LC feedback path and reconfigure the second stage to be a common-gate (CG) configuration instead of a common-source (CS) configuration. This change reduces the feedback from the second stage to the first, improving phase margin. Additionally, I removed the LC load on the second stage and replaced it with a direct connection to VDD, which simplifies the output and improves stability. The first stage now uses a series inductor (L1) for input matching, and the second stage is driven directly from the first stage's drain, avoiding the LC interstage coupling that caused instability.
