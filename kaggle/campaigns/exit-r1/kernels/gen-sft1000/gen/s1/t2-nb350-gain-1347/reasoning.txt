The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` measures the stability margin of the LNA over a wide frequency range. The negative margin indicates that the circuit is unstable at some frequencies beyond the design band, likely due to poor feedback or improper impedance matching in the output network. In the anchor netlist, the output is loaded with a simple LC tank (L2+C2), which may resonate poorly with the amplifier's output impedance over a wide range, leading to phase shifts that reduce the stability margin. Additionally, the current-reuse topology may not adequately isolate the output from feedback paths at off-band frequencies.

To address this, I modified the output loading network to include a series inductor (L3) between the output and the main LC tank (L2+C2). This creates a more controlled impedance transformation at the output, reducing the likelihood of resonant feedback that destabilizes the circuit. The inductor also helps to isolate the output from the internal nodes, improving the phase margin across the wide frequency range. This change directly addresses the stability issue without affecting the gain or noise figure, which are already within specification.
