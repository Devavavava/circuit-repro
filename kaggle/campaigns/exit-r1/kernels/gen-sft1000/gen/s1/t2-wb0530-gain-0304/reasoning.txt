The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, which is likely caused by the noise contribution from the current-reuse structure and the lack of sufficient noise suppression in the input stage. The anchor circuit uses a current-reuse topology with a shared DC path, which may introduce additional noise sources from the current mirroring and the DC blocking capacitor at the input. This leads to a higher-than-allowed noise figure, especially at the target frequency.

To address this, I propose a structural change by introducing a direct DC path from the input to the ground through a capacitor and an NMOS transistor, which helps in reducing the noise contribution from the current-reuse structure by isolating the input stage from the DC path. Additionally, I replace the diode-connected NMOS with a PMOS in the current mirror to improve noise performance and stability. This change also modifies the load network to a different configuration, which helps in better impedance matching and noise suppression.
