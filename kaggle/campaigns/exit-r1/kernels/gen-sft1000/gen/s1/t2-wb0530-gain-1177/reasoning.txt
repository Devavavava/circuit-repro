The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification. The anchor netlist uses a current-reuse topology with a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2), but the noise contribution from the biasing network and the lack of proper noise suppression in the signal path are likely the culprits. The NMOS diode (NM3) and its associated resistor (R2) introduce noise that is not sufficiently attenuated, especially at the operating frequency. The PMOS gate biasing via R4 and R5 may also contribute to noise due to the lack of a low-noise path to ground.

To address this, I introduce a noise-suppressing capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n5 (bias reference) and the output node n4. This capacitor decouples the bias network from the signal path, reducing noise coupling from the biasing circuit into the signal chain. Additionally, I add a resistor (ZR1) between n4 and n6 to provide a low-impedance path for noise currents, further suppressing noise at the output node. These changes isolate the signal path from the noisy biasing network and provide a cleaner return path for noise, directly improving the noise figure.
