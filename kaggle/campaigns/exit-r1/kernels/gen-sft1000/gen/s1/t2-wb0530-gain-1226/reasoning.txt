The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.32 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the bias network, particularly the NMOS diode NM3 and its associated resistor R2, which are directly connected to the signal path via n5 and n1. These components introduce noise that is amplified by the LNA, especially at the target frequency of ~1.225 GHz. Additionally, the lack of a direct DC path from the signal node n1 to the bias network may lead to improper noise suppression.

To address this, I added a DC coupling capacitor (ZC1) between n1 and n5, providing a low-impedance path for DC bias while blocking AC signals. This isolates the noise-generating bias network from the signal path, reducing its impact on the noise figure. I also added a resistor (ZR1) from n5 to n3, which helps stabilize the bias node and reduce noise coupling. These changes improve the noise performance by decoupling the signal from the bias network and enhancing DC stability.
