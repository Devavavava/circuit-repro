To address this, I propose a structural change that isolates the biasing network from the signal path. This is achieved by adding a capacitor (ZC1) between the bias node (n5) and the input node (n0), which blocks DC while allowing AC signals to pass. This change ensures that the noise from the bias network does not couple into the signal path.
