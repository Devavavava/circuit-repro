The binding constraint, `nf_max_db`, is failing due to noise contributions from the current-reuse structure and the shared DC path. The noise figure is being degraded by the NMOS diode (NM3) and the PMOS current-setting diode (PM2), which introduce noise into the signal path. Additionally, the shared DC path and the lack of a dedicated noise-grounding node contribute to noise coupling and poor isolation, which limits the achievable noise figure.

To address this, I added a dedicated noise-grounding node (x1) and connected it through a capacitor (ZC1) to the shared DC path (n5). This isolates the noise-sensitive nodes from the shared DC path, reducing noise coupling and improving the noise figure. The capacitor ZC1 acts as a high-frequency ground for noise, allowing the signal to pass while blocking noise from propagating back into the signal path.
