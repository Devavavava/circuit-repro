The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, despite the other metrics being within bounds. This suggests that the noise contribution from the input transistor (NM1) or the biasing network is too high for the given frequency and gain requirements. The current-reuse topology, while effective for gain and power efficiency, may not be optimally suppressing noise in this wideband scenario. The noise contribution from the biasing network (NM3, R1, R2, etc.) and the lack of a noise-shaping mechanism are likely the root causes.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n2 and the node n6, which is part of the biasing network. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise from the biasing network to ground, effectively decoupling the noise from the signal path and reducing the noise contribution to the input transistor. This change should lower the noise figure without significantly affecting the gain or stability of the circuit.
