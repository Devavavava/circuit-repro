The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_max_db), which is too high due to the noise contributions from the input transistor NM1 and the current-reuse path. The current-reuse configuration introduces additional noise sources, particularly from the diode-connected NM3 and the PMOS current mirror PM2, which are not effectively suppressed in the given topology. This leads to a higher-than-allowed noise floor, especially at the target frequency.

To address this, the topology needs to reduce the noise contribution from the current-reuse path. The proposed solution eliminates the current-reuse structure by removing the diode-connected NM3 and the PMOS current mirror PM2, and instead uses a direct NMOS transistor (NM2) to provide a low-noise path from the inductor L2 to the output. This change reduces the number of noise-generating components in the signal path and simplifies the feedback mechanism, leading to a lower noise figure.
