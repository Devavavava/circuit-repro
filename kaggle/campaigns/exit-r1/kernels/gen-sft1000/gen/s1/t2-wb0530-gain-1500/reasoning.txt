The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, which is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure. The NMOS diode (NM3) and the resistors (R1, R2, R4, R5) in the biasing network introduce noise that degrades the overall noise performance of the LNA. Additionally, the current-reuse topology may be causing an imbalance in the signal path, leading to higher noise at the output. The shared DC path and the presence of multiple resistive elements in the biasing network also contribute to noise accumulation.

To address this, I propose replacing the NMOS diode (NM3) with a PMOS diode (PM3) connected to the same internal node (n5) and ground (VSS). This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network by using a PMOS diode, which typically has lower noise characteristics in this configuration. The PMOS diode also provides a more stable current reference, improving the noise performance of the LNA.
