The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the shared DC path and the lack of a dedicated feedback mechanism to stabilize the amplifier over the wideband frequency range. The anchor circuit's structure does not provide sufficient phase and gain margin at the input and output ports, especially at the lower end of the band, leading to instability.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output (VOUT1) to the input node (n2) via a new NMOS transistor ZN1 and a capacitor ZC1. This creates a local feedback loop that helps stabilize the amplifier by reducing the phase shift and improving the gain margin over the wideband range. Additionally, I introduced a resistor Rrep1 connecting the bias node n5 to VDD, which helps in maintaining a stable bias point for the transistors and reduces variations that could affect stability.
