The failure is due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.12 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 being too high, possibly due to insufficient impedance transformation or inadequate noise suppression from the load network. The current-reuse topology may be amplifying noise from the DC bias network, especially from the diode-connected NM3 and the resistor R2, which may introduce noise into the signal path.

To address this, I added a PMOS transistor ZP1 between the internal node n5 (which connects to the gate of NM3) and the output node n2. This creates an additional feedback path from the output to the input bias network, helping to suppress noise by providing a low-noise path for signal feedback. This change improves the noise figure by reducing the impact of noise sources in the bias network on the signal path.
