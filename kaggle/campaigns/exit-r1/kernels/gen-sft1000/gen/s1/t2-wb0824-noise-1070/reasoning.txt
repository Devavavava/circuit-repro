The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.28 dB. The root cause is the lack of a noise-shaping mechanism in the current-reuse topology, which allows noise from the second stage to couple into the input and degrade the overall noise performance. Additionally, the absence of a noise-canceling element at the output node leads to higher noise contributions from the output load and parasitic capacitances.

To address this, I introduce a noise-canceling NMOS transistor (ZN1) connected between the output node (n2) and a new internal node (x1), with its source tied to the output load node (n4). This creates a feedback path that actively suppresses noise contributions from the output side, effectively reducing the noise figure. The addition of ZN1 improves the noise performance by providing a low-impedance path for noise currents to ground, thereby decoupling the noise from the input and output nodes.
