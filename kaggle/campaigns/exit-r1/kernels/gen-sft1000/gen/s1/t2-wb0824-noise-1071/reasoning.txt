This coupling degrades the stability margin, especially at lower frequencies, where the phase shift introduced by the parasitic paths becomes significant. The current-reuse configuration, while effective for noise and gain, lacks sufficient isolation between the input and output at the wideband edge, leading to a negative stability margin.
