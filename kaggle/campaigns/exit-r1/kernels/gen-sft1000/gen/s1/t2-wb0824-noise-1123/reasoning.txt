The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification. The anchor circuit's noise performance is limited by the current-reuse topology and the lack of a dedicated noise-shaping element at the output. The shared DC path and the absence of a noise-canceling feedback mechanism cause noise from the current-reuse stage to propagate into the output, raising the overall noise figure beyond the allowed threshold.

To address this, I introduce a noise-canceling feedback path using an additional NMOS transistor (ZN1) and a capacitor (ZC1) to the output. ZN1 forms a current mirror that provides a noise-canceling signal from the output back to the input stage, effectively reducing the noise contribution from the current-reuse path. ZC1 decouples the output node from the noise-sensitive internal nodes, preventing noise from the load and output from coupling back into the amplification path. This structural change enhances the noise figure by isolating the output from noise sources and introducing a feedback mechanism that suppresses noise contributions.
