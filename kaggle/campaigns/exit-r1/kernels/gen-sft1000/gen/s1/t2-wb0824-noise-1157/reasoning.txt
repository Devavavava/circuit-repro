The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, which is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure. The diode-connected NM3 and PM2 biasing network introduces additional noise, especially at the input node, which degrades the overall noise performance. Additionally, the current-reuse topology may be causing unwanted noise coupling through the shared DC path.

To address this, I modified the biasing structure by connecting the NM3 drain to VDD instead of n5, which isolates the biasing network from the input node and reduces noise coupling. I also added a resistor Rrep1 to connect the internal node n5 to VDD, providing a more stable and lower-noise biasing reference. This change helps decouple the biasing network from the signal path, reducing its noise contribution to the input. These structural changes improve the noise figure by minimizing the impact of biasing noise on the input signal.
