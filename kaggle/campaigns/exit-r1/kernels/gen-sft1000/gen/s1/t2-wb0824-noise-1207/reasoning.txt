The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is limited by the noise contributions from the input transistor (NM1) and the current-reuse path. The anchor netlist uses a current-reuse configuration with NM3 and PM2, but the noise from the current-reuse path and the input transistor is not sufficiently suppressed, leading to an NF that exceeds the specification by ~0.007 dB. This is likely due to the noise contributions from the diode-connected NM3 and PM2, as well as the lack of additional noise suppression mechanisms in the signal path.

To address this, I introduce a noise-canceling feedback mechanism by adding a resistor (Rrep1) from the internal node n5 (which is connected to the gate of NM3) to the node n2 (the drain of NM1). This creates a low-impedance path for noise currents from the current-reuse path to be shunted to the signal node n2, effectively reducing the noise contribution from the current-reuse path to the input signal. This structural change improves the noise figure by suppressing the noise from the current-reuse path without significantly affecting the gain or power consumption.
