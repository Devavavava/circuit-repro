The failure is primarily due to noise figure (NF) being too high, exceeding the specified maximum of 1.8613 dB by about 0.26 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the current mirror and the lack of noise suppression from the load network. The current mirror (NM3 and R1, R2) introduces noise that is amplified due to the high gain of the amplifier. Additionally, the load network (L2 and C2) is not effectively suppressing noise at the operating frequency. The structure lacks a noise-canceling component or a more effective noise-shaping mechanism.

To address this, I added a noise-canceling capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n2 and n6, which helps in reducing the noise contribution from the current mirror by providing an alternative noise discharge path. Also, I introduced a resistor (ZR1) between n2 and VDD, which helps in stabilizing the voltage at n2 and reducing noise fluctuations. Additionally, a capacitor (ZC2) between n4 and n6 further suppresses noise at the output node by filtering out high-frequency noise components.
