The current-reuse structure, while efficient, does not inherently provide the necessary feedback to stabilize the amplifier at higher frequencies or in the presence of process variations.

To address this, I introduced a feedback mechanism by adding a PMOS transistor (`ZP1`) and a resistor (`ZR1`) to create a feedback path from the output to the input, which helps to improve phase margin and stabilize the amplifier over the wideband range. This feedback structure ensures that the amplifier remains stable even at the edges of the frequency band, directly addressing the `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` constraint. Additionally, the feedback helps to dampen oscillations and improve impedance matching, which also benefits other metrics like `s11_max_db` and `s21_ripple_db`.
