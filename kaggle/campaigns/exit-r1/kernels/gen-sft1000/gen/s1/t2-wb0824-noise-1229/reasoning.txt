The noise figure (nf_max_db) is failing by approximately 0.2 dB, which is likely due to the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and a resistor (R1) to set the bias, which introduces noise into the signal path. Additionally, the current-reuse topology may be causing an increase in noise due to the feedback and shared DC paths.

To address this, the biasing network is restructured to isolate the biasing from the signal path. The NM3 device is connected to a different internal node (x1) instead of VSS, and a new resistor (Rrep1) is added to provide a separate bias reference. This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network by isolating it from the main signal path, thereby improving the noise figure. The PMOS (PM2) is also connected to a different internal node (n6) to further decouple the biasing network from the signal path. These changes reduce the noise coupling from the bias network to the signal path, directly addressing the noise figure limitation.
