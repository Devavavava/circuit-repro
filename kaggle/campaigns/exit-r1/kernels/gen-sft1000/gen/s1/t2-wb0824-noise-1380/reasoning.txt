The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) over the wideband frequency range. The original anchor netlist uses a resistive shunt-feedback CS LNA with a shunt-peaked inductive load, which, although effective for noise and gain, lacks sufficient phase margin and feedback control over the entire band, leading to instability at certain frequencies.

To address this, I introduced a dual-stage topology with an additional NMOS transistor (NM2) and inductor (L2) in a cascode configuration, along with a second feedback resistor (R3) and a DC blocking capacitor (C2) to the output. The addition of NM2 and L2 provides better control over the output impedance and enhances the phase margin, improving stability over the wideband range. The second feedback resistor (R3) and capacitor (C2) help in shaping the frequency response and further stabilize the circuit. The inclusion of a PMOS transistor (PM1) in a current source configuration adds a controlled bias and improves the overall linearity and stability of the LNA.
