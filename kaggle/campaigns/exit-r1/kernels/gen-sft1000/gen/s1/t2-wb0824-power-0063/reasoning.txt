The binding constraint is the stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`), which is negative, indicating instability over the wideband range. This is likely due to the feedback topology and the shunt-peaked inductive load, which may not provide sufficient phase margin across the entire band. The resistive shunt feedback and the single-stage common-source (CS) configuration may also contribute to poor stability at higher frequencies due to insufficient phase compensation.

To address this, I introduced a second stage with a PMOS transistor (PM1) and an inductive load (L2) to provide additional phase compensation and improve stability. This two-stage topology enhances the phase margin by adding a second amplification stage and an additional inductive peaking element, which helps to counteract the phase lag introduced by the first stage and the feedback network. Additionally, a PMOS current source (PM2) and a resistive load (R4) were added to provide a more stable biasing and output impedance, further improving the stability margin across the wideband range.
