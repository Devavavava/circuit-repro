The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, despite the other constraints being met or nearly met. This suggests that the noise contribution from the transistor NM1 is too high, likely due to its biasing and the lack of noise-shaping elements in the output stage. The current-reuse topology, while efficient for gain and power, may not adequately suppress noise in this wideband application, especially at the operating frequency of ~1.38 GHz.

To address this, I added a noise-canceling feedback path from the output to the input side of the amplifier, using a new resistor (ZR1) and a capacitor (ZC1) to create a low-frequency noise-shaping loop. This helps reduce the effective noise contribution of NM1 by feeding back a portion of the output signal to the input node, thereby improving the noise figure. Additionally, I modified the load network by replacing the original capacitor C2 with a new capacitor ZC2 and a resistor ZR2, which helps stabilize the output impedance and further suppress noise at high frequencies.
