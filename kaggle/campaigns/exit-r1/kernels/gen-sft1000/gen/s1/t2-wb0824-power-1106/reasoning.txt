The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` is failing due to insufficient stability margin across the wideband frequency range. The current topology relies on a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2), which may not provide sufficient phase and gain margin across the entire band, especially at the lower end of the frequency range (0.01–50 GHz). The lack of a dedicated feedback or stability-enhancing element in the output path likely causes the stability factor (mu) to dip below the required threshold at certain frequencies.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output to the input through a new capacitor (ZC1) and a PMOS transistor (ZP1), which introduces a controlled phase shift and dampens oscillatory behavior. Additionally, I added a resistor (Rrep1) and a PMOS transistor (Rrep2) to the bias network to provide a more stable and predictable current reference, improving the overall stability of the amplifier over the entire frequency range. These changes enhance the phase margin and reduce the likelihood of instability, directly addressing the failure in the mu_min constraint.
