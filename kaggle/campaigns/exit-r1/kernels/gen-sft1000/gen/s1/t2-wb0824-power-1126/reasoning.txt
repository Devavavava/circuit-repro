The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the feedback path from the output through the inductive load L2 and capacitive load C2, which introduces a resonant peak in the frequency response and degrades stability. This is exacerbated by the current-reuse topology, where the shared DC path and the feedback through the inductive load create a phase shift that reduces the phase margin at the operating frequency.

To address this, I restructured the circuit to eliminate the resonant feedback path by moving the inductive load directly to ground and reconfiguring the current-reuse path to avoid the shared DC node. This reduces the feedback loop's impact on the phase margin and improves stability. The new topology uses an inductive load connected to ground, and the current-reuse path is restructured with a separate NMOS transistor and a resistive divider to set the bias point without introducing feedback through the inductive load.
