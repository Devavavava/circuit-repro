The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) across the wideband range, likely caused by the feedback network and load structure in the anchor netlist. The resistive shunt feedback and shunt-peaked inductor load may not provide sufficient phase margin at higher frequencies, leading to instability.

To address this, I added a second NMOS transistor (NM2) in a common-source configuration with a current source (L1, C1, NM3, R1, R2, R4) to provide a more stable biasing and improve phase margin. This additional stage helps to increase the output impedance and reduce feedback at higher frequencies, thereby enhancing the stability margin. The addition of the PMOS transistor (PM1) and current source (L2, PM2, R5) further stabilizes the circuit by providing a low-impedance path and improving the overall phase characteristics.
