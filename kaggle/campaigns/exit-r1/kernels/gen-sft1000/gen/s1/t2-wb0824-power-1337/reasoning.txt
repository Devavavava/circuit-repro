The failure is primarily due to the insufficient stability and poor impedance matching in the wideband operation, as indicated by the negative margin in the mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz constraint. The original topology lacks sufficient feedback and impedance control mechanisms to maintain stability across the entire frequency range, especially at lower frequencies where the stability factor (mu) drops below the required minimum. The absence of a direct feedback path from the output to the input and the lack of impedance tuning elements contribute to this instability.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output to the input using an NMOS transistor (ZN1) and a capacitor (ZC1) to provide a controlled impedance path at high frequencies. Additionally, a resistor (ZR1) and a capacitor (ZC2) were added to the output side to improve impedance matching and provide a low-impedance path at DC, which helps in stabilizing the circuit across the wideband range. These changes enhance the stability factor by introducing feedback and impedance tuning, ensuring that the circuit remains stable over the entire frequency range.
