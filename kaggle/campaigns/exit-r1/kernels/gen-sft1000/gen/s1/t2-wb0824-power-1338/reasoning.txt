The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, which is likely caused by the noise contributions from the current mirror and the lack of sufficient noise suppression in the signal path. The current-reuse topology, while effective in power efficiency, introduces additional noise sources from the mirror transistors and the biasing network, especially at high frequencies. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for biasing, which may introduce noise and degrade the noise figure.

To address this, I propose a structural change that eliminates the current mirror biasing network and replaces it with a simpler, more noise-robust configuration. This includes removing NM3, R1, R2, and R4, and reconfiguring the signal path to avoid unnecessary noise contributors. The PMOS biasing is simplified by connecting it directly to VDD and the signal node, reducing the number of noise-generating components in the signal path. This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network and improves the signal-to-noise ratio at the output.
