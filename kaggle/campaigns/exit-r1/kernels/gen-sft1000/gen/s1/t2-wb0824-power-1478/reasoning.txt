The failure is due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.14 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the current-reuse path and the shared DC path, which introduces additional noise sources and degrades the overall noise performance. The current-reuse structure, while beneficial for power efficiency, may not be optimally configured for noise suppression in this wideband application, especially at the given frequency (1.38 GHz).

To address this, I introduce a direct connection from the internal node n4 (the tuned load node) to the gate of NM3, which is the diode-connected NMOS in the current mirror. This change ensures that the noise contribution from the current mirror is better isolated and controlled, reducing its impact on the noise figure. Additionally, I add a PMOS transistor (ZP1) and a resistor (Rrep1) to form a noise-canceling feedback path from n5 to n4, which helps in balancing the noise contributions and improving the noise figure.
