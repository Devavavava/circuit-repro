The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_db) exceeding the specification, which is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for biasing, which introduces additional noise into the signal path and may not provide sufficient isolation or noise suppression at the operating frequency. Additionally, the current-reuse topology may be causing an imbalance in signal paths, increasing noise.

To address this, I restructured the biasing network to eliminate the diode-connected NM3 and replaced it with a direct connection to ground, reducing noise contributions. The PMOS biasing was also restructured to use a simpler current mirror configuration, minimizing noise and ensuring better signal integrity. The load inductor (L2) was moved to a different node to avoid coupling with the bias network, and the output capacitor (C3) was repositioned to ensure a cleaner signal path. These changes reduce noise and improve the signal-to-noise ratio, directly addressing the nf_db constraint.
