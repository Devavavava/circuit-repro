The binding constraint, mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz, is failing due to poor stability across the wide frequency range, likely caused by the interstage LC network and the lack of sufficient feedback or damping in the second stage. The structure lacks a mechanism to suppress unwanted feedback paths and stabilize the amplifier at off-band frequencies, leading to a negative margin in the stability factor (mu) across the wideband.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output of the second stage (n6) to the source of the first stage (n2) using a capacitor (ZC1), which helps to dampen oscillations and improve stability. Additionally, I introduced a resistor (ZR1) from the drain of the first stage (n2) to VDD, providing a controlled path for high-frequency signals and reducing instability. A new node (x1) was added to facilitate the connection between these components without disrupting the existing topology. These changes enhance stability by reducing feedback and providing damping at off-band frequencies, directly addressing the mu_min_wide constraint.
