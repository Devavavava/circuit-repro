The second-stage gate is driven by the LC interstage network, which may introduce phase shifts that reduce the stability margin across the band.

To address this, I modified the structure to decouple the interstage LC network from the second stage's gate. Instead of using a series LC network (L2 and C2) driving the gate of NM2, I replaced it with a shunt LC network (L2 and C2) connected between VDD and the source of NM2. This change reduces the feedback path from the interstage network to the second-stage gate, thereby improving stability. Additionally, I restructured the output coupling to use a direct connection from the second stage to the output, with an output capacitor (C3) to block DC, which simplifies the signal path and reduces potential phase shifts that could destabilize the circuit.
