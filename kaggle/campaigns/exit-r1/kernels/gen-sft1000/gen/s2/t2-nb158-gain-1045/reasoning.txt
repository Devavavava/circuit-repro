The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the poor impedance matching and signal transfer between the two stages. The current topology uses a DC-blocked capacitor (C1) at the input and a series inductor (L2) for the interstage LC network, which may introduce parasitic effects and degrade the signal integrity at the narrowband frequency. The second stage's gate is driven through an LC network (C2 and L2), which may not provide sufficient AC coupling and impedance transformation, leading to a loss in gain.

To address this, I propose a structural change that improves the impedance matching and signal transfer between stages. The revised topology replaces the LC interstage network with a direct inductive coupling from the first stage's drain to the second stage's gate, which enhances the signal transfer and reduces parasitic losses. Additionally, the second stage's drain load is restructured to use a series inductor and capacitor, improving the impedance match and gain at the target frequency.
