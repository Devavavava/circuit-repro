The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the output impedance of the common-gate stage and the tuning of the load network. The original topology uses a single inductor (L2) as the load, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation or tuning at the target frequency to maximize gain. Additionally, the output capacitor (C2) may not be optimally placed to ensure a low-impedance path to the output while maintaining signal integrity.

To address this, I restructured the load network to include an additional inductor (L3) and a second NMOS transistor (NM2) as an active load. This creates a current mirror configuration, which increases the effective output impedance of the LNA and helps boost gain. The new inductor (L3) provides better impedance matching and tuning at the operating frequency, while the active load (NM2) enhances the transconductance and gain of the amplifier. The addition of the capacitor (ZC1) between the output of the current mirror and the main NMOS transistor helps decouple the output and reduce feedback, which improves stability and gain.
