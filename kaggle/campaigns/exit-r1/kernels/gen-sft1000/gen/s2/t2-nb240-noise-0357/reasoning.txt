The structure's LC interstage and tuned drain loads create a resonant peak at f0, but the phase response is not sufficiently robust across the 0.01–50 GHz range, resulting in a stability margin deficit.

To address this, I replaced the LC interstage and tuned drain load configuration with a source-degeneration inductor (L2) and a direct connection from the second-stage gate to the first-stage drain, while retaining the DC-blocking capacitor (C2) at the input. This change reduces the resonant feedback and broadens the phase margin across the wideband range.
