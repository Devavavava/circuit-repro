The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is negative, indicating instability over a wide bandwidth. The current topology, while optimized for narrowband performance, lacks sufficient feedback and impedance control across the broader frequency range. The LC interstage and tuned loads provide excellent narrowband matching but do not suppress oscillations or ensure stability at off-band frequencies. The absence of a direct feedback path from the output to the input or a damping element in the signal path exacerbates this issue.

To address this, I introduce a feedback capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the internal node n4, which is part of the LC interstage network. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, effectively damping unwanted oscillations and improving stability across the wideband range. Additionally, I add a resistor (ZR2) between the output node n6 and VOUT1, which helps control the output impedance and ensures a more stable feedback loop. These changes enhance the phase margin and reduce the likelihood of instability at frequencies outside the narrowband center.
