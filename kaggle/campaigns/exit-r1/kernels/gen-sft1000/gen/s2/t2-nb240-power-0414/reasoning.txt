The binding constraint is the stability metric `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is negative, indicating instability over a wide frequency range. This is likely due to the LC interstage network (L2 and C2) creating a resonant feedback path that causes phase shifts and reduces the stability margin at frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (f0 = 2.4 GHz). The interstage LC network introduces a second resonance that interacts poorly with the amplifier's internal feedback, especially at higher frequencies.

To address this, I replaced the LC interstage network with a series inductor (L2) directly connecting the output of stage 1 (n2) to the input of stage 2 (n5). This eliminates the resonant feedback path introduced by the LC interstage network and reduces the number of inductors from 3 to 2. This change improves stability by simplifying the feedback path and reducing the number of resonant nodes in the circuit.
