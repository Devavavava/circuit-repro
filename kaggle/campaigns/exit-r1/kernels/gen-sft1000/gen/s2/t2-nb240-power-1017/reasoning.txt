The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is negative, indicating instability over a wide frequency range. This is likely due to the LC interstage network (L2 and C2) creating a resonant feedback path that destabilizes the circuit, especially at frequencies near the resonance of L2 and C2. This resonance introduces a phase shift that reduces the stability margin, particularly at off-center frequencies.

To address this, I added a compensation capacitor `ZC1` between the bias node `n7` and the interstage node `n4`. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, effectively shorting out the resonant feedback path at higher frequencies. This change reduces the phase shift caused by the LC interstage network, thereby improving the stability margin across the wide frequency band.
