The second-stage drain load is now a direct inductor to VDD, and the output is coupled via a capacitor to the output node. The biasing network is preserved but reconnected to ensure proper gate biasing without introducing additional feedback paths. This change improves the stability margin by reducing the number of resonant elements and feedback loops, especially at off-band frequencies.
