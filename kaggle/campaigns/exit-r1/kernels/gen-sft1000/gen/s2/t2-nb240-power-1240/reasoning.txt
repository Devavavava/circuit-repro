The binding constraint is the s21_db, which is too low by ~0.32 dB. The two-stage LNA's interstage LC network is likely causing excessive signal loss or phase shift at f0, limiting gain. The second stage's drain load (L3) is tuned to VDD, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation or may be causing resonance that degrades gain at the operating frequency. Additionally, the source-degeneration inductors in the anchor netlist are absent, which could be contributing to poor gain and stability.

To address this, I restructured the LNA to use a single-stage topology with a source-degeneration inductor (L2) and a shunt inductor (L1) for input matching. The second stage is replaced with a common-gate configuration (NM2) to improve gain and reduce phase shift. The drain load of the first stage is now a shunt inductor (L3) to VDD, which provides better impedance transformation. This configuration avoids the interstage LC network, reducing signal loss and phase distortion. The bias network is simplified with a current mirror (NM3) and resistors (R1, R2) to ensure proper biasing without additional components.
