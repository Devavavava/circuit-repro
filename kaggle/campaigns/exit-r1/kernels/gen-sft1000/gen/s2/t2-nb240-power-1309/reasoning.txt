The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` failed due to insufficient stability in the wideband region, likely caused by the lack of a feedback mechanism to suppress oscillations and improve phase margin. This absence leads to a lower stability factor (`mu`) at frequencies beyond the design band, particularly in the 0.01–50 GHz range, where the phase shift accumulates and causes instability.

To address this, I introduced a feedback path from the output to the input stage via an additional NMOS transistor (ZN1) and a resistor (ZR1). This creates a small-signal feedback loop that helps maintain phase margin and suppress unwanted oscillations, thereby improving the stability factor (`mu`) across the wideband range. Additionally, a capacitor (ZC1) was added to the first stage's drain node to provide a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, further stabilizing the circuit at higher frequencies.
