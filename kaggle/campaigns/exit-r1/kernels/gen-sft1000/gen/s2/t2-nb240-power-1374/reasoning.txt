The noise figure (nf_db) is the binding constraint, and it is failing by a small margin (−0.21 dB). This suggests that the noise contribution from the second stage is too high, likely due to the lack of a noise-shaping mechanism or impedance mismatch at the interstage node. The current topology lacks a direct feedback or impedance control mechanism at the interstage node, allowing noise from the second stage to propagate back and degrade the overall noise figure.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the interstage node (n4) to the bias node (n7) using a resistor (Rrep1). This creates a low-impedance path to the bias network, effectively isolating the second stage from noise sources and improving the noise figure by reducing the noise contribution from the second stage. This change also helps in stabilizing the bias point and reducing the noise coupling between stages.
