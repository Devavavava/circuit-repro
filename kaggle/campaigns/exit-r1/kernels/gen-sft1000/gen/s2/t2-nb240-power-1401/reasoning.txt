The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) caused by the feedback and load structure in the original circuit. The resistive shunt feedback and shunt-peaked inductor load, while beneficial for bandwidth and noise, do not provide enough phase margin across the wide frequency band, leading to instability at the edges of the operating range.

To address this, I propose a structural change that introduces a current-reuse topology with an additional NMOS transistor and inductor in the feedback path. This configuration enhances the phase margin by introducing a controlled feedback path with a higher impedance at higher frequencies, thereby improving stability without degrading the noise or gain performance. The new topology also ensures better DC biasing and feedback control, which helps maintain stability across the entire band.
