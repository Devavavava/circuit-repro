The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is slightly negative, indicating instability at some frequencies in the band. This is likely due to the second-stage NMOS (NM2) having a weak feedback path to the output, leading to potential oscillations at higher frequencies. The original topology's LC interstage (L2 and C2) may not provide sufficient damping or phase margin at the output node, especially when the second stage is driven by a resonant LC network. This resonant loading can cause a phase shift that reduces the stability margin.

To address this, I added a resistor (ZR1) from the output (VOUT1) to the drain of the first stage (n2), creating a feedback path from the output to the first stage. This introduces a small amount of damping and helps stabilize the output node by providing a controlled impedance path for high-frequency signals. This change increases the phase margin at the output and suppresses oscillatory behavior, directly improving the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`.
