The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, which is likely caused by the noise contributions from the input transistor NM1 and the biasing network. The current-reuse topology may be introducing additional noise paths or not sufficiently isolating the input from the biasing network. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NM3 and PM2 for biasing, which may add noise due to their nonlinear behavior and current variations. Additionally, the shared DC path and the lack of proper impedance matching at the input may be contributing to higher noise.

To address this, I modified the biasing network by replacing the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor (Rrep1) connected from n2 to VDD. This change helps reduce the noise contribution from the biasing network by providing a more stable and linear current path. The resistor Rrep1 also helps in better impedance matching and isolates the input node from the biasing network, thereby reducing the overall noise figure. This change should lower the noise figure and meet the nf_max_db constraint.
