The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is below the required threshold. This indicates that the circuit is unstable over a wide frequency range, likely due to poor feedback and poor impedance matching in the current-reuse topology. The shared DC path and the load configuration (L2+C2) may be contributing to this instability by not sufficiently isolating the output from the input and by not providing adequate phase margin.

To address this, I propose a structural change that introduces a dedicated feedback path from the output to the input through an inductor (L3), which helps to stabilize the circuit and improve the phase margin. Additionally, a new PMOS transistor (ZP1) is added to provide a more controlled current path and improve impedance matching. The addition of a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the output node further helps to decouple the output and improve stability.
