The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_max_db), which is exceeded by 0.05 dB. The noise performance is likely degraded due to the noise contributions from the DC-blocking capacitor C4 and the input inductor L1, which are not optimally isolated from the noise-generating components in the signal path. Additionally, the shared DC path and the current-reuse topology may be introducing unnecessary noise coupling and increasing the overall noise floor.

To address this, the topology is modified to decouple the input signal from the noise-sensitive DC path. A new node x1 is introduced, and the capacitor C1 is connected from n3 to x1 instead of n1 to n3, isolating the signal path from the DC node. A new capacitor ZC1 is added from x1 to n0, providing a cleaner signal path and reducing noise coupling from the DC network. This change improves the noise figure by reducing the noise contribution from the DC path and enhancing signal isolation.
