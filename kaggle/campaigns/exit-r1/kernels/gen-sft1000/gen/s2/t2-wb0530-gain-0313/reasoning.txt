The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the current-reuse structure and the lack of sufficient noise suppression in the signal path. The original topology's noise performance is limited by the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and the current-setting PMOS (PM2), which introduce noise into the signal chain. Additionally, the absence of a noise-canceling component in the signal path prevents effective noise suppression at the input.

To address this, a noise-canceling capacitor (ZN1) is added between the internal node n2 (the output of the main amplifier) and the node n6 (the current-setting PMOS's source). This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise, effectively canceling noise contributions from the current-reuse structure and reducing the overall noise figure. This change enhances the signal-to-noise ratio at the output without introducing additional noise sources or increasing power consumption.
