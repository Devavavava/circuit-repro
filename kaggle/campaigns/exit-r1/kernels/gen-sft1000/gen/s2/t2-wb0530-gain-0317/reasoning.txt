The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.12 dB. The noise performance is likely being degraded by the current-reuse topology and the presence of the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) in the bias network, which introduces additional noise into the signal path. The NM3 diode is directly connected to the signal node (n5), which is part of the main signal path, and its noise contribution is not adequately suppressed, especially in the wideband operation.

The proposed structural change replaces the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor (Rrep1) connecting the internal node x1 (originally n5) to the signal node n3. This change decouples the noise-generating diode from the signal path and instead uses a resistor to provide a stable reference for the bias network. This reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network to the signal path, thereby improving the noise figure.
