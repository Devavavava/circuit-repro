The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the lack of a feedback mechanism to dampen oscillations across the wideband frequency range. The current topology lacks a direct connection between the output and the input DC path, which limits the circuit's ability to maintain phase and gain stability at all frequencies in the band.

To address this, I introduce a feedback path from the output to the input DC path by connecting the output node (VOUT1) to the internal node n4 via a capacitor (ZC1). This addition provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals to return to the input, enhancing phase margin and stabilizing the amplifier across the wideband range. This change is structural and does not affect the core LNA topology or introduce biasing networks.
