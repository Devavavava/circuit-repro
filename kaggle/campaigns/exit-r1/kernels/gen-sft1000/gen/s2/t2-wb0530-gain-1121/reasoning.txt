To address this, I introduce a second stage with a common-gate (CG) configuration, which inherently provides a higher phase margin and better stability. The CG stage also helps to isolate the feedback loop from the output, reducing the risk of oscillation. Additionally, I add a second inductor (L2) and a capacitor (C3) to the output to further shape the impedance and improve the phase response. The second NMOS (NM2) and PMOS (PM1) devices in the second stage provide additional gain and stability.
