The failure is primarily due to the poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being violated by ~0.48 dB. This indicates that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source, likely due to the lack of a direct DC path from the input to the gate of the NMOS transistor (NM1) and the absence of a proper impedance transformation mechanism at the input. The anchor netlist lacks a capacitor or resistor that could help shape the input impedance and improve matching at the target frequency.

To address this, I added a resistor (ZR1) connecting the internal node n0 (the node after the input capacitor C4) to the DC bias node n5. This provides a controlled DC path to the gate of NM1, helping stabilize the input impedance and improving the matching at the operating frequency. This change enhances the input matching by allowing the gate of NM1 to see a more predictable impedance, reducing reflections and thus improving the s11_max_db metric.
