To address this, I propose a structural change to the topology by introducing a second NMOS transistor (NM2) in a cascode configuration, along with an additional inductor (L2) and capacitor (C2) to provide a more stable feedback path and enhance the phase margin. This change introduces a second stage that helps isolate the input from the output, reducing feedback-induced phase shifts and improving stability across the wideband range.
