The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_max_db), which is slightly above the target (2.74635 vs 2.5043). This suggests that the noise contribution from the transistor NM1 is too high, likely due to its high transconductance and low output impedance, which increases the noise floor. The current-reuse topology in the anchor netlist may not be effectively isolating the noise contributions from the input stage.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n5 and the output node VOUT1. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise to ground, effectively reducing the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 by decoupling its noise from the output. This change improves the noise figure by reducing the noise coupling from the input stage to the output.
