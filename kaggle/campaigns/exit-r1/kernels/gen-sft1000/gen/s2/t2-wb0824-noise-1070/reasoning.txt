The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification. This is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the lack of proper noise-shaping elements in the current-reuse topology. The NMOS bias diode (NM3) and the resistive biasing (R1, R2, R4, R5) introduce significant noise at the input node, especially at the high-frequency operating point (f0 = ~1.38 GHz). Additionally, the absence of a noise-cancelling or noise-reducing element in the signal path exacerbates the noise floor.

To address this, I added a noise-canceling capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n5 and n4, which helps to decouple the noise from the biasing network and the signal path. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise from the biasing network to ground, effectively reducing its contribution to the input noise. This change is structural and does not introduce any new biasing or value elements.
