The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification by approximately 0.39 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the input transistor (NM1) and the current-reuse path being too high, particularly due to the lack of a noise-shaping mechanism in the input stage and the presence of a DC-blocking capacitor (C4) that may not adequately filter out noise from the input signal. Additionally, the absence of a noise-canceling feedback path or a noise-suppressing element in the signal path contributes to the elevated noise floor. The structure lacks a mechanism to suppress or shape the noise from the input transistor, which is critical for achieving a low-noise figure in a wideband LNA.

To address this, I introduce a noise-shaping element by adding a capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n2 (output of NM1) and the node n6 (bias reference point of PM2). This capacitor introduces a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise, effectively shorting it to ground and reducing its contribution to the noise figure. This change helps in shaping the noise spectrum and reducing the overall noise at the input frequency band, thereby improving the noise figure.
