The failure is primarily due to the poor input matching, as indicated by the s11_max_db constraint being violated by approximately 0.3 dB. This suggests that the input impedance of the LNA is not sufficiently matched to the source impedance at the target frequency. The root cause lies in the lack of a dedicated input impedance tuning element, which is critical for wideband matching. The anchor netlist lacks a direct mechanism to adjust the input impedance independently of the active device's intrinsic characteristics, leading to an insufficient reflection coefficient at the input port.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the internal node n5, which is connected to the gate of the NMOS current mirror (NM3). This capacitor provides a tunable impedance path at high frequencies, allowing for better control over the input impedance without affecting the DC biasing. The addition of ZC1 introduces a new degree of freedom in the impedance matching, especially in the wideband region, and helps to flatten the input reflection coefficient across the operating band. This structural change directly improves the input matching, thereby increasing the s11_max_db and resolving the constraint violation.
