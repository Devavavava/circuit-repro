The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (NF) exceeding the specification, which is caused by the lack of a proper noise-shaping mechanism in the current-reuse topology. The anchor netlist lacks a dedicated noise-canceling element at the input stage, leading to an elevated noise contribution from the input transistor NM1. This is exacerbated by the absence of a feedback or impedance-matching element that could help suppress noise at the desired frequency band.

To address this, I added a noise-canceling capacitor (ZC1) between the bias node n5 and the input node n0. This capacitor helps to decouple the noise from the bias network at the input, effectively reducing the noise contribution from the biasing path into the signal path. This change introduces a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise at the input, improving the noise figure by attenuating unwanted noise components at the operating frequency.
