The lack of an explicit input matching network or impedance transformation mechanism results in a mismatch at the input, especially at the high-frequency edge of the band.

To address this, I introduce an inductive degeneration technique at the input stage by adding an inductor (L3) in series with the NMOS transistor (NM1). This inductor provides a low-impedance path at DC while presenting a high impedance at RF frequencies, thereby improving the input impedance match and reducing reflections. Additionally, I add a resistor (Rrep1) from the input node (n0) to the bias node (n5) to provide an alternative DC path and help stabilize the bias point, improving the overall impedance behavior at the input port.
