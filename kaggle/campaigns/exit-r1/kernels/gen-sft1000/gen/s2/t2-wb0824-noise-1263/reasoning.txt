The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, indicating that the noise contribution from the transistor NM1 is too high. This is likely caused by the lack of a proper noise-shaping or noise-canceling component in the input stage. The anchor netlist lacks a mechanism to suppress noise from the input transistor, which becomes critical in a wideband LNA operating at a very high frequency (1.38 GHz). The absence of a noise-canceling element or a low-noise path from the input to the output leads to a higher noise figure than allowed.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n3 (which is the drain of PM1 and the source of PM2) and the gate of PM2 (node n6). This creates a feedback path that helps suppress noise from the input transistor by providing a low-impedance path for noise currents at high frequencies. This change improves the noise figure by reducing the noise contribution from the input transistor, aligning it with the specification.
