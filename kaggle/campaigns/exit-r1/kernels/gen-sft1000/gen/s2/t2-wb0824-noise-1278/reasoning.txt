The binding constraint is the minimum stability factor (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`), which is negative, indicating instability in the circuit over the specified bandwidth. The root cause is the lack of a proper feedback path to stabilize the amplifier, especially at higher frequencies. The resistive shunt feedback in the anchor netlist provides limited phase compensation, and the shunt-peaked inductor load may introduce resonances that reduce stability margins.

To address this, I added a second NMOS transistor (ZN1) and a capacitor (ZC1) to create an additional feedback path from the output to the drain of the first transistor. This additional feedback loop enhances phase compensation and stabilizes the amplifier over the wideband frequency range. The capacitor ZC1 provides a high-frequency bypass, helping to suppress unwanted oscillations and improve the stability factor.
