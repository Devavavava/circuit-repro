The failure is primarily due to poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being -4.78539 dB (well below the required -7.8474 dB). This indicates that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source, likely due to the lack of a direct capacitive coupling from the input to the inductor L2, which is critical for broadband impedance tuning. The absence of such a coupling limits the ability of the LNA to maintain a low reflection coefficient across the wideband frequency range specified.

To address this, I added a direct capacitive coupling between the input node (n0) and the inductor L2 (node n2), using a new capacitor ZC1. This addition allows for more effective impedance tuning at the input, improving the matching by providing an additional path for AC signals to bypass the inductor and directly couple to the active device, thereby reducing reflections and improving s11 performance.
