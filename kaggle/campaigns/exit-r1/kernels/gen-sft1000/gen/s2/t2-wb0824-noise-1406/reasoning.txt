The binding constraint is the s11_max_db, which is failing by ~0.48 dB. This indicates that the input return loss is not sufficiently low, meaning the input impedance is not well-matched to the source. In the anchor netlist, the input is coupled to the LNA through a capacitor (C4) and inductor (L1), forming a series LC network. However, the lack of a direct resistive or capacitive shunt from the input node to the DC path may be causing impedance mismatches at the operating frequency, especially in a wideband context. Additionally, the absence of a resistive component to ground at the input node may be limiting the ability to dampen unwanted resonances or reflections.

To address this, I added a resistive shunt (Rrep1) from the internal node n3 (which is connected to the source of PM1 and the drain of NM1) to the bias node n5. This provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency components, improving input impedance matching and reducing reflections. This change helps stabilize the input impedance over the wideband range, directly improving s11_max_db by providing a controlled damping mechanism without introducing significant loss or noise.
