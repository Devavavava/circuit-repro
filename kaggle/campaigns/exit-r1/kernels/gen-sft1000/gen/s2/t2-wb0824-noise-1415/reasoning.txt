The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_max_db), which is failing by approximately 0.17 dB. This suggests that the noise contribution from the transistor NM1 is too high, likely due to its high impedance at the input and the lack of a proper noise-shaping mechanism. The current topology uses a simple common-source stage with a current-reuse structure, but it lacks a noise-canceling component or a low-noise path from the input to the output.

To address this, I added a PMOS transistor (ZP1) connected between the input node n0 and the gate of PM1, with its source connected to VDD and its body to VSS. This PMOS transistor acts as a noise-canceling element by providing an alternative low-noise path from the input to the output, thereby reducing the effective noise contribution from the input transistor NM1. This change improves the noise figure by redirecting some of the input signal through a lower-noise path, reducing the overall noise floor.
