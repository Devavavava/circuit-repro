The binding constraint is the s11_max_db, which is failing by approximately 0.18 dB. This indicates that the input matching is insufficient, likely due to the poor impedance transformation at the input port. The anchor netlist uses a simple common-source configuration with a DC-blocking capacitor (C4) at the input, but it lacks a dedicated input matching network or a more sophisticated impedance transformation mechanism. The input impedance is dominated by the gate of NM1 and the series inductance L1, but without a deliberate impedance-matching element, the reflection coefficient at the input is too high, especially at the target frequency of ~1.38 GHz.

To address this, I added a shunt NMOS transistor (ZN1) between the internal node n2 and VDD, with its source connected to the same node n2 and drain to VDD. This creates a controlled impedance at the output node n2, which helps in improving the input impedance matching by providing an additional path for AC signals and effectively acting as a resistive load at the output. This change enhances the input matching by allowing better control over the input impedance, thus reducing the reflection coefficient (S11) and improving the s11_max_db metric.
