

The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification limit. This is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-setting resistors, particularly R4 and R2, which introduce significant noise due to their high impedance and the lack of a low-noise path to ground. The NMOS diode NM3 and PMOS diode PM2 also contribute noise, especially since they are not isolated from the signal path and are used for biasing. This results in an overall noise figure that is too high for the desired performance.

To address this, I have restructured the biasing network to minimize noise contributions. In the revised netlist, the NM3 diode is connected to a new internal node x1, which is connected to the drain of NM1 and the source of PM2. This isolates the biasing network from the signal path and reduces noise coupling. Additionally, the PM2 diode is now connected to the new node x1, which is also connected to the drain of NM1, allowing for a more stable and low-noise biasing configuration. This structural change improves the noise figure by reducing the noise contributions from the biasing network and ensuring a cleaner signal path.
