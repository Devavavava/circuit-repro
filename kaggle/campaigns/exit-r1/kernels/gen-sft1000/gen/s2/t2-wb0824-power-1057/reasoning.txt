The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the lack of a feedback mechanism that suppresses oscillations in the wideband range. The anchor netlist lacks a direct connection between the output and the feedback path, leading to insufficient phase and gain margin across the band. This causes the amplifier to become unstable at certain frequencies, particularly in the lower end of the wideband range, which is reflected in the negative margin for mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz.

To address this, I introduce a feedback path from the output (VOUT1) to the internal node n5, which is connected to the gate of the NMOS transistor NM3. This feedback path helps to stabilize the amplifier by introducing a controlled phase shift and reducing the gain in the feedback loop, thereby improving the stability margin across the entire frequency range. The addition of the capacitor ZC1 between n0 and n6 also helps to decouple the feedback path from the input, preventing unwanted coupling and further enhancing stability.
