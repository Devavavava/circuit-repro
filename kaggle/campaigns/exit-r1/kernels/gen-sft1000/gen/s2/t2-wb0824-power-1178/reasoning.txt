The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by about 0.137 dB. This suggests that the noise contribution from the transistor NM1 or the load network is not adequately suppressed. In the original topology, the noise from the NMOS transistor NM1 is not sufficiently attenuated by the load network, and the current-reuse structure may be introducing additional noise due to the shared DC path and the lack of a dedicated noise-suppressing component.

To address this, I added a resistor ZR1 between the internal node n2 and the gate of NM3. This resistor introduces a controlled impedance between the output and the biasing network, effectively isolating the noise-generating components from the sensitive input and output nodes. This change helps reduce the noise contribution from the transistor NM1 by providing a more stable and predictable impedance path, thus lowering the overall noise figure of the circuit.
