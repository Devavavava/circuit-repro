The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, which is likely caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure. The NMOS NM3 and PMOS PM2 biasing network introduces additional noise due to their diode-connected configuration, which is amplified in the current-reuse topology. Additionally, the shared DC path and the lack of a noise-shaping component at the output contribute to higher noise levels.

To address this, I introduce a noise-shaping inductor (L3) between the internal node n4 (connected to the tuned load) and the internal node x1, which is connected to the gate of PM2. This inductor helps isolate the noise from the biasing network and provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, thereby reducing the noise contribution at the output. This change structurally modifies the current-reuse feedback path and improves noise performance without affecting the gain or stability.
