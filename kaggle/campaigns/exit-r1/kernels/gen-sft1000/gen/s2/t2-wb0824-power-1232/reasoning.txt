The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by approximately 0.12 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the lack of a proper noise-shaping mechanism in the current-reuse topology. The existing structure lacks a direct path to ground for the noise-sensitive nodes, and the bias network introduces unnecessary noise at the input and output stages.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n6 and the drain of NM3 (n2), and a resistor (ZR1) connecting the bias node n5 to the drain of NM1 (n2). The capacitor ZC1 provides a low-impedance path to ground for high-frequency noise, reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network. The resistor ZR1 introduces a controlled feedback path, helping to stabilize the noise performance and reduce the noise figure by providing a local noise suppression mechanism.
