To address this, I modify the topology by replacing the original load network (L2 + C2) with a series inductor (L3) and a capacitor (C3) connected to an internal node (n7), creating a resonant structure at the operating frequency. This change enhances noise suppression by providing a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise to ground, improving noise matching. Additionally, I connect the gate of NM3 to n7 instead of VSS, allowing for better noise cancellation and feedback. This structural change ensures the noise contribution from the input transistor is minimized, directly addressing the nf_max_db constraint.
