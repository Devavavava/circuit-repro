The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the current-reuse topology and the shared DC path, which leads to poor phase and amplitude balance in the feedback loop, especially at lower frequencies. This results in instability and a reduced stability margin. The shared DC path and the current-reuse configuration contribute to a higher noise figure and increased power consumption, which further degrade the stability.

To address this, I introduced a separate DC path for the PMOS transistor (PM2) by connecting its source to an internal node (n6) instead of VDD, and added a resistor (Rrep1) from n6 to VDD. This decouples the PMOS gate biasing from the main DC path, reducing feedback loop interference and improving phase balance. Additionally, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between n2 and VOUT1 to provide a local feedback path for the NMOS transistor, enhancing stability and reducing noise. These changes help to isolate the feedback paths and reduce the impact of the current-reuse topology on the stability margin.
