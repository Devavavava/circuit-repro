The single-stage CS topology with resistive feedback and shunt peaking is insufficient to maintain stability across the entire wideband range.

To address this, I introduce a second-stage common-gate (CG) amplifier in parallel with the first stage, using a PMOS transistor (PM1) and a shunt inductor (L2) to provide additional phase boost and improve stability. This dual-stage topology helps maintain a higher phase margin across the band. Additionally, a second NMOS transistor (NM3) is used as a current source to provide a stable bias point, and a second inductor (L2) is placed in parallel with the output capacitor (C2) to further enhance stability and bandwidth.
