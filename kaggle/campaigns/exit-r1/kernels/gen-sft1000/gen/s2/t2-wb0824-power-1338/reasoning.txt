The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, which is likely caused by the noise contribution from the current-reuse structure and the shared DC path. The anchor netlist uses a shared DC path (via n3) for both the NMOS and PMOS stages, which introduces noise coupling and limits the noise performance. Additionally, the absence of a dedicated noise-suppressing element in the feedback or output path allows noise from the current mirror and DC path to propagate into the signal chain.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n2 (the output of the NMOS stage) and the node n6 (the gate of the PMOS current mirror). This capacitor decouples the noise from the current mirror and DC path, reducing their impact on the signal chain and improving the noise figure. The capacitor also helps to stabilize the feedback loop and reduce noise coupling between stages.
