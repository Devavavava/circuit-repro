The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.29 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the lack of proper noise shielding in the signal path. The NMOS diode NM3 and the PMOS diode PM2 are directly connected to the power rails, introducing noise into the signal path. Additionally, the shared DC path and the absence of a dedicated noise-ground node exacerbate noise coupling.

To address this, I added a capacitor ZC1 between the internal node n6 and n5, which helps decouple the bias network from the signal path, reducing noise coupling. I also added an NMOS transistor ZN1 between n5 and n3, providing a low-noise current path and improving signal integrity by isolating the signal nodes from the power supply. These changes help reduce the noise contribution from the bias network and improve the signal-to-noise ratio, directly addressing the noise figure constraint.
