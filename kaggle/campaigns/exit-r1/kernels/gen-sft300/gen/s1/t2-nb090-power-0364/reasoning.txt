The anchor netlist uses a current-reuse NMOS/PMOS push-pull configuration, which may not be optimal for noise performance in a narrowband LNA. The lack of a noise-canceling or noise-shaping mechanism in the topology contributes to the poor noise figure. Additionally, the bias network is not integrated into the signal path, which can introduce noise and degrade the overall performance.

To address this, I propose a structural change that replaces the current-reuse push-pull configuration with a common-gate (CG) configuration for the input stage, which is known to provide better noise performance. The CG stage offers a low input impedance and a high output impedance, which helps in reducing noise contributions from the input transistor. Additionally, the CG configuration can be paired with a common-source (CS) stage for gain boosting, which helps in achieving the required gain without compromising the noise figure.
