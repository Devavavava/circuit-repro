The failure is primarily due to poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being violated by -0.77 dB. The anchor netlist lacks an explicit input matching network, relying instead on the LC tank at the output of the first stage to provide impedance transformation. However, this approach is insufficient for achieving the required -7.86 dB reflection coefficient at 900 MHz. The absence of a dedicated input matching component results in a mismatch between the source impedance and the input impedance of the first-stage transistor, leading to significant signal reflection and poor performance.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and node n4, which is part of the LC interstage network. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path to ground at the operating frequency, effectively tuning the input impedance of the first stage and improving the input matching. This change enhances the reflection coefficient (s11) by reducing the impedance mismatch at the input port, thereby meeting the s11_max_db constraint.
