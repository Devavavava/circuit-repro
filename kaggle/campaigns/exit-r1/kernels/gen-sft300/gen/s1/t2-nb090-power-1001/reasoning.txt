The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the biasing network and the lack of proper noise cancellation mechanisms in the current-reuse topology. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for biasing, which introduces noise into the signal path and limits the overall noise performance. Additionally, the current-reuse configuration, while beneficial for gain, may not be optimally noise-shaped due to the lack of a noise-canceling feedback path or a noise-isolated biasing scheme.

To address this, I introduce a noise-canceling feedback path by adding a capacitor (ZC2) between the input (VIN1) and the gate of the NMOS transistor (NM1), and a resistor (ZR2) from the gate of NM1 to VSS. This forms a noise-canceling feedback loop that reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network and improves the noise figure. Additionally, I replace the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor (ZR1) from VDD to n5, which isolates the bias network from the signal path and reduces noise injection. Finally, I add a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and n2 to provide a noise-isolated power supply for the output stage, further reducing noise coupling.
