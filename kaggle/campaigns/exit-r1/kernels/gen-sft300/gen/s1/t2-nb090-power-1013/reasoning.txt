The noise figure (nf_db) is the binding constraint, and it is failing by ~1.9 dB. This is likely due to the noise contribution from the second stage, which is not adequately suppressed. In the anchor netlist, the second stage’s gate is driven through a resistor (R3) from the current mirror reference (n7), which introduces noise and degrades the noise figure. Additionally, the lack of a noise-canceling component or a direct connection between stages may be contributing to the elevated noise.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the source of NM3, which helps in reducing the noise contribution from the current mirror reference by providing a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise. I also added a resistor (ZR2) between VDD and n2, which helps stabilize the first stage and reduce noise coupling. Additionally, a resistor (ZR1) was added between VOUT1 and VSS to provide a low-noise path for the output, improving the noise figure. These changes reduce noise coupling and improve the overall noise performance of the LNA.
