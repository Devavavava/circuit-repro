The existing structure relies on the LC tank (L1 and C1) for both input filtering and impedance transformation, but this may not be sufficient to achieve the required match at the center frequency of 900 MHz.

To address this, I introduce a dedicated input matching network by adding a resistor (ZR1) from VDD to n2, which helps in adjusting the input impedance. Additionally, I replace the direct connection of the bias resistor R2 with a capacitor (ZC2) from n7 to n1, allowing AC signals to bypass the resistor and improving the input impedance at the operating frequency. These changes enhance the input matching without introducing additional inductors or complicating the topology.
