The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is failing due to insufficient phase margin across the wideband frequency range. The original circuit employs a resistive shunt-feedback configuration with a shunt-peaked inductive load. While this topology provides good gain and noise figure performance, it lacks sufficient phase margin at higher frequencies, leading to instability in the wideband range. This is exacerbated by the fact that the feedback resistor `R1` sets the DC operating point but does not provide sufficient phase compensation at higher frequencies.

To address this, I introduced a second inductor `L2` and an additional capacitor `C3` to form a resonant feedback path that enhances phase margin at the upper end of the band. The inductor `L2` is connected between the internal node `n1` (the gate of the NMOS transistor) and ground, while the capacitor `C3` is connected between `n2` (the drain of the NMOS transistor) and the internal node `n4`. This additional LC structure introduces a controlled phase shift at higher frequencies, improving the stability factor without compromising gain or noise performance.
