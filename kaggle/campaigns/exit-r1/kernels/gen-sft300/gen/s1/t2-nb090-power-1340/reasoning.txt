The failure is primarily due to the poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being violated by approximately -0.54 dB. This indicates that the input reflection coefficient is too high at the center frequency, likely due to the lack of an explicit input matching network. The original topology relies on the LC tank in the first stage for tuning, but this is insufficient to achieve the required impedance transformation at the input. The absence of an input matching network limits the ability to achieve a low reflection coefficient, especially when the input is DC-blocked by C1 and connected directly to the gate of NM1.

To address this, I added an input matching network consisting of a capacitor (ZC1) and an inductor (ZL1) in parallel with the first stage’s LC tank. This provides a more controlled impedance transformation at the input, improving the reflection coefficient. Additionally, I introduced a shunt capacitor (ZC2) across the second stage's LC tank to help stabilize the output impedance and reduce unwanted coupling between stages, which may have been contributing to the degradation of S21 and S11. The new capacitor ZC2 helps in fine-tuning the output impedance and reducing any parasitic effects that might be degrading the performance.
