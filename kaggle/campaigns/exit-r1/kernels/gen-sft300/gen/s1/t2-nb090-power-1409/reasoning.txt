The binding constraint is the insufficient forward gain (s21_db), which is far below the required 26.5137 dB. This is likely due to the second stage's transconductance being too low or its load being too heavy, which is exacerbated by the LC interstage network not providing sufficient impedance transformation or phase matching at the operating frequency. The LC interstage network (L2 and C2) may be causing a mismatch between the stages, reducing the overall gain.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and n4, which is the node connecting the LC interstage network and the second stage. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, helping to stabilize the interstage network and improve impedance matching between the two stages. This structural change enhances the signal transfer from the first stage to the second, thereby boosting the overall forward gain (s21_db).
