The binding constraint is the input return loss (s11_max_db), which is failing by ~0.59 dB. This indicates that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source at the target frequency (900 MHz), leading to excessive signal reflection. The anchor netlist uses a simple DC-blocked input capacitor (C1) and a tuned inductor (L1) at the drain of the first stage, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation or bandwidth control at the center frequency. The lack of additional impedance-matching elements at the input or interstage nodes likely causes the mismatch, especially in a narrowband design where precise impedance control is critical.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (ZC1) from the interstage node (n4) to VDD, which helps in fine-tuning the impedance at the interstage point and improves the input matching by providing an additional degree of freedom in the impedance network. This structural change enhances the input reflection coefficient by allowing better control over the phase and magnitude of the input impedance, thereby improving s11_max_db.
