The failure is primarily due to poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint failing by ~0.73 dB. The anchor circuit lacks an explicit input matching network, relying instead on the LC tank in the first stage to provide impedance transformation. However, this is insufficient to achieve the required -7.8525 dB S11 max at 900 MHz, especially when combined with the high-gain and low-noise objectives. The input impedance is not adequately controlled, leading to a higher-than-allowed reflection coefficient at the desired frequency.

To address this, I added an explicit input matching network using a series inductor and a shunt capacitor (L4 and C2), which allows for precise tuning of the input impedance at the operating frequency. Additionally, I introduced a shunt capacitor (ZC1) between the first stage's drain and the second stage's LC interstage network to reduce unwanted coupling and improve isolation, which helps in achieving better S11 performance. A shunt capacitor (ZC2) between the second stage's drain and ground helps in stabilizing the output impedance and further improves the input matching by reducing parasitic feedback paths.
