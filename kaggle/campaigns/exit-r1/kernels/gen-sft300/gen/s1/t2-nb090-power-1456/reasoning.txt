The binding constraint is the minimum stability factor (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`), which is failing by -0.034 due to poor phase margin across the band. The current topology's stability is limited by the shared DC path and the lack of a dedicated feedback mechanism to ensure robust phase margin at all frequencies. The absence of a direct feedback path from the output to the input or a dedicated stabilization capacitor in the signal path leads to insufficient phase compensation, especially at the edges of the band.

To address this, I added a capacitor `ZC1` between the internal node `n5` (which is connected to the gate of NM3 and the drain of NM3) and the output node `VOUT1`. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, effectively introducing a zero in the feedback loop that improves the phase margin. This change enhances the stability factor across the band without degrading the noise figure or gain, as it does not introduce additional noise or loss in the signal path.
