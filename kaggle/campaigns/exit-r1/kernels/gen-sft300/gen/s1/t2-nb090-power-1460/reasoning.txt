The noise figure (nf_db) is the binding constraint, failing by approximately 1.06 dB. This is likely due to the noise contributions from the second stage and the biasing network not being adequately suppressed. The current topology uses a current-mirror bias network, which introduces noise and degrades the noise figure. Additionally, the lack of a proper noise-canceling component in the second stage may be contributing to the elevated noise floor.

To address this, I added a noise-canceling capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the drain of the first stage (n2), which helps reduce the noise contribution from the power supply and improves the noise figure. I also replaced the current-mirror bias network with a simpler resistor-based biasing scheme by adding a resistor (Rrep1) between the output (VOUT1) and the bias node (n7), which reduces the noise contribution from the bias network. These changes improve the noise figure by isolating the sensitive amplifier stages from noise sources and simplifying the biasing to reduce noise coupling.
