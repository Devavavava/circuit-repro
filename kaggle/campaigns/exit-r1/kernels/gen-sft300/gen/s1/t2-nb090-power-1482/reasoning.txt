The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contributions from the biasing network and the lack of noise isolation between stages. The current anchor netlist uses a current-mirror bias network that introduces noise through the resistors R2 and R3 and the diode-connected NM3. This configuration increases the noise floor, especially at the input stage, and reduces the overall noise figure. Additionally, the absence of noise isolation between the two stages allows noise from the second stage to couple back into the first, degrading the NF further.

To address this, I added a noise-canceling feedback path from the output of the second stage (n6) to the bias reference node (n7) using a capacitor (ZC1). This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise to ground, effectively suppressing noise from the second stage and reducing its contribution to the input stage. This change improves the noise figure by isolating the stages and reducing the noise from the bias network.
