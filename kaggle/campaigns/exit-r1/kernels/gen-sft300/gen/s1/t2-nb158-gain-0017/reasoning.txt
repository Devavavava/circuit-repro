The binding constraint is the stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`), which is failing due to insufficient phase margin across the band. The anchor circuit uses a resistive shunt-feedback configuration with a shunt-peaked inductive load, which, while effective for gain and noise figure, may not provide sufficient phase margin at the band edges due to the feedback topology and the inductive load's frequency-dependent behavior. This leads to instability in the wideband metric.

To address this, I propose replacing the resistive shunt-feedback configuration with a common-gate (CG) stage in parallel with a common-source (CS) stage, forming a two-stage topology with a capacitive coupling between stages. This configuration enhances phase margin by introducing a zero in the feedback path and reducing the Miller effect on the CS stage, which helps stabilize the amplifier over a wide frequency range. Additionally, adding a second inductor in the output path (L2) and a PMOS transistor (PM1) in a cascode configuration provides further impedance transformation and helps manage the output impedance for better stability and bandwidth extension.
