The failure is primarily due to insufficient gain (s21_db) in the current-reuse push-pull configuration. The anchor netlist uses a shared DC path with a tuned load (L2+C2), but the gain is limited by the lossy nature of the shared DC path and the current-reuse topology, which introduces additional noise and impedance mismatches. The NMOS/PMOS push-pull configuration also suffers from poor impedance matching at the output, leading to a suboptimal gain.

To address this, I replaced the shared DC path with a separate DC path for the PMOS transistor, effectively decoupling the output stage from the input and reducing impedance mismatches. This is achieved by connecting the PMOS transistor (PM2) to a separate internal node (x1) instead of the shared DC path (n3). This change improves the output impedance matching and reduces signal loss, thereby boosting the gain. Additionally, I removed the NMOS diode (NM3) and the resistor R1, which were contributing to noise and impedance mismatches, and replaced them with a direct connection from the PMOS gate (n6) to the new internal node (x1), simplifying the biasing and reducing noise contributions.
