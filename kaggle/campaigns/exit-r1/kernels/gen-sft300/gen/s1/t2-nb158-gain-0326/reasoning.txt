The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) of the two-stage LNA, which is insufficient across the wide frequency band. The binding constraint is failing because the interstage LC network and the lack of a feedback mechanism are not sufficiently suppressing oscillations outside the narrowband center frequency (f0). The current topology lacks a direct feedback path from the output to the input or a stabilizing component that can dampen parasitic oscillations across the wide band.

To address this, I added a feedback path from VOUT1 to the gate of the second-stage transistor (NM2) by introducing a capacitor (ZC1) and a resistor (ZR1), and also added a resistor (ZR2) from VDD to the drain of the first-stage transistor (NM1). These changes provide a stabilizing feedback mechanism and help balance the gain and stability trade-off across the wide frequency range.
