The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` is failing due to insufficient stability margin across the wide frequency band. The original topology relies on LC resonances for tuning and impedance matching, which may not provide adequate phase margin or feedback suppression over the entire 0.01–50 GHz range. This leads to potential oscillations or instability at off-band frequencies, causing the stability factor (`mu`) to dip below the required minimum. The two-stage structure with LC interstage coupling may not sufficiently suppress feedback paths or dampen resonant modes outside the narrowband center frequency.

To address this, I added a feedback suppression element: a resistor `ZR1` connecting VDD to node `n2` (the drain of NM1), and another resistor `ZR2` connecting node `n7` (the bias node) to VOUT1. These resistors provide additional damping and suppress unwanted feedback paths, particularly at off-band frequencies, thereby improving the stability margin over the wide frequency range.
