The binding constraint is the insufficient forward gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second stage's inability to provide sufficient amplification at the target frequency. This is likely due to the second-stage NMOS (NM2) being underdriven or having poor transconductance at f0, possibly due to the interstage LC network (L2 and C2) not properly matching or coupling the signal from the first stage to the second. Additionally, the lack of source degeneration or impedance control in the second stage may be causing signal loss or instability.

To address this, I introduce a source degeneration inductance (L4) in the second stage, connecting from VSS to the source of NM1 (the first stage NMOS). This adds a controlled impedance at the input of the first stage, improving its transconductance and stability while allowing the second stage to operate with better signal coupling and amplification. This change enhances the overall gain without compromising the noise figure or stability.
