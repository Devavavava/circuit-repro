The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is below the required 22.4403 dB by ~0.23 dB. This is likely due to the second stage's drain load being too weak at the operating frequency, as the LC tank (L3) is resonant at f0 and thus presents a high impedance only at that frequency. However, the second stage's gain is also limited by the input impedance mismatch and the lack of a direct feedback path to stabilize and enhance gain.

To address this, adding a shunt capacitor (ZC1) from VDD to the second stage's source (n5) introduces a low-impedance path at DC and high frequencies, which helps stabilize the second stage and improve its transconductance by reducing the effective source impedance. This enhances the gain of the second stage without compromising the narrowband tuning at f0.
