The failure is due to insufficient gain in the second stage, as evidenced by the s21_db margin being -0.304591, which indicates the achieved gain of 18.43 dB is below the required 26.50 dB. The two-stage topology with LC-tuned loads is inherently limited in gain due to the impedance mismatch between the stages and the high impedance of the inductive loads. The LC interstage network (C2 and L2) provides good isolation and matching but does not sufficiently amplify the signal due to the low transconductance of the second stage and the high impedance of the inductive load.

To address this, I replaced the second stage's inductive load (L3) with a capacitive load (ZC1) connected to VDD. This change allows for a lower impedance at the output of the second stage, increasing the transconductance contribution to the overall gain. The capacitive load also helps in maintaining a better impedance match at the center frequency (1.575 GHz), which enhances the signal transfer efficiency from the second stage to the output. The addition of a new capacitor (ZC1) and the removal of L3 is a structural change that improves the gain by reducing the impedance mismatch and enabling a more effective signal amplification in the second stage.
