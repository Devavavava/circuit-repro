The binding constraint failing is `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which measures the minimum stability factor over a wide frequency range. The negative margin indicates instability outside the narrowband design frequency, likely due to poor impedance matching and feedback paths in the original topology. The two-stage LC-tuned structure introduces resonant feedback at off-band frequencies, reducing stability. Additionally, the lack of a direct DC path from the output to ground in the original netlist may contribute to instability by allowing unintended feedback paths.

To address this, I added a direct DC path from the output to ground by introducing a capacitor `ZC1` between `VOUT1` and `VSS`. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path at DC, preventing unwanted feedback and improving stability across the wideband range. It also helps in isolating the output from ground at RF frequencies, reducing the risk of resonant feedback that could destabilize the LNA.
