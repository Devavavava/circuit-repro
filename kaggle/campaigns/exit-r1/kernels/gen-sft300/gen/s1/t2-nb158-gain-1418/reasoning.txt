The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second stage's inability to provide enough amplification. The second stage's gate is driven by a DC-blocked LC network (C2 and L2), which introduces a phase shift and impedance mismatch at the center frequency, reducing the gain. Additionally, the second stage's drain load (L3) is a pure inductor, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation at the operating frequency, leading to a lower voltage gain.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the internal node n5, which is the source of the second stage (NM2). This provides a low-impedance path for AC signals at the operating frequency, effectively increasing the transconductance of the second stage by reducing the source impedance seen by the gate. This improves the voltage gain of the second stage, thereby boosting the overall gain (s21_db) of the amplifier.
