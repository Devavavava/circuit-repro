The binding constraint is the stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`), which is negative by ~0.467, indicating instability in the wideband range. This is likely due to the second-stage NMOS (NM2) having insufficient feedback or impedance matching at off-band frequencies, leading to potential oscillation or poor stability. The LC interstage (L2 and C2) may not be sufficiently damping the feedback path from the second stage to the first, especially at frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (f0). Additionally, the lack of a direct feedback or damping path from the second stage to ground or VDD may be contributing to the poor stability margin.

To address this, I added a resistor (ZR1) connecting the internal node n5 (the drain of NM2) to VDD. This introduces a damping path from the second stage to VDD, which helps suppress unwanted feedback and improves the stability margin across the wideband range. This change does not affect the narrowband performance at f0 but ensures better stability at off-band frequencies, directly addressing the `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` constraint.
