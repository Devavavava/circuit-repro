To address this, I introduce a new NMOS transistor (NM2) connected between the internal node n5 (which is tied to the gate of NM3) and the internal node n4 (which is connected to the tuned load L2+C2). This addition provides a local feedback path that helps to counteract the destabilizing feedback from the NM3/PM2 path. The new transistor NM2 acts as a phase-shifting element, improving the stability margin by altering the feedback characteristics of the circuit. This change does not affect the noise figure, gain, or power consumption significantly, but it enhances the stability of the amplifier across a wide frequency range.
