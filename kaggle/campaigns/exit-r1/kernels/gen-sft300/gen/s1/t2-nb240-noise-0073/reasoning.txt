The failure is due to insufficient gain (s21_db) in the current-reuse push-pull LNA topology. The binding constraint is s21_db, which is too low by ~0.6 dB. The original topology uses a current-reuse configuration with a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2), but the gain is limited by the output impedance mismatch between the NMOS and PMOS stages and the load. The lack of a direct current path from the output to the load and the absence of a dedicated output node for the PMOS stage reduces the effective transconductance and load coupling, leading to poor gain.

To address this, I propose a structural change: adding a direct connection from the output of the PMOS stage (n6) to the output node (VOUT1) via an additional capacitor (ZC1), and also adding a direct connection from the output of the NMOS stage (n2) to the output node (VOUT1) via a resistor (ZR2). This change introduces a dedicated output path for both stages, improving the load coupling and transconductance contribution from both the NMOS and PMOS transistors, thereby boosting the overall gain (s21_db).
