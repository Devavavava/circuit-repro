The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is failing due to insufficient phase margin across the band. The anchor netlist employs a resistive shunt-feedback configuration with a shunt-peaked inductive load, which limits the phase margin at higher frequencies. This topology introduces a second pole in the feedback loop, which, when combined with the inductive load, causes instability at the edges of the band. The lack of a dedicated stability-enhancing element, such as a series inductor or a second feedback path, exacerbates this issue.

To address this, I introduce a series inductor `L3` in the feedback path between the gate of the NMOS transistor and the input node. This adds a zero to the feedback loop, which improves the phase margin at higher frequencies. Additionally, a resistor `R3` is added between the input node and ground to provide a DC path for the feedback current and improve input matching. These structural changes enhance stability without significantly degrading gain or noise performance.
