The binding constraint is the gain (s21_db), which is too low by ~0.48 dB. This is likely due to the current-reuse topology and the shared DC path in the anchor netlist. The shared DC path introduces parasitic elements and increases the signal path impedance, reducing the achievable gain. Additionally, the current-reuse configuration may not be effectively boosting the gain due to the load configuration and the lack of proper impedance matching at the output.

To address this, I restructured the circuit to remove the shared DC path and reconfigure the load to improve gain and impedance matching. I replaced the shared DC path with a direct connection from the output of the NMOS transistor (n2) to the output node (VOUT1), and connected the load (L2 and C2) directly to VDD and VSS. This change reduces parasitic effects and improves the signal path. I also removed the PMOS current mirror (PM2 and R4) to simplify the biasing and reduce the complexity of the current-reuse configuration. Finally, I added a direct connection from the output of the PMOS transistor (n3) to the load node (n4), which helps in achieving better impedance matching and gain.
