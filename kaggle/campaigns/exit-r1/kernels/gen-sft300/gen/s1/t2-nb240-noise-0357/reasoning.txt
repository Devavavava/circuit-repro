The failure is due to the poor stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) caused by the lack of a feedback or damping mechanism in the interstage LC network. The LC interstage (C2 and L2) introduces a resonance that may amplify unwanted feedback paths, especially at frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (f0). This resonance can destabilize the circuit, reducing the stability margin below the required threshold.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output of the second stage (n6) to the drain of the current mirror reference transistor (NM3) via a capacitor (ZC1) and a resistor (ZR1). This introduces a low-frequency feedback path that damps the resonance and improves the stability margin. Additionally, a resistor (ZR2) was added from the biasing node (n7) to the drain of the first stage transistor (NM1) to provide a small amount of source degeneration, which helps in stabilizing the first stage and reducing noise contributions.
