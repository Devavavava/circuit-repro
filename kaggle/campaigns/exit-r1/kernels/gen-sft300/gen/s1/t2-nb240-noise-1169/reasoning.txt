The binding constraint is the insufficient forward gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second-stage transistor NM2's inability to provide enough amplification due to its low transconductance and poor impedance matching at the drain. The LC interstage network (L2 and C2) is not effectively transferring the signal from the first stage to the second, leading to a significant loss in gain. Additionally, the second stage's drain load (L3) is not optimally tuned to resonate at the operating frequency, resulting in a mismatch and reduced gain.

To address this, I replaced the LC interstage network with a capacitive coupling (C2) directly between the first-stage drain (n2) and the second-stage gate (n5), effectively removing the inductor L2. This change improves signal transfer between stages by reducing the impedance mismatch and allowing for a more direct signal path. Additionally, I replaced the series inductor L3 with a parallel inductor (L3) connected between VDD and the second-stage drain (n6), which better resonates with the load at the operating frequency and improves impedance matching. The addition of a resistor (Rrep1) between the second-stage drain (n6) and the interstage node (n4) provides a controlled feedback path, stabilizing the second stage and improving its transconductance. These changes collectively enhance the gain of the second stage and improve the overall forward gain of the LNA.
