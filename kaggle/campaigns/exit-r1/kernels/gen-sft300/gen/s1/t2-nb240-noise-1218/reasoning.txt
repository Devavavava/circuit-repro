The binding constraint is the insufficient forward gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second stage's inability to maintain sufficient voltage swing due to the lack of a proper DC bias path and the absence of a current mirror for output impedance control. The second stage's drain is directly connected to VDD through L3, which may cause a voltage drop and reduce the available signal swing, especially at high frequencies. Additionally, the output is DC-blocked by C3, but the lack of a pull-up or active load at VOUT1 may lead to poor signal transmission and reduced gain.

To address this, I added a current mirror (PMOS ZP1 and NMOS ZN1) and a pull-up resistor (Rrep1) at the output stage. The current mirror provides a low-impedance path for the output signal, improving the voltage gain and ensuring a stable DC operating point. The pull-up resistor Rrep1 helps maintain a proper DC bias at VOUT1, preventing signal loss due to insufficient voltage headroom. The addition of ZP1 and ZN1 also helps stabilize the output impedance and improve the overall gain.
