The binding constraint is the stability factor (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is failing due to poor stability across the wide frequency range. The original topology lacks sufficient feedback or impedance control to maintain stability, especially at off-band frequencies. The LC interstage and tuned loads, while beneficial for narrowband performance, do not inherently provide the necessary phase or amplitude margin for stability over a wide band.

To address this, I added a resistor (ZR2) from VDD to node n4, which is the node connecting the interstage LC network. This resistor introduces a controlled impedance path that helps dampen oscillations and improve the phase margin across the frequency range. Additionally, I added a capacitor (ZC2) between VDD and node n5, which helps to stabilize the second stage by providing a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, reducing unwanted feedback and improving stability. These changes enhance the stability factor without significantly impacting the noise figure, gain, or input/output matching.
