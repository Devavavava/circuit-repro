The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_db), which is too high due to the poor noise performance of the current-reuse topology in the anchor netlist. The noise is dominated by the noise contributions from the NMOS and PMOS transistors, particularly NM3 and PM2, which are used for biasing and current setting. These devices add significant noise due to their low impedance and high current flow, especially in the absence of effective noise cancellation mechanisms. The structure also lacks a noise-canceling feedback path, leading to a higher overall noise floor.

To address this, I restructured the circuit by replacing the diode-connected NM3 with a NMOS transistor (NM3) connected to an internal node (n2) instead of VSS, which allows for better noise cancellation by sharing the noise path with the main amplifier stage. Additionally, I introduced a resistor (ZR1) from VSS to n5 to provide a controlled current path for NM3, reducing its noise contribution. The PMOS transistor PM2 is now connected to a different internal node (n6), and a resistor (ZR2) from VDD to n6 helps stabilize the bias current and reduce noise. These changes improve the noise figure by reducing the noise contributions from the biasing network and enabling better noise cancellation through shared noise paths.
