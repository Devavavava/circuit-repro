The failure is due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the LC interstage network (C2 and L2), which introduces resonant feedback that destabilizes the amplifier over a wide frequency range. This feedback reduces the phase margin at frequencies beyond the design band, leading to a negative stability margin.

To address this, I restructured the LC interstage network by replacing the series inductor L2 with a shunt inductor connected to a new internal node (n8), and added a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the output node (n6) to improve the stability margin. This change suppresses the resonant feedback by decoupling the interstage LC network from the output, thereby increasing the phase margin at higher frequencies.
