The binding constraint `s21_db` is failing by ~0.72 dB, indicating insufficient gain at the design frequency (2.4 GHz). This is likely due to poor impedance matching and/or signal loss in the signal path, particularly in the current-reuse configuration. The anchor netlist uses a shared DC path for the NMOS and PMOS stages, which introduces impedance mismatches and signal degradation at high frequencies. Additionally, the output is loaded directly by a resonant LC network (L2+C2), which may not provide optimal impedance transformation at the operating frequency.

To address this, I replaced the shared DC path with a dedicated inductor (L2) connecting the NMOS drain (n2) to the output node (n4), and added an additional capacitor (C3) between the input node (n1) and a new internal node (x1). This reconfigures the signal path to reduce impedance mismatches and improve the impedance transformation at the operating frequency. The new capacitor C3 helps in decoupling the input node from the resonant LC load, ensuring a more stable and efficient signal transfer. This structural change should enhance the gain at 2.4 GHz by improving impedance matching and reducing signal loss in the critical path.
