The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the lack of a feedback path or damping mechanism in the two-stage LNA. This results in poor phase and gain stability across the wide frequency range, particularly at the band edges, leading to instability and a negative margin for the stability factor.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output to the input stage by connecting a capacitor (ZC1) from VDD to node n2 and another capacitor (ZC2) from VOUT1 to VDD. This introduces a controlled feedback mechanism that helps stabilize the circuit over the wide frequency range. Additionally, I added a resistor (ZR2) from node n2 to VSS and a resistor (ZR3) from VOUT1 to VDD to provide additional damping and control over the feedback loop, ensuring stability without degrading the gain or noise figure.
