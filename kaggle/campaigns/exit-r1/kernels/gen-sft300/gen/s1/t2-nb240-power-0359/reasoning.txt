The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the poor transconductance and output impedance of the NMOS transistor NM1 due to the shunt-peaked load configuration. The resistive shunt feedback and the inductor-based peaking network reduce the effective transconductance and load impedance at the operating frequency, leading to a lower-than-required gain. This is exacerbated by the lack of a proper DC biasing network and the absence of a second transistor stage to amplify the signal further.

To address this, a common-gate (CG) stage is added in parallel with the original common-source (CS) stage. This CG stage provides a low input impedance and high output impedance, which helps increase the overall transconductance and output impedance of the LNA. The CG stage also helps to isolate the CS stage from the load, improving the gain and stability. Additionally, a second NMOS transistor (NM2) is added to provide a second stage of amplification, and a resistor (R3) is added to bias the CG stage properly. These changes improve the gain and meet the required s21_db constraint.
