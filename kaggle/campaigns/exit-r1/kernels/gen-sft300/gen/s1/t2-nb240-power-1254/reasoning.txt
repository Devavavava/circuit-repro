The failure is primarily due to insufficient gain in the second stage of the two-stage LNA. The achieved S21 is 17.27 dB, which is 0.4 dB below the required minimum of 28.92 dB. This indicates that the second stage is not contributing enough gain, likely due to the load impedance seen by NM2 being too low or the transconductance of NM2 being insufficient for the required gain.

To address this, I added a PMOS transistor (ZP1) and an inductor (ZL1) to create a current mirror that provides a higher impedance load for NM2, thereby increasing its effective transconductance and the overall gain of the second stage. The PMOS transistor is connected to the drain of NM2 (n6), and the inductor ZL1 is connected from n6 to VDD, forming a high-impedance load. This change increases the gain contribution from the second stage, helping to meet the required S21 specification.
