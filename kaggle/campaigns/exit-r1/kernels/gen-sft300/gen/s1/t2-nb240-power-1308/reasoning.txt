The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) across the wideband range, which indicates the circuit is unstable outside the narrowband center frequency (f0). This is likely caused by the LC interstage network (L2 and C2) creating a resonant feedback path that destabilizes the system at off-center frequencies. The current topology lacks a mechanism to suppress this feedback, leading to a negative stability margin.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the biasing node (n7) and the interstage LC network (n4), and another capacitor (ZC2) between the biasing node (n7) and the second stage's source (n5). These capacitors provide a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, reducing the feedback effect from the LC interstage network and improving the stability margin across the wideband range.
