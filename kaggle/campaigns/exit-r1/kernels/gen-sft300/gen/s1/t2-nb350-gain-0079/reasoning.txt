The failure is primarily due to insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the current-reuse topology and the load configuration in the anchor netlist. The tuned load L2+C2, while effective for impedance matching, may not be optimally configured to maximize gain in this specific frequency band. Additionally, the current-reuse structure introduces additional noise and impedance mismatches that degrade the gain and noise figure, especially at the target frequency of 3.5 GHz.

To address this, I propose a structural change that replaces the current-reuse push-pull configuration with a simpler common-gate stage for the NMOS transistor, which is known to provide higher gain and better noise performance at high frequencies. This change eliminates the PMOS current-reuse path and simplifies the load configuration to a single inductor, which improves impedance matching and gain. This structural change is expected to increase the s21_db by approximately 3 dB, addressing the binding constraint.
