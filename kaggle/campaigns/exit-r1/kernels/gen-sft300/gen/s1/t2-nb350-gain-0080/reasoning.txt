The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the poor transconductance and impedance mismatch in the current-reuse topology. The NMOS and PMOS stages are not optimally coupled, and the tuned load (L2+C2) is not effectively boosting the gain at the target frequency. The current-reuse structure introduces unnecessary parasitic effects and impedance mismatches that reduce the overall gain and stability.

To address this, I restructured the topology to use a cascode configuration with a common-gate stage, which improves the transconductance and impedance matching. I also replaced the current-reuse diode-connected NMOS and PMOS with a simpler NMOS and PMOS configuration, and simplified the load network to a direct inductor connection to the output, which enhances the gain and bandwidth. These changes improve the gain at the target frequency and ensure better stability and noise performance.
