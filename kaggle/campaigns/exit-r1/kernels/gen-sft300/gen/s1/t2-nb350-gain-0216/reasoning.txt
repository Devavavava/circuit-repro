The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the current-reuse architecture and the shared DC path in the anchor netlist. The shared DC path and the complex biasing network (NM3, R1, R2, R4, R5, PM2) introduce parasitic effects and degrade the signal path, particularly at the operating frequency of 3.5 GHz. The current-reuse configuration, while effective in reducing power consumption, may not provide sufficient gain in this context due to the interaction between the NMOS and PMOS stages and the load network (L2 and C2).

To address this, the structural change involves simplifying the biasing network and reconfiguring the load to improve signal integrity and gain. This is achieved by removing the shared DC path and biasing network components (NM3, R2, R4, R5, PM2) and replacing them with a direct connection from VDD to n6, which simplifies the signal path and reduces parasitic interactions. Additionally, the load is restructured to use a single inductor (L2) directly connected to n2 and VSS, while the capacitor (C2) is moved to connect n4 to n6, which helps in stabilizing the output and improving gain.
