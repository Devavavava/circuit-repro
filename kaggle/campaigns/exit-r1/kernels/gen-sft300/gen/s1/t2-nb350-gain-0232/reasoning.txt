The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the poor transconductance and poor impedance matching in the current-reuse topology. The NMOS and PMOS stages are not effectively amplifying the signal due to poor biasing and impedance mismatch, particularly at the output node, which is loaded with a resonant LC network (L2+C2). This load is not effectively driving the output due to poor impedance transformation and signal loss in the current-reuse configuration.

To address this, I replaced the current-reuse configuration with a cascode topology, which improves gain and impedance matching. The NMOS transistor NM3 is now connected to the resonant LC network (L2+C2), and its drain is connected to a new node n4, which is tied to the output through a capacitor C3. This change ensures that the resonant load is directly coupled to the output, improving signal transfer and gain. The PMOS transistor PM2 is now connected to a new node n6, which is tied to the output through a resistor R5, ensuring proper biasing and impedance matching. Additionally, a new capacitor ZC1 is added between VDD and n4, which helps in stabilizing the output and improving the overall gain by reducing signal loss at the resonant frequency.
