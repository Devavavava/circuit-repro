The binding constraint is the stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`), which is failing by ~0.16 due to poor stability in the wideband range. This is likely caused by the LC interstage resonant network (L2 and C2) creating a narrowband resonance that destabilizes the amplifier outside the design band, particularly in the lower and upper edges of the 0.01–50 GHz range. This resonance may be causing a phase shift that reduces the stability margin.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (`ZC1`) between VDD and the interstage node (n4), which helps dampen the resonant peaking and improve stability. Additionally, I added a shunt inductor (`ZL1`) between the output node (n6) and VDD to suppress high-frequency oscillations. These changes help broaden the stability bandwidth and reduce the resonant effects that are degrading the stability margin.
