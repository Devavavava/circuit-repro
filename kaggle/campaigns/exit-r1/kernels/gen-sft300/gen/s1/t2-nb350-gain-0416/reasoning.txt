Additionally, the absence of a source degeneration inductor or a more robust biasing scheme may limit the second stage's ability to amplify the signal effectively.

To address this, I propose a structural change that introduces a source degeneration inductor in the second stage to improve the transconductance and stabilize the biasing. This is achieved by adding a new inductor, ZL1, connecting the source of NM2 to VDD, and modifying the biasing network to include a resistor, Rrep1, connecting VOUT1 to the drain of NM3.
