The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` is failing due to poor stability margins across the wide frequency band, which is likely caused by the LC interstage network and the lack of a direct feedback path from the output to the input. This results in a weak suppression of oscillations at off-band frequencies, leading to a low stability factor (`mu`) at the lower end of the band. The current topology relies heavily on the LC tank for impedance matching and gain, but it lacks a mechanism to actively stabilize the system over a broader range.

To address this, I added a feedback capacitor `ZC1` from the output node `VOUT1` to the internal node `n5` (the gate of the second-stage transistor), and a resistor `ZR1` from `VDD` to the output node `VOUT1`. The capacitor introduces a feedback path that helps suppress oscillations at off-band frequencies, thereby improving the stability factor over the wide frequency range. The resistor provides a controlled load to the output, reducing the risk of instability caused by a purely capacitive load.
