The binding constraint is the gain (s21_db), which is far below the required minimum. The anchor netlist employs a current-reuse push-pull configuration with a tuned load (L2+C2), but the gain is insufficient due to poor signal coupling and high loss in the output path. The output capacitor C3 is directly connected to the shared node n2, which is also the drain of NM1 and the source of PM1. This configuration likely introduces significant signal loss and poor impedance matching at the output, limiting the gain.

To address this, I restructured the output path by moving the tuned load (L2+C2) to a separate node (n5) and connecting the output capacitor C3 to this new node. This change isolates the output path from the shared node n2, reducing signal loss and improving impedance matching. Additionally, I reconnected the PM2 diode to the new output node (n5), ensuring that the current-reuse mechanism is more effectively coupled to the output. This structural change enhances signal transmission and impedance matching, directly addressing the gain limitation.
