The binding constraint is the insufficient forward gain (s21_db), which fell short of the required 29.3269 dB by approximately 0.3447 dB. This indicates that the second stage's transconductance or gain contribution is not sufficient to meet the target. The original topology uses a two-stage common-source amplifier with LC-tuned loads and an interstage LC network. While this structure is effective for noise and matching, it may not provide enough gain due to the second stage's drain load being too high-impedance (L3) at the operating frequency, limiting the voltage swing and transconductance contribution.

To address this, I added a source degeneration inductor (L4) in the second stage, connecting the source of NM2 to an internal node (n8) and grounding it through L4. This introduces a controlled impedance at the source, which can increase the transconductance of the second stage by reducing the effective source impedance and allowing for a larger voltage swing at the output. This change enhances the second stage's gain contribution, thereby improving the overall forward gain (s21_db) to meet the specification.
