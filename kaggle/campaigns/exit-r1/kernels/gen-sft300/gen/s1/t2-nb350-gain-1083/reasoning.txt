The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which measures the stability of the LNA across a wide frequency range. The current design fails this constraint by a margin of -0.9828, indicating insufficient stability at frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (3.5 GHz). The root cause is likely due to the lack of a dedicated stability-enhancing element in the feedback path or the absence of a mechanism to suppress oscillations at off-band frequencies.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor `ZC1` between VDD and the internal node `n4`, which is the drain of the second-stage transistor `NM2`. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, helping to dampen oscillations and improve stability across the wide frequency range. By introducing this element, the feedback loop is effectively modified to reduce the likelihood of instability at off-band frequencies, directly addressing the `mu_min_wide` constraint.
