The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the lack of a direct feedback path from the output to the input stage. The original topology relies on the interstage LC network for impedance matching and gain, but this configuration does not provide sufficient phase compensation over a wide frequency range, leading to a stability margin that falls below the required threshold. The absence of a direct feedback path from the output to the input stage results in poor phase margin at higher frequencies, especially in the 0.01–50 GHz band.

To address this, I added a direct feedback path from the output (VOUT1) to the first stage's input (n1) through a resistor (Rrep1) and a capacitor (ZC2) in parallel with the input capacitor (C1). This introduces a controlled feedback mechanism that enhances the phase margin across the band, ensuring that the stability metric meets the required specification.
