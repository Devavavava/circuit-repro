The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which measures the stability of the LNA over a wide frequency range. The negative margin indicates that the circuit is unstable at some frequency within this band, likely due to the second stage's feedback path or poor phase margin caused by the LC resonant structures. The LC interstage network (L2 and C2) and the second-stage load (L3) may be creating a resonant feedback path that reduces the stability margin.

To address this, I added a resistor (ZR1) from the gate of NM3 (the current mirror reference transistor) to VDD. This resistor provides a small additional feedback path that helps dampen any resonant oscillations and improves the stability margin over the wide frequency range. The resistor does not interfere with the biasing or the gain of the circuit but adds a small amount of damping to the feedback loop, preventing instability.
