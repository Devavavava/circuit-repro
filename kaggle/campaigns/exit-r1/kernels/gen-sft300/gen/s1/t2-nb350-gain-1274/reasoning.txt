The binding constraint is the gain (s21_db), which is insufficient by ~0.34 dB. The two-stage topology, while effective for noise and matching, suffers from a gain degradation due to the second stage's drain inductor (L3) and the interstage LC network (C2 and L2). These components introduce a resonant frequency that may not align perfectly with the operating frequency, leading to a reduction in the overall gain at f0. Additionally, the second stage's gate is driven through the LC interstage network, which can introduce phase shifts and impedance mismatches, further reducing the gain.

To address this, I propose a structural change that enhances the second stage's gain by introducing a direct DC-blocking capacitor (ZC1) from the second stage's drain to the VDD node, bypassing the LC load. This ensures a more stable and higher impedance path for the signal, reducing the impact of the LC network on the gain. Additionally, I replace the NMOS current mirror reference (NM3) with a PMOS current mirror (PM1) and add a PMOS device (PM2) to provide a more stable bias current. This change improves the biasing stability and reduces the impact of the LC network on the gain by providing a more robust current reference.
