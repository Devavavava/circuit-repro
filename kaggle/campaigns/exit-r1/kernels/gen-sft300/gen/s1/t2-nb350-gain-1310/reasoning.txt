The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` failed due to insufficient stability margin across a wide frequency range, particularly at frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (f0=3.5 GHz). The original two-stage LNA structure, while optimized for gain and noise figure at f0, lacks sufficient stabilization mechanisms to suppress oscillations or instability at off-band frequencies. This is likely due to the absence of a feedback path or damping element that would help maintain stability over a broader spectrum.

To address this, I added a small feedback path from the output of the second stage (n6) to the bias reference network (n7) via a capacitor (ZC2) and a resistor (ZR1) to ground. This introduces a low-frequency feedback mechanism that helps stabilize the circuit at frequencies outside the narrowband operation, improving the stability margin over the entire 0.01–50 GHz range. Additionally, I added a resistor (ZR3) from the bias reference network (n7) to the output of the first stage (n2), which helps dampen oscillations and improve the phase margin.
