The failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the lack of a stabilizing element in the feedback path. The original topology lacks a direct connection between the output and the bias network, leading to a weak feedback mechanism that fails to suppress oscillations across the wide frequency band. This results in the stability margin falling below the required threshold.

To address this, I added a direct connection from the output (VOUT1) to the bias network (node n7) by adding a capacitor (ZC1) between VOUT1 and VDD, and a capacitor (ZC2) between VDD and the bias network (n7). This creates a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals to return to the bias network, enhancing the feedback and improving the stability margin. Additionally, I introduced a resistor (ZR1) from the output (VOUT1) to the bias network (n7), which helps in shaping the frequency response and further stabilizing the circuit.
