The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` failed due to insufficient stability margin across the wide frequency band. This is likely caused by the second-stage NMOS (NM2) having a weak feedback path to the source, leading to poor phase margin and instability at frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (f0 = 3.5 GHz). The lack of a direct feedback path from the output to the source of NM2 limits the phase compensation, especially at higher frequencies, causing the stability factor (mu) to dip below the required minimum.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output of the second stage (n6) to the source of NM2 (n5) via a capacitor (ZC2). This creates a low-impedance path at high frequencies, improving phase margin and enhancing stability across the wide band. This change introduces a new feedback mechanism that stabilizes the second stage without compromising gain or noise figure.
