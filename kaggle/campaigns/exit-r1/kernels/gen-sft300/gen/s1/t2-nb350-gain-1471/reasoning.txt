The failure is primarily driven by the noise figure (nf_db), which exceeds the specification by ~0.266 dB. This is likely due to the noise contribution from the current-reuse configuration and the biasing network, particularly the NMOS diode NM3 and the resistors R1, R2, and R5. These components introduce additional noise and degrade the noise performance, especially at the operating frequency of 3.5 GHz. The current-reuse topology, while beneficial for gain and power efficiency, may not be optimal for noise in this configuration without careful noise shaping and impedance matching.

To address this, I propose a structural change that simplifies the biasing network and reduces noise contributions. The revised netlist removes the NM3 diode and associated resistors (R1, R2, R5), and instead uses a direct NMOS transistor (NM3) with its drain connected to the shared node n2 and its source connected to VSS. This change eliminates the noise-inducing resistors and diode configuration in the bias path, improving the noise figure by reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network and ensuring a more direct and stable current path. This simplification also helps in better impedance matching at the input and output, which can further improve the noise performance and meet the specification.
