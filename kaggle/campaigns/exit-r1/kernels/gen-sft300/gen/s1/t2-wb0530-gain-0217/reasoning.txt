The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_max_db), which is slightly above the specification limit. This suggests that the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 or the biasing network is too high. The current configuration uses a diode-connected NM3 and a resistor R1 to set the bias, which may introduce additional noise due to the resistor and the diode-connected transistor. This noise is amplified and contributes to the overall noise figure of the LNA.

To address this, I replaced the resistor R1 with a PMOS transistor (ZP1) connected from VDD to n5 and to VSS, effectively creating a current source for the bias network. This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network by replacing the resistor with a low-noise PMOS transistor. The PMOS transistor provides a more stable and lower-noise current source, which helps reduce the overall noise figure of the LNA. This structural change directly targets the noise contribution from the biasing network, improving the noise figure without affecting the gain or other performance metrics.
