Additionally, the lack of a dedicated impedance-matching network at the input port (VIN1) may be contributing to the poor s11 performance.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (ZC2) between VDD and n6, which helps in tuning the input impedance and improving the return loss by reducing the mismatch at the input port.
