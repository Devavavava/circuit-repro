To address this, I replace the NM3 diode with a PMOS transistor (ZP1) biased directly from VDD, eliminating the noise path through the NMOS diode and reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network. I also add a capacitor (ZC1) between n0 and VDD to provide a low-impedance AC path for the input signal, improving the input matching and reducing noise coupling. Furthermore, I add a resistor (ZR2) between n6 and VDD to stabilize the bias point of the PMOS current source (PM2), ensuring a more consistent and lower-noise current reference.
