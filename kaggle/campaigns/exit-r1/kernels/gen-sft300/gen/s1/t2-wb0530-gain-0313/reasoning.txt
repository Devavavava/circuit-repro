The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, indicating that the noise performance of the LNA is degrading. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the current-reuse configuration and the biasing network, which introduces additional noise sources and degrades the overall noise figure. The existing topology may be suffering from poor noise matching at the input and insufficient isolation between the output and the noise sources in the biasing network.

To address this, I propose a structural change that introduces a noise-canceling feedback path from the output to the input, which helps in reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network and improves the noise figure. This is achieved by adding a capacitor and a resistor to create a low-noise feedback path, and by reconfiguring the NMOS transistor to provide better noise matching and isolation.
