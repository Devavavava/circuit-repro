The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) across the wideband frequency range, which is caused by the feedback topology and the inductive load configuration in the original netlist. The resistive shunt feedback and the shunt-peaked inductive load (L1+R2) create a resonant peak that may lead to instability at certain frequencies, especially when the feedback loop interacts with the inductive load's frequency response.

To address this, I replaced the resistive shunt feedback with a PMOS transistor (PM1) in a common-gate configuration, which provides a more controlled and stable feedback path. I also replaced the resistive load (R2) with a PMOS transistor (PM2) in a current-source configuration, which improves the stability margin by reducing the feedback loop's susceptibility to resonance. Additionally, I introduced a resistor (Rrep1) and a capacitor (Crep1) to provide a low-frequency feedback path and to dampen any potential resonances, respectively. These changes collectively enhance the stability margin across the wideband frequency range.
