The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, which is likely caused by the noise contribution from the NMOS transistor NM3 in the biasing network. This transistor, configured as a diode, introduces additional noise due to its high impedance and lack of current steering, especially in a wideband application. Additionally, the current-reuse structure may not be optimally configured to suppress noise from the bias network, leading to a higher-than-allowed noise figure.

To address this, I replaced the NM3 diode configuration with a PMOS transistor (ZP1) connected to a current source (R4) and a capacitor (ZC1), which provides a low-noise, stable bias reference. This change reduces the noise contribution from the bias network and improves the noise figure. I also added a capacitor (ZC3) between VDD and n6 to filter out high-frequency noise from the power supply, further reducing noise in the signal path. Finally, I added a resistor (ZR2) from n4 to VSS to provide a stable DC path for the load, improving stability and reducing noise coupling.
