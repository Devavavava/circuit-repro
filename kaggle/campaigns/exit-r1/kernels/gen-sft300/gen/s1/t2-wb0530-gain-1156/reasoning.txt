The failure is primarily due to poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being violated by ~0.415821 dB. The anchor netlist uses a standard current-reuse topology with a series inductor (L1) and capacitor (C4) for input DC blocking, but the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source, especially at the target frequency. This is likely due to the lack of a direct impedance transformation mechanism at the input, leading to a mismatch that limits the reflection coefficient (s11).

To address this, I introduce a new topology that includes a shunt NMOS transistor (NM3) directly connected to the input node (n2), which provides a tunable impedance at the input. This additional transistor helps in improving the input matching by allowing a more controlled impedance transformation. The original NM3 was connected to a bias network, but in this revised topology, it is repositioned to directly influence the input impedance, effectively acting as a variable impedance element. This change enables better control over the input reflection coefficient, thereby improving s11_max_db.
