The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification. The noise contribution from the input transistor (NM1) and the biasing network is not adequately suppressed, especially at the high frequency (f0=1.22 GHz), leading to a noise figure of 2.52 dB, which is above the allowed maximum of 2.0068 dB. This is likely exacerbated by the lack of an explicit noise-cancelling path or a noise-suppressing element at the input stage.

To address this, I introduce a noise-canceling capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n6 and VDD. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise at the gate of PM2, effectively suppressing noise contributions from the biasing network and reducing the overall noise figure. This change does not interfere with the gain or stability of the circuit but improves noise performance by isolating the noise-sensitive nodes from the biasing network at high frequencies.
