The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_max_db), which is slightly over the specification. This indicates that the noise performance of the LNA is insufficient, likely due to the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse configuration. The NMOS NM3 is connected to VSS and its gate is tied to n5, which is part of the biasing network. This configuration introduces noise into the signal path, degrading the noise figure. Additionally, the current-reuse topology, while beneficial for gain, can introduce additional noise if not properly managed.

To address this, I modified the topology to reduce the noise contribution from the biasing network. In the revised netlist, NM3 is now connected to n0 instead of VSS, which is the node after the input capacitor C4. This change isolates the biasing network from the signal path, reducing noise coupling. Additionally, I replaced the PMOS PM2 and its associated resistors (R4, R5) with a direct connection from n6 to VDD and a capacitor C4 between n6 and VSS. This simplifies the biasing network, reducing noise and improving stability.
