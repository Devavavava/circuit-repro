The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.1 dB. The current-reuse topology may be introducing additional noise due to the diode-connected NM3 and the resistive biasing network, which degrades the noise performance at the target frequency.

To address this, I modified the biasing network by replacing the NM3 diode and R1 with a PMOS current source (PM2) connected to a resistor (R4) and a capacitor (C2) to ground, effectively creating a low-noise biasing path. This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network by eliminating the resistive path and using a PMOS current source, which has lower noise compared to an NMOS diode and resistor combination. Additionally, I reconnected the load capacitor C2 to a different node to avoid coupling noise from the biasing network into the signal path. The revised netlist ensures a cleaner signal path and lower noise contribution from the biasing structure, directly addressing the noise figure constraint.
