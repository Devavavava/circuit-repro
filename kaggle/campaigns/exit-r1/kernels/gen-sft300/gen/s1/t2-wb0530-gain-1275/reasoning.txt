The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, failing by ~0.17 dB. This is likely due to the noise contribution from the NMOS transistor NM3, which is used as a current mirror reference. In the anchor netlist, NM3 is a diode-connected NMOS with its source tied to VSS and drain connected to n5. This configuration introduces additional noise due to the high impedance at the drain node, which is particularly problematic at high frequencies. The noise from NM3 is amplified through the current mirror and contributes to the overall noise figure of the LNA.

To address this, I replace NM3 with a PMOS transistor in a similar diode-connected configuration (PM3), connecting its drain to n5 and source to VDD. This change reduces the noise contribution from the reference current mirror, as PMOS transistors typically exhibit lower noise at high frequencies compared to NMOS transistors in this configuration. This structural change helps lower the noise figure, bringing it within the required specification.
