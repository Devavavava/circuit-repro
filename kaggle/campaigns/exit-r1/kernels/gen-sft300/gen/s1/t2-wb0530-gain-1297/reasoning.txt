The original topology, while achieving good gain and noise figure, lacks sufficient feedback or impedance control mechanisms to ensure stability over the entire band. The resistive shunt feedback and shunt-peaked inductor load may not provide enough phase margin or damping at higher frequencies, leading to instability.

To address this, I propose adding a second NMOS transistor (NM2) in a common-gate configuration, along with a feedback capacitor (ZC1) and two inductors (L2 and L3), to provide additional phase compensation and stability. The NM2 transistor introduces a second feedback path, which helps to dampen oscillations and improve the stability factor over the wideband. The inductors and capacitor work together to shape the frequency response, ensuring sufficient phase margin and reducing the likelihood of instability. This structural change enhances the stability factor without significantly degrading other performance metrics.
