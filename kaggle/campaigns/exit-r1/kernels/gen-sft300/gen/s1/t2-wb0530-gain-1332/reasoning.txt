The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by approximately 0.23 dB. In the original topology, the noise performance is likely compromised by the shared DC path and the lack of a dedicated low-noise path for the signal. The NMOS transistor NM3 is connected in a diode configuration with R1 and R2, which may introduce additional noise or reduce the signal-to-noise ratio due to the lack of a proper noise-canceling mechanism.

To address this, I introduce a new NMOS transistor, NM4, connected between n4 (the node of the inductor L2 and capacitor C2) and n5 (the gate reference node of NM3), with its source connected to n0 (the input node). This change adds a noise-canceling path by introducing a feedback mechanism that reduces the noise contribution from the transistor NM1 and the shared DC path. This structural change helps to lower the noise figure by providing a more direct and noise-optimized signal path.
