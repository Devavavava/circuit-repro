The binding constraint failure is due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) caused by the lack of a feedback path from the output to the input, which limits the phase margin and leads to instability at certain frequencies. This is exacerbated by the absence of a direct connection between the output node and the internal nodes that influence the feedback loop.

To address this, I introduce a feedback path by connecting the output node (VOUT1) directly to the internal node n6, which is part of the current-setting network. This addition creates a feedback loop that enhances the stability of the amplifier across the wideband frequency range, thereby improving the mu_min_wide metric.
