The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the NMOS transistor NM3, which is operating in a diode-connected configuration. This configuration introduces significant noise because it is not optimized for low-noise performance. The current-reuse topology, while effective for gain and power efficiency, does not inherently suppress noise from the biasing network, especially when NM3 is used as a current mirror reference.

To address this, I replaced the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor Rrep1 that connects to VDD and a new internal node n6. This change eliminates the noise source from the NM3 transistor and provides a more stable and lower-noise bias reference. Additionally, I added a capacitor ZC1 between VDD and VSS to decouple the power supply and reduce noise coupling into the signal path. These structural changes reduce the noise contribution from the bias network and improve the overall noise performance of the LNA.
