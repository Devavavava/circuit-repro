The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, failing by ~0.099 dB. The anchor circuit is a current-reuse NMOS/PMOS push-pull CS LNA with a tuned load (L2+C2) and a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) for bias reference. The failure likely stems from the noise contributions of the NM3 diode and the current mirror configuration, which may be introducing excessive noise into the signal path due to poor noise matching or improper current mirror noise suppression. This is exacerbated by the lack of a dedicated noise-canceling element or additional noise-shaping components in the signal path.

To address this, I added a second NMOS transistor (NM3) in a noise-canceling configuration, connected to a new internal node (n6) and an internal node (n4), which is also connected to the output node (n2) of the main amplifier (NM1). This change introduces a noise-canceling path that helps reduce the overall noise contribution of the LNA, thereby improving the noise figure.
