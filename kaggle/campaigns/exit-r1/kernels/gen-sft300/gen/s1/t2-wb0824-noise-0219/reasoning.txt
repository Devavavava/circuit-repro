The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the current-reuse structure and the lack of effective noise suppression in the output stage. The anchor netlist uses a current-reuse configuration with a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for biasing, but this introduces additional noise paths and limits the noise performance at the target frequency. The noise is exacerbated by the lack of a low-noise path from the input to the output, and the shared DC path introduces noise coupling.

To address this, the topology is restructured to eliminate the noise-coupling path and introduce a more direct, low-noise signal path. This is achieved by replacing the current-reuse configuration with a direct NMOS transistor (NM2) connected from VDD to n0, with its source tied to n1, and adding a capacitor (ZC2) between VDD and n2 to decouple the power supply and reduce noise coupling. Additionally, a new NMOS transistor (NM3) is added between n6 and VSS, with its gate connected to the gate of NM1, to provide a more stable and noise-optimized feedback path. The resistor R5 is connected from n6 to the source of NM1, creating a more controlled current path and reducing noise contributions from the bias network.
