The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (NF) exceeding the specification, which is caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) in the original netlist. The noise from NM3 and the resistive biasing network (R1, R2, R4, R5) adds up in the signal path, degrading the overall noise performance of the LNA. This is especially problematic in a current-reuse topology where the noise from the biasing network is not well isolated from the signal path.

To address this, I modified the biasing network by replacing the diode-connected NM3 with a separate NMOS transistor (ZN1) that is biased through a different path (R2 and R4), which isolates the noise from the signal path. Additionally, I added a new NMOS transistor (NM3) to the biasing network, and reconfigured the connections of R2 and R4 to ensure that the noise from the biasing network is decoupled from the signal path. This structural change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network and improves the overall noise figure.
