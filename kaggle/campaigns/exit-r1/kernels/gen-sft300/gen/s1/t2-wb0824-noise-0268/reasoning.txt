The failure is primarily due to poor input impedance matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint failing by ~0.22 dB. The anchor netlist uses a simple inductor-capacitor (L1-C4) input DC-blocking network, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation across the wideband frequency range. This results in a mismatch at the input port, limiting the return loss and causing the s11_max_db to fall below the specification. The current-reuse topology, while effective for noise and gain, may not be optimally configured for wideband impedance matching without additional structural modifications.

To address this, I propose adding a shunt capacitor (ZC1) from VDD to n4, which is the node connected to the tuned load (L2-C2). This addition provides an additional impedance path at high frequencies, helping to stabilize and improve the input impedance match. The capacitor introduces a parallel resonant path at the operating frequency, reducing the reflection coefficient at the input and thereby improving s11_max_db. This structural change directly addresses the impedance mismatch without altering the core current-reuse topology or adding biasing components, ensuring compatibility with the downstream sizing tool.
