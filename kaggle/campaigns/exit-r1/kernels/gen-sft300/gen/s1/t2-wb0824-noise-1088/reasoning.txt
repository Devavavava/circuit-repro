The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 and the current-reuse path. The current-reuse configuration introduces additional noise sources, particularly from the NM3 diode and the R1 bias resistor, which increase the overall noise figure. This is exacerbated by the fact that the current-reuse structure does not provide sufficient noise suppression at the input stage, especially at the high-frequency operating point of the LNA. The noise from the bias network and the diode-connected NM3 transistor is not adequately suppressed, leading to a higher-than-allowed noise figure.

To address this, the topology is modified to reduce noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse path. This is achieved by reconfiguring the current-reuse structure to use a separate current mirror for biasing, and by introducing a direct DC path from the input to ground via a shunt capacitor, which decouples the input from the noise sources in the bias network. Additionally, the load network is restructured to be a more stable and noise-friendly configuration, using a series inductor and capacitor to provide a low-noise, high-impedance load at the output node.
