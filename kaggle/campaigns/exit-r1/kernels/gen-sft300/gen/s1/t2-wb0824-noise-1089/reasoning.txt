The binding constraint is the input return loss (s11_max_db), which is failing by approximately 0.42 dB. This indicates poor impedance matching at the input, likely due to the lack of a direct DC path from the input to ground, which is essential for proper biasing and impedance control. The anchor netlist uses a capacitor (C4) to block DC at the input, but this also prevents any DC current from flowing through the input, leading to a mismatch in the high-frequency domain. Additionally, the absence of a direct DC path from the input to ground may be causing a phase shift or impedance discontinuity that degrades the reflection coefficient.

To address this, I added a direct DC path from the input (VIN1) to ground (VSS) using a resistor (ZR1) and a capacitor (ZC1) in parallel. This allows DC current to flow through the input while maintaining AC coupling, which helps in achieving better impedance matching at the input. The addition of ZR1 and ZC1 ensures that the input node has a stable DC reference, reducing the phase shift and improving the reflection coefficient (s11_max_db).
