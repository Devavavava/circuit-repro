The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, exceeding the maximum allowed value of 2.4786 dB by approximately 0.0436 dB. This suggests that the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 or the biasing network is too high for the given frequency and topology. The current-reuse structure with a shared DC path and the diode-connected NM3 and PM2 transistors may be introducing additional noise due to the biasing and current mirroring mechanisms. The shared DC path may also be degrading the noise performance due to increased thermal noise from the biasing network and the current mirror.

To address this, I added a PMOS transistor ZP1 in a feedback configuration from the output to the gate of NM3, and added a resistor ZR1 from the output to VDD. This creates a feedback path that helps stabilize the biasing and reduce noise contributions from the current mirror and bias network. The PMOS ZP1 provides an additional noise-canceling path, and the resistor ZR1 helps in shaping the impedance seen at the output, reducing noise coupling and improving the overall noise figure.
