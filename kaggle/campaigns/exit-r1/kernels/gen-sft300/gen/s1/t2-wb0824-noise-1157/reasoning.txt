The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the current-reuse structure in the anchor netlist. The noise is primarily introduced by the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) in the bias network, which are not optimized for noise performance. The shared DC path and the lack of a noise-isolated load also contribute to increased noise at the input. Additionally, the current-reuse configuration can introduce noise coupling between the input and output paths, degrading the overall noise performance.

To address this, I restructured the circuit to isolate the noise-sensitive components and reduce their impact on the input signal. I replaced the diode-connected NM3 with a NMOS transistor (NM2) connected to a separate bias node (n5), which is now driven by a different current path (R1 and PM2). This change allows for better noise isolation and reduces the noise contribution from the bias network. I also added a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) connected to a separate bias node (n7), which helps in stabilizing the bias current and reducing noise coupling. Additionally, I added a resistor (ZR1) to provide a noise-isolated path for the bias current, ensuring that the noise from the bias network does not affect the signal path.
