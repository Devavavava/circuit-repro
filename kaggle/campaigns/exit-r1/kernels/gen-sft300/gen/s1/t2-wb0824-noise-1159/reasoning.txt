The binding constraint is the stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is failing due to insufficient phase margin across the wideband operation. The original circuit uses a resistive shunt feedback topology with a shunt-peaked inductive load, which limits the phase margin at higher frequencies, especially in the wideband range. This results in a lower stability margin than required.

To address this, I propose replacing the resistive shunt feedback with a two-stage topology that includes an additional NMOS transistor and a PMOS transistor in a common-gate configuration. This change introduces a second amplification stage and provides a more controlled feedback path, improving the phase margin and stability across the wideband. The addition of a PMOS transistor (PM1) and an inductor (L2) in series with the output capacitor (C2) helps to shape the frequency response and enhance the stability margin.
