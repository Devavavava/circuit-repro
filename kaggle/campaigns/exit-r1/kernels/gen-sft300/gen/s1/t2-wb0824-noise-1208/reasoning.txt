The noise figure (nf_max_db) is failing due to the noise contribution from the biasing network, particularly the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and the resistive divider (R1, R2). The NM3 device, acting as a current reference, introduces significant noise due to its high impedance and the lack of a low-noise current source. Additionally, the resistive divider (R1 and R2) adds thermal noise, which degrades the noise figure. The current-reuse topology, while beneficial for gain and power efficiency, is not sufficiently noise-optimized in this configuration, especially at the high-frequency band (1.38 GHz).

To address this, I restructured the biasing network to eliminate the noise-prone resistive divider and diode-connected NMOS. Instead, I replaced the NM3 and R2 with a direct connection from n5 to VDD, effectively removing the noise source. I also modified the PMOS gate biasing by connecting PM2's source to n5 instead of VDD, ensuring a more stable and lower-noise current path. This change reduces the noise contribution from the bias network and improves the noise figure by isolating the signal path from the noisy biasing elements.
