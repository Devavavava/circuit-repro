To address this, I propose replacing the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor (ZR1) in series with a capacitor (ZC1) to provide a low-noise bias path. This change decouples the noise from the biasing network, reducing its contribution to the input noise. Additionally, I replace the diode-connected PM2 with a resistor (Rrep1) and a capacitor (ZC2) to stabilize the gate voltage of PM1 and reduce noise coupling from the biasing network. These structural changes improve the noise figure by reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network and improving the signal integrity at the gate of PM1.
