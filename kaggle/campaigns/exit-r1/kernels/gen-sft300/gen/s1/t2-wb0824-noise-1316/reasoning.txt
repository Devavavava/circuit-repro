The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_max_db), which is exceeded by ~0.3 dB. This suggests that the noise contribution from the transistor NM3 is too high, likely due to its diode-connected configuration and lack of a proper noise-cancelling mechanism. The current-reuse topology is effective for gain and power efficiency but may not be sufficient for noise suppression in this wideband application. The noise from NM3 is not being adequately suppressed or canceled, leading to a higher-than-allowed noise figure.

To address this, I introduced a noise-cancelling NMOS transistor (ZN1) connected between VDD and n5, with its source connected to n4 (the node of the tuned load L2+C2). This provides a noise-canceling path from the tuned load to the gate of NM3, reducing the noise contribution from NM3. This change is structural and does not involve any biasing or sizing components, as required.
