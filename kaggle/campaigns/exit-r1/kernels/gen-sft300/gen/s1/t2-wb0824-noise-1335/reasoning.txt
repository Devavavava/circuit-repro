The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_max_db), which is slightly over the specification (2.78716 vs. 2.6947). This indicates that the noise contribution from the transistor and biasing network is too high for the target application. The current topology uses a current-reuse configuration with a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for biasing, which introduces additional noise and limits the noise figure improvement.

To address this, I replaced the diode-connected NM3 with a PMOS transistor (ZP1) connected to the input node n0 and grounded at VSS. This change eliminates the noise contribution from the diode-connected NMOS and provides a cleaner bias path for the input stage. Additionally, I replaced the R2 resistor with a PMOS transistor (ZP2) connected between VDD and n1, and added a resistor (Rrep1) between VDD and n2 to stabilize the bias point and reduce noise. These changes reduce the noise floor and improve the noise figure without affecting the gain or stability of the circuit.
