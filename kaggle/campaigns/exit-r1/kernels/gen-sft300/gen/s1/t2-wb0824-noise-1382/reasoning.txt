The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is too high due to the noise contributions from the biasing network and the lack of noise shielding in the signal path. Specifically, the NMOS diode NM3 and the resistor R1 in the anchor netlist introduce significant noise because they are directly connected to the signal path and are not isolated from it. The noise from these components is amplified by the gain of the LNA, leading to a higher-than-allowed NF.

To address this, I replaced the NM3 diode with a PMOS transistor (PM3) and connected it to a new internal node n6, which is biased through a resistor R4 to VSS. This change isolates the biasing network from the signal path, reducing noise coupling. Additionally, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n6 and the gate of the NMOS transistor NM1, which helps to filter out noise from the bias network and improve the noise performance of the LNA.
