The binding constraint is the poor input matching (s11_max_db), which is failing by ~0.33 dB. This is likely due to the lack of a dedicated input impedance-matching network in the anchor netlist. The input is coupled through a capacitor (C4), but there is no active or passive component to adjust the impedance at the desired frequency. Additionally, the shared DC path and the lack of a dedicated input tuning element may be causing a mismatch in the wideband operation.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and n5 to provide a tunable impedance path at the input, which helps in improving the input matching. This change introduces a new impedance control point near the input gate of the NM3 transistor, which is crucial for wideband matching. I also added a capacitor (ZC2) between VDD and n6 to provide additional impedance tuning at the PMOS side, which helps in stabilizing the input impedance over a wide frequency range. Finally, I added a capacitor (ZC3) between n2 and VSS to provide a low-impedance path for AC signals, reducing the impact of parasitic capacitances and improving the overall input matching.
