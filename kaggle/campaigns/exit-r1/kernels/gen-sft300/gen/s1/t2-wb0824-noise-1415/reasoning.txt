The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is too high due to the noise contribution from the biasing network and the lack of a noise-canceling structure in the current-reuse topology. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and a PMOS (PM2) for biasing, which introduces significant noise at the input node, especially in a current-reuse configuration. The absence of a noise-canceling mechanism at the input and the lack of a low-noise DC path contribute to the elevated NF.

To address this, I introduce a noise-canceling structure at the input by adding a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the source of NM1, and another capacitor (ZC2) between VSS and the drain of NM3. These capacitors help decouple the noise from the bias network and provide a cleaner signal path to the amplifier core. Additionally, I modify the current-reuse structure by replacing the diode-connected NM3 with a NMOS transistor (NM2) connected to VDD, which allows for better noise control and improved impedance matching. The addition of a resistor (ZR1) between VDD and the gate of PM1 helps stabilize the gate voltage and reduce noise coupling. These changes collectively reduce the noise contribution from the bias network and improve the overall noise figure of the LNA.
