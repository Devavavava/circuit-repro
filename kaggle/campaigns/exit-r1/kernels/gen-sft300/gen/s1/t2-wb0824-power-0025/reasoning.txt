The failure is primarily due to the insufficient stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) caused by the narrowband nature of the shunt-peaked inductive load. While the anchor netlist provides good gain, noise figure, and input match, the stability margin is compromised over the wideband frequency range due to the resonant behavior of the inductive load and the feedback topology. This leads to a negative stability margin at certain frequencies, violating the `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` constraint.

To address this, the inductive load is replaced with a more stable, wideband configuration. The shunt-peaked inductor is replaced with a series inductor and a parallel capacitor, forming a band-stop filter at the resonant frequency of the inductor-capacitor combination. This suppresses the resonant peak and enhances stability across the wideband range. Additionally, an extra NMOS transistor is added in a common-gate configuration to provide additional phase margin and improve stability. The input capacitor is also replaced with a series inductor to enhance the input match and reduce the impact of the resonant frequency on the input impedance.
