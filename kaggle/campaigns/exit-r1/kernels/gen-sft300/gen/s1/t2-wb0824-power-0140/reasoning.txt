The failure is primarily due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is critical for wideband operation. The original topology, while effective for narrowband performance, lacks sufficient feedback and impedance control over a broad frequency range. This results in instability and poor phase margin, especially at the lower end of the band, leading to the negative margin in the mu_min_wide constraint.

To address this, I introduced a feedback path from the output to the input via a resistor (Rrep1) and a capacitor (ZC2) to provide additional phase compensation and stabilize the amplifier over the wideband. Additionally, I added a series inductor (Lrep1) and a resistor (Rrep2) in the DC path to improve impedance matching and reduce unwanted feedback. These changes enhance the phase margin and improve stability across the entire frequency range, directly addressing the binding constraint.
