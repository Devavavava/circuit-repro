The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the biasing network, specifically from NM3 and the resistors R1, R2, and R5. The current-reuse topology in the anchor netlist relies on a diode-connected NM3 and a resistor-based biasing network, which introduces significant noise at the input due to the thermal noise of R2 and R5. Additionally, the NM3 diode configuration has a higher noise contribution compared to a more optimal biasing approach.

To address this, the biasing network is restructured to reduce noise contributions. The NM3 diode is replaced with a PMOS transistor (ZP1) in a current mirror configuration with PM2, which provides a lower-noise biasing path. The resistors R2 and R5 are removed, and their roles are taken over by the PMOS current mirror and a new resistor (Rrep1) that is strategically placed to minimize noise coupling. Additionally, a capacitor (ZC1) is added to decouple the biasing network from the signal path, reducing noise injection into the input.

These changes reduce the noise contribution from the biasing network, directly improving the noise figure while maintaining the performance of the LNA in terms of gain, input matching, and power consumption.
