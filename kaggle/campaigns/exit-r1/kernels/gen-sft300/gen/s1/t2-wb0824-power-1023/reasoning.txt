The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.18 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the current-reuse configuration and the lack of noise cancellation mechanisms in the anchor netlist. The noise figure is dominated by the input transistor NM1 and the noise coupling through the shared DC path and the current mirror NM3, which introduces additional noise into the signal path.

To address this, I added a noise-canceling NMOS transistor (ZN1) in a feedback configuration, connecting from n4 (the tuned load node) to n6 (the current mirror node) with its source tied to VSS. This creates a noise-canceling path that helps reduce the overall noise contribution from the current mirror and the shared DC path. Additionally, I added a resistor (ZR2) between VDD and n5 to provide a more stable bias point for the current mirror, and a capacitor (ZC2) between n6 and n3 to filter noise on the current mirror's gate node. These changes help reduce the noise figure while maintaining the desired performance in other metrics.
