The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is failing due to insufficient phase margin across the wideband frequency range. This is likely caused by the single-stage common-source (CS) topology with shunt peaking, which has limited inherent stability and is prone to oscillation at higher frequencies, especially when the feedback network cannot adequately control the phase shift across the band.

To address this, I modified the topology to a two-stage structure with active feedback and additional inductive peaking. The first stage is a common-gate (CG) configuration, which inherently provides better phase margin and stability. The second stage is a common-source configuration with a shunt inductor, which enhances gain and bandwidth. The addition of a PMOS transistor (PM1) and a second inductor (L3) introduces active feedback and additional peaking, improving the phase margin and stabilizing the circuit across the wideband range.
