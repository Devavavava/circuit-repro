The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, failing by ~0.185 dB. The original circuit uses a current-reuse topology with a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2), but the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current mirror (NM3, R1, R2) is likely degrading the NF. The diode-connected NM3 and the resistive biasing (R1, R2) add noise and reduce the overall noise performance, especially at the target frequency.

To address this, the biasing network is restructured to minimize noise contributions. The NM3 diode is replaced with a PMOS transistor (PM3) in a different configuration, and the resistive biasing is replaced with a direct DC path using a capacitor (ZC1) and a resistor (Rrep2) to a new internal node (n6), which is connected to the output of the PM2 current mirror. This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network and improves the noise figure by providing a cleaner signal path and reducing the impact of resistive noise sources.
