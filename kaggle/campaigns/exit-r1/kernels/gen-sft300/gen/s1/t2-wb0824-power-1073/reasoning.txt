The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current-reuse configuration in the anchor netlist. The NMOS NM3 and PMOS PM2 are connected to internal nodes that are not directly tied to the signal path, leading to increased noise coupling and degradation in the noise figure.

To address this, I restructured the biasing network by connecting the NM3 and PM2 transistors to the signal path through VDD and VSS, which reduces noise coupling. Additionally, I introduced a direct connection from the NMOS NM1 to the output through an inductor L3, which helps in isolating the signal path from the biasing network and reduces noise contribution. This change also enhances the stability and linearity of the amplifier.
