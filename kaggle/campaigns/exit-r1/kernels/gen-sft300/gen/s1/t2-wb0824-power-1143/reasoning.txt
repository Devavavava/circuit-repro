The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, failing by ~0.2 dB. This is likely due to the noise contribution from the current-reuse path and the biasing network. The NMOS NM3 and PMOS PM2 are part of the biasing network, and their configuration may be introducing noise or degrading the noise performance at the operating frequency. The current-reuse structure, while beneficial for gain, may be introducing noise if not properly managed, especially at high frequencies.

To address this, I replaced the NMOS NM3 with a resistor Rrep1 connected from VDD to n5, and connected the source of NM3 to n2 instead of VSS. This change eliminates the NMOS NM3 from the noise path and replaces it with a resistor, which has a lower noise contribution. Additionally, I added a capacitor ZC1 between n4 and VDD to provide a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, improving stability and reducing noise coupling. These changes reduce the noise contribution from the biasing network and improve the noise figure at the operating frequency.
