The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, despite meeting all other constraints. The noise figure is limited by the noise contribution from the transistor NM3, which is biased via R1 and R2 in the anchor netlist. This configuration introduces a noise source at the input node n5, which propagates through the circuit and degrades the noise figure. The noise contribution from NM3 is amplified due to the current-reuse structure, especially at high frequencies, leading to a noise floor that exceeds the allowed specification.

To address this, the topology is modified by replacing the NM3 diode-connected NMOS with a PMOS transistor (ZP1) and connecting it to a new internal node n6, which is tied to the gate of PM2. This change effectively isolates the noise contribution from the input biasing network, reducing the noise coupling into the signal path. The addition of a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and n6 helps to stabilize the biasing point and reduce high-frequency noise coupling. These changes reduce the noise contribution from the bias network, thereby improving the noise figure while maintaining the desired performance in other metrics.
