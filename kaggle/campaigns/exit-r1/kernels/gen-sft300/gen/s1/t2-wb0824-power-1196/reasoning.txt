The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 being too high. This is likely because the current-reuse topology, while beneficial for gain and power efficiency, introduces additional noise paths through the biasing network and the current mirror (NM3 and R1, R2). The noise from the current mirror and the biasing resistors is coupling into the signal path, increasing the overall noise figure beyond the specification.

To address this, the topology is modified to reduce the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current mirror. This is achieved by connecting the gate of NM3 to n4 (the node connected to the tuned load L2+C2) instead of n5, which is the node connected to the current mirror's reference. This change decouples the noise from the current mirror from the signal path and routes it through the tuned load, which is more effective at filtering out noise at the operating frequency. This structural change reduces the noise contribution from the bias network and improves the noise figure.
