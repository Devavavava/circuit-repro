The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, likely caused by the noise contribution from the current-reuse configuration and the lack of noise suppression in the signal path. The anchor netlist uses a current-reuse topology with a shared DC path, which can increase noise due to the additional noise sources from the current mirror and the load network. The noise from the NMOS and PMOS transistors in the current-reuse structure is not adequately suppressed, especially at the operating frequency, leading to a higher-than-allowed noise figure.

To address this, I restructured the signal path to isolate the noise-sensitive components and reduce the noise contribution from the current-reuse structure. I introduced a series inductor (L2) and a capacitor (C2) to decouple the signal path from the current mirror and load network. Additionally, I replaced the shared DC path with a separate ground connection for the NMOS transistor (NM1) and added a new capacitor (ZC2) to the output path to further suppress noise. This change reduces the noise coupling from the current mirror and load network into the signal path, thereby lowering the noise figure.
