The failure is driven by noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, which is primarily due to the noise contribution from the NMOS transistor NM3 in the bias network. NM3 is connected as a diode (n5-n5), which introduces a noise source that is not effectively suppressed in the current topology. This noise is amplified through the signal path and degrades the overall noise figure, especially at the wideband frequency of ~1.386 GHz.

To address this, I replaced the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor Rrep1 connecting n5 to VDD. This change eliminates the noise contribution from NM3 by removing the active transistor from the signal path, thereby reducing the noise floor. The resistor Rrep1 provides a stable bias reference point without introducing additional noise, which helps meet the noise figure constraint.
