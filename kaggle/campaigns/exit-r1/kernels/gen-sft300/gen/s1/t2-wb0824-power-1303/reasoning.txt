The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the current mirror and biasing network. Specifically, the NMOS NM3 and the resistor R2 in the anchor netlist introduce noise that degrades the NF. The NM3 is connected in a diode configuration, which is inherently noisy and contributes to the overall noise figure. Additionally, the resistor R2, which connects the gate of NM1 to the bias node n5, adds thermal noise that further degrades the NF.

To address this, the NM3 and R2 are replaced with a more noise-efficient configuration. The NM3 is removed, and R2 is replaced with a capacitor (ZC1) connecting n5 to VDD, effectively decoupling the gate of NM1 from the noise sources in the bias network. This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network and improves the noise figure of the LNA.
