The failure is primarily due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is below the required threshold. This is likely caused by poor impedance matching and phase shifts in the feedback paths, exacerbated by the current-reuse topology and the shared DC path. The shared DC path between the NMOS and PMOS stages introduces unwanted coupling and phase shifts, degrading the stability of the amplifier over the wideband frequency range.

To address this, I restructured the circuit to isolate the NMOS and PMOS stages from the shared DC path. This was achieved by removing the shared DC path and introducing an additional NMOS transistor (NM2) and a capacitor (ZC1) to decouple the output from the feedback network. This change improves the stability by reducing the feedback-induced phase shifts and enhancing the impedance matching across the wideband frequency range.
