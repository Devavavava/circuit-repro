The binding constraint is the minimum stability factor (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is failing due to insufficient phase margin and poor feedback control across the wideband frequency range. The original circuit's stability is compromised by the shared DC path and lack of a dedicated feedback loop to control the phase and magnitude of the signal across the band. The current-reuse topology and the shared load network (L2+C2) contribute to a weak feedback mechanism, especially at the lower end of the wideband frequency range, leading to instability.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the input node (n0) to provide a low-frequency feedback path and improve phase margin. Additionally, I introduced a resistor (Rrep1) between the internal node n6 and the shared DC path (n2), which helps stabilize the feedback loop by providing a controlled impedance for the current mirror and the PMOS gate biasing. These changes enhance the stability factor across the entire wideband range without affecting the gain or noise figure.
