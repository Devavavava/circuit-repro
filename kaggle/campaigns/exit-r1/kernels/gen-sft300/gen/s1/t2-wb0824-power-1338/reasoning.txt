The failure is primarily driven by the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, which is likely due to the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse topology. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for biasing, which introduces additional noise and may not provide sufficient noise isolation from the signal path. The current-reuse configuration, while beneficial for gain, may also amplify noise if not carefully managed.

To address this, I propose a structural change that reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network. This is achieved by replacing the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor Rrep1 that connects n5 to n2, and adding a resistor Rrep2 that connects VDD to n6. These resistors provide a more controlled and lower-noise biasing path by decoupling the signal node n2 from the biasing network. This change minimizes the noise coupling from the biasing network into the signal path, thereby reducing the overall noise figure.
