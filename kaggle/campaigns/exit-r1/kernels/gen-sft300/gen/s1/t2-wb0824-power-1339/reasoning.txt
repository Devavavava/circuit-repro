The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification by approximately 0.3 dB, which is critical for a low-noise amplifier. To address this, I restructured the biasing network to isolate it from the signal path and reduce noise coupling. I replaced the NM3 transistor with a PMOS transistor (NM3 → PM3) and reconnected it to a different node (n4 instead of n5), which helps in reducing noise from the biasing network. Additionally, I removed the R4 resistor and replaced it with a direct connection from n6 to VSS, simplifying the biasing path and reducing noise sources. These changes improve the noise figure by isolating the biasing network and reducing the number of noise contributors in the signal path.
