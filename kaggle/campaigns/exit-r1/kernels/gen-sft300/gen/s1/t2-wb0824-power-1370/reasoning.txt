The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, likely caused by the noise contribution from the NMOS transistor NM3 and the resistor R1 in the bias network. The current-reuse topology, while effective in boosting gain, introduces additional noise paths through the biasing network, especially at high frequencies. The resistor R1 and the diode-connected NM3 contribute significant noise, which is amplified in the gain path, pushing the noise figure beyond the allowed limit.

To address this, I replaced the diode-connected NM3 with a PMOS transistor (ZP1) connected to the same bias network, but with its source tied to VDD instead of VSS. This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network by leveraging the lower noise characteristics of the PMOS transistor in this configuration. Additionally, I introduced a new capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n6 and VDD to further filter out high-frequency noise from the bias network, reducing its impact on the noise figure.
