The binding constraint is the minimum stability factor (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is failing by -0.256177. This indicates that the circuit is unstable over part of the 1–50 GHz frequency range, likely due to the feedback topology and the inductive load configuration. The resistive shunt feedback and shunt-peaked inductor load may be creating a phase shift that reduces stability margins at higher frequencies, especially when the feedback is not properly controlled.

To address this, I propose replacing the resistive shunt feedback with a current-reuse topology that uses an additional transistor and inductor to provide more controlled feedback and improve phase margin. This change introduces a current mirror and an additional inductor to stabilize the feedback loop and enhance the stability factor across the wideband range.
