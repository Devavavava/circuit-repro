The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification. The anchor netlist uses a current-reuse topology with a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2), which may not be optimal for noise performance at the specified wideband frequency. The noise is likely dominated by the NMOS transistor NM1 and the biasing network, particularly the diode-connected NM3 and the resistor R2, which may introduce unnecessary noise contributions. Additionally, the lack of a dedicated noise-canceling or noise-shaping element in the signal path may be contributing to the elevated noise figure.

To address this, I propose a structural change that introduces a noise-canceling feedback mechanism. This is achieved by adding a capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n5 and the output VOUT1, and a resistor (ZR1) from the output VOUT1 to ground. These additions provide a low-impedance path for noise currents, effectively reducing the noise contribution from the bias network and improving the noise figure. The capacitor ZC1 also helps in stabilizing the output and reducing noise coupling from the bias network to the signal path.
