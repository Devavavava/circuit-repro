The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) across the wideband operation, which is likely caused by the single-stage common-source (CS) topology with resistive shunt feedback and shunt-peaked inductive load. This configuration limits the phase margin and stability across the wideband, especially at higher frequencies, leading to a negative margin for the stability factor (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz).

To address this, I replace the single-stage CS LNA with a two-stage topology: a common-gate (CG) stage followed by a common-source stage with active feedback. The CG stage improves the input matching and bandwidth by providing a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals. The second CS stage with active feedback enhances stability and linearity. The addition of a PMOS transistor (PM1) in the feedback path provides a low-noise, high-linearity active feedback mechanism, improving the stability margin and overall performance across the wideband.
