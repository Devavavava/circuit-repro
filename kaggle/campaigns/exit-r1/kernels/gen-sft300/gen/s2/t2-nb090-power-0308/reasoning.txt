The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing by a tiny margin (−0.000164457). This suggests that the noise contribution from the biasing network or the transconductance mismatch is pushing the noise floor slightly above the specification. In the anchor netlist, the noise contribution from the diode-connected NM3 and the current-setting PM2 may be introducing additional noise due to their biasing configuration and the lack of a low-noise reference path.

To address this, I modified the topology to include a direct low-noise reference path from the gate of NM1 to VDD, bypassing the current mirror and reducing noise coupling from the bias network. This change ensures that the gate of NM1 is driven by a cleaner signal, reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network. Additionally, I moved the load capacitor C2 to be directly connected to n0, which simplifies the signal path and reduces parasitic effects that could contribute to noise. These structural changes reduce the noise floor and improve the noise figure margin.
