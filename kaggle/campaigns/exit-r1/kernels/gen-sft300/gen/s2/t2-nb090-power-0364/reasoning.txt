The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.275 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current-reuse topology, which introduces additional noise sources and degrades the noise performance. The current-reuse configuration, while beneficial for power efficiency, may introduce noise through the biasing resistors and the diode-connected transistors, especially if the biasing network is not optimally designed.

To address this, I modified the topology to reduce noise contributions from the bias network. I replaced the original biasing structure with a simpler and lower-noise configuration by removing the NM3 diode and the R1–R2–R4–R5 network, which were the main contributors to noise. Instead, I used a direct connection from VDD to the gate of PM1, and from VSS to the source of NM1, to simplify the biasing and reduce noise. Additionally, I adjusted the load network by connecting C3 directly from n2 to VOUT1, ensuring a cleaner signal path with fewer noise sources.
