The failure is primarily due to the poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being violated by approximately 0.35 dB. The anchor netlist relies on the LC tank in the first stage for both tuning and impedance matching, which may not be sufficient for achieving the required -7.8462 dB minimum for S11 at 900 MHz.

To address this, the proposed structural change adds an input matching network consisting of a resistor (ZR2) and a capacitor (ZC1) in parallel with the first stage's input capacitor (C1). This additional network helps to fine-tune the input impedance, ensuring a better match at the operating frequency. The resistor ZR2 and capacitor ZC1 are connected to an internal node n2, which is the drain of NM1, and to VDD and VSS respectively. This modification enhances the input matching without significantly affecting the gain or noise figure, as the added components are passive and do not introduce additional noise or loss.
