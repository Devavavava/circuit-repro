The binding constraint is the reflection coefficient (s11_max_db), which is failing by approximately 0.45 dB. This indicates poor input impedance matching at the center frequency (900 MHz), leading to excessive signal reflection and reduced performance. The original topology relies on a single DC-block capacitor (C1) at the input and a tuned inductor (L1) at the drain of the first stage. This configuration may not provide sufficient impedance transformation or may be too sensitive to process variations, especially at the narrowband operating frequency.

The proposed structural change introduces a shunt capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the gate of the second stage (n5). This addition provides an additional impedance path at the operating frequency, helping to stabilize and improve the input impedance matching. By adding this capacitor, the second stage's gate is better decoupled from the interstage LC network, reducing the sensitivity of the input impedance to variations in the interstage inductance and improving the overall matching at the center frequency.
