The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which measures the stability of the LNA over a wide frequency range. The negative margin indicates that the circuit is unstable at some frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (f0 = 900 MHz). This instability likely arises from the lack of a direct feedback path or a stabilizing component that suppresses oscillations in the wideband regime. The absence of such a component, especially in the interstage and output paths, makes the circuit prone to instability when operating outside the narrowband design window.

To address this, adding a shunt capacitor between the output node (VOUT1) and ground (VSS) introduces a low-impedance path at higher frequencies, which helps dampen any parasitic oscillations and improves the stability factor over the wideband range. This change adds a structural element that was absent in the original topology, without modifying the core two-stage LC-tuned amplifier structure.
