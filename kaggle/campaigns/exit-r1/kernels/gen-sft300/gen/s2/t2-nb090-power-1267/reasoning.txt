To address this, an input matching network is added by introducing a new inductor (ZL1) connecting the internal node n7 (which is part of the bias network) to VDD. This inductor helps to tune the input impedance at the target frequency, improving the reflection coefficient. Additionally, a new capacitor (ZC1) is added between VDD and VOUT1 to provide a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, improving output matching and reducing signal loss. A new NMOS transistor (ZN1) is introduced as a cascode device, connected between n2 (the drain of NM1) and VDD, with its gate tied to VOUT1.
