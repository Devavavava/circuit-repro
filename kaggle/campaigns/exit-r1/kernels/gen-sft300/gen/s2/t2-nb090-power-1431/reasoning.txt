The binding constraint is the input return loss (s11_max_db), which is failing by approximately 0.7 dB. This indicates that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source at the target frequency (900 MHz), leading to poor power transfer and signal reflection. The anchor circuit uses a simple common-source configuration with an LC tank at the drain of the first stage, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation or tuning flexibility at the operating frequency. The second stage, while also using an LC tank, may not be optimally coupled to the first stage, resulting in a mismatch at the input.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and n5, which helps in fine-tuning the input impedance by providing an additional path for high-frequency currents. This capacitor effectively adjusts the phase and magnitude of the input impedance, improving the match at the target frequency. Additionally, I introduced a resistor (ZR2) between VDD and n4 to provide a controlled impedance path, which helps in stabilizing the input impedance and reducing reflections. These changes enhance the input matching without significantly affecting the gain or noise figure, addressing the s11_max_db constraint.
