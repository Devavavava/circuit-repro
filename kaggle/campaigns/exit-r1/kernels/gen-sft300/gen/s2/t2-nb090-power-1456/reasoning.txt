The binding constraint is the minimum stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is failing due to insufficient phase margin across the band, likely caused by the resonant LC load (L2+C2) and the feedback paths in the current-reuse topology. This resonant load introduces a phase shift that reduces stability, especially at the edges of the band, and the current-reuse configuration may be amplifying this effect through feedback paths. The lack of a damping mechanism in the load and the absence of a direct path for excess phase to be dissipated contribute to the instability.

To address this, I added a damping resistor `ZR1` between the output node `n4` of the resonant load and ground, and a resistor `ZR2` between the internal node `n5` and the power supply `VDD`. These resistors help dissipate excess energy and reduce resonant peaks, thereby improving the phase margin and stabilizing the circuit over the wide frequency range.
