The original topology uses a current mirror (NM3) biased by R1, with resistive gate biasing (R2 and R3) for both NM1 and NM2. This configuration introduces significant noise from the biasing resistors and the current mirror, degrading the noise performance of the LNA. Additionally, the lack of a direct DC path from the second stage's gate to ground may lead to poor noise matching at the input.

To address this, I have modified the biasing network to reduce noise contributions. Specifically, I have replaced the resistive gate biasing (R2 and R3) with a direct DC path from the second stage's gate (n5) to ground via a new resistor R2. This change ensures a more stable and lower-noise biasing scheme for NM2. Additionally, I have added a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) connected between VDD and n2 with its gate tied to VSS, which acts as a noise-canceling device, reducing the noise contribution from the first stage. These changes improve the noise figure by reducing the noise sources in the biasing network and enhancing the noise matching at the input.
