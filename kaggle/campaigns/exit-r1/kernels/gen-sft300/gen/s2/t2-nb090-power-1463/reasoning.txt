The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~2.26 dB. This suggests that the noise contribution from the second stage is too significant, likely due to the lack of noise isolation between stages. The current structure uses a direct LC interstage coupling (C2 and L2), which may not sufficiently suppress noise from the second stage from coupling back into the first stage. Additionally, the absence of a noise-shaping element in the second stage's drain load may be allowing higher noise contributions from the second transistor, NM2, to dominate the overall noise figure.

To address this, I introduce a noise-shaping inductor (L4) in the second stage's drain load, connecting VDD to n7 (the reference node of the current mirror). This inductor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise from the second stage to ground, thereby reducing its contribution to the noise figure. This change adds a noise isolation mechanism between the two stages, helping to suppress the noise from the second stage and improve the overall noise performance of the LNA.
