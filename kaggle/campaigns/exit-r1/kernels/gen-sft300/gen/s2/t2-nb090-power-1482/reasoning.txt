The noise figure (nf_db) is failing by approximately 2.1 dB, which suggests that the noise contribution from the second stage is too high, likely due to the lack of a noise-canceling mechanism or an insufficient noise suppression structure. In the anchor netlist, the second stage (NM2) is directly connected to the interstage LC network (C2 and L2), and its drain load is a simple inductor (L3) to VDD. This configuration does not provide a path for noise cancellation or a way to isolate the second stage from noise sources.

To address this, I added a resistor (ZR1) from VDD to the drain of NM1 (n2) and a capacitor (ZC2) from VDD to n4. These additions provide a noise-canceling path and help suppress noise contributions from the second stage. The resistor ZR1 provides a low-impedance path for noise from the first stage to ground, while the capacitor ZC2 helps filter out high-frequency noise from the interstage LC network, reducing its impact on the noise figure. These structural changes improve the noise performance of the LNA without affecting the gain or power consumption significantly.
