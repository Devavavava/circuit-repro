The failure is primarily due to poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being violated by nearly 1 dB. The original topology lacks an explicit input matching network, relying instead on the LC tank in the first stage to provide impedance transformation. However, this approach is insufficient for achieving the required -9.0379 dB return loss at 900 MHz. The input impedance is not adequately matched to the source, leading to signal reflection and reduced performance.

To address this, I added a series inductor (ZL1) between VDD and the drain of NM1, which helps in tuning the input impedance. Additionally, a shunt capacitor (ZC1) was placed between VDD and the drain of NM1 to further refine the input matching. A resistor (ZR2) was added between the drain of NM2 and VDD to provide a controlled impedance path and stabilize the output. These changes improve the input matching and help meet the s11_max_db constraint.
