The shared DC path between NM1 and PM1, along with the current-reuse structure (NM3 and PM2), may be causing signal degradation and limiting the gain.

To address this, I modified the topology to isolate the output from the shared DC path and reduce parasitic coupling. This was achieved by replacing the shared DC path with a separate inductor (L2) and capacitor (C2) that provide a high-impedance path at the operating frequency, thus reducing signal loss. Additionally, I reconfigured the current-reuse structure to avoid direct coupling between the output and the shared DC path, which helps maintain a higher gain. The addition of the PMOS transistor (PM2) and its connection to the output (VOUT1) provides a more direct and stable signal path, improving the gain.
