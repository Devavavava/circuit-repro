The failure is primarily due to insufficient gain (s21_db), which is below the required minimum of 25.7303 dB. The anchor circuit is a current-reuse NMOS/PMOS push-pull CS LNA, which has a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2). However, the gain is limited by the current-reuse configuration and the shared DC path, which introduces additional noise and reduces the signal gain at the target frequency (1.575 GHz). The current-reuse structure may also cause signal degradation due to the shared node (n2), and the tuned load is not effectively boosting the gain at the desired frequency.

To address this, I replaced the shared DC path with a direct connection from the tuned load (L2+C2) to VDD, which improves the signal path and reduces noise coupling. Additionally, I replaced the PMOS current source with a resistor (Rrep1) connected to VDD, which provides a more stable current reference and avoids the noise and nonlinearity associated with the PMOS current source. This change simplifies the signal path and improves gain performance at the target frequency.
