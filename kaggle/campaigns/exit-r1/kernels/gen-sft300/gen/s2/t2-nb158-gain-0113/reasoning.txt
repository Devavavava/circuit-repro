The binding constraint is the insufficient forward gain (s21_db), which is limited by the poor transconductance and poor impedance matching in the current-reuse push-pull configuration. The NMOS and PMOS stages are not effectively contributing to the gain, and the shared DC path may be causing signal degradation and poor impedance transformation at the operating frequency.

To address this, I redesigned the topology to use a single-stage NMOS common-gate configuration with a properly tuned inductive load. This structure ensures a low-noise, high-gain operation by directly coupling the input to the gate of the NMOS transistor, while the inductive load provides a high-impedance path for the signal at the operating frequency. The addition of a second inductor (L2) and a capacitor (C1) helps in achieving better impedance matching and stabilizing the output. This change eliminates the push-pull configuration and the current-reuse structure, which were likely causing signal cancellation and poor gain.
