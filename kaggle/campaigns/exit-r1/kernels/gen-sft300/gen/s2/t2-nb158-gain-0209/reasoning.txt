The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is below the required 24.6229 dB by a margin of -0.662236. This is likely due to poor impedance matching and signal degradation in the signal path, exacerbated by the current-reuse topology and the shared DC path. The NMOS and PMOS transistors are configured in a push-pull configuration, which may lead to signal cancellation or poor phase alignment at the output. Additionally, the load network (L2+C2) is connected to the shared DC path, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation for optimal gain.

To address this, I replace the push-pull configuration with a single NMOS transistor in a common-source configuration, which is more straightforward for achieving high gain in an LNA. I also modify the load network to be directly connected to the output, improving impedance matching and signal transfer. This change simplifies the signal path and avoids potential cancellation effects from the push-pull arrangement.
