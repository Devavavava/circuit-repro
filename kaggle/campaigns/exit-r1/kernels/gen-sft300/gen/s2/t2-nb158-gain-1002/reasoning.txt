The failure is primarily due to insufficient gain in the second stage, as evidenced by the binding constraint s21_db being 19.1959 dB instead of the required 33.994 dB. This indicates that the second stage is not contributing enough gain, likely due to the lack of a proper current mirror or active load to boost the signal. The anchor netlist uses a passive inductor (L3) as the load for the second stage, which limits the achievable gain. Additionally, the current mirror biasing (R1, NM3, R2, R3) may not be effectively driving the second stage, leading to a suboptimal signal amplification.

To address this, I added a PMOS current mirror (PM1 and PM2) to provide an active load for the second stage, replacing the passive inductor L3. This change enhances the transconductance of the second stage, thereby increasing the overall gain. The PMOS current mirror also helps in maintaining a stable bias point and reduces the dependence on the passive inductor for amplification. This structural change should significantly improve the s21_db metric, bringing it within the required specification.
