The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second stage's inability to provide enough amplification at the center frequency. The second stage's drain load (L3) is a pure inductor, which, while resonant at the operating frequency, does not provide a strong enough impedance transformation or sufficient voltage gain due to the lack of a resistive component. This results in a lower-than-required forward gain (s21_db), which fails by approximately 0.39 dB.

To address this, I introduce a resistive component in parallel with the second-stage drain load inductor (L3), which increases the effective impedance seen by the second stage at resonance, thereby boosting the gain. This is achieved by adding a resistor (ZR1) between VDD and the drain of NM2 (n5), which provides a resistive path that enhances the voltage gain of the second stage without disrupting the resonant tuning.
