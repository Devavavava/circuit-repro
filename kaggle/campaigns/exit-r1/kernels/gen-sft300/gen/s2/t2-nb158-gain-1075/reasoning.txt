The binding constraint is the input return loss (s11_max_db), which is too high (i.e., not sufficiently matched) by about 0.23 dB. This suggests that the input impedance mismatch is limiting the performance, likely due to the lack of an explicit input impedance matching network. The anchor netlist relies on the LC tank and the direct coupling from the input to the gate of NM1, which may not be sufficient to achieve the required impedance transformation at the center frequency.

To address this, I added a series inductor (ZL1) between VDD and the drain of NM2, and a capacitor (ZC1) between the drain of NM3 and VDD. These components help to provide additional impedance tuning at the operating frequency, improving the input impedance match. The inductor ZL1 provides a low-impedance path at the center frequency, while the capacitor ZC1 helps to shunt high-frequency noise and improve the stability of the biasing network. This structural change enhances the input matching without altering the core topology of the two-stage LNA.
