The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is below the required minimum of 10.5362 dB. The anchor netlist uses a common-gate topology with an inductive source match and a tuned drain load. However, this configuration may not be optimal for achieving high gain at the target frequency, especially when the inductor L2 in the drain load may not be sufficiently resonant or may introduce unwanted phase shifts that reduce the gain. Additionally, the common-gate topology inherently has lower gain compared to common-source configurations, which may be limiting the performance.

To address this, I propose a structural change to a common-source topology with an inductive source match and a resistive load. This change increases the gain potential by leveraging the higher transconductance of the common-source configuration. The resistive load provides a more stable and predictable impedance at the target frequency, which helps in achieving the required gain. The addition of a capacitor in the output stage ensures proper AC coupling and isolation from DC biasing, while the resistive load (ZR1) helps in maintaining a stable operating point and improving gain.
