The second stage is also constrained by its tuned load, which may reduce the available voltage gain. To address this, I replaced the second stage's NMOS transistor (NM2) with a PMOS transistor (PM2), which allows for a higher output impedance and potentially greater voltage gain when paired with an NMOS transistor in a common-source configuration. I also added a PMOS transistor (PM3) and a resistor (R3) to provide a more stable and higher current reference, which helps increase the transconductance of the second stage. These changes improve the overall gain by enhancing the current drive and reducing the voltage drop across the load.
