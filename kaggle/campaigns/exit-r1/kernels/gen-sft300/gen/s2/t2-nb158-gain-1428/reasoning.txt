The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second-stage transistor NM2 and its load network. The current topology has a tuned drain load (L3) for NM2, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation at the operating frequency, leading to a lower-than-required gain. Additionally, the LC interstage network (C2 and L2) may not be optimally coupling the signals between the two stages, resulting in signal loss and reduced overall gain.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the output of the second stage (n6) and the interstage LC network (n4). This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, improving the coupling between the two stages and enhancing the gain. The capacitor also helps in stabilizing the second stage by reducing the Miller effect and improving the output impedance matching at the operating frequency.
