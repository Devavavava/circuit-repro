The failure is primarily due to the poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being violated by ~0.58 dB. The anchor netlist lacks an explicit input matching network, relying instead on the LC tank of the first stage for impedance transformation. This results in a mismatch at the input, especially when the second stage's interstage LC network is not optimally aligned with the first stage's tuning.

To address this, the structural change introduces an explicit input matching network using a capacitor (ZC1) and a resistor (ZR1) to provide a controlled impedance transformation from the input (VIN1) to the gate of NM1. This ensures a better impedance match at the operating frequency, directly improving the s11_max_db metric.
