The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second-stage transistor NM2. The second stage is underdriven due to the lack of a direct biasing path to its gate, causing it to operate in a suboptimal region. Additionally, the LC interstage network (C2 and L2) introduces a voltage drop that reduces the signal amplitude reaching NM2, further degrading gain. The absence of a direct DC path to the gate of NM2 leads to poor biasing stability and reduced transconductance, which limits the achievable gain.

To address this, I introduced a direct biasing path to the second-stage transistor (NM2) by adding a resistor Rrep1 from the output node n6 to VDD. This ensures that NM2 is properly biased and operates in the linear region, improving its transconductance and, consequently, the overall gain of the amplifier. Additionally, I added a capacitor ZC1 between n4 and VDD to provide a low-impedance path for AC signals, reducing the voltage drop across the interstage network and improving signal integrity at the second stage. These changes help increase the gain (s21_db) by stabilizing the biasing of NM2 and reducing signal loss in the interstage network.
