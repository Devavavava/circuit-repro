The lack of a second-stage feedback or active compensation mechanism limits the stability margin, especially in the wideband operation.

This introduces a second-stage feedback loop that helps stabilize the amplifier over a wider frequency range. Additionally, I added a resistor (R R3) and a capacitor (C C3) to provide a low-frequency feedback path from the output to the drain of the second transistor, enhancing stability at lower frequencies. The resistor R R2 was also replaced with a smaller value to fine-tune the feedback and stability.
