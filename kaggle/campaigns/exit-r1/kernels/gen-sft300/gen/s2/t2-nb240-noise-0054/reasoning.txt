The binding constraint is the insufficient forward gain (s21_db), which is limited by the poor transconductance and impedance mismatch in the current-reuse topology. The anchor circuit uses a push-pull configuration with a shared DC path, which introduces parasitic effects and reduces the effective transconductance of the amplifier. Additionally, the tuned load (L2+C2) is disconnected from the main signal path, reducing the ability to boost gain effectively.

To address this, the topology is restructured to a single-stage common-gate configuration with a separate tuned load, which improves impedance matching and allows for higher gain. The current-reuse structure is replaced with a simpler common-gate configuration that minimizes parasitic coupling and enhances signal transmission. The removal of the push-pull configuration and the reconnection of the tuned load directly to the signal path help increase the forward gain and meet the s21_db requirement.
