The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_db) being too high, which is caused by the poor noise performance of the current-reuse topology in the anchor netlist. The noise is exacerbated by the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) used for biasing, which introduce additional noise sources and degrade the noise figure. Additionally, the current-reuse configuration, while beneficial for gain, can introduce noise if not carefully managed, especially at the input stage.

To address this, the topology was modified to replace the current-reuse configuration with a cascode structure. The NMOS transistor NM3 is now connected as a cascode device (NM3 n5 n1 VSS VSS), which helps in improving the noise performance by reducing the gate-source voltage noise at the input. The PMOS transistor PM2 is now connected as a cascode device (PM2 x1 n6 VDD VDD), which enhances the output impedance and stabilizes the gain. A new node x1 is introduced to decouple the biasing network from the signal path, reducing noise coupling. The resistor R2 is now connected from n5 to VDD instead of n5 to n1, which helps in better biasing and noise management.
