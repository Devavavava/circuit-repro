The binding constraint is the stability factor (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is failing due to poor stability in the wideband range. This is likely caused by the LC interstage network (C2 and L2) creating a resonant peak that introduces instability outside the narrowband center frequency (f0 = 2.4 GHz). The LC interstage network, while effective for narrowband tuning, may not provide sufficient phase margin across the wideband range, especially when combined with the tuned drain loads (L1 and L3). This results in a reduced stability factor (mu) at frequencies outside the narrowband, leading to the failure of the mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz constraint.

To address this, I replaced the LC interstage network (C2 and L2) with a resistor (ZR2) connecting n2 to VDD. This change introduces a damping effect, reducing the resonance and improving the phase margin across the wideband range. The resistor provides a more stable impedance path, preventing the LC interstage from creating a resonant peak that degrades stability. This structural change directly targets the stability factor, improving mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz by ~0.6 dB.
