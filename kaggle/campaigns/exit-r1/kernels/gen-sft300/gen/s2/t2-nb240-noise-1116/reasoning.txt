The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) across the band, likely caused by poor phase compensation and resonance effects in the LC-tuned interstage network. The interstage LC (L2 and C2) introduces resonant peaks and phase shifts that can destabilize the amplifier, especially at frequencies far from the center frequency (f0 = 2.4 GHz), leading to a reduced stability factor (mu_min) over the wide band.

To address this, I propose adding a feedback path from the output to the bias network, which helps in stabilizing the amplifier by reducing the phase shift and improving the phase margin. This is achieved by connecting an inductor (L4) from VDD to the bias reference node (n7) and adding a capacitor (C2) from the output node (n6) to ground (VSS), which provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals and helps in phase compensation.
