The binding constraint is the insufficient forward gain (s21_db), which is below the required minimum of 28.4931 dB. This is likely due to the second stage's weak transconductance and the lossy nature of the LC interstage network, which degrades the signal transfer from stage 1 to stage 2. The LC interstage network introduces impedance mismatches and phase shifts that reduce the overall gain and stability of the system, especially at the operating frequency of 2.4 GHz.

To address this, I restructured the interstage network by replacing the LC series connection with a capacitive coupling from the first stage's drain to the second stage's gate. This change eliminates the inductive component in the interstage path, reducing parasitic losses and impedance mismatches. Additionally, I added a capacitive coupling between the output of the second stage and the drain of the first stage to enhance signal feedback and improve gain. These changes simplify the interstage coupling, reduce signal degradation, and improve the gain margin at the target frequency.
