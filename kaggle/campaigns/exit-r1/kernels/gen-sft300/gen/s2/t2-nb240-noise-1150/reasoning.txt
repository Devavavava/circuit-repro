The binding constraint is the minimum stability factor (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is failing due to instability in the wideband range. The original topology lacks sufficient feedback or damping mechanisms to ensure stability across the entire frequency band of interest, particularly at frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (f0 = 2.4 GHz). This is likely due to the absence of a direct feedback path from the output to the input or a stabilizing element in the second stage. The LC interstage network may not be sufficient to suppress oscillations or parasitic feedback paths across the wideband range.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the second stage (node n6) to the first stage's drain (node n2) using a capacitor (ZC1), and connected it to a new internal node (n10). This introduces a stabilizing feedback mechanism that helps dampen oscillations and improve the stability factor across the wideband range. Additionally, I added two resistors (ZR2 and ZR3) to provide a controlled path for DC bias and to help stabilize the operating point of the transistors, especially in the second stage. These changes enhance the stability without significantly degrading the noise figure or gain.
