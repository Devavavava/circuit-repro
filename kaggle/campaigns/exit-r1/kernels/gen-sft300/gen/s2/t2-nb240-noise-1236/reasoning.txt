This leads to a negative margin in the stability factor, causing potential oscillations or instability outside the narrowband center frequency.

To address this, I introduce a feedback path from the output to the bias network, adding a resistor Rrep1 from VDD to the bias node n7 and a capacitor ZC2 from VOUT1 to n5. This creates a low-frequency feedback path that helps stabilize the circuit over a wider bandwidth. The additional resistor Rrep1 introduces a small amount of negative feedback, which increases the phase margin and suppresses oscillations. The capacitor ZC2 provides a high-frequency path for feedback, further enhancing stability without degrading the narrowband performance significantly.
