The failure is primarily due to the poor stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz), which is below the required minimum of 1.0. This is likely caused by the lack of sufficient feedback or damping in the circuit, especially in the second stage, which may be resonating with the LC tank and causing instability across a wide frequency range. The current topology lacks a mechanism to stabilize the second stage, particularly at off-band frequencies, which is critical for the mu_min constraint.

To address this, I added a resistor (ZR1) from the internal node n4 (which is connected to the LC interstage network) to VDD. This provides a small amount of damping at the second stage, reducing the resonant peaks and improving the stability margin. This change is structural, as it adds a new component to the existing topology without altering the core two-stage CS amplifier configuration.
