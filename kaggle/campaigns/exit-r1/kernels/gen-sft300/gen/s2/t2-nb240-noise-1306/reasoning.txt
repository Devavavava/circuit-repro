The failure is primarily due to insufficient gain (s21_db) in the current-reuse push-pull LNA topology. The binding constraint s21_db is marginally below the target by ~0.1985 dB, which suggests that the gain is not sufficiently boosted at the operating frequency. This is likely due to the shared DC path and the tuned load (L2+C2) configuration, which may be causing a reduction in the effective transconductance or an increase in the output impedance at the operating frequency, thereby limiting the gain.

To address this, I propose a structural change that introduces a direct inductive coupling from the output node to the power supply (VDD) via an additional inductor, L3, and adds a new internal node, n6, to the existing PMOS current mirror configuration. This change provides a more direct and stable path for the amplified signal, improving the gain by reducing the effective output impedance and enhancing the signal transfer from the active devices to the output. The addition of L3 helps in tuning the output impedance at the operating frequency, thus boosting the gain without increasing the power consumption or noise figure.
