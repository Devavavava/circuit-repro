The failure is due to the inadequate stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) of the two-stage LNA. The original topology lacks sufficient feedback or damping mechanisms to ensure stability across the entire frequency band of interest (0.01–50 GHz). This is particularly critical in narrowband designs where resonant components like LC tanks can cause unwanted oscillations or instability at frequencies near or far from the operating point.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output of the second stage to the input of the first stage using a resistor (ZR1) and a capacitor (ZC1), along with a shunt inductor (ZL1) at the input to help dampen resonant effects and improve stability. These components help to suppress oscillations and ensure a higher stability margin across the wide frequency range.
