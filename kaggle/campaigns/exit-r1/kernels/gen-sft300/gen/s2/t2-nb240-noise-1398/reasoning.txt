The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_db), which is failing by ~0.83 dB. This is likely due to the noise contributions from the transistor NM1 and the current-reuse structure, which can increase noise if not properly managed. The current-reuse topology, while beneficial for power efficiency, may introduce additional noise paths or reduce the effective transconductance at the input stage, degrading the noise performance.

To address this, I propose replacing the NMOS NM3 with a PMOS transistor, and reconfiguring the biasing network to ensure a more stable and lower-noise current reference. This change improves the noise performance by reducing the noise contribution from the current reference and ensuring a more optimal biasing condition for the input transistor.
