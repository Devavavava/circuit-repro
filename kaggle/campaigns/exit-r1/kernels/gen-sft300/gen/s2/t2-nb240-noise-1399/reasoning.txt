The binding constraint is the stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`), which is failing due to poor phase and amplitude stability across the band. This is likely caused by the LC interstage network (L2 and C2) creating a resonant peak that introduces a phase shift and reduces the stability margin at frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (f0 = 2.4 GHz). The current topology lacks a mechanism to dampen or control this resonance, leading to instability at the edges of the band.

To address this, I added a series resistor (ZR2) between VDD and the drain of NM1, and a capacitor (ZC2) between the bias node (n7) and the interstage node (n4). The resistor ZR2 introduces a controlled damping effect on the resonant peak, reducing the amplitude and phase shift caused by the LC interstage network. The capacitor ZC2 provides an additional feedback path from the bias network to the interstage node, helping to stabilize the phase response across the band. These changes improve the stability margin without significantly affecting the noise figure or gain.
