The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is failing by ~0.186 due to instability in the wideband range. This is likely caused by the second-stage NMOS (NM2) having insufficient feedback or damping, leading to potential oscillations at frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (f0 = 2.4 GHz). The current topology relies on LC tank circuits for tuning, but lacks explicit stabilizing elements in the second stage, especially at off-band frequencies.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between the source of NM2 and ground (VSS), and a resistor (ZR1) between the drain of NM1 (n2) and the node connected to the interstage LC network (n4). These changes introduce additional feedback and damping, improving the stability factor over a wide frequency range. The capacitor ZC1 provides a low-impedance path to ground at high frequencies, reducing the gain of NM2 in the unstable region. The resistor ZR1 introduces a controlled amount of negative feedback from the first stage to the interstage LC network, helping to suppress oscillations and improve stability.
