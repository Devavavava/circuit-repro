The binding constraint is the gain (s21_db), which is slightly below the required minimum by about 0.0015 dB. This suggests that the amplifier's gain is marginally insufficient at the operating frequency. The inductively-degenerated cascode topology, while effective in reducing noise and improving linearity, may be suffering from a slight reduction in transconductance or impedance mismatch at the operating frequency due to parasitic effects or the sizing of the inductors and transistors.

To address this, I propose a structural change that enhances the gain by introducing a shunt capacitor at the output node (VOUT1) to improve impedance matching and reduce signal loss. Additionally, I add a PMOS transistor in parallel with the NM2 cascode transistor to increase the effective transconductance and improve gain. This change also introduces a new internal node (n6) to facilitate the connection of the PMOS transistor and the new capacitor. These modifications improve the gain margin without increasing power consumption or degrading noise performance.
