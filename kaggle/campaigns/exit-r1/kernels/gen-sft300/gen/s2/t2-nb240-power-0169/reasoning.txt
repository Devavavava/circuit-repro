The binding constraint is the insufficient forward gain (s21_db), which is limited by the poor impedance transformation and signal degradation caused by the shared DC path and the lack of effective impedance matching at the output. The current-reuse topology in the anchor netlist uses a shared DC path through the NMOS and PMOS transistors, which introduces parasitic effects and reduces the gain due to poor impedance matching at the output. Additionally, the output is loaded by a simple LC tank (L2 and C2), which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation at the operating frequency.

To address this, I replaced the shared DC path with a separate DC path for the output stage, using a new NMOS transistor (NM2) and a resistor (Rrep1) to decouple the output from the shared DC path. This change improves impedance matching and reduces signal degradation. I also added a resistor (Rrep1) to provide a stable DC path for the output stage, and a capacitor (Crep1) to further decouple the output from the shared DC path. These changes help in achieving better impedance matching and higher gain at the operating frequency.
