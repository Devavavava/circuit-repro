The binding constraint is the insufficient forward gain (s21_db), which is limited by the poor impedance matching and poor transconductance performance of the current-reuse topology. The anchor netlist uses a push-pull configuration with a shared DC path and a tuned load, which introduces parasitic effects and reduces the signal swing at the output. The lack of a direct signal path from the input to the output also contributes to poor gain.

To address this, I introduce a direct signal path from the input to the output using an additional NMOS transistor (NM3) connected between n5 and n4, and a capacitor (ZC1) between n5 and n4. This creates a shunt feedback path that enhances the transconductance and improves impedance matching. Additionally, I added a capacitor (ZC2) between VDD and n0 to decouple the power supply and reduce noise coupling. The capacitor (ZC3) between n6 and VSS helps stabilize the bias node and reduce noise. These changes improve the gain and reduce noise, addressing the binding constraint.
