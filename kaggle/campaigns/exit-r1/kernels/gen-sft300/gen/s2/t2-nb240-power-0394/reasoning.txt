The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) at the operating frequency, which is critical for robustness across the band. The anchor netlist lacks a direct feedback mechanism that would help stabilize the system over a wide frequency range, especially in the second stage. The current structure relies on LC tank circuits for tuning and impedance matching, which are insufficient for maintaining stability at off-frequency points.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output (VOUT1) to the first stage's gate (n1) via a resistor (ZR2) and a capacitor (ZC1) in parallel with an inductor (ZL1). This creates a low-impedance path at the operating frequency, ensuring that the feedback stabilizes the system without degrading the gain or noise figure. The additional capacitor (ZC2) between VDD and n2 helps in shaping the frequency response and further improves stability by providing a controlled phase shift at higher frequencies.
