The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` fails due to poor stability across a wide frequency range. To address this, I added a feedback path from the output of the second stage (VOUT1) to the bias network (n7) via a capacitor `ZC1`. This introduces a low-frequency feedback path that helps stabilize the amplifier across the wideband range without degrading the narrowband performance. Additionally, I introduced an inductor `ZL1` between VDD and the output of the second stage (n6) to provide a high-frequency damping effect. These changes enhance the stability factor (μ) across the entire 0.01–50 GHz range while maintaining the desired gain and noise figure.
