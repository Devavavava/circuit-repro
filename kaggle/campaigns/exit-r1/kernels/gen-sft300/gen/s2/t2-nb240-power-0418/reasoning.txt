The failure is due to insufficient gain in the second stage, causing the overall $ S_{21} $ to fall short of the required minimum. The second stage's NMOS transistor (NM2) is loaded by a series inductor (L3) directly to VDD, which limits the transconductance contribution and reduces the gain. Additionally, the interstage LC network (L2 and C2) may be introducing unwanted impedance mismatches or phase shifts that further degrade the gain at the operating frequency. The biasing network, while effective for stability and noise, does not directly enhance the gain of the second stage.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (ZC1) from the gate of NM2 to VDD, which helps to reduce the Miller effect and increase the effective transconductance of NM2 by lowering the gate-to-drain capacitance at high frequencies. This improves the gain of the second stage and, consequently, the overall $ S_{21} $.
