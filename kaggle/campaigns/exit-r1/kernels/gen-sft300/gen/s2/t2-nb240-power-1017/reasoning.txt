The absence of a direct feedback or stabilization element between the two stages leads to a lower stability margin than required.

To address this, I introduce a shunt capacitor (ZC1) from VDD to node n4, which is the node connecting the LC interstage network. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path to ground at high frequencies, helping to stabilize the circuit by reducing the phase shift introduced by the LC network. Additionally, I add a resistor (ZR1) from node n5 (the drain of NM2) to VDD, which helps dampen any oscillatory behavior and improve the stability margin across the band. These changes add a direct feedback path and stabilize the interstage LC network without affecting the overall gain or noise figure.
