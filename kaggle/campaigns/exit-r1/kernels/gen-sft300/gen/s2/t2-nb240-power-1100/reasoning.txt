This is likely caused by the lack of a direct feedback path or stabilizing element in the second stage, leading to instability at frequencies outside the narrowband design point. The current topology relies on LC tank circuits for tuning and impedance matching, but these do not inherently provide stability across a wide range of frequencies.

To address this, I added a capacitor `ZC1` between VDD and the internal node `n4`, which is connected to the interstage LC network. This capacitor introduces a high-frequency feedback path from the power supply to the interstage node, helping to dampen oscillations and improve stability across the wide band. This structural change does not affect the narrowband performance but enhances the stability margin at off-band frequencies.
