The failure is primarily due to poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being violated by -0.31 dB. This indicates that the input reflection coefficient is too high, likely due to the lack of an explicit input matching network. The anchor netlist relies on the LC tank in the first stage for resonance but does not include a dedicated input matching component, leading to a mismatch at the operating frequency.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (ZC1) from the output of the first stage (n2) to an internal node (n3), and a series inductor (ZL1) connecting n3 to the gate of the second stage (n5). This creates a new resonant path that enhances input impedance matching at f0 = 2.4 GHz. The capacitor ZC1 provides a low-impedance path to ground at resonance, while the inductor ZL1 helps to tune the input impedance seen by the second stage, thereby improving the reflection coefficient (s11_max_db).
