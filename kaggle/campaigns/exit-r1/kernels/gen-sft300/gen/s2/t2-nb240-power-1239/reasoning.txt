The binding constraint is the minimum stability factor (mu_min) over a wide frequency range (0.01–50 GHz), which is failing by approximately 0.078459. This indicates that the circuit is unstable at certain frequencies outside the narrowband design center (2.4 GHz). The two-stage LNA with LC-tuned loads and interstage coupling may be causing resonances or feedback paths that reduce stability at off-band frequencies, especially due to the lack of explicit stabilization elements like resistive dampers or additional feedback loops.

To address this, I added a resistive element (ZR1) from the interstage node (n5) to ground (VSS), and a capacitor (ZC1) from the second-stage drain (n6) to VDD. These additions help dampen resonant modes and improve stability across a wider frequency range without significantly affecting the narrowband performance. The resistor ZR1 provides a low-frequency damping path, while the capacitor ZC1 helps suppress high-frequency oscillations.
