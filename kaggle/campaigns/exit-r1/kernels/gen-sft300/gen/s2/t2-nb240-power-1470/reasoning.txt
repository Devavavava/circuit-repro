The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which is failing due to poor stability across a wide frequency band. The original two-stage LNA topology lacks sufficient feedback or impedance control to maintain stability at off-band frequencies, particularly due to the resonant LC loads and the absence of any stabilizing elements in the feedback path.

To address this, a structural change was introduced: a feedback capacitor `ZC1` from the output node `n6` to the gate of the first-stage transistor `NM1`, and a resistor `ZR2` from the output node `n6` to `VDD`. These additions provide a stabilizing feedback path that helps dampen oscillations and improve the stability factor across the wide frequency range. Additionally, a resistor `ZR1` is added from the interstage node `n4` to `VDD`, which helps control the impedance seen by the second stage and further enhances stability.
