The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) at the operating frequency. The original topology lacks sufficient feedback or damping mechanisms to suppress oscillations outside the narrowband tuning, especially at frequencies beyond the LC resonance. This results in a negative stability margin, causing instability in the wider band.

To address this, I added a capacitor (`ZC2`) between the output of the first stage (`n2`) and the interstage LC network (`n4`). This introduces a feedback path that stabilizes the system by reducing the gain at higher frequencies, improving the stability margin. Additionally, I introduced a resistor (`ZR2`) from the output node (`VOUT1`) to ground, which helps dampen oscillations and further improves stability. These changes are structural and do not affect the core LNA topology or its gain and noise characteristics.
