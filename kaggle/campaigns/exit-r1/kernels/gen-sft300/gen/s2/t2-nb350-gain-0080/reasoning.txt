The failure is primarily due to insufficient gain from the LNA, as indicated by the binding constraint `s21_db` being 6.2082 dB, which is well below the required 25.6085 dB. The structure of the anchor netlist, while robust in other aspects such as noise figure and power consumption, lacks sufficient gain due to the configuration of the transistor stages and load network. The current-reuse topology, while effective in reducing noise, does not provide the necessary amplification at the target frequency (3.5 GHz) due to the limited transconductance contribution from the NMOS and PMOS stages and the load configuration.

To address this, I restructured the topology to enhance gain by introducing a cascode configuration for the NMOS stage and modifying the load network to increase impedance at the operating frequency. This is achieved by adding a NMOS transistor (ZN1) in a cascode configuration, which increases the output impedance and thereby amplifies the signal. Additionally, I replaced the DC-blocking capacitor (C4) with a resistor (ZR2) to provide a DC path and improve stability, and added a new capacitor (ZC2) to the output node to enhance the load impedance at the target frequency. These changes improve the gain by increasing the effective transconductance and load impedance, directly addressing the binding constraint.
