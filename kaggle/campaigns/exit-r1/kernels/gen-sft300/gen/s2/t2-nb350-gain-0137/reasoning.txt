The anchor circuit uses a current-reuse push-pull configuration with a tuned load, but it lacks a direct feedback path or a mechanism to dampen oscillations outside the narrowband tuning range.

To address this, I added a feedback path from the output (VOUT1) to the gate of the NMOS transistor (NM3), which is connected to a current mirror reference. This forms a negative feedback loop that stabilizes the amplifier over a broader frequency range. Additionally, I introduced a second NMOS transistor (ZN1) connected between the output and ground, providing an additional impedance-matching and stabilization mechanism. These changes enhance the phase margin and suppress oscillations, directly improving the stability metric.
