The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is limited by the poor impedance matching and the weak amplification of the current-reuse topology. The current-reuse configuration in the anchor netlist has a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2) that may not provide sufficient gain at the center frequency due to the mismatch between the input and output impedances. The lack of a direct feedback path also limits the gain-bandwidth product and stability, contributing to the low s21_db.

To address this, I propose a structural change by replacing the shared DC path with a separate DC path for the NMOS and PMOS stages. This allows for better impedance matching and improved gain. Additionally, I introduce a new capacitor (ZC1) between the output and the shared node (n5), which helps in stabilizing the output and improving the gain by reducing feedback losses. The addition of the capacitor ZC1 creates a more direct signal path from the output to the shared node, enhancing the overall gain without introducing additional noise or power consumption.
