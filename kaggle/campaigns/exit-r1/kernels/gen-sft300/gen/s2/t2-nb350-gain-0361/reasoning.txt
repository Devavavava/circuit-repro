The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` failing by -0.22 indicates insufficient stability margin across a wide frequency range. This is likely due to the LC interstage network (C2 and L2) introducing resonant peaks or phase shifts that reduce stability at frequencies outside the narrowband design point. The current topology lacks explicit feedback or damping mechanisms to suppress oscillations or resonant modes in the wideband context.

To address this, I added a capacitor (ZC1) from the output node (VOUT1) to the internal node (n5) of the second stage. This provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals to ground, thereby suppressing unwanted resonances and improving the stability margin across the wideband range. This change does not affect the narrowband performance at 3.5 GHz but enhances the stability at off-band frequencies.
