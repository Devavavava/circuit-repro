The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is far below the required minimum of 26.1318 dB. The anchor netlist employs a current-reuse push-pull configuration with a shared DC path, which likely introduces significant signal loss and impedance mismatches at the operating frequency of 3.5 GHz. The shared DC path and the use of a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) for biasing may also contribute to poor gain due to increased noise and reduced transconductance. Additionally, the lack of a direct signal path from the input to the output through the active devices may limit the gain-bandwidth product.

To address this, I propose a structural change that simplifies the signal path and improves the gain by eliminating the shared DC path and using a direct NMOS amplifier with a resonant load. This configuration allows for better signal amplification and reduces the number of parasitic elements that could degrade performance.
