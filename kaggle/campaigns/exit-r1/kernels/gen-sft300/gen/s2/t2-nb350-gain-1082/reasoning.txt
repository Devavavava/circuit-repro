The binding constraint is the insufficient gain (s21_db), which is failing by ~0.34 dB. This suggests that the second stage is not providing enough amplification at the center frequency, likely due to the interstage LC network (L2 and C2) loading the second stage's input and reducing its transconductance effectiveness. The LC interstage network may be causing a mismatch in impedance between the stages, reducing the overall gain.

To address this, I added a PMOS transistor (ZP1) in parallel with the second stage's drain load (L3), connected from VDD to n5 and n7. This PMOS acts as a current mirror, providing additional current to the second stage and improving its transconductance, thereby boosting the gain. The PMOS also helps in stabilizing the second stage's operating point, reducing the impact of the LC interstage network on the second stage's input impedance.
