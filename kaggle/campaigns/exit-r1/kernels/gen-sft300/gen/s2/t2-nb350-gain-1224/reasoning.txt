To address this, I propose a structural change by replacing the shared DC path with an isolated load for the PMOS stage, thereby decoupling the signal paths and reducing parasitic interactions. This is achieved by adding a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and n2, and replacing the NMOS diode (NM3) with a capacitor (ZC2) between VSS and n5. This change isolates the PMOS stage from the NMOS stage's DC path, allowing for a more independent and efficient signal amplification without compromising the noise figure. This structural modification should improve the gain by reducing the degradation caused by parasitic coupling and shared load effects.
