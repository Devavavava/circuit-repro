The binding constraint is the insufficient forward gain (s21_db), which is limited by the second-stage transistor NM2 and the LC interstage network. The second stage is underdriven due to the high impedance of the LC interstage (L2 and C2), leading to a weak signal transfer from the first stage to the second. This results in a suboptimal gain of ~19.2 dB, falling short of the required 29.3269 dB. The LC interstage network is not effectively coupling the two stages, and the second-stage transistor is not seeing a sufficient signal due to the impedance mismatch.

To address this, I replaced the LC interstage network with a direct connection between the first and second stages, removing the inductor L2 and capacitor C2 from the interstage path. This change reduces the impedance mismatch and allows for a more efficient signal transfer between the two stages. Additionally, I added a capacitor C2 to the output of the second stage to help in impedance matching and reduce signal loss at the output. This structural change improves the signal transfer from the first to the second stage, thereby increasing the overall gain and meeting the required s21_db specification.
