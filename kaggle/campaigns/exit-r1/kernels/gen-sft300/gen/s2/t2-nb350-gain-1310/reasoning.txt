The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which measures the stability of the LNA across a wide frequency range. The failure indicates that the circuit is marginally unstable at frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (f0=3.5 GHz), likely due to the lack of a feedback mechanism or impedance mismatch in the interstage network. The LC interstage network (L2 and C2) may be causing a resonant feedback path at certain frequencies, leading to a reduction in the stability factor.

To address this, I introduced a feedback path from the output of the second stage (n6) to the source of the first stage (VSS) via a resistor (ZR2), and from the output of the second stage to the gate of the first stage via an NMOS transistor (ZN1) with its source connected to VDD. This creates a feedback loop that helps stabilize the circuit across a wider frequency range. Additionally, a new capacitor (ZC1) was added between VDD and n2 to improve impedance matching and suppress unwanted resonances. These changes enhance the stability factor without significantly affecting the gain or noise figure.
