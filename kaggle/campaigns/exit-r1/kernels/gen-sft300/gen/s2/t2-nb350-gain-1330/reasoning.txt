This introduces a controlled feedback mechanism that stabilizes the amplifier over the wide frequency range. The capacitor ZC1 provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, helping to dampen oscillations, while the resistor ZR2 adds a small amount of negative feedback at the interstage node, improving phase margin and stability.
