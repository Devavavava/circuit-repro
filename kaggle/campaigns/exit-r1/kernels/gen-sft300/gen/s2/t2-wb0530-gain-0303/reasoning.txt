The failure is primarily due to noise figure (NF) being too high, which is caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the lack of noise isolation between the input and the biasing path. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) biased through R1 and R2, and a PMOS (PM2) biased through R4 and R5, which introduces noise coupling into the signal path. Additionally, the lack of a noise-isolating component between the input and the biasing network allows noise from the biasing network to propagate into the signal path, increasing the overall noise figure.

To address this, I have introduced a noise-isolating capacitor (ZC2) between VDD and n6, which is the node connected to the gate of PM2. This capacitor blocks low-frequency noise from the VDD rail from reaching the gate of PM2, thereby reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network. Additionally, a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) is added between VDD and n6, with its source connected to n1 (the drain of NM1), which helps in isolating the noise from the input signal path. This structural change improves the noise figure by reducing noise coupling from the biasing network into the signal path.
