The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the current-reuse NMOS (NM3) and the diode-connected PMOS (PM2), which are part of the bias network. These devices introduce additional noise into the signal path, especially at high frequencies, due to their non-ideal behavior and the lack of noise isolation from the signal path. The noise from these biasing devices is not effectively suppressed, leading to an increase in the overall noise figure beyond the specification limit.

To address this, I introduced a noise-canceling capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n5 (gate of NM3) and the output node n2. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency noise from the bias network to ground, effectively decoupling the noise sources from the signal path. This structural change reduces the noise contribution from the bias network, thereby lowering the noise figure without affecting the gain or stability of the amplifier.
