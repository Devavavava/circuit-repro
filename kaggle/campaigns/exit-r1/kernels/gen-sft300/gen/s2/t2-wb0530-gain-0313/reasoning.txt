The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the NMOS transistor NM3 in the bias network. NM3 is connected as a diode-connected NMOS (n5-n5) and is biased through R1 and R2. This configuration introduces significant noise due to the gate-source noise of NM3, which is not effectively suppressed in the current topology. Additionally, the current-reuse structure with PMOS PM2 and R4/R5 may not be optimally suppressing noise contributions from the bias network.

To address this, I reconfigured the bias network to eliminate the noise-generating diode-connected NM3. Instead, I directly connected the gate of PM2 to the DC path via a new internal node n6, and removed the diode connection of NM3. This change reduces the noise contribution from the bias network by removing NM3 from the signal path and simplifying the biasing structure. The NMOS NM3 is now connected as a standard NMOS with its drain and source tied to VSS, and its gate connected to the new internal node n6. This change reduces the noise contribution from NM3 while maintaining the necessary biasing for the circuit.
