The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contributions from the NMOS transistor NM3 and the current mirror configuration. The NM3 transistor is connected in a diode-connected configuration with its drain tied to VSS, which introduces significant noise due to its low impedance and the lack of a proper noise suppression mechanism. This configuration increases the noise floor of the LNA, pushing the NF above the specification limit.

To address this, the topology is modified by replacing the NM3 transistor with an inductor (L3) connected between the internal node n5 and VDD. This change decouples the noise from the current mirror reference and reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network. The inductor L3 acts as a high-impedance path at RF frequencies, effectively isolating the noise from the input and output paths, thereby lowering the overall noise figure of the LNA.
