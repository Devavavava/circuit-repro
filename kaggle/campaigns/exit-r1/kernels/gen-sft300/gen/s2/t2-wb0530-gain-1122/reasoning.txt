The failure is primarily driven by the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification due to the noise contribution from the NMOS transistor NM3, which is used as a current reference in the biasing network. The NM3 transistor is connected to VSS via R2, and its drain is connected to n5, which is tied to VDD via R1. This configuration introduces a significant noise path from the reference node to the signal path, degrading the noise figure. Additionally, the NM3 transistor is not directly connected to the signal path, leading to poor noise cancellation and increased noise contribution at the input.

To address this, I replaced the NM3 transistor with a resistor Rrep1, connecting n5 to n2 instead of to VSS. This change eliminates the noise contribution from the NM3 transistor by removing the active device from the signal path. The resistor Rrep1 provides a passive current reference, which is less noisy than an active transistor and avoids introducing additional noise sources. This structural change effectively reduces the noise figure and ensures that the circuit meets the nf_max_db constraint.
