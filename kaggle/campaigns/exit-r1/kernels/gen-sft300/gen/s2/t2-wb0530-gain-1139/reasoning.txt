The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by approximately 0.23 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure, particularly from the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and the resistor R1, which are part of the biasing path. These components introduce additional noise into the signal path, degrading the overall noise performance of the LNA.

To address this, I modified the biasing network to reduce its noise contribution. Specifically, I replaced the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) with a PMOS transistor (ZP1) and reconnected the biasing network to a different node (n6) to isolate it from the signal path. This change helps reduce the noise contribution from the biasing network by using a different transistor type and reconfiguring the biasing structure to minimize noise coupling into the signal path.
