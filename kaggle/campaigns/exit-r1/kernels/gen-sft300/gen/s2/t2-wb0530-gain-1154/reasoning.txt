The failure is primarily due to the poor input matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint being violated by ~0.225 dB. This is likely caused by the lack of a dedicated input impedance transformer or a more controlled input node configuration, which limits the ability of the LNA to present a low-impedance load at the desired frequency.

To address this, I introduce a dedicated input impedance transformer using an additional NMOS transistor (ZN1) and a capacitor (ZC1) to provide a more controlled and tunable input impedance. This change decouples the input from the shared DC path and allows for better impedance matching at the operating frequency. Additionally, I replace the shared DC path with a separate DC blocking capacitor (C1) and reconfigure the internal nodes to ensure that the input node (n0) is not loaded by the DC path, which improves the reflection coefficient and helps meet the s11_max_db constraint.
