The binding constraint is the input return loss (s11_max_db), which failed by ~0.08 dB, indicating poor impedance matching at the input. The anchor netlist uses a capacitive DC-block (C4) at the input, which introduces a high-impedance node at VIN1, causing a mismatch with the active device (NM1). The current-reuse topology, while effective for gain, may not provide sufficient impedance transformation at the input without additional tuning.

To address this, I modified the input coupling by adding a shunt inductor (L3) between VDD and n0, and a series capacitor (C5) between n0 and n1. This creates a resonant network at the input frequency, improving impedance matching and reducing reflections. Additionally, I replaced the NMOS diode (NM3) with a NMOS transistor connected to VDD instead of VSS, which helps stabilize the gate bias and reduce noise contributions. These changes enhance the input impedance matching and improve the overall stability and noise performance of the LNA.
