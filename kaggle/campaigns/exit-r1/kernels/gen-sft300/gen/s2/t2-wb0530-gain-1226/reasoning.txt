The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the NM3 diode-connected NMOS transistor in the bias network. In the anchor netlist, NM3 is connected to VSS via R1, forming a noise-generating path that is not effectively shielded or isolated from the signal path. This introduces additional noise into the system, particularly at the input node, which degrades the noise figure.

To address this, I reconfigured the bias network to isolate the noise-generating components from the signal path. Specifically, I moved the NM3 transistor to a separate node (n4) and connected it to the output node (n2) instead of VSS. This change ensures that the noise from NM3 is not directly coupled into the signal path, thereby reducing its contribution to the overall noise figure. Additionally, I connected the R2 resistor to the new internal node (n4), which helps in stabilizing the bias point without introducing noise into the signal path.

These changes structurally isolate the noise sources from the signal path, which should improve the noise figure and bring it within the required specification.
