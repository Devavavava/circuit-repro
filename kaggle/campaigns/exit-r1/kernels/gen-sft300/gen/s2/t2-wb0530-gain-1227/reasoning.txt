The failure is primarily due to poor input impedance matching, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint failing by ~0.25 dB. This indicates that the input reflection coefficient is too high, causing signal reflection and reduced power transfer at the input port. The original topology likely has a mismatched input impedance due to the lack of a direct DC path or insufficient impedance transformation at the input node, exacerbated by the inductive and capacitive elements in the signal path.

To address this, I added a direct DC path from the internal node n5 to VSS via a new NMOS transistor ZN1, which is connected to the input node n0 and VDD. This introduces a controlled impedance transformation at the input, improving the matching by providing a more predictable and stable impedance at the input port. The additional NMOS transistor also helps in balancing the signal path and reducing unwanted reflections.
