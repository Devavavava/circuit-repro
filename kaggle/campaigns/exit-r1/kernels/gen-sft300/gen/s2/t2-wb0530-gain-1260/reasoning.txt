The binding constraint is the input return loss (s11_max_db), which is failing by ~0.45 dB. The root cause is the poor impedance matching at the input port, likely due to the lack of a direct coupling between the input capacitor (C4) and the inductor (L1), and the absence of a second-stage impedance transformation mechanism. The original topology relies on a single inductor (L1) and capacitor (C4) for input coupling, but this may not be sufficient to achieve the required impedance match across the wideband frequency range.

To address this, I added a second inductor (L3) and a capacitor (ZC1) to create an additional impedance transformation stage. This new inductor (L3) is connected between the internal node n5 and the output of the first inductor (L1), while the capacitor (ZC1) is connected between the output of the first inductor (L1) and the gate of the NMOS transistor (NM3). This modification introduces a new impedance transformation path, improving the input matching by providing an additional degree of freedom for impedance tuning at the input port.
