The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being slightly above the specification, which suggests that the noise contribution from the input transistor (NM1) or the biasing network is too high. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) for biasing, which may introduce unnecessary noise due to its high transconductance and the lack of noise isolation from the signal path. Additionally, the noise contribution from the biasing resistor R1 and the current-setting resistor R4 may be contributing to the noise floor. The structure lacks a noise-isolating component between the signal path and the biasing network, leading to a higher-than-allowed noise figure.

To address this, I introduced a noise-isolating capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n2 (the drain of NM1) and the biasing node n5. This capacitor decouples the signal path from the biasing network, reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network into the signal path. This change is structural and does not require any changes to the existing device connections or biasing strategy.
