The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_max_db), which is exceeded by ~0.43 dB. This is likely due to the noise contribution from the current-reuse configuration and the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) in the bias network. The diode-connected NMOS introduces additional noise due to its high transconductance and the current mirroring, which increases the overall noise figure of the LNA. Additionally, the shared DC path and the lack of noise isolation between the biasing network and the signal path may be contributing to the noise degradation.

To address this, I introduce a noise-canceling NMOS (NM4) connected between the internal node n6 and VDD, with its source connected to VSS. This NMOS acts as a noise-canceling transistor, reducing the noise contribution from the bias network by providing a low-noise path for the bias current. This structural change isolates the noise from the bias network and reduces its impact on the signal path, thereby lowering the noise figure of the LNA.
