The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by ~0.43 dB. This suggests that the noise contribution from the transistor NM1 is not sufficiently suppressed, likely due to its high noise coefficient at the operating frequency. The current-reuse topology may be introducing noise through the NM3 and PM2 biasing network, or the NM1 is operating in a regime where its noise performance is suboptimal.

To address this, I propose a structural change that improves noise performance by reducing the noise contribution from the input transistor. This is achieved by replacing the NM3 diode-connected NMOS with a PMOS transistor in a different configuration. This change modifies the biasing path and reduces the noise contribution from the input transistor. Additionally, I added a capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the gate of PM1 to stabilize the gate voltage, reducing noise coupling. The addition of an inductor (ZL1) and a resistor (ZR2) to the output path helps in further noise suppression and improves the stability of the output signal.
