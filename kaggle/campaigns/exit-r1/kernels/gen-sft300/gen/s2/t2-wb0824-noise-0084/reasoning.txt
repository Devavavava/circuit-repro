The binding constraint is the stability factor `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`, which measures the stability of the LNA over a wide frequency range. The negative margin indicates that the circuit is unstable at some frequency within the 0.01–50 GHz band, likely due to poor feedback or poor impedance matching in the feedback path. This instability is likely exacerbated by the current-reuse topology and the shared DC path, which can introduce parasitic feedback paths and reduce the phase margin at high frequencies.

To address this, I replaced the shared DC path with a separate DC path for the NMOS and PMOS stages, isolating their feedback loops. This was done by introducing a new node `n5` and connecting the PMOS gate through an independent DC path using a separate resistor and diode configuration. This structural change isolates the feedback paths and improves phase margin, thereby increasing the stability factor across the wideband frequency range.
