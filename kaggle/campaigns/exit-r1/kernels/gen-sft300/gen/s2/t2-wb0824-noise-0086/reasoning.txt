The binding constraint failure is due to insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) across the wideband frequency range. The anchor netlist uses a resistive shunt-feedback configuration with a shunt-peaked inductive load, which, while effective for broadband gain and noise, may not provide sufficient phase margin across the entire band, especially at the edges of the operating range. This leads to instability, as evidenced by the negative margin for the mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz constraint.

To address this, I introduce a second inductor (L2) in parallel with the output capacitor (C2), creating a dual inductor-capacitor (LC) network. This adds additional phase compensation and improves the stability margin by shaping the frequency response of the output network. The second inductor provides a controlled phase shift that counteracts the phase lag introduced by the shunt peaking, thereby enhancing stability across the wideband range. This structural change is sufficient to meet the mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz constraint while maintaining the desired performance in other metrics.
