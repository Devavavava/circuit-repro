The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, failing by ~0.29 dB. This is likely due to the noise contribution from the current-reuse feedback path and the shared DC path, which introduces additional noise sources and reduces the effective noise performance. The structure also has a shared DC path (n1-n3), which may cause noise coupling and degrade the NF. Additionally, the current-reuse configuration may not be optimally suppressing noise from the feedback path.

To address this, I introduce a decoupling inductor (L3) and capacitor (C4) to isolate the noise-sensitive nodes, while also adding a separate biasing network (NM2, R1, R2) to ensure a stable and low-noise bias point. The new NMOS (NM2) and resistors (R1, R2) provide a dedicated biasing path, reducing noise coupling from the main signal path. The inductor (L3) and capacitor (C4) help to filter out noise from the shared DC path and improve the isolation between the input and output.
