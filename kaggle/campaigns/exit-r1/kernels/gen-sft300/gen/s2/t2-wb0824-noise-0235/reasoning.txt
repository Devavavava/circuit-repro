The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure. In the original netlist, the NMOS NM3 is connected in a diode-connected configuration to set the bias point, but this introduces additional noise due to the current mirror and resistive biasing network (R1, R2, R4, R5). These components add thermal noise and degrade the noise performance of the LNA, pushing the NF above the specification limit of 1.8408 dB.

To address this, the biasing network is restructured to minimize noise contributions. In the revised netlist, the NMOS NM3 is replaced with a PMOS PM3, and its configuration is changed to a current-source-like setup (connected to VDD and VSS with R4), which reduces noise by eliminating the resistive current mirror and diode-connected NMOS configuration. Additionally, the internal node n6 is connected directly to VDD, and the R5 resistor is moved to connect VDD to n2, simplifying the DC path and reducing noise coupling. The addition of Rrep1 and C1 in the new netlist introduces a feedback path that helps stabilize the noise performance and reduce noise contributions from the bias network.
