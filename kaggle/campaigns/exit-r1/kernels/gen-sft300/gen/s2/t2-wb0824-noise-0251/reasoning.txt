The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the current-reuse biasing network. In the anchor netlist, the noise from the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and the resistive biasing network (R1, R2, R4, R5) is not sufficiently suppressed, leading to an NF that exceeds the specification. This is exacerbated by the fact that the noise from the current-reuse path is not adequately isolated from the signal path.

To address this, I restructured the biasing network to reduce noise coupling. The original NM3 is replaced with a PMOS transistor (ZP1) in a diode configuration, and its gate is connected to a new internal node (n6) to decouple the biasing network from the signal path. Additionally, a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) is added to provide a low-noise path from VDD to the signal node (n1), reducing the noise contribution from the biasing network. This change isolates the noise sources and improves the noise figure by reducing the noise contribution from the bias network.
