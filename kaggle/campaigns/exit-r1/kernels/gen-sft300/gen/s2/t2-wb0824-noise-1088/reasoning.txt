The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 being too high. This is likely caused by the lack of a noise-canceling or noise-shaping mechanism in the current topology. The anchor circuit uses a current-reuse configuration but lacks a noise-canceling feedback path or a noise-shaping element, leading to a higher-than-allowed noise figure. The noise from NM1 is not adequately suppressed, and the noise from the bias network (NM3, R1, R2, R4, R5) may also be contributing to the noise figure.

To address this, I added a noise-canceling NMOS transistor (ZN1) and a capacitor (ZC1) to the circuit. ZN1 is connected from the bias node n5 to n1 and VSS, creating a local feedback path that helps cancel noise from the input transistor NM1. ZC1 is connected from n5 to n4, helping to filter out high-frequency noise from the bias network. These changes improve the noise figure by reducing the noise contribution from the input and biasing components.
