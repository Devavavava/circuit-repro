The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, failing by a small margin of -0.0176 dB. This suggests that the noise contribution from the biasing network or the transistors is slightly too high for the target specification. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and a current-setting PMOS (PM2) with resistive biasing (R1, R2, R4, R5), which introduces noise and limits the achievable NF. The noise from the biasing resistors and the diode-connected transistor is likely contributing to the excess noise floor.

To address this, I replace the current-setting PMOS (PM2) with an NMOS (NM2) connected to the same gate node (n5) as the diode-connected NMOS (NM3). This change introduces a feedback mechanism that reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network by stabilizing the gate voltage of NM3 and reducing the noise current from the current mirror. This structural change improves the noise performance by reducing the noise figure without compromising the gain or stability of the circuit.
