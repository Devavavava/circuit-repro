The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to the noise contribution from the current-reuse configuration and the lack of a proper noise-shaping mechanism. The original topology uses a current-reuse structure with a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for biasing, but it lacks a dedicated noise-canceling or noise-reducing component in the signal path. The noise from the current-reuse path and the biasing network is not adequately suppressed, leading to a higher-than-allowed noise figure.

To address this, I added a noise-canceling NMOS (ZN1) in a cascode configuration with an inductive load (L2) and a capacitor (C2), which helps in reducing the noise contribution from the current-reuse path. Additionally, I introduced a noise-shaping capacitor (ZC1) between the output and the gate of NM3 to suppress noise from the biasing network. These changes improve the noise figure by reducing the noise contributions from the biasing and current-reuse paths.
