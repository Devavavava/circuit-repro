The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) across the wideband frequency range. The anchor circuit uses a resistive shunt-feedback topology with a shunt-peaked inductive load, which, while effective for broadband gain and noise, lacks sufficient phase margin at higher frequencies. This leads to a marginal stability constraint violation, as evidenced by the negative margin for mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz.

To address this, I replace the resistive shunt-feedback topology with a common-gate (CG) stage in parallel with a common-source (CS) stage, forming a two-stage structure. This configuration enhances stability by introducing a low-impedance path at higher frequencies, thereby improving phase margin. Additionally, I replace the shunt-peaked inductive load with a series inductor and a capacitive load, which helps to further stabilize the circuit and reduce peaking at higher frequencies. This change increases the phase margin and ensures stability across the wideband frequency range.
