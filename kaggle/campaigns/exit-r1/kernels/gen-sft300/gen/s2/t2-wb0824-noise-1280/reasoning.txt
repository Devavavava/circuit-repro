The noise figure (nf_max_db) is the binding constraint, and it is failing by approximately 0.135 dB. This suggests that the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 or the biasing network is too high for the target specification. The anchor netlist uses a current-reuse topology with a diode-connected NM3 and a PMOS current source PM2, which may introduce noise due to the biasing network and the current mirror configuration. The noise performance is likely being degraded by the biasing network (R1, R2, R4, R5, NM3, PM2) and the shared DC path, which may not be optimal for noise suppression at the target frequency.

To address this, I replaced the diode-connected NM3 with a capacitor (ZC1) connected from n5 to VDD, effectively decoupling the bias network from the signal path. This change removes the noise contribution from the NM3 transistor and reduces the noise coupling from the biasing network to the signal path. Additionally, I added a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) in a feedback configuration from n4 to n5, which helps in stabilizing the bias point and reducing noise by providing a low-noise feedback path. These structural changes aim to reduce the noise figure while maintaining the required gain and stability.
