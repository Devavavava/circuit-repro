The failure is primarily due to poor impedance matching at the input, as evidenced by the s11_max_db constraint failing by ~0.17 dB. This indicates that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source, leading to signal reflection and reduced performance. The original topology uses a simple NMOS and PMOS cascode configuration with a current-reuse structure, but lacks sufficient impedance transformation at the input to meet the required reflection coefficient.

To address this, I added a series inductor (L3) and a capacitor (C4) to the input path. The inductor (L3) provides a low-impedance path at DC while offering high impedance at RF frequencies, helping to tune the input impedance. The capacitor (C4) helps in shunting high-frequency noise to ground, further improving the input impedance match. These additions enhance the input matching without compromising the gain or noise figure.
