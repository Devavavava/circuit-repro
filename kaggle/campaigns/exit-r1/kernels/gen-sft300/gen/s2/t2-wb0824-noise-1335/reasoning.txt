The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (NF) being slightly above the specification, likely caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for biasing, which introduces noise and degrades the NF. Additionally, the current-reuse topology may not be optimally configured for noise performance at the given frequency.

To address this, I replaced the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor (ZR2) and reconnected it to VSS, effectively removing the noise source from the signal path. I also reconfigured the biasing network by connecting the PM2 gate to a new internal node (n6) and adding a resistor (Rrep1) to VDD, which helps stabilize the bias point without introducing noise. These changes reduce the noise contribution from the biasing network and improve the overall noise performance of the LNA.
