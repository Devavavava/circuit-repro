The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is too high due to the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure. The NMOS NM3 is connected to VSS, which introduces a noise path from the biasing network to the signal path. This configuration increases the overall noise figure because the NM3 is not properly isolated from the signal chain. The PMOS PM2 is also connected to VDD, and its gate is controlled by a resistor R4, which introduces noise into the signal path. These noise sources are amplified by the LNA and contribute to the high NF.

To address this, I replaced the NM3 with a capacitor (ZC1) connecting n5 to n4, effectively decoupling the biasing network from the signal path. This change isolates the noise sources from the signal chain, reducing their contribution to the noise figure. Additionally, I replaced the PM2 with a capacitor (ZC2) connecting n6 to n3, which helps to filter out noise from the PMOS biasing network and reduce its impact on the signal path. These changes improve the noise performance by reducing the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure.
