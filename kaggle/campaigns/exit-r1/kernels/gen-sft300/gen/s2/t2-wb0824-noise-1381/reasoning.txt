The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) in the wideband frequency range. The original topology, while achieving good noise figure, gain, and input matching, lacks sufficient phase compensation to maintain unconditional stability across the entire 0.01–50 GHz band. The shunt-peaked inductive load (L1+R2) and resistive feedback (R1) are insufficient to suppress oscillations at higher frequencies, leading to a negative stability margin.

To address this, I introduce a second inductor (L2) in parallel with the feedback resistor (R1), forming a series inductor-capacitor (C1) network at the input. This modification provides additional phase compensation by introducing a zero and a pole in the feedback path, which helps to increase the phase margin across the wideband. The new inductor (L2) and capacitor (C2) work together to stabilize the amplifier at higher frequencies, ensuring unconditional stability.
