The failure is due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, exceeding the specification by approximately 0.17 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure in the anchor netlist. Specifically, the NMOS NM3 and PMOS PM2 are connected to internal nodes that may introduce noise or degrade the noise performance, especially at the target frequency. The current-reuse structure, while effective for gain and power efficiency, can introduce noise if not properly managed, and the biasing network (R1, R4, R5) may not be optimized for minimal noise contribution.

To address this, I replaced the NMOS NM3 and PMOS PM2 with a direct connection from VDD and VSS to the internal nodes n5 and n6, respectively. This eliminates the noise contribution from the NM3 and PM2 transistors by removing them from the signal path and simplifies the biasing network, reducing noise sources. The revised netlist ensures that the signal path is cleaner and the biasing is handled through direct connections, which should lower the noise figure and meet the specification.
