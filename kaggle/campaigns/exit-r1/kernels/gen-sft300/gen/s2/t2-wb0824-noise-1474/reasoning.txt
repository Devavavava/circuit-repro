The binding constraint is the input return loss (s11_max_db), which is failing by approximately 0.2 dB. This indicates poor impedance matching at the input port, likely due to the input network not effectively transforming the impedance to match the source and load. The current anchor netlist uses a simple capacitive DC-block (C4) at the input, which may not provide sufficient impedance transformation or filtering at the high-frequency operating point (f0 ≈ 1.386 GHz). This leads to a higher-than-allowed reflection at the input, causing the s11_max_db to fall below the specification.

To address this, I have restructured the input network to include an inductive component (L1) in series with the DC-block capacitor (C4), forming a series LC resonant circuit. This configuration improves impedance matching by tuning the input impedance to better match the source and load at the operating frequency. The addition of an inductor in series with the input capacitor provides a more controlled impedance transformation, reducing the reflection coefficient and improving s11_max_db. This change is structural, not involving any biasing or sizing, and directly addresses the impedance mismatch at the input.
