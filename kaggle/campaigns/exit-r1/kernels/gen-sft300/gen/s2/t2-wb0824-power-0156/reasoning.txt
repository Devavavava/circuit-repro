The failure is primarily due to the insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz) in the wideband operation, which is a result of the current-reuse topology and the shared DC path in the anchor netlist. This configuration leads to a narrow stability band, particularly at lower frequencies, due to the feedback paths and the lack of sufficient phase margin in the wideband range. The shared DC path between the NMOS and PMOS stages introduces parasitic feedback that degrades stability, especially when the circuit is sized for high gain and low noise.

To address this, I replaced the shared DC path with a separate DC path for the PMOS stage, decoupling the NMOS and PMOS stages to reduce parasitic feedback and improve phase margin. This is achieved by moving the PMOS stage's DC connection from VSS to VDD and using a separate internal node n6 for its DC path. Additionally, I removed the direct DC connection from the NMOS stage to VSS, and instead connected it to the internal node n5, which is tied to the bias network. This change isolates the NMOS and PMOS stages, reducing the feedback paths that contribute to instability in the wideband range.
