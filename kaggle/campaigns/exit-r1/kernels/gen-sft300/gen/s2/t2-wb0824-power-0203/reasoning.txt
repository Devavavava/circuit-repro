The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_max_db), which exceeds the specification by ~0.3 dB. This is likely due to the noise contribution from the current-reuse configuration, particularly from the NM3 diode and the R1 pull-up resistor, which introduce additional noise into the signal path. The current-reuse topology, while beneficial for gain, can degrade noise performance if not carefully managed, especially at high frequencies.

To address this, I replaced the NM3 diode with a PMOS transistor (PM3) connected to VDD, and added a capacitor (ZC1) between n5 and VDD to provide a low-noise path for the gate bias of PM2. This change reduces the noise contribution from the bias network and provides a more stable and lower-noise reference for the gate of PM2. The capacitor ZC1 also helps in decoupling high-frequency noise from the bias network, improving the overall noise performance of the LNA.
