This is likely due to the noise contribution from the NMOS biasing network (NM3) and the lack of noise-shaping elements in the signal path.

To address this, I introduce a noise-canceling NMOS transistor (NM2) in a feedback configuration between VDD and the internal node n5, with its source connected to n2. This addition helps to cancel noise contributions from the bias network and improve the signal-to-noise ratio at the output. The NM2 transistor is placed in a position that allows it to actively counteract noise from the biasing path, effectively lowering the overall noise figure.
