The binding constraint is the input matching (s11_max_db), which is failing by ~0.15 dB. This indicates poor impedance matching at the input port, likely due to the input capacitor C4 and the inductor L1 not providing sufficient reflection cancellation at the target frequency. The current topology may not be effectively suppressing unwanted reflections due to the mismatch between the input network and the transconductance stage.

To address this, I replaced the input capacitor C4 with a series combination of an inductor (L3) and a resistor (R3) between VDD and n0, and also added a capacitor (Crep) between n5 and VDD. These changes improve input impedance matching by introducing a tunable reactive component (L3) and a shunt capacitor (Crep) that helps in shaping the input impedance curve, thereby reducing reflections and improving s11_max_db.
