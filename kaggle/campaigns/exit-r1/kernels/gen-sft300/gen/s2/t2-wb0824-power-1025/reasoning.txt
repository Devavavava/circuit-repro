The shunt-peaked inductor (L1) and resistor (R2) combination may not be sufficient to stabilize the amplifier across the full 0.01–50 GHz range, leading to a marginally unstable system.

To address this, I replace the resistive shunt-feedback configuration with a common-gate (CG) stage in parallel with the common-source (CS) stage, forming a cascode-like structure. This change introduces an additional feedback path that helps improve phase margin and stabilize the amplifier over the wideband range. The addition of a PMOS transistor (PM1) and a capacitor (ZC1) between the gate of PM1 and VDD provides a high-frequency bypass, enhancing phase compensation and reducing instability. The PMOS transistor also helps in providing a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals, improving the stability and bandwidth of the LNA.
