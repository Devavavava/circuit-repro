The failure is primarily due to the noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, which is limited by the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 and the biasing network. The current-reuse topology in the anchor netlist introduces noise from the biasing resistors and the diode-connected NM3, which degrades the noise performance. Additionally, the shared DC path and the lack of a dedicated low-noise path for the input signal contribute to the elevated noise floor.

To address this, I replace the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor Rrep1 connecting n0 to VSS, and remove the PMOS PM2 and its associated resistors R4 and R5. This eliminates the noise sources from the PMOS current mirror and its biasing network, simplifying the biasing and reducing noise contributions. The resistor Rrep1 provides a stable DC path while minimizing noise impact on the input signal.
