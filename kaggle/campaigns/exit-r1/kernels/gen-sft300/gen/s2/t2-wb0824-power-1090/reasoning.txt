The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_db) exceeding the specification, which is driven by the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 and the biasing network. The anchor netlist employs a current-reuse topology with a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for biasing, but this configuration introduces noise through the biasing resistors (R1, R2, R4, R5) and the diode-connected transistors, particularly at the operating frequency of ~1.38 GHz. The noise figure is also affected by the mismatch and non-ideal behavior of the biasing network, especially in a wideband context.

To address this, the structural change introduces a new node (n6) and adds a capacitor (ZC1) between n5 and n6, which decouples the biasing network from the signal path, reducing noise coupling. Additionally, a new NMOS transistor (ZN1) is added between n6 and VDD, with its source connected to n2 and its body to VDD, providing a low-noise, stable bias reference. This change reduces noise contributions from the bias network and improves noise figure performance by isolating the signal path from noisy biasing components. This structural change also helps maintain the stability and performance of the LNA over the wideband frequency range.
