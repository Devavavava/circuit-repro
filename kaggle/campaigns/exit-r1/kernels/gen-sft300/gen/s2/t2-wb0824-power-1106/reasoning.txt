The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` is failing due to insufficient stability margin across the wideband frequency range. The original topology lacks sufficient feedback or damping mechanisms to suppress oscillations or instability at the edges of the band. The current-reuse push-pull configuration, while efficient, does not inherently provide adequate phase margin or stability across such a wide frequency range, especially when the load is tuned (L2+C2) and the input is DC-blocked (C4). This leads to a reduced stability factor (`mu_min`) at certain frequencies within the band, particularly at the lower end (0.01 GHz), where the phase shift accumulates more rapidly due to the lack of damping.

To address this, I added a shunt capacitor (ZC1) between VDD and the internal node n6, which is connected to the gate of PM2. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path to ground at high frequencies, introducing a stabilizing feedback mechanism that helps maintain the phase margin across the wideband range. This change enhances the stability factor by reducing the likelihood of oscillation and improving the phase response at the lower end of the band, directly addressing the binding constraint.
