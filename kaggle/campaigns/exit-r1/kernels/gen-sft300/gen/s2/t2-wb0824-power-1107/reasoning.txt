The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) over the wideband frequency range. The original circuit relies on a resistive shunt feedback and a shunt-peaked inductor load, which may not provide sufficient phase margin across the entire band, especially at lower frequencies. This results in a negative stability margin, indicating potential oscillation or instability.

To address this, I introduced a second inductor (L2) and a capacitor (C3) to create a series-peaking network at the output, which helps in enhancing the phase margin across the wideband. Additionally, a PMOS transistor (PM1) is added in a common-gate configuration to provide a low-impedance path for AC signals, improving the stability and bandwidth. The capacitor (ZC1) and resistor (ZR1) are used to provide a DC bias path for the gate of NM1, ensuring stable operation without introducing additional noise or power consumption.
