The noise figure (nf_max_db) is the binding constraint, failing by ~0.2 dB. This is likely due to the noise contribution from the current-reuse biasing network, particularly the NMOS NM3 and PMOS PM2 diode-connected transistors. These transistors introduce additional noise because they are not optimized for low-noise operation in this configuration. The noise is amplified through the current-reuse structure, especially at the target frequency, pushing the noise figure above the specification.

To address this, the diode-connected transistors in the bias network are replaced with resistive elements. This eliminates the noise contribution from the transistors themselves and simplifies the biasing path, reducing the noise floor. The resistors R4 and R5 are replaced with resistors Rrep1 and Rrep2, which provide a stable bias current without introducing significant noise. This change also improves the noise matching at the input, helping to meet the noise figure specification.
