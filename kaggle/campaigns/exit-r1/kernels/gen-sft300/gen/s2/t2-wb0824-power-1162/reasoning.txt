To address this, I propose adding a PMOS transistor (PM2) in a common-source configuration with a separate bias node (n4) and connecting it to the feedback node (n1) and output node (VOUT1). This introduces a second-stage active element that provides additional phase margin and stability by acting as a low-pass filter at higher frequencies, thereby reducing the likelihood of oscillation. The PMOS transistor also helps to isolate the feedback path from the output, improving stability without degrading the input match or gain. The addition of a capacitor (C3) between the bias node (n4) and ground ensures a stable bias point for the PMOS transistor without introducing unwanted coupling.
