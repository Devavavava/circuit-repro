The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, exceeding the specification by approximately 0.45 dB. This is likely due to the noise contribution from the biasing network and the diode-connected NMOS (NM3), which introduces additional noise into the signal path. The current-reuse topology, while beneficial for gain, may not be optimally configured for noise performance at this high frequency. The noise from the biasing resistor (R1) and the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) is not being adequately suppressed, contributing to the elevated NF.

To address this, the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) is replaced with a PMOS transistor (ZP1), which has lower noise at high frequencies due to its lower thermal noise and better noise matching in this configuration. Additionally, the resistor R2, which connects the gate of NM1 to the internal node n1, is removed to reduce noise coupling from the bias network into the signal path. This change reduces the noise contribution from the biasing network and improves the noise performance of the LNA.
