The failure is primarily due to noise figure (NF) being too high, exceeding the maximum allowed value of 2.2682 dB. The current anchor netlist uses a current-reuse topology with a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2), which may be introducing noise through the shared node and the DC path. The noise contribution from the DC path and the shared node likely increases the overall noise figure, especially at the operating frequency of ~1.386 GHz.

To address this, I restructured the circuit to eliminate the shared DC path and reduce noise coupling. The new netlist introduces an additional NMOS transistor (NM2) and a resistor (Rrep1) to provide a separate DC path for the NMOS amplifier stage, reducing noise contribution from the shared node. Additionally, the resistor Rrep1 serves as a noise-reducing feedback element, helping to stabilize and lower the noise figure. The capacitor C1 is now connected directly to the source of NM1, improving signal integrity and reducing noise coupling from the shared node.
