The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification by ~0.25 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution of the NMOS transistor NM3 in the bias network, which is directly connected to the gate of NM1 via R2. This configuration introduces additional noise into the signal path, degrading the overall noise figure. Additionally, the current-reuse topology, while beneficial for gain, may not be optimally configured for noise performance in this wideband application.

To address this, the NMOS transistor NM3 is replaced with a PMOS transistor PM3, and its connection is rearranged to provide a more noise-friendly biasing scheme. This change reduces the noise contribution from the bias network by decoupling it from the signal path, ensuring that the gate of NM1 is driven by a lower-noise reference point. The new PM3 configuration also allows for better control over the bias current without introducing unnecessary noise into the signal chain.
