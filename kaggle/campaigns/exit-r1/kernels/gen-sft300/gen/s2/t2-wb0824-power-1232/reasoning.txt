The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, and it is failing due to excessive noise contributions from the transistors and passive components in the current-reuse topology. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) for biasing and a PMOS (PM2) for current setting, which may introduce noise due to the non-ideal behavior of the diode-connected transistors and the mismatch between the current paths. Additionally, the shared DC path and the current-reuse configuration may lead to increased noise due to the coupling between the input and output paths.

To address this, I replace the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor (ZR1) connected between VDD and n5, effectively removing the noise contribution from the diode-connected NMOS. This change simplifies the biasing network, reduces noise from the diode-connected transistor, and improves the noise figure by decoupling the biasing network from the signal path. The resistor ZR1 provides a stable bias point without introducing the noise of a transistor, which is the primary cause of the NF failure.
