The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, which is caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the current-reuse topology. The NMOS NM3 and PMOS PM2 in the anchor netlist are part of the biasing network, but their configuration introduces unnecessary noise into the signal path, especially at the high frequencies of the wideband operation. The current-reuse structure, while beneficial for gain, can amplify noise if not properly managed.

To address this, I modified the biasing network by replacing the NMOS NM3 with a resistor Rrep1 connected between n5 and VDD. This change simplifies the biasing network and reduces noise contributions by eliminating the NMOS NM3 and its associated noise sources. Additionally, I added a resistor Rrep2 between n6 and VDD to stabilize the biasing of PM2, ensuring that the current set by R4 is more accurately maintained without introducing additional noise. These changes help reduce the overall noise figure while maintaining the required gain and power consumption.
