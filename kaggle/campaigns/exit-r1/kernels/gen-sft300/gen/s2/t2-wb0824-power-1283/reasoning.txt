The failure is primarily due to noise figure (NF) being too high, exceeding the specified maximum of 2.2704 dB by approximately 0.18 dB. This is likely caused by the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current-reuse structure, particularly the NMOS NM3 and the resistor R1, which introduce additional noise at the input stage. The current-reuse topology, while beneficial for gain, may be degrading noise performance due to the shared noise path and the lack of isolation between the input and biasing networks.

To address this, I modified the topology by replacing the NMOS NM3 with a PMOS PM3, which provides a lower noise contribution due to its lower flicker noise density compared to NMOS at high frequencies. Additionally, I added a PMOS PM3 to the bias network, connecting it to the internal node n5 and VDD, which helps in stabilizing the bias point and reducing noise injection into the signal path. This change improves noise isolation and reduces the overall noise figure by providing a more stable and lower-noise current reference.
