The failure is primarily due to noise figure (NF) being too high, exceeding the maximum allowed value of 2.2546 dB. The anchor netlist uses a current-reuse topology with a shared DC path and a tuned load (L2+C2), but the noise contribution from the biasing network and the current mirror (NM3, R1, R2) is not adequately suppressed. The NM3 diode-connected NMOS and the resistor R1 introduce noise that propagates through the signal path, degrading the noise figure. This is exacerbated by the lack of noise isolation between the bias network and the signal path, leading to an overall increase in the noise contribution at the input.

To address this, I introduce a noise-isolating structure by adding a capacitor (ZC1) between the input (VIN1) and the internal node (n4) where the tuned load (L2+C2) is connected. This capacitor provides a low-impedance path for AC signals at the operating frequency, effectively decoupling the noise from the bias network and the tuned load from the input signal. This change reduces the noise contribution from the bias network and improves the noise figure without compromising the gain or stability of the LNA.
