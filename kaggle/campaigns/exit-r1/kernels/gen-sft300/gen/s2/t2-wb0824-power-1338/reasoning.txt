The failure is primarily due to noise figure (nf_max_db) being too high, which is likely caused by the noise contributions from the biasing network and the lack of sufficient noise shielding in the signal path. The anchor netlist uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for current setting, which introduces noise into the signal path and degrades the noise figure. The noise is further amplified due to the lack of a low-noise, stable biasing scheme that isolates the signal path from the bias network.

To address this, I replaced the diode-connected NM3 with a resistive load (Rrep1) connected to the signal node n1 and VDD. This change decouples the noise from the bias network and provides a more stable and lower-noise path for the signal. Additionally, I introduced a new capacitor (ZC1) between the internal node n6 and the signal node n1 to provide a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals and reduce noise coupling. This structural change improves the noise figure by isolating the signal path from the biasing network and reducing noise contributions from the current-setting transistors.
