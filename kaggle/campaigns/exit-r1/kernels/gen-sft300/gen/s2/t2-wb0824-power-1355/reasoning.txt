The binding constraint is the noise figure (nf_max_db), which is exceeded by ~0.24 dB. This is likely due to the noise contribution from the input transistor NM1 being too high, possibly due to insufficient transconductance or poor noise matching at the input. The current-reuse topology in the anchor netlist may not be optimally configured to suppress noise at the target frequency, especially given the wideband requirement.

To address this, I propose a structural change that improves noise performance by modifying the noise matching and transconductance of the input stage. This is achieved by replacing the NMOS NM3 with a PMOS transistor (NP1) connected to a different node (n4), which helps in better noise cancellation and improves the noise figure by altering the noise contribution from the input transistor. This change also helps in improving the power consumption margin slightly by adjusting the current flow through the bias network.
