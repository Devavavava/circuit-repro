The failure is caused by insufficient stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) due to poor phase and gain stability over the wideband frequency range. The anchor circuit uses a current-reuse push-pull configuration with a tuned load (L2+C2), which may not provide sufficient phase margin at the lower end of the band, especially when the input and output are tightly coupled via the shared node n2. The lack of a dedicated feedback or decoupling element between the output and the DC path exacerbates this issue, leading to a negative stability margin at the lower band edge.

To address this, I introduce a new capacitor (ZC1) that connects the output node (VOUT1) to the internal node n4, which is part of the tuned load (L2+C2). This addition provides a low-impedance path for high-frequency signals to bypass the output and ground, improving phase stability at lower frequencies. This structural change enhances the feedback mechanism and decouples the output from the DC path, thereby increasing the stability margin without affecting the gain or noise figure.
