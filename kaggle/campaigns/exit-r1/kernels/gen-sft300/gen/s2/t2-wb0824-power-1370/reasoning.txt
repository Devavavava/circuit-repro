The noise figure (NF) is the binding constraint, failing by ~0.156 dB due to noise contributions from the biasing network and the current mirror. The original topology uses a diode-connected NMOS (NM3) and PMOS (PM2) for biasing, which introduces noise and limits the noise performance. Additionally, the current mirror (NM3 and PM2) is not optimally configured for noise suppression at the target frequency.

To address this, I replaced the diode-connected NM3 with a resistor (Rrep1) connecting n5 to n1, which simplifies the biasing network and reduces noise contributions from the current mirror. This change eliminates the need for a diode-connected transistor in the biasing path, reducing noise and improving the noise figure. The resistor Rrep1 provides a more stable and predictable biasing path, which is less sensitive to process variations and noise.
