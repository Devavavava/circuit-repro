In the anchor netlist, the NMOS NM3 is connected as a diode (n5-n5), which may introduce unnecessary noise due to its high impedance and lack of current steering. This diode connection is likely the source of excessive noise, especially since it is tied to the bias network (R1) and the DC path (R2), which can introduce noise coupling. Replacing this with a current source or a more controlled biasing structure would reduce noise.

To address this, I modified the NM3 connection from a diode (n5-n5) to a current source configuration by connecting its drain to n2 and its source to VSS, while keeping its body tied to n5. This change ensures that NM3 operates in a more controlled manner, reducing noise contributions from the bias network and improving the overall noise figure. This structural change is isolated and does not affect other parts of the circuit.
