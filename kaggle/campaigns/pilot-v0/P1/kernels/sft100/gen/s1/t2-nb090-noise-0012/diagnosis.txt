The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) caused by the shunt-peaked inductive load, which introduces resonant behavior and reduces stability across the band. The single inductor and resistor in the load network create a narrowband peaking effect, leading to a reduction in phase margin at higher frequencies, especially when combined with the feedback and input matching network.

To address this, I propose a structural change that introduces a second inductor in the load network, creating a dual inductor configuration. This change broadens the phase margin and improves stability by distributing the resonant effects across a wider frequency range. The second inductor (L2) is connected between VDD and a new internal node (n4), which is then connected to the output node (VOUT1) through a resistor (R3). This configuration introduces a second pole-zero pair, improving the stability margin over the wideband range without degrading the gain or noise performance.
