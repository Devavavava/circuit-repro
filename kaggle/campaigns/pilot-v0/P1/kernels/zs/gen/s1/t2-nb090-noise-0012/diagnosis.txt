The failure is due to insufficient phase margin in the wideband stability factor (mu_min_wide), caused by the shunt-peaked load (L1+R2) and feedback resistor R1 creating a resonant pole-zero pair that destabilizes the circuit at frequencies outside the narrowband center frequency (900 MHz). The shunt feedback and peaking network create a phase lag that reduces stability margins across the 0.01-50 GHz range.
