The failure is due to excessive noise figure (nf_db), which is primarily dominated by the input transistor (NM1) and biasing network noise. The current-reuse push-pull topology introduces noise from the PMOS stage (PM1) and resistive bias components (R2, R5), which are amplified in the noise budget. The LC load (L2+C2) is not the issue as S21 is met, but the noise contributions from the biasing network and input transistor are too high.
