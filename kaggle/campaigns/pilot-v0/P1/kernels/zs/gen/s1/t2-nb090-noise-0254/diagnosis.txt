The failure is due to inadequate input impedance matching in the first stage, causing the S11 (input return loss) to be too high (i.e., the circuit is not properly absorbing the input signal). The lack of source degeneration in the first stage (NM1) and the direct connection to ground (VSS) likely creates a low-impedance input path, degrading the reflection coefficient at the desired frequency. The LC interstage network and second stage do not sufficiently compensate for this mismatch.
