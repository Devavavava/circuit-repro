The failure is due to poor input matching, causing the S11 (reflection coefficient) to be too high (i.e., the input impedance is not properly matched to 50 Ω). The current topology lacks an explicit input matching network, relying only on the gate capacitance of NM1 and the LC interstage network, which are insufficient for narrowband matching at 900 MHz. The LC interstage (L2, C2) and drain inductors (L1, L3) do not contribute to input matching, leading to a mismatch and excessive S11.
