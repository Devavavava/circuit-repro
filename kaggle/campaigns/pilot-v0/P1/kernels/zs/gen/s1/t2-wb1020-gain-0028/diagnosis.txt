The failure is driven by the noise figure (nf_max_db) exceeding the specification, primarily due to the diode-connected NMOS (NM3) in the bias network and the resistive components (R1, R2, R4, R5) contributing thermal noise. The current-reuse topology with a push-pull configuration amplifies noise from the input transistor and biasing network, while the PMOS in the push-pull stage introduces additional noise and reduces the noise matching.
