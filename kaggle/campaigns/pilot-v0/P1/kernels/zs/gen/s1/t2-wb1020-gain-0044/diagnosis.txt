The failure is due to insufficient stability (mu_min) across the 0.01–50 GHz band. The shunt-feedback topology with a resistive feedback path (R1) and shunt-peaked inductor (L1) introduces phase shifts that degrade stability at certain frequencies, even though the DC bias and small-signal gain appear acceptable. The stability margin is compromised by the interaction between the feedback network and the inductive peaking, which causes resonant instability over the wideband.
