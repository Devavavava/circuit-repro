1. **Diagnosis**: The failure stems from insufficient stability margin (mu_min_wide) due to the current-reuse push-pull topology's inherent feedback and phase shifts. The PMOS/NMOS push-pull output stage creates a low-impedance path that destabilizes the circuit at wideband frequencies, especially when combined with the resonant load (L2+C2). The lack of a resistive component in the output path exacerbates this by allowing excessive phase shift and gain at critical frequencies.

---

2. **Structural Edit 1**: Replace the output capacitor (C3) with a resistor-capacitor (RC) series network to dampen resonant effects and improve stability. This changes the output coupling to a resistive-dominant path, reducing phase shift.
