1. **Diagnosis**: The failure stems from wideband stability limitations. The current topology's push-pull configuration with a tuned load (L2+C2) introduces resonance and phase shifts that cause the stability factor (mu_min) to dip below 1.0 across parts of the 0.01–50 GHz band, despite meeting narrowband stability at the center frequency (1.414 GHz). The LC load's narrowband tuning and push-pull symmetry exacerbate instability in off-center frequencies.

---

2. **Structural Edit 1**: Replace the tuned load with a resistive load and add a shunt capacitor for wideband stability. This reduces resonance and improves phase margin across the band.

```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n2 n3 VDD VDD
R Rload n2 VSS

C C3 n2 VOUT1

```

**Predicted Effect**: The resistive load (Rload) and shunt capacitor (Cshunt) will dampen resonance, improving wideband stability. The binding metric `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` should improve by ~0.15–0.2 (margin becomes +0.07–0.12).
