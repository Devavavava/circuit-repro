### Diagnosis
The primary failure is in the input return loss (S11), which is too high (i.e., poor impedance matching at the input port). The current structure uses a simple DC-block capacitor (C1) at the input, but this does not provide sufficient impedance tuning at 900 MHz. The first stage's input impedance is likely too high, leading to poor reflection coefficient (S11) and failing the -7.84 dB constraint. The LC tank in the first stage's drain (L1) and the interstage LC network (C2, L2) are not adequately shaping the input impedance.

---

### Proposed Structural Change (Edit 1)
Replace the first-stage input capacitor (C1) with a **series LC network** to explicitly tune the input impedance. This adds a resonant circuit at 900 MHz, improving impedance matching. The topology now includes a new inductor (L4) in series with C1.
