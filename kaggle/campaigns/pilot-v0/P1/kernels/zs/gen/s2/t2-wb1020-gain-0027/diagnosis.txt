1. **Diagnosis**: The failure stems from insufficient stability (mu_min) across the 0.01–50 GHz band. The current-reuse push-pull topology with an LC tank load introduces feedback and resonant peaks that degrade stability at higher frequencies. The LC tank (L2+C2) may resonate with parasitic capacitances, causing phase shifts that reduce the stability margin.

---

2. **Structural Edit 1**: Replace the LC tank load with a resistive load and add a shunt capacitor for wideband matching. This reduces resonant effects and improves stability via damping.
