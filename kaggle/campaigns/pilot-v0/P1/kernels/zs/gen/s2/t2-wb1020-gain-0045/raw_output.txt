1. **Diagnosis**: The binding constraint `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` fails due to insufficient transconductance gain at lower frequencies. The current-reuse push-pull topology splits the transconductance between NMOS and PMOS stages, reducing the effective `mu` at low frequencies. The tuned load (L2+C2) may also introduce phase shifts that degrade stability and transconductance performance in the wideband range.

---

2. **Structural Edit**: Replace the push-pull current-reuse topology with a **single-ended common-source stage with a cascode** to enhance transconductance and stabilize `mu` across the band.

```netlist
* lna-cs-cascode: Single-ended CS LNA with cascode for improved mu.
C C4 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 n1
NMOS NM1 n1 n2 VSS VSS
C C1 n1 n3
PMOS PM1 n3 VDD VDD
C C2 n3 n4
NMOS NM2 n4 n2 VSS VDD
C C3 n2 VOUT1
```

3. **Predicted Impact**: `mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz` improves by **~0.05** (reaches ~1.02), as the cascode structure boosts transconductance and reduces frequency-dependent degradation. S21 remains stable, but S11 may worsen slightly (~0.5 dB) due to increased input capacitance from the cascode.
