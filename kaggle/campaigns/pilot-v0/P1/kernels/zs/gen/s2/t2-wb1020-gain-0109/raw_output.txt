1. **Diagnosis**: The failure stems from insufficient stability (mu_min) over the wideband frequency range (0.01-50 GHz). The current-reuse push-pull topology introduces feedback paths and resonances (e.g., L2+C2) that degrade phase margin and stability, particularly at low frequencies. The tuned load (L2+C2) and push-pull configuration exacerbate impedance mismatches and phase shifts, causing mu_min to drop below 1.0.

---

2. **Proposed Structural Edit**  
Replace the push-pull topology with a **common-source LNA with source degeneration and a resistive load** to improve stability. This reduces feedback-induced phase shifts and provides damping.

```netlist
C C4 VIN1 n0
NMOS NM1 n0 n1 VSS VSS
R R1 n1 VSS
L L1 n1 n2
C C1 n2 VDD
C C2 n2 VOUT1
PMOS PM1 VDD n3 VDD VDD
R R2 n3 n2
```

3. **Predicted Impact**  
The stability factor (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) should improve by **~0.15** (from 0.92 to >1.07), as the resistive load (R1, R2) and single-stage configuration reduce resonant feedback and stabilize the phase margin. The push-pull configuration is replaced with a single-stage topology that avoids destabilizing feedback paths.
