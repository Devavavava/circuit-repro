The shunt-feedback topology with a shunt-peaked load (L1+R2) creates a narrowband stability trade-off, causing the stability factor to dip below 1.0 at certain frequencies. The shunt peaking enhances bandwidth but destabilizes the circuit at high frequencies due to phase shifts in the feedback path and load network.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
R R1 n1 n2
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
L L1 n2 n3
R R2 n3 VOUT1
C C2 n3 VSS
```

Predicted improvement: mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz improves by ~0.3–0.5 (reaches ~1.0–1.2), as series feedback and series peaking reduce phase shifts at high frequencies while maintaining wideband stability.
