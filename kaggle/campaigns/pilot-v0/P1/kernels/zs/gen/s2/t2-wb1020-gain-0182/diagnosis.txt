The failure is due to insufficient stability margin (`mu_min_wide_0.01-50GHz`) across the full 0.01–50 GHz band. The anchor design's shunt-peaked load and resistive feedback provide adequate stability at the center frequency but fail to maintain a minimum stability margin at band edges, likely due to phase shifts introduced by the inductor (L1) and feedback resistor (R1) at high/low frequencies. This causes instability in parts of the band, violating the wideband `mu_min` constraint.
