The LNA's failure is due to insufficient gain (s21_db) caused by the push-pull current-reuse topology's inherent trade-off between noise and gain. The current-reuse structure reduces transconductance (g_m) of the main amplifier, while the tuned load (L2+C2) may not provide sufficient impedance at the operating frequency (1.4 GHz) to achieve the required gain. The PMOS/NMOS push-pull configuration also introduces phase shifts and potential gain degradation.
