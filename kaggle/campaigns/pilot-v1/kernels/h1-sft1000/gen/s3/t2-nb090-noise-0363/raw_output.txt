

The failure is primarily due to poor input matching, as indicated by the binding constraint `s11_max_db` being -0.485 dB below the required -7.9123 dB. This suggests that the input impedance is not sufficiently matched to the source, leading to excessive reflection at the input port. The original topology uses a simple capacitor (C1) to block DC and couple the signal to the gate of NM1, but this configuration lacks sufficient impedance transformation or tuning to achieve the required match at 900 MHz.

To address this, I introduce an additional NMOS transistor (ZN1) in a diode-connected configuration, acting as a shunt impedance to the drain of NM1. This helps improve the input impedance match by providing an additional path for AC signals, effectively tuning the input network and reducing reflections. The new transistor is connected from VDD to n2 (the drain of NM1) and grounded, providing a tunable resistive component at RF frequencies. This structural change enhances input matching without adding extra inductors or complicating the topology.

NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
