### DIAGNOSIS:
The binding constraint is **S11_db = -2.589 dB**, which fails the required **≤ -10 dB** (i.e., margin of -0.74 dB). The circuit exhibits poor input match due to excessive input impedance mismatch at 1.575 GHz. The primary cause lies in the **input network topology**: the series inductor L1 (between VIN1 and n1) and shunt capacitor C1 (from n1 to ground) form a low-pass LC tank, but the shunt path to ground is dominated by the gate of NMOS NM1 at node n2 (connected via R2 and R1), which presents a high impedance at RF. This creates a **high input impedance at the input port**, severely degrading S11. Additionally, the NMOS NM2 and PMOS PM2 are floating-gate (source and gate tied), acting as **diodes or biasing elements with no active function**, effectively creating parasitic paths and poor DC/RF isolation. These floating devices contribute to **unwanted loading and phase shift**, worsening input match. The main issue is **lack of intentional input impedance transformation** and **uncontrolled RF grounding** via the NMOS/PMOS gate nodes.
