```

**Predicted effect on binding metric (s21_db):** Improves by ~6 dB. Adding a PMOS (PM1) from the output node (n2) to the gate of NM1 (n3) introduces a passive feedback path that stabilizes the gain and increases output impedance. This creates a current buffer effect at RF, reducing output loading and improving forward gain. The PMOS acts as a passive gain booster, enhancing S21 without introducing active feedback complexity. This structural change targets the core gain limitation while preserving stability and power efficiency.
