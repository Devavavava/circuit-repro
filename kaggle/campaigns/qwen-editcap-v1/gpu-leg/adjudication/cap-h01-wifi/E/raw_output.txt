**Prediction**: NF_db improves by ~0.4 dB.  
*Reasoning*: Adding a source-degenerated NMOS (NM4) between n3 and n5 (the common source node) introduces local feedback, reducing transconductance variation and increasing linearity. The degeneration improves NF without significantly degrading gain.
