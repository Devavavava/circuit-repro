### DIAGNOSIS:
The binding constraint is **NF_db = 1.888 dB (margin -0.259)**, which exceeds the 1.5 dB target. The primary physical limitation is **excessive noise from the input stage**, exacerbated by **high input-referred noise current** due to the **parasitic source degeneration and low transconductance gain**. The NMOS NM1 is the input transistor but is operated with **very low transconductance** (likely due to poor biasing or lack of effective gate drive), and the **source is not properly degenerated** for noise performance. Additionally, **PMOS PM1 is in a floating-gate configuration** (gate tied to drain, no gate control), making it ineffective as a current source and contributing to **high noise and poor gain**. The **NMOS NM2 and PMOS PM2 are disconnected (gate tied to source)**, acting as diodes or resistors, further degrading performance. The **input impedance is poorly matched** due to the lack of an intentional input matching network, and **the output is not properly terminated**. The **current consumption is slightly over budget**, but that is not the dominant failure — the **noise figure is the main bottleneck**.
