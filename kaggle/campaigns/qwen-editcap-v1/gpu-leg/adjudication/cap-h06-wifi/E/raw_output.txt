**Prediction**: NF_db improves by ~0.5 dB.  
*Rationale*: The current topology has a floating gate node (n2) for NM1 biased through a resistive chain that is sensitive to process and temperature variations, leading to suboptimal biasing and degraded noise figure. This stabilizes the transconductance and reduces thermal noise. The PM3 acts as a current source in the feedback loop, improving the input stage’s noise performance without adding DC power consumption.

**Prediction**: NF_db improves by ~0.6 dB.  
*Rationale*: The primary failure is due to poor noise matching and high input-referred noise. The gate of NM1 (n2) is floating except through resistors, leading to a high impedance node that is sensitive to noise and instability. Adding C5 (a capacitor from n2 to VSS) creates a low-impedance AC ground at the gate, which stabilizes the gate voltage and reduces gate noise. This is a standard technique in LNA design: bypassing the gate to ground at RF reduces the effective noise contribution from the input transistor. This directly improves the noise figure by reducing the input-referred thermal noise and improving matching.
