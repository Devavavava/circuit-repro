The binding constraint is **S11_db = -0.69 dB**, which fails the target of **≤ -14 dB** by **~13.3 dB** (margin = -0.95). This indicates **poor input match** — the LNA is reflecting too much signal at the input. The root cause lies in the **input impedance mismatch** due to the **high input inductance (L1)** and **lack of proper input impedance shaping**.

In the anchor netlist:
- The input is driven through **C1 (VIN1 → n1)** and **L1 (n1 → n3)**.
- The gate of NM1 is at **n3**, and the source is connected to **n4 (VSS)** via **NM1**, with the drain at **n5**.

However, **NM2 is not properly configured** — it is **diode-connected (gate to drain)** but **drain is at VDD**, so it's not functioning as a biasing device. Instead, **R1 and R2** form a **voltage divider**, and **NM3** is **diode-connected (D=S=VSS)**, which **shorts VDD to VSS**, **violating the power supply**.

This is a **critical error**: **NM3 has D=S=VSS**, so it acts as a **short between VDD and VSS** — **this is a fatal structural flaw**.

The **real cause** of the failure:

Thus, the **primary structural flaw** is **NM3 creating a VDD-VSS short** → **circuit is broken**.
