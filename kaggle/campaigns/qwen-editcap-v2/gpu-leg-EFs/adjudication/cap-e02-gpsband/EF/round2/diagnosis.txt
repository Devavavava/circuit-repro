DIAGNOSIS: The failure stems from insufficient transconductance (gm) and load impedance (RL) in the common-source stage (NM1), limiting voltage gain (s21_db). The diode-connected NM2 load (R1/R2 gate bias) provides low output impedance, while the shunt inductor L1 reduces gate-source impedance at f0. Structural edits must decouple biasing from AC impedance, increase RL, or add gain stages.
