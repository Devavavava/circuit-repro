DIAGNOSIS: The circuit fails to meet the minimum S21 gain requirement (6.12 dB vs required 10 dB) due to a suboptimal gain topology. The cascode structure (NM2) limits transconductance efficiency while the series resistor R2 at the gate of NM1 introduces unnecessary signal attenuation. The current shunt inductors (L3) and capacitors form an impedance-matched but low-gain configuration.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n1
C C2 n2 VOUT1
L L1 n1 n3

L L3 VDD n2
NMOS NM1 n5 n3 n4 VSS
NMOS NM3 n6 n6 VSS VSS
R R1 VDD n6
L L4 n3 VSS
```
