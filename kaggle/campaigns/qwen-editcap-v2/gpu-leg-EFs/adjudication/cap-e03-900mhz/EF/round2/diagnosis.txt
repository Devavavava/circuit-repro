DIAGNOSIS:
The binding constraint (s21_db) is limited by insufficient transconductance (gm) in the original cascode configuration. NMOS1's gate is over-biased by the R1-R2 resistor chain, which starves current and reduces gm. The cascode structure (NMOS1/NMOS2) also adds a voltage drop that further limits headroom, reducing the available transconductance. The inductor L1 in series with the gate acts as a high-impedance choke at 915 MHz, attenuating the input signal before it reaches the transistor. This structural limitation creates a fundamental gain bottleneck.
