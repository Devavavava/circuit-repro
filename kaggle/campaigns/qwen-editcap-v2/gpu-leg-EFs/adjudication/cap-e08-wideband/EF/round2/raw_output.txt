DIAGNOSIS: The circuit fails to meet the S11_max_db requirement (-3.22 dB vs. -8 dB) due to poor input impedance matching. The existing series inductor L1 and resistor-based gate biasing for NM1 create a high input impedance mismatch at 1.75 GHz. The diode-connected NM2/PM2 biasing topology lacks sufficient AC impedance control to shape the input match. The input capacitor C1 and inductor L1 form a series LC but are insufficient to cancel the transistor's input reactance over the wideband.
