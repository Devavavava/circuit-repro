DIAGNOSIS: The binding constraint is noise figure (nf_db=2.18 vs target 1.8). The primary physical limitation is the suboptimal gate biasing network for NM1, which introduces excess thermal noise via R2. The resistor chain (R1-R2) degrades NF by adding Johnson-Nyquist noise to the gate node (n3). Additionally, the cascode structure (NM1-NM2) inherently increases noise due to the second transistor's thermal contribution, while the diode-connected NM3 current source is inefficient in reducing gate noise.
