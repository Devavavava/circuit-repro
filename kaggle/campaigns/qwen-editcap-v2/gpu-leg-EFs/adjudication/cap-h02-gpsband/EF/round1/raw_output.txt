DIAGNOSIS: The primary failure is due to poor input impedance matching (S11_db = -0.22 dB vs required -12 dB). The gate bias network (R1-R2) creates a high-impedance path that fails to resonate with C1/L1 at 1.58 GHz, leading to severe mismatch. The LC network (C1-L1) is improperly configured to achieve 50Ω matching at the gate of NM1. Additionally, the diode-connected NM3 resistor (R1-R2) introduces series resistance that degrades input matching.
