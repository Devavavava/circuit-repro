DIAGNOSIS: The circuit's S11 failure stems from poor input impedance matching caused by the high gate resistance (R2) creating a resistive load at the gate of NM1. The series inductor L1 and capacitor C1 form a high-pass filter, but the resistive gate bias path (R2) dominates at the design frequency, leading to a high input VSWR. The gate bias network (R1+R2) introduces a high AC impedance at the gate, preventing proper matching to the 50Ω source.
