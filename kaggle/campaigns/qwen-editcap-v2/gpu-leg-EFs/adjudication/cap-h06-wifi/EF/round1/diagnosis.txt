DIAGNOSIS: The primary limitation is the resistor R2 (n6-n2) in the gate path of NM1, which contributes thermal noise degrading the noise figure. The NM1 gate is biased through a resistive divider (R1-R2), which inherently adds noise. The PMOS PM1 operates in a common-source configuration without cascode isolation, allowing noise from the output to couple back. The diode-connected NM2/PM2 bias networks are passive but do not mitigate noise. Reducing resistive elements in the signal path and improving transistor-level noise matching are critical.
