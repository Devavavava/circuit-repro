DIAGNOSIS:  
The binding constraint (NF) fails due to the input transistor's gate biasing network (R1-R2-NM2) introducing excessive thermal noise and poor noise matching. The common-source topology with series inductor (L1) at the gate is inherently noisier compared to alternatives like common-gate or cascode with noise-optimized biasing. The resistor-based biasing chain (R1-R2) and diode-connected NM2/PM2 add thermal noise and degrade noise figure, while the PMOS load (PM1) contributes additional noise due to its lower transconductance efficiency.
