DIAGNOSIS: The binding constraint (S11_db) is failing due to poor input impedance matching. The original topology uses a series inductor (L1) and capacitor (C1) for input matching, but this LC network is not resonating properly with the gate capacitance of NM1 at 1.58 GHz. The gate bias resistor chain (R1-R2) introduces excessive series resistance at the input, degrading return loss. Additionally, the NMOS-based biasing lacks a low-impedance path for RF signals, causing impedance mismatch.
