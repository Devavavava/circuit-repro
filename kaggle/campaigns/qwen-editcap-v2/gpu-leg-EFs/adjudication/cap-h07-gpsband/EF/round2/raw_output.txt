DIAGNOSIS: The original circuit fails due to poor input matching (S11_db -0.69 dB vs -14 dB required). The LC tank (C1-L1) and resistor chain (R1-R2) create an impedance mismatch at 1.58 GHz. The NMOS common-source stage's gate is resistively biased (R2), which adds series resistance and degrades matching. The NM2 active load (NMOS) lacks DC conduction path flexibility, further complicating matching.
