DIAGNOSIS: The binding constraint (s11_max_db) is failing due to poor input impedance matching. The original topology uses a series inductor (L1) and capacitor (C1) to match the input, but this LC network is not achieving sufficient impedance transformation to meet the -15 dB S11 requirement. The gate node (n2) is loaded by the resistor chain (R1-R2) and capacitor (C2), degrading the matching at the input. The NMOS input stage's transconductance and the LC network's Q factor are insufficient to achieve the required reflection coefficient.
