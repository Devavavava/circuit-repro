DIAGNOSIS: The primary failure is input impedance mismatch (S11_db = -1.59 dB vs required -10 dB), caused by the gate biasing network loading the input. The NMOS gate is biased through a high-impedance resistor chain (R1+R2) that dominates at RF, presenting a resistive load to the input matching network (L1+C1). The series L1+C1 combination fails to resonate with the gate's input capacitance (C2 + NMOS Cgs) at 1.58 GHz, resulting in poor impedance transformation. The diode-connected PMOS/NMOS pairs (PM2/NM2) contribute no RF function and are likely parasitic.
