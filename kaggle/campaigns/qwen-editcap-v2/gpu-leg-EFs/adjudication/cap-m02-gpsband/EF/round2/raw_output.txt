DIAGNOSIS:  
The primary failure is **input impedance mismatch**, indicated by the S11 margin of -0.84 dB (-1.59 dB achieved vs -10 dB target). The input matching network (C1 + L1) lacks sufficient tuning to resonate at 1.58 GHz, resulting in poor impedance transformation. Additionally, the gate bias resistor chain (R1+R2) introduces series resistance that degrades both input matching and noise figure. The original topology's inductive peaking (L1) is insufficient to cancel the capacitive reactance of the transistor's input, leading to high return loss.
