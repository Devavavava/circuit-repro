DIAGNOSIS: The primary failure is poor input matching (S11_db=-1.84 dB vs -12 dB requirement). The original topology uses a series inductor (L1) and series capacitor (C1) for input matching, which fails to achieve the required impedance transformation at 1.58 GHz. The gate bias resistor chain (R1-R2) creates a high-impedance node at n2, exacerbating the mismatch. The PMOS cascode (PM1) and NMOS (NM1) configuration lacks proper inductive degeneration or shunt elements to cancel reactive components.
