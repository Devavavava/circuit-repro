1. **Diagnosis**: The s21_db failure (9.79 dB < 10 dB) is primarily due to insufficient voltage gain in the cascode topology. The NMOS cascode (NM2) limits the output impedance and transconductance efficiency of NM1, reducing overall gain. Additionally, the series inductor L1 at the input may introduce parasitic losses or impedance mismatches at 1.58 GHz.

---

2. **Structural Edits**:

```netlist
# Edit 1: Replace NMOS cascode with PMOS current mirror load for higher gain
C C1 VIN1 n1
C C2 n2 VOUT1
L L1 n1 n3
L L2 n4 VSS
PMOS PM1 n2 n5 VDD VDD  # Active load (current mirror)
NMOS NM1 n5 n3 VSS VSS   # Common-source stage
NMOS NM3 n6 n6 VSS VSS

```

**Prediction**: s21_db improves by ~0.5 dB (increased gain from PMOS active load).
