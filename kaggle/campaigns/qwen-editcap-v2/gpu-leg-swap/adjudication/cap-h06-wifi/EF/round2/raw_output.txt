### DIAGNOSIS  
The binding constraint (nf_db=1.89 dB vs. 1.5 dB) fails due to:  
1. **Resistor R2 in NM1's gate bias chain** (VDD→n6(R1)→n2(R2)) introduces thermal noise.  
2. **PM1 diode-connected load** has poor noise performance compared to a current-source load.  
3. **Poor input matching**: The inductor L1/C1 series resonance may not optimally match the input impedance for minimum noise.
