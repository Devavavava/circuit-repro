The failure is due to insufficient gain (s21_db) in the common-gate topology. The inherent low gain of the common-gate configuration, combined with the inductive source match and resonant drain load, fails to achieve the required 12 dB gain at 1.6 GHz. The low transconductance (gm) of the single NMOS stage and lack of impedance boosting mechanisms limit the amplification.
