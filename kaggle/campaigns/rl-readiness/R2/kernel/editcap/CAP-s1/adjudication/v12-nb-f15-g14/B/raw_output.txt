1. **Diagnosis**: The common-gate (CG) topology inherently limits gain due to its transconductance-limited nature (gain ≈ 1/gm). The drain load (L2) is reactive and cannot provide sufficient impedance to boost gain to 14 dB. Additionally, the CG input matching (L1/C1) prioritizes broadband impedance but sacrifices gain, leading to insufficient S21.
