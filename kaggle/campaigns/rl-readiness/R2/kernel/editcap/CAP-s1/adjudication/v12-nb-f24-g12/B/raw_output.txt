1. The LNA's low gain (s21_db) is due to the common-gate topology's inherent limitations. The source-degenerated input and inductive load (L2) reduce transconductance gain and fail to provide sufficient voltage amplification at 2.4 GHz. The feedback resistor R2 and source inductor L1 also introduce impedance mismatches that degrade gain.

```netlist
C C1 VIN1 n0
L L1 n0 VSS
NMOS NM1 n2 n1 VSS VSS
R Rload n2 VDD
C C2 n2 VOUT1
R Rbias VDD n3
NMOS NM3 n3 n3 VSS VSS
R Rfb n3 n1
C Cbyp n1 VSS
```
**Predicted change**: s21_db improves by ~4 dB (common-source topology increases gain by leveraging higher transconductance and resistive load).
