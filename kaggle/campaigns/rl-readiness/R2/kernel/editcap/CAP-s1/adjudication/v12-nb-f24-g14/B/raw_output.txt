1. **Diagnosis**: The binding constraint (S21_db) fails due to insufficient gain in the common-gate topology. The output load (inductor L2) provides high impedance but lacks sufficient transconductance amplification. The drain-to-source voltage gain in common-gate configurations is inherently limited by the output impedance and load matching, exacerbated by the inductive load's frequency-dependent behavior, leading to a suboptimal S21.
