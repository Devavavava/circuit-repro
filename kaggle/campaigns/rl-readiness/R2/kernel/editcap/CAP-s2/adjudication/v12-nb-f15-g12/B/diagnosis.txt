### Diagnosis
The binding constraint failure (s21_db = 8.45 dB < 12 dB) is caused by **insufficient transconductance contribution** and **poor output impedance matching** in the common-gate topology. The tuned inductive load (L2) provides poor real-part impedance at 1.6 GHz, and the common-gate configuration inherently limits gain due to its low input impedance and lack of direct voltage amplification. The output is also poorly matched to the load, leading to significant signal reflection and loss.
