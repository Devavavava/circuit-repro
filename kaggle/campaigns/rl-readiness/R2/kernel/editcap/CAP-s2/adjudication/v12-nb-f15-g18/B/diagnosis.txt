The LNA's failure is due to the **common-gate topology's inherently low gain** and **poor output impedance matching**, exacerbated by the reactive load (L2) and DC-blocking capacitor (C2) at the output. The s21 is critically low because the drain load (L2) and output capacitor (C2) do not provide sufficient impedance transformation or bandwidth for high-gain signal transfer at 1.6 GHz. The structure lacks direct signal amplification from input to output, relying instead on a low-impedance source input with a reactive load, which cannot deliver the required 18 dB gain.
