1. **Diagnosis**: The common-gate (CG) topology inherently limits gain due to the transconductance not being directly amplified. The CG configuration's output impedance is dominated by the load inductor (L2), which may not provide sufficient impedance to achieve the required 16 dB gain at 2.4 GHz. The low gain (achieved ~9.5 dB) is the binding constraint, likely due to insufficient amplification from the CG stage.
