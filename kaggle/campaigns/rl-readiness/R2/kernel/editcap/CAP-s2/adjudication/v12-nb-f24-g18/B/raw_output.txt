1. **Diagnosis**: The common-gate topology inherently has low gain (S21) due to the transconductance being multiplied by a low output impedance (inductive load). The inductor L2 as a load provides insufficient impedance at 2.4 GHz, and the output capacitor C2 may short the AC signal. The gain is further limited by the lack of a high-impedance load and poor transconductance utilization in the CG configuration.

---

2. **Structural Edits**:

NMOS NM1 n2 n1 n0 VSS

R R2 n3 n1
