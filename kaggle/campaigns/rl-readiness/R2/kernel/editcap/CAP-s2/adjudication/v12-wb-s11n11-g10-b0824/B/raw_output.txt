1. **Diagnosis**: The binding constraint (S11_max_db) fails because the input matching network (C4 and L1) is inherently narrowband. Reactive components (L/C) alone cannot maintain a low S11 across the wideband (0.8–2.4 GHz). The input impedance at VIN1 is dominated by the series LC network, which resonates at a single frequency and degrades return loss outside that point. The current-reuse push-pull topology lacks broadband impedance control at the input.
