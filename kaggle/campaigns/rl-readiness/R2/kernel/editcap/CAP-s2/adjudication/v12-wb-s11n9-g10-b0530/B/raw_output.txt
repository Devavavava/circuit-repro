1. **Diagnosis**: The failure stems from insufficient broadband input matching. The existing reactive (L/C) input network (C4, L1) cannot maintain a low S11 across the 0.5–3 GHz band. The input impedance is too inductive at lower frequencies and capacitive at higher frequencies, leading to a poor return loss (S11_max_db = -6.13 dB vs. required -9 dB). The circuit lacks a resistive element to stabilize the input impedance over the wideband.
