1. **Diagnosis of Failure**:  
The binding constraint `s11_max_db` is failing by -0.29 dB, meaning the worst-case return loss across the band is only -7.79 dB instead of the required -11 dB. This suggests that the input matching is insufficient for the wideband operation. The current topology relies on a series inductor and capacitor (C4 and L1) for DC blocking and input matching, but these reactive components are inherently narrowband. As a result, the input impedance is not sufficiently matched across the full 0.8–2.4 GHz band, leading to a poor S11. Additionally, the current-reuse topology may not provide sufficient impedance transformation or phase control to achieve broadband matching without additional feedback or resistive elements.
