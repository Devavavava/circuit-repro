### 1. Diagnosis of the Failure

The LNA is failing due to an insufficient gain (S21_db), which is below the required 16 dB at 1.6 GHz. The anchor netlist implements a **common-gate (CG) LNA** with a **source-degenerated inductor (L1)** and a **tuned drain load (L2)**. While the CG topology inherently provides a broadband input match (due to the 1/gm impedance), it suffers from **inherently low gain** and **poor gain-bandwidth product**, especially when the drain is loaded with a reactive element like an inductor (L2). The L2 inductor provides a high-impedance load at resonance, but the gain is still constrained by the transconductance (gm) of the transistor and the overall impedance matching. Additionally, the input is DC-blocked by C1 and matched via L1, which is not sufficient to ensure high gain at 1.6 GHz. The circuit also lacks **active feedback or impedance boosting** to increase gain and stability.

### 2. Structural Edits (Proposed Topologies)

#### Edit 1: **Common-Source (CS) LNA with Source Degeneration and Resistive Feedback**
This topology improves gain by using a CS configuration and adds a **resistive feedback** path from the output to the source to help stabilize and improve gain.
