Frozen NDL@256 protocol (nb class, `--spec wifi24` screen, seed 1337, n=256),

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P5-v3 (baseline) | **100** | 100.0 | 0.224 | 0.573 | 80.5% |

**Verdict: v6 is NOT adopted as the headline generator.** The gate is
adopt-only-if-better vs the v3 baseline of 100, and v6 measures **93 — a 7-point
regression**. Tripwires are all quiet (termination 100%, inductor ratio 0.222 vs
0.224, median NN-sim flat at 0.575), so this is a diversity shortfall, not a collapse.

Two things keep v6 useful and it is the checkpoint Track B sized from:
* it is the **best of the rfb_cs-era generators** (v6 93 > v4 89 > v5 84) and the only
  one trained on the `rfb_cs3`-bearing set — the family that closed Gate D1;
* it has the **highest structural screen-pass rate** measured (84.0% wifi24 /
  85.9% dhruva-l1).

The honest read: NDL has been *falling* since v3 (100 → 89 → 84) as successive
fine-tunes concentrate mass on the hand-added rfb families. v6 partly recovers it
(+9 over v5) but does not restore v3's diversity.

## 4. ⚠ NDL overstates novelty — measured (unplanned finding)

The frozen NDL protocol scores a sample "novel" if its WL hash is **not in the
41-circuit corpus**. It does **not** check the `templates.py` archetype set — which is
what the generator now trains on. Measuring against both references:

| pool | screen-passing distinct | archetype copies | corpus copies | **truly novel** | samples that are archetype copies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P5-v3 | 113 | 48 | 13 | **52** | 97/209 (46.4%) |
| P5-v5 | 95 | 49 | 11 | **35** | 119/220 (54.1%) |
| **P5-v6** | 103 | 50 | 10 | **43** | **113/220 (51.4%)** |

(vs `dhruva-l1`'s L0 screen; archetype reference = the 148 archetypes `templates.py`
mints as of the check, a conservative superset of the 135 v6 trained on.)

**About half of every "novel" P5 pool is verbatim regeneration of training
archetypes.** The ordering between checkpoints is unaffected (v6 43 > v5 35, both
< v3 52 — same ranking as NDL), so no past adopt/reject decision flips, but any claim
of the form "the generator discovered X" must be checked against the archetype set,
not just the corpus. Track B enforced this for every candidate below
(`trackb_g4.py --novel-only`).

## 6b. ⚠ `size.polish` escapes the spec device box (bug, for Track A)

`size.polish` (`lna/size.py:298`) ascends the min-margin by scaling each sizable
parameter by `(1 ± step)` and **never clamps to `size.kind_ranges(spec)`**, which is
the box ZOAF searches inside. Consequence: polish-derived "feasible" points can use
devices the spec forbids.

Audit of **every feasible row in the store** (18 rows with params + tokens):
**6 are out-of-box, and all 6 are polish-derived.**

| spec | wl_hash | how | violation |
|---|---|---|---|
| wifi24 | `624fc609363db6f9` | polish | L1 12.45 nH > l_max 12 nH |
| wifi24 | `396b90321529157a` | polish | L1 13.8 nH > 12 nH |
| wifi24 | `c2023255140d34c7` | polish | L1 **18.25 nH > 12 nH (+52%)** |
| wifi24 | `c0e71d7934f521cc` | polish | C4 14 pF > c_f max 10 pF |
| gps-l1 | `0f1917c4fdcdd6e3` | polish+polish | C1 16 pF, C3 15 pF > 10 pF |
| dhruva-l1 | `20bca9a7c3a5f263` | polish-r0 (**Track B, withdrawn**) | L1 18.1 nH, L2 15.6 nH > 15 nH |

The dhruva **4-band D1/D2 family is clean** (its inductors are 13.5 / 10.9 nH, inside
the box) — Gates D1/D2 are unaffected. The affected rows are 4 of the wifi24
feasible set and 1 of the 2 gps-l1 Gate-B1 designs, which should be re-derived under
a clamped polish before they are quoted again.

**Track B did not edit `size.py`** (concurrency contract). The clamped implementation
is `lna/_trackb_bpolish.py::bounded_polish` — a copy of `size.polish` that clamps
every trial into its kind's range and refuses to step outward from a bound; it is
what produced the verified §6 result. Landing it in `size.py` is a ~5-line change.

**Track B's own bad row is still in the append-only store** and must be filtered:
`spec=dhruva-l1`, `wl_hash=20bca9a7c3a5f263`, `provenance.how="polish-r0"`. The
superseding good row is the same wl_hash with
`provenance.how="bounded-polish"`, `provenance.in_box_verified=true`,
recipe `p5v6-gen-boxed`.
