# 24 — P5-v8: corpus + 2 new externals, warm retrain from P5-v7

**Status:** EXECUTED 2026-08-21 (contained CPU host). Verdict recorded below.
**Adoption pointer NOT flipped** — held for the user's review round (all findings

## 0. What this is

The §24 "add structure the model has never seen" lever, applied to the two
externals ingested in round 2 (main `5d55f0a`): `paper-sige-hbt-resfb` and
`paper-nc-cc-inductorless`. The corpus went **9 → 11 externals** (manifest
`n_ingested: 11`). P5-v8 = the adopted P5-v7 (`ft_p5v7_v2.pth`, nb 79 / wb 41)
warm-started **one stage** on the expanded corpus, evaluated under the frozen
NDL@256 protocol with adopt-only-if-better. Ties go to the incumbent.

This is a direct sequel to §24 (adopted v7 by adding 9 real circuits) and a
deliberate contrast to §28 (P5-v8 winners-recycle, REJECTED nb 79→67 because the
winners channel re-injects archetype structure). **The winners channel is
excluded here** so the two new externals are the single new-structure variable.

## 2. Recipe drift vs v7 — recorded, all forced by the contained-host artifact state

The pre-authored `lna/_v8_train.sh` / `_v8_launch.sh` were written for the §28
**winners-recycle** experiment (Stage-3 Loop B), not this 2-externals lever, and
they assume corpus/era state that has since moved. They were NOT used. This run
is a new sidecar `lna/_v8b_train.sh`. Deviations from v7's stage-B recipe, each
recorded:

1. **One warm-start stage from `ft_p5v7_v2.pth`** (task spec), vs v7's two stages
   from `Pretrain.pth`. Every arm in this program takes best-val at epoch 0–1
   (§18.3), so a single warm stage is the right shape and the drift is minor for
   the shipped artifact.
2. **Templates = current 148-arch emission.** v7's stage B used the 118-arch
   `templates_train.pre_dhruva.json`, which is gitignored and gone; the archetype
   set has since grown 118 → 135 → **148**. Reproducing v7's exact template
   channel is impossible on this host; the current set is the faithful successor.
3. **Winners channel excluded** (see §0).
4. **General-corpus replay disabled** (empty `Training.npy` → the `if gen else []`
   guard skips it). v7's exact replay is unreproducible here (the full augmented
   AnalogGenie pretraining corpus never existed on this host; the checkpoint was
   imported from the GPU era). Replay is `<OTHER>`-classed regularization,
   orthogonal to the LNA NDL metric, and best-val ships at epoch 0–1 so it barely
   touches the shipped checkpoint. Omission is **comparison-neutral and
   conservative**: any resulting handicap to v8 can only cause a false *reject*
   (ties → incumbent), never a false adopt.

**Reference stick.** v7's published nb 79 / wb 41 were under the then-frozen
`ref-v3[198h/d05390da]` (9 externals). The corpus expansion re-freezes the
reference to **`ref-v3[200h/3c3da3d9]`** (41 corpus + 11 external + 148 arch).
Per §24.2's own logic (a regurgitation of the corpus's own new training data must
count as a copy, not novelty), the current 200h stick is the correct one. **Both
v7 and v8 are re-scored on the 200h stick** for an apples-to-apples verdict (v7's
pools were re-sampled from `ft_p5v7_v2.pth` since its seq*.txt were gitignored and
not on disk).

CPU only, `nice -n 10`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=8` (~5 effective cores on this box).

| **train** (warm from v7, 40 requested) | **best val 0.2355 @ epoch 1**; train collapses 0.230→0.180→0.164→0.153 while val rises 0.2363→**0.2355**→0.2387→0.2403 |

| re-sample v7 nb / wb (for same-stick eval) | 158 s / 138 s |

**⚠ Recorded deviation (mirrors §24.1).** The run was **stopped after epoch 3**,
not run to 40. Best-val is epoch 1 and the checkpoint was written there; val rose
monotonically at epochs 2 and 3 (0.2387, 0.2403), and §18.3 measured 40
consecutive rising epochs on this codebase — §24.1 shipped v7ctl's epoch-1 best-val
checkpoint on exactly this reasoning. The on-disk `ft_p5v8b.pth` is the epoch-1
artifact (2 saves total: epoch 0 then epoch 1; epochs 2–3 did not overwrite it).
The box's per-epoch CPU throughput was erratic (2.8 → >6 min/epoch with no memory
pressure and no competing process), so completing 36 more epochs to re-confirm known
overfitting was not spent. Approx **≈1.6 CPU-hours** total for the whole task's
train+sample+augment.

## 4. NDL@256 comparison — frozen protocol, n=256, seed 1337, `ref-v3[200h/3c3da3d9]`

nb pool screened under `wifi24`, wb pool under `wideband-sdr` (the frozen
per-channel specs, §_ndl_refv3). **Both v7 and v8 were sampled on THIS host
(CPU, seed 1337, n=256) and scored on the identical 200h stick** — the only
apples-to-apples comparison, since v7's published nb 79 / wb 41 were measured on
the GPU-era pool under the frozen 198h stick, and CPU vs GPU sampling is not
bit-identical (v7's own checkpoint re-samples to nb 57 / wb 35 here — see the
caveat below).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P5-v7** (re-sampled, same stick) | nb | **57** | 65.2% | 52.3% (**24.6%** / 27.7% / 0.0%) | 1.000 | 99.2% | 99.2% | 0.211 |
| **P5-v8** | nb | **50** | **76.2%** | 70.3% (**38.3%** / 29.7% / **2.3%**) | 1.000 | 100.0% | 99.2% | 0.228 |
| **P5-v7** (re-sampled, same stick) | wb | **35** | 28.1% | 41.8% (14.5% / 27.3% / 0.0%) | **0.853** | 99.6% | 98.4% | 0.128 |
| **P5-v8** | wb | **38** | **58.6%** | 68.0% (16.0% / **50.8%** / 1.2%) | 1.000 | 99.2% | 100.0% | **0.062** |

For historical continuity: v7's **published** numbers (GPU era, frozen
`ref-v3[198h/d05390da]`) were **nb 79 / wb 41**.

**Reading.**
* **★ nb REGRESSES: NDL 57 → 50, and archetype copies rebound 24.6% → 38.3%.**
  This is the §28 mechanism via a different channel: the single warm stage over
  the **larger 148-arch** template set (v7 stage-B used 118) re-emphasized
  archetype structure, back near the P5-v3/v7ctl baseline (37.9%). The two new
  externals are 128 rows / 1.8% of the mix — too small to overcome it. nb spec-L0
  actually *rises* (65→76%), i.e. the generator emits more screen-passers but more
  of them are archetype copies, so distinct-novel falls.
* **★ wb IMPROVES on three axes but pays the median.** NDL 35 → 38, spec-L0
  28→59%, and **inductor ratio 0.128 → 0.062** — repairing the exact regression
  §24 adopted v7 *despite* (wb ind ratio the wrong way for an inductorless spec).
  The two new externals include `paper-nc-cc-inductorless` (0 inductors, 18
  devices) and `paper-sige-hbt-resfb`, both wideband — the wb channel is where the
  new structure landed. **Cost:** wb median NN-sim 0.853 → 1.000 (v8's median
  screen-passing wb sample is again an exact copy) and wb corpus copies 27→51%.

## 6. Adopt-only-if-better verdict (verbatim rule)

Rule (§14.5, unchanged): **adopt only if it beats the re-frozen NDL at
equal-or-better inductor ratio, with every tripwire quiet; ties go to the
incumbent.**

| clause | nb | wb |
|---|---|---|
| NDL beats baseline (same stick) | **50 < 57 ✗** | 38 > 35 ✓ |
| inductor ratio equal-or-better | 0.228 ≥ 0.211 ✓ | **0.062 < 0.128 ✓** |
| every tripwire quiet | ✓ | ✓ |
| median NN-sim (recorded) | 1.000 = 1.000 | **1.000 ← 0.853 ✗** |
| spec-L0 (recorded, not a gate) | 65→76% | 28→59% |
| copy fraction (recorded) | 52→70% ✗ | 42→68% ✗ |

**Verdict: WORSE — REJECT.** The nb channel is the one the NDL baseline is
defined on and the one carrying every feasible design this program has produced
(§24.3), and its NDL **regresses 57 → 50** on the same stick. The rule requires
NDL to *beat* the baseline; it does not, so v8 does not replace v7 — and this is
not even a tie (ties → incumbent would also hold). The wb channel's gains are real
and worth carrying to a future arm (they repair §24's wb inductor-ratio
regression), but they cannot rescue a nb regression under a per-channel-honest
reading, and copy fraction rises on **both** channels.

**★ The reusable finding, third instance of one law.** Only *new* structure the
model has never seen raises NDL; re-weighting structure it already has lowers it.
§18 removed templates late (NDL down), §28 recycled winners (down), §24 added 9
real circuits (up). Here the single warm stage over the grown 148-arch template
set behaved like a re-weighting of already-seen archetypes (arch copies 24.6 →
38.3%), and the 128 new-external rows (1.8% of the mix) were too small a dose of
genuinely-new structure to move nb — where §24's 481 new rows (5.8%) moved it
+27. **The lever remains dose of new structure, and 2 circuits is below
threshold for the nb channel.**

**⚠ Adopted-baseline pointer NOT flipped.** Per the standing ruling that all
findings are reviewed together, the pointer stays at P5-v7 (`ft_p5v7_v2.pth`) and
the flip decision — here a *non*-flip, since the verdict is REJECT — is left for
the user's review round. Nothing about the incumbent changed.
